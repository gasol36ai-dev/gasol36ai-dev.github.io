# Proprietary Indicator: Orbital-Biological-Compute (OBC) Convergence Gate

## 🌐 Overview
The OBC Convergence Gate identifies systemic inflection points where the three pillars of future sovereign capability—Orbital Intelligence (Space), Biological Autonomy (Wetware), and Heterogeneous Compute (Silicon/Quantum)—converge to create a non-linear step-function in global productivity and strategic dominance.

## ⚡ Transmission Mapping
**[Event]**: Simultaneous achievement of (1) orbital AI-on-chip density, (2) scalable bio-digital interface latency, and (3) quantum-optical compute interconnect scalability.
**[Mechanism]**: The convergence of these domains enables "Hyper-Distributed Autonomy," where intelligence is ubiquitous, biologically integrated, and computationally unbounded.
**[Reaction]**: A massive, non-linear shift in economic and military power, characterized by the emergence of "Sovereign Autonomy Premiums" in capital and talent markets.

### 1. Map (The Convergence Vector)
$$\mathbf{\Psi}_{OBC} = w_1 \cdot \mathcal{D}_{orb} + w_2 \cdot \mathcal{L}_{bio} + w_3 \cdot \mathcal{C}_{comp}$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{D}_{orb}$: Orbital Edge Intelligence Density (bits/s/kg in LEO).
- $\mathcal{L}_{bio}$: Bio-Digital Interface Bandwidth (bits/s/Hz in neural-wetware interfaces).
- $\mathcal{C}_{comp}$: Heterogeneous Compute Elasticity (FLOPS/Watt across optical-quantum fabrics).

### 2. Trigger (The Convergence Threshold)
The gate opens when the joint probability of all three vectors exceeding their respective critical thresholds $\theta$ simultaneously crosses a systemic confidence level $\Gamma$:

$$\text{Condition}_{OBC} = P(\mathcal{D}_{orb} > \theta_o \land \mathcal{L}_{bio} > \theta_b \land \mathcal{C}_{comp} > \theta_c) > \Gamma$$

### 3. Lock (The Strategic Response)
**"Extreme Autarky Protocol"**: 
Upon triggering, the system transitions to a **High-Density Sovereign State**, characterized by:
- **Physical Asset Hardening**: Immediate shift to un-tethered, distributed orbital and biological infrastructure.
- **Compute Isolation**: Transition to quantum-secure, optical-mesh-only communication protocols.
- **Liquidity Reallocation**: Massive capital migration from centralized financial rails to decentralized, programmable, bio-digital and orbital asset classes.

## 📈 Strategic Value
