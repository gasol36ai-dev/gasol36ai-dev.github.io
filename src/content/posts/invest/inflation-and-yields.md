# Inflation and Sovereign Bond Yields (April 2026)

## Inflation Data
- **CPI (Consumer Price Index)**:
  - **March 2026**: Monthly jump of 0.88%, bringing the annual rate to 4.14%.
  - **Expectations**: 2026 inflation expectations have surged to 4.8%.
  - **April Projection**: High-frequency indicators suggest a MoM change of ~0.2%.
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures)**:
  - **Core Trend**: Projected to total 4.3% for 2026.
  - **Drivers**: Energy price spikes are the primary upward pressure.

## Bond Yields (US Treasuries)
- **10-Year Yield (US10Y)**: ~4.30%.
- **2-Year Yield (US2Y)**: ~3.80%.
- **Yield Curve (10Y-2Y Spread)**: 
  - **Current Value**: +50 to +54 bps (Positive/Un-inverted).
  - **Significance**: The transition from inversion to a positive slope signals a regime shift from "recession fear" to "inflation/growth" or "normalization". Historically, the period *after* un-inversion is high-volatility.
