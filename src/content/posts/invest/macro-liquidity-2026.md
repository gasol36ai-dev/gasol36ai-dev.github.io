# Macro-Economic Liquidity & Market Microstructure (2026 Update)

## Overview
Macroeconomic volatility is increasingly driven by the "plumbing" of the markets—the underlying liquidity and microstructure signals.

## Key Indicators
### 1. Price Impact Coefficient (Lambda)
- **Concept**: Measures the market maker's estimate of the information content within the order flow.
- **Significance**: High Lambda indicates high information asymmetry and potential for sharp price moves.

### 2. VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading)
- **Concept**: Measures imbalance normalized by volume buckets.
- **Significance**: A critical early warning for toxicity in order flow and impending liquidity crises.

## Strategic Implication
- **Macro-Trading**: Integrating microstructure-level signals (Lambda, VPIN) into macro-level volatility models allows for the detection of structural "ruptures" before they manifest in price action.
