# Proprietary Indicator: Abyssal-Compute Resilience Gate (ACRG-2026)

## 🔍 Overview
The **Abyssal-Compute Resilience Gate (ACRG-2026)** is a composite decision indicator designed to evaluate the sovereign viability of subsea compute and resource extraction infrastructures. It quantifies the intersection of energy autonomy, physical security, and communication resilience in extreme oceanic environments.

## 🧬 Transmission Mapping: [Event] $\rightarrow$ [Mechanism] $\rightarrow$ [Reaction]
1. **[Energy Scarcity Pulse]** $\rightarrow$ **[OTEC/Tidal Flux Instability]** $\rightarrow$ **[Autonomous Power-State Reconfiguration]**
2. **[Acoustic Jamming Detection]** $\rightarrow$ **[Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) Triangulation]** $\rightarrow$ **[Adaptive Waveform/Frequency Hopping]**
3. **[Unauthorized Physical Proximity]** $\rightarrow$ **[SNN-based Acoustic Fingerprinting]** $\rightarrow$ **[Kinetic Deterrence/Drone Deployment]**
4. **[Cyber-Physical Integrity Breach]** $\rightarrow$ **[Hardware-Rooted Trust (TPM) Verification]** $\rightarrow$ **[Local Node Isolation/Air-gapping]**

## 🛠️ Mathematical Formalization
The indicator is defined by the **Subsea Autarky Coefficient ($\Psi_{sub}$)**:

$$\Psi_{sub} = \left( \frac{E_{harvested}}{E_{compute}} \right) \times \left( \frac{C_{link\_uptime}}{C_{total}} \right) \times \left( 1 - \frac{\text{Threat\_Density}}{\text{Security\_Resilience}} \right)$$

Where:
- $E_{harvested}$: Average energy harvested from subsea sources (OTEC, Tidal, etc.) per unit time.
- $E_{compute}$: Average energy consumed by the subsea compute mesh per unit time.
- $C_{link\_uptime}$: Ratio of successful communication link durations to total operational time.
- $C_{total}$: Total scheduled operational time.
- $\text{Threat\_Density}$: Frequency and severity of detected kinetic/acoustic/cyber threats.
- $\text{Security\_Resilience}$: The quantified effectiveness of automated defense protocols (e.g., response time to jamming).

## 🚦 Decision Gates
| $\Psi_{sub}$ Value | Status | Strategic Action |
|---|---|---|
| $\Psi_{sub} \ge 1.2$ | **Resilient Autarky** | Expand compute density; initiate deep-sea resource expansion. |
| $0.8 \le \Psi_{sub} < 1.2$ | **Contested Stability** | Optimize energy harvesting; implement redundant communication links. |
| $\Psi_{sub} < 0.8$ | **Critical Vulnerability** | Immediate withdrawal of high-value assets; prioritize kinetic/cyber defense. |

## 📈 Strategic Use Case
Used by sovereign oceanic powers and autonomous DePIN operators to monitor the health of "Abyssal Fortresses" and determine the timing for large-scale subsea infrastructure investments.
