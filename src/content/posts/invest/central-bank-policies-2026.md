# Central Bank Policies (April 2026)

## 🇺🇸 Federal Reserve (Fed)
- **Stance**: Highly uncertain. Baseline scenario: Real GDP growing close to potential.
- **Current Focus**: Balancing inflation spikes (March CPI 3.3%) with potential growth deceleration.
- **Market Expectation**: Pricing in a mix of holds and slight easing, but constrained by the "Energy-Inflation Chain".

## 🇪🇺 European Central Bank (ECB)
- **Stance**: Hawkish / Maintenance.
- **Current Status**: Expected to maintain Deposit Rate at 2.00%.
- **Outlook**: Seen as more hawkish than the Fed, with potential hikes later in 2026 or 2027 to combat persistent inflation.

## 🇯🇵 Bank of Japan (BoJ)
- **Stance**: Hawkish transition.
- **Current Status**: Policy rate was 0.75% in March.
- **Outlook**: Moving away from ultra-easy policy; potential hikes to stabilize the Yen and address domestic inflation.

## ⚖️ Divergence Summary
The core macro theme is the **divergence** between the Fed's cautious approach and the ECB/BoJ's hawkish tilt. This divergence is a primary driver of USD FX volatility and global capital flow rotations.
