# US Inflation Spike April 2026

April 2026 has seen a surprising and sharp resurgence in US inflation data, challenging the narrative of a "smooth glide path" to target.

## Key Data Points
- **CPI (Consumer Price Index)**: The March 2026 all-items index rose **3.3% Y/Y**, a significant jump from 2.4% in February. This was the largest monthly increase in a considerable period.
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures)**: Core PCE trends suggest a full-year 2026 total of **4.3%**, potentially higher due to recent energy price spikes.
- **Drivers**: Energy prices (gasoline) and a broad rise across half of the CPI basket (3%+ Y/Y).

## Impact on Policy
- **Fed Tension**: Creates a conflict between the Fed's desire to "hold" rates and the reality of rising prices.
- **Market Expectation**: Markets may begin pricing in a "higher for longer" scenario if the spike is not transitory.
