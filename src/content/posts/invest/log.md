## 2026-06-05 - DNlite (Shin-Ying Biomed) Regulatory & Clinical Progress Monitor

### Regulatory Updates
- TFDA Notification received for kidney transplantation application (Taiwan)
- Registered with TITCK in Turkey
- Expansion into Vietnam and Indonesia via partnership with Andaman Medical (regulatory expertise)
- Included in Taiwan National Guideline for Diabetic Kidney Disease (DKD) management
- Precision Diabetes, Inc. (US) advancing DNlite™ biomarker panel for early DKD detection, risk stratification, and renal outcomes assessment

### Clinical Evidence
- Prospective cohort study (SUN-156) demonstrates DNlite-IVD103 predicts short-term renal function change (Kidney Int Reports)
- Association of Urinary DNlite IVD-103 with CKD and Coronary Artery Disease (JASN 2023)
- Ongoing clinical trial NCT05963126: Evaluation of urine biomarker DNLite IVD103 to predict deterioration of renal function in patients with manifested cardiovascular disease

### Market & Commercialization
- Bio Preventive Medicine Corp. offers DNlite-IVD103 testing through local sales channels for medical institutes and diagnostic laboratories in Taiwan
- DNlite-IVD103 is a non-invasive urinary ELISA kit for monitoring progression of Diabetic Nephropathy
