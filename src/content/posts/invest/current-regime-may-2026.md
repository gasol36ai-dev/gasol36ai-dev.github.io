# Global Macro Regime - May 2026

## 🌍 Current State Overview
As of May 12, 2026, the global macro environment is characterized by a "Geopolitical Inflation Shock" overlapping a "Structural AI-Driven Growth" cycle. The dominant catalyst is the instability in the Middle East (Iran/Hormuz), which has shifted the inflation narrative from "last-mile" to "supply-side structural spike."

### 🏦 Central Bank Policy Matrix
| Central Bank | Current Rate | Stance | Primary Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fed** | 3.50% - 3.75% | Cautious Hold | Balancing AI-driven growth vs. Iran-related inflation. |
| **ECB** | 2.00% (Deposit) | Data-Dependent | Managing stagflation risks (high energy, low growth). |
| **BoJ** | 0.75% | Hawkish Normalization | Fighting second-round effects of imported inflation. |
| **BoE** | 3.75% | Potential Tightening | High sensitivity to energy-driven CPI spikes. |

### 📈 Key Macro Variables
- **Inflation**: US Core PCE at 3.2% (March 2026), showing renewed stickiness. Headline PCE at 3.5%.
- **Energy**: Brent Crude trading above $100/bbl, with spikes toward $120 during Hormuz blockade threats.
- **Yields**: US 10Y Nominal yields are seeing an "inflation premium" surge. Real Yields (10Y TIPS) have spiked recently to ~1.78%, breaking traditional asset correlations.
- **Currency**: USD (DXY) remains a pivot point; structural de-dollarization continues despite short-term safe-haven flows.

## ⚠️ Critical Risks
1. **Stagflation (Europe)**: High energy costs + low growth $\rightarrow$ potential mild recession.
2. **Fiscal Dominance (USA)**: Debt-to-GDP > 120% creates a permanent risk premium on Treasuries.
3. **Yen-Carry Trade Unwinding**: BoJ's aggressive normalization could trigger global liquidity shocks.
