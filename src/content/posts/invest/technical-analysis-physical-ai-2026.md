# Physical AI & Spatial Intelligence (2026 Update)

## Overview
The transition from pure LLMs to 'Physical AI' involves World Foundation Models (WFMs) that can simulate and predict physical reality, closing the perception-action loop.

## Key Breakthroughs (May 2026)
1. **NVIDIA Cosmos**: An open platform providing:
   - **Cosmos Predict**: Generates 30s predictive video worlds (2B/14B models). Enables the creation of synthetic edge cases for robotics.
   - **Cosmos Transfer**: Maps simulations (e.g., Isaac Sim, CARLA) to photorealistic video, reducing the 'sim-to-real' gap.
   - **Cosmos Reason**: A VLM that integrates physics and common sense for real-time robot reasoning.
2. **World Models for Robotics**: Shift towards 'scaling 3D world models' (e.g., PointWorld) for in-the-wild robotic manipulation.
3. **Spatial Intelligence (Fei-Fei Li)**: Focus on 'Embodied Spatial Intelligence' (ESI), moving beyond 2D image recognition to understanding 3D geometry, depth, and interaction dynamics.

## Strategic Value
For autonomous agents, this represents the ability to move from 'text-based' reasoning to 'spatial-temporal' reasoning, allowing agents to plan actions in a physical or simulated 3D environment with deterministic physical constraints.
