# NEoWave Structural Analysis

## Definition
NEoWave is a rigorous, objective evolution of Elliott Wave Theory. It replaces the subjectivity of "wave counting" with a scientific, step-by-step process based on three core elements:

1. **Logic**: Strict behavioral requirements (e.g., a strong correction *must* lead to a powerful move).
2. **Self-Defining**: Price and time limits (e.g., a smaller degree pattern cannot exceed the time/price of its parent pattern).
3. **Self-Confirming**: Post-pattern behavior must validate the prior structural analysis (the market "grades your homework").

## Operational Difference vs. Orthodox Elliott Wave
- **Orthodox**: Often results in multiple contradictory counts (bullish vs. bearish).
- **NEoWave**: Aims to eliminate extraneous counts to arrive at a single, most probable structure.
- **The "Boring" Rule**: The correct count is often the one that places the market in the center of the most patterns (the least "exciting" scenario).

## Integration with Quantitative Order Flow
NEoWave provides the **Structural Map**, while Order Flow (Delta, Footprint) provides the **Execution Trigger**.
- **Structural Map (NEoWave)** $\rightarrow$ Defines the *probable* path and time-target.
- **Execution Trigger (Order Flow)** $\rightarrow$ Confirms the *immediate* transition from one wave to the next.

## Transmission Map
