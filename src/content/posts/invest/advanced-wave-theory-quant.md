# Advanced Wave Theory and Quantitative Patterns

## 1. Elliott Wave Theory (EWT) - Macro Perspective
EWT identifies cyclical patterns driven by crowd psychology. The core structure is an 8-wave cycle (5 Impulse, 3 Corrective).

### Core Structure
- **Impulse Waves (1, 3, 5):** Move in the trend direction. Wave 3 is typically the strongest and longest.
- **Corrective Waves (2, 4, A, B, C):** Move against the trend.
- **Wave Degrees:** Patterns repeat fractally across all timeframes.

### Modern Refinements
- **Wave 3 Displacement:** Modern quant analysis identifies Wave 3 through high-velocity "displacement" and the creation of significant Fair Value Gaps (FVGs).
- **Wave 5 Exhaustion:** Wave 5 often targets major liquidity pools (Equal Highs/Lows) before reversing.

---

## 2. Smart Money Concepts (SMC) - Precision Execution
SMC focuses on institutional behavior and the "manipulation" of retail liquidity.

### Core Concepts
- **Order Blocks (OB):** The last opposite candle before a strong move. Represents institutional entry zones.
- **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** A three-candle sequence where the first and third candle's wicks do not overlap, leaving a "gap" in price delivery.
- **Break of Structure (BOS):** When price closes above/below a previous structural high/low, confirming trend continuation.
- **Change of Character (ChoCh):** The first sign of a trend reversal; price breaks the last structural low/high that led to the new high/low.
- **Liquidity Sweeps:** Price probes beyond a known high/low to trigger stop-losses before reversing.

---

## 3. Synthesis: EWT + SMC Fusion
The most powerful application is using EWT for directional bias (The "What") and SMC for the entry (The "How").

### Fusion Mapping
| Elliott Wave | SMC Equivalent | Trading Logic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wave 1** | Initial BOS | Marks the end of the prior trend and start of new impulse. |
| **Wave 2** | OB Retest | Price retraces to the Wave 1 Order Block $\rightarrow$ High probability entry. |
| **Wave 3** | FVG Creation | Strong displacement. Trade the FVG re-entry. |
| **Wave 4** | OB / FVG Fill | Complex correction. Look for Wave 3 OB retest. |
| **Wave 5** | Liquidity Sweep | Targets liquidity pools $\rightarrow$ Prepare for major reversal. |
| **Wave A-B-C** | Structural Shift | ChoCh $\rightarrow$ New trend initiation. |

### Integrated Strategy
1. **Macro Bias:** Use EWT to identify if we are in an Impulse (1-5) or Corrective (A-B-C) phase.
2. **Zone Identification:** Mark Order Blocks and FVGs within the current wave.
3. **Execution:**
    - Entry at Wave 2 / Wave 4 OB retests.
    - Entry on Wave 3 FVG fills.
    - Exit at Wave 5 liquidity targets.
