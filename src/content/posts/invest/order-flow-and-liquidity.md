# Order Flow and Liquidity Trading (2026 Perspective)

## Overview
Order Flow trading focuses on the actual buy and sell orders entering the market, rather than lagging indicators. In 2026, the focus has shifted toward **Liquidity Mapping** and **Order Flow Imbalance**.

## Key Concepts
- **Order Flow Imbalance**: Occurs when there is a significant disparity between aggressive buyers and sellers at a specific price level.
- **Liquidity Pools**: Areas where a large number of orders are clustered (stops, limit orders). Price tends to gravitate toward these pools to "fuel" the next move.
- **Market Profile**: Analysis of the time spent at each price level, identifying the Value Area (VA) and Point of Control (POC).

## Strategic Transmission
`[Liquidity Pool Identification] -> [Price Gravity/Attraction] -> [Liquidity Sweep] -> [Reversal/Trend Continuation]`

## 2026 Trends
- Integration with real-time AI visualization tools to "see" the order book depth in 3D.
- Higher correlation between high-frequency order flow and macro-economic volatility spikes.
