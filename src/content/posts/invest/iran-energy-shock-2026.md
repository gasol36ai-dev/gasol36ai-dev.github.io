# Iran Energy Shock 2026

## Overview
The escalation of the Iran-Israel conflict in early 2026 triggered a severe global energy supply disruption, marking the most significant shock since the 1973 Oil Embargo.

## Key Impacts
- **Oil Prices**: Brent crude surged from $72 to a peak of **$120 per barrel**.
- **Logistics**: The **Strait of Hormuz**, through which ~20% of global oil passes, became commercially non-functional for periods, disrupting LNG and oil shipping.
- **Inflation**: Directly caused a jump in US Headline CPI to **3.3% in March 2026** (from 2.4% in February).

## Systemic Shifts
- **Sovereign Wealth Fund (SWF) Pivot**: Gulf SWFs are reviewing trillion-dollar strategies, accelerating diversification into **AI infrastructure, Renewables, and Asian growth markets** to hedge against regional instability.
- **Supply Chain Architecture**: The shock has accelerated the shift toward regionalized energy security and "resilience" over "efficiency."
