# Proprietary Indicator: Agentic Liquidity-Volatility Resonance (ALVR)

## 1. Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### **Map** (Multi-Domain Inputs)
*   **Domain 1: Agentic Micro-Microstructure** 
    *   Input: *Clustered Flow Divergence* (Difference between raw LOB imbalance and agentic execution footprint).
*   **Domain 2: Macro-Regime Volatility** 
    *   Input: *Regime-Transition Index (RTI)* (Detecting shifts from low-vol/high-liquidity to high-vol/low-liquidity).
*   **Domain 3: Cognitive/Semantic Herding** 
    *   Input: *Semantic Sentiment Dispersion* (Spread of sentiment across agentic decision-making nodes).

### **Trigger** (The Inflection Gate)
*   **Condition:** $\text{ALVR Signal} = \left( \text{Clustered Flow Divergence} \uparrow \right) \wedge \left( \text{RTI} \uparrow \right) \wedge \left( \text{Semantic Dispersion} \downarrow \right)$
*   **Observation:** When clustered flow becomes highly synchronized (low dispersion) during a regime transition (high RTI), it indicates a non-linear, agent-driven liquidity vacuum.

### **Lock** (Operational Response)
*   **Immediate Action:** 
    *   **Risk Budget:** Automatically trigger a $50\%$ haircut to active position sizes.
    *   **Liquidity Buffer:** Reallocate $20\%$ of capital to high-convexity "Deep Liquidity" assets (e.g., short-duration Treasuries, highly liquid crypto-staples).
    *   **Execution Mode:** Switch from *Aggressive Market-Taking* to *Passive Limit-Order Harvesting*.
