# Central Bank Policy Tracker (May 2026)

## 🇺🇸 Federal Reserve (Fed)
- **Current Rate**: 3.50% - 3.75%
- **Stance**: **Hawkish Hold**. 
- **Latest Action (April 29)**: Maintained rates.
- **Key Driver**: Balancing "solid economic activity" vs "elevated inflation" (energy-driven).
- **Forward Outlook**: Most members see a "Hold" for the near term. Some project one 25bp cut in 2026, but others warn of potential hikes if inflation persists above 3%.
- **Critical Risk**: A "Pivot to Hike" in H2 2026 to combat sticky inflation.

## 🇪🇺 European Central Bank (ECB)
- **Current Rate**: 2.00%
- **Stance**: **Hawkish Pivot**.
- **Latest Action (April 30)**: Maintained rates, but signaled urgency.
- **Key Driver**: Energy price inflation (jumped to 10.9% in April).
- **Forward Outlook**: High probability of a **June hike (to 2.25%)** to anchor inflation expectations.
- **Critical Risk**: Stagflation (Low growth + High energy-driven inflation).

## 🇯🇵 Bank of Japan (BoJ)
- **Current Rate**: 0.75%
- **Stance**: **Normalization**.
- **Latest Action**: Signaling further hikes.
- **Key Driver**: Broadening inflation and Yen weakness.
- **Forward Outlook**: Expected **June hike (to 1.0%)**.
- **Critical Risk**: Rapid Yen appreciation triggering a global carry trade unwind.
