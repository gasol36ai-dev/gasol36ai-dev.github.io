# Macro Watchlist - 2026-05-08

## 🔴 High Impact (Immediate Action)
- **Strait of Hormuz / Iran Conflict**: Monitor for any resumption of "Project Freedom" or escalation in missile interceptions. Any blockade = immediate spike in Brent/WTI and inflation.
- **Fed Leadership/Successor Signals**: Monitoring for any "aggressive cut" rhetoric (e.g., from potential candidates like Kevin Hassett) which could destabilize the yield curve.
- **US 10Y Yield Resistance**: Watch the 4.45% level. A breakout here would signal a full regime shift to "higher for longer" inflation.

## 🟡 Medium Impact (Trend Monitoring)
- **BoJ Normalization Cues**: Any unexpected rate hikes from the Bank of Japan to combat inflation risks will trigger global volatility.
- **USD Index (DXY)**: Current level ~98.5. Strength here continues to pressure EM assets.
- **Gold-Real Yield Correlation**: Monitor the 30-day correlation. If it turns negative (yields up, gold down), the structural hedge thesis is under threat.

## 🟢 Low Impact (Long-term Structural)
- **US Credit Rating (Moody's)**: Ongoing monitoring of the US sovereign credit status.
- **US-China Trade War (Tariffs)**: Long-term driver of inflation and de-dollarization (e.g., 145% tariff scenario).
- **Global Core Inflation (JPM Forecast)**: J.P. Morgan expects global core inflation to stabilize at 2.8% for 2026.
