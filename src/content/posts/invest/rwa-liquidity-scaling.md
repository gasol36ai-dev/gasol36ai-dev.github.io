# Concept: RWA Liquidity Scaling

## Definition
The process of increasing the depth, velocity, and accessibility of Real World Asset (RWA) tokenized versions on-chain, moving from fragmented, slow-settlement representations to high-throughput, chain-abstracted financial instruments.

## Core Pillars
- **Chain Abstraction:** Removing the user's need to interact with specific blockchains to access RWA liquidity.
- **24/7 Settlement:** Transitioning from TradFi business hours to algorithmic, instant mint/redeem cycles.
- **High-Performance Rails:** Using AppChains and CLOBs to support institutional-grade market making.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Crossroads Layer:** A smart contract layer for chain-abstracted assets.
- **Inter-Bank Rails:** (e.g., Kinexys, Ripple) for near real-time cross-border redemptions.
- **CLMMs:** Concentrated Liquidity Market Makers for tight-spread RWA trading.
