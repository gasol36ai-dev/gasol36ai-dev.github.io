# Inflation Data (April 2026)

## United States
- **Headline CPI**: ~3.3% (March 2026).
- **Core CPI (MoM)**: ~0.21% (April 2026).
- **Core PCE (MoM)**: ~0.26% (April 2026).
- **Annual Core CPI Forecast**: ~3.2% (J.P. Morgan).
- **Trend**: "Sticky" inflation. While core indicators are stable, headline inflation is pushed up by energy prices.

## Eurozone
- **Annual Inflation**: 2.6% (March 2026).
- **Trend**: Upward revision due to energy and service sector persistence.

## Global Trend
- Core CPI expected at 2.8% globally.
- US remains a high-inflation outlier compared to the Euro area (1.9% projection) and UK (2.4% projection).
