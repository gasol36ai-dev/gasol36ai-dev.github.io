# Geopolitical Risk Analysis (April 2026)

## ⚔️ Middle East Conflict
- **Status**: Active war in the Middle East.
- **Financial Impact**: IMF Global Financial Stability Report (April 2026) identifies "elevated financial stability risks".
- **Transmission**: 
  - [Conflict] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ Energy Prices] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ Headline Inflation] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ Bond Yields].
  - [Conflict] $\rightarrow$ [Systemic Risk $\uparrow$] $\rightarrow$ [Safe Haven Demand $\uparrow$] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ Gold / USD].

## 🌏 Emerging Markets (EM) & Capital Flows
- **Carry Trade Fragility**: EM flows are heavily dominated by carry-trade-driven debt portfolios.
- **Risk**: A sharp reversal in global risk appetite (due to Middle East escalation) could trigger a liquidity crisis in lower-rated EM economies.
- **Imbalance**: Diversification away from USD is happening, but the mechanism is volatile.
