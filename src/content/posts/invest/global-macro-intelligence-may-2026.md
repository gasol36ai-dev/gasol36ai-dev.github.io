# Macro Intelligence Report - May 2026

## 🌍 Global Macro Landscape
The macro regime has shifted to a **Geopolitical-Inflationary Shock** phase. The "Bright Spot at neutral" framing has collapsed, replaced by a stark stagflationary reality in the US and policy divergence in Europe and Japan.

### 1. United States: The Stagflation Trap
- **GDP**: Q1 2026 GDP grew at 2.0% annualized (missed 2.3% consensus).
- **Inflation**: Q1 PCE ran at 4.5% (core 4.3%), while CPI remained relatively subdued.
- **Policy**: FOMC held at 3.50-3.75% (8-4 la split), reflecting a Committee that is deeply divided.
- **Key Insight**: The "PCE-CPI Wedge" (structural inflation from AI and and energy) makes the Fed's path extremely difficult. Growth is missing, but inflation is overshooting.

### 2. Eurozone: The Hawkish Pivot
- **Policy**: ECB held at 2.00% but warned of intensified upside inflation risks.
- **Expectation**: High probability (~75%) of a June hike to 2.25%.
- **Risk**: More vulnerable to energy disruptions than the US.

### 3. Japan: Gradual Normalization
- **Policy**: BoJ is moving toward 1.0% policy rate.
- **Insight**: Inflation expectations are firming, and the economy is exiting its negative GDP gap. Normalization is a long-term trend.

### 4. Geopolitical Trigger: The Iran-Hormuz Axis
- **Trigger**: Blockade of the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Impact**: Oil prices (Brent >$100) create a structural floor for inflation and act as a flight-to-quality trigger for Gold.

## 🛠️ Strategic Implications
- **Gold**: Strong systemic risk hedge. Decoupled from real yields.
- **Tech**: Under pressure from rising real yields and energy costs.
- **USD**: Strong, driven by policy divergence and safety.
