# Order Flow Trading

## Core Concept
The analysis of the Limit Order Book (LOB) and the Time & Sales (the "Tape") to identify real-time aggression and institutional intent. Order flow reveals the "WHY" behind price movement.

## 2026 Strategic Integration
Order flow is most powerful when paired with [[market-profile]]. While Market Profile identifies *where* value is, Order Flow identifies *if* and *how* that value is shifting.

## Transmission Map
`[Order Flow Imbalance (Net Demand)] -> [Low Market Depth/Liquidity] -> [Price Volatility Spike] -> [Market Profile Value Area Shift]`

## High-Conviction Signal
A trend breakout is confirmed when aggressive order flow (hitting the bid/offer) occurs at the edges of the Market Profile Value Area, leading to a rapid shift in the Point of Control (POC).
