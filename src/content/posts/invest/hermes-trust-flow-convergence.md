# Hermes Trust-Flow Convergence (TFC) Indicator

## Overview
The TFC Indicator is a proprietary Hermes framework designed to identify high-probability regime reversals by detecting the convergence of **Systemic Trust Divergence** (Macro) and **Clustered Flow Exhaustion** (Micro). 

Traditional quantitative models often fail during "Black Swan" or "Systemic Trust" regimes where historical correlations (e.g., Gold vs. Real Yields) break. TFC filters these anomalies to prevent "fighting the trend" until the structural foundation has actually shifted.

## Logic Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

The TFC operates on a deterministic three-stage gate:

### 1. Map (Macro Regime)
**Condition**: $\text{Systemic Trust Divergence}$
- **Signal**: Gold $\uparrow$ while Real Yields $\uparrow$.
- **Interpretation**: The market has shifted from "Rate-Logic" (interest rate driven) to "Systemic-Trust-Logic" (hedge against institutional/sovereign failure).
- **Role**: This establishes the "Permissive Zone." If this divergence is not present, the TFC remains inactive for systemic-hedge trades.

### 2. Trigger (Micro Signal)
**Condition**: $\text{Clustered Flow Exhaustion}$
- **Signal**: Identification of a "Depth Vacuum" in the dominant trend direction (e.g., USD Longs) accompanied by a surge in Price Velocity in the opposite direction.
- **Requirement**: Confirmation of 3/4 Clustered Flow signals (Velocity, Volume, Flow, Depth).
- **Role**: Identifies the precise moment where the dominant liquidity provider has stopped supporting the current trend.

### 3. Lock (Confirmation)
**Condition**: $\text{VOS Regime Shift}$
- **Signal**: The Volatility Operating System (VOS) transitions from **Exhaustion** $\rightarrow$ **Expansion**.
- **Verification**: Price breaks and holds above/below the TPO Value Area High/Low of the previous session.
- **Role**: The final filter to ensure the move is not a "fake-out" or temporary liquidity gap.

## Convergence vs. Divergence Lens

| State | Macro (Trust) | Micro (Flow) | Result | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Convergence** | Divergent (Trust $\uparrow$) | Exhausted (Trend $\downarrow$) | **High-Conviction Reversal** | Aggressive Entry |
| **Divergence** | Aligned (Rate-Logic) | Exhausted (Trend $\downarrow$) | **Mean Reversion** | Scalp Only |
| **Divergence** | Divergent (Trust $\uparrow$) | Strong (Trend $\uparrow$) | **Absorption / Trap** | Wait/Avoid |

## Historical Application Hypothesis
In a scenario where the USD is trending strongly due to "The Great Divergence" (US resilience vs EU stagflation), a standard flow-imbalance model would suggest continuing long USD. However, if the TFC detects:
1. Gold rising despite US yields climbing (Trust Divergence),
2. A depth vacuum appearing on USD bid-side (Flow Exhaustion),
3. VOS shifting to Expansion (Confirmation),
The TFC would signal a structural top in USD, providing a proprietary edge over purely technical or purely macro models.
