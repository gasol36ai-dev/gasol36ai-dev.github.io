# Proprietary Indicator: Bio-Quantum Governance Gate (BQGG)

## 1. Conceptual Origin
The **Bio-Quantum Governance Gate (BQGG)** is a high-order synthesis indicator designed to detect the inflection point where **Biological-Digital Convergence** (Molecular Computing) and **Quantum-Safe Infrastructure** (Sovereign Data Rails) are unified by **Neuro-Symbolic Governance**. 

Unlike shallow indicators that track a single tech stack, the BQGG monitors the *co-evolution* of storage, transit, and legality. When all three reach a critical maturity threshold, it signals a "Sovereign Intelligence Step-Function"—a transition from AI as a tool to AI as a formally verified, biologically durable, and quantum-secure state organ.

The BQGG triggers a **"Sovereign Inflection Signal"** when:
$$\text{Convergence} = \int (M_{active} + Q_{sovereign} + G_{verified}) \Delta t > \text{Threshold}_{\text{Sovereign}}$$

- **Condition B**: Quantum rails prevent HNDL (Harvest Now, Decrypt Later) for these archives.
- **Condition C**: NSG ensures that the access and modification of these archives are formally proven to be legal.

### Lock: The Decision Gate
Upon BQGG trigger, the following strategic locks are activated in VIP reports:
