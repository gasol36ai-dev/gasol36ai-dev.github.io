# Proprietary Indicator: Physical Autonomy Sovereignty Gate (PASG)
*synthesized: 2026-05-30 | type: Nation-State Convergence Gate | domains: CBDC × Nuclear SMR/Fusion × Autonomous Robotics*
*HPI designation: HPI-15 | template: "Triple Sovereignty Stack Gate"*

---

## 💡 Core Insight

Three simultaneous infrastructure transitions — programmable digital settlement (CBDC), domestic nuclear baseload energy (SMR/Fusion), and indigenous physical AI execution (Robotics fleets) — represent **three distinct foreign-dependency elimination vectors**. Prior indicators operated within single infrastructure domains. PASG operates at the **nation-state sovereignty layer**: when a country simultaneously crosses independence thresholds in all three, a non-linear re-rating cascade occurs across its sovereign bonds, currency, and industrial equities.

**The Isomorphism:**
> Each layer removes a critical structural dependency. When all three are removed simultaneously, compounding removes the "sovereign discount" embedded in asset prices, triggering a non-linear premium expansion that markets systematically underweight because each layer is priced in isolation.

## 🛠️ Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. Map (The Observable Signals)

**[CRCI] — CBDC Rail Completeness Index**
- Wholesale CBDC operational for institutional settlement (not just pilot)
- At least 2 cross-border corridors active with non-Western partners
- Programmable compliance layer live (AML/KYC embedded in transaction logic)
- *Scoring: 0.0 (no CBDC) → 1.0 (full institutional + cross-border rails active)*

**[DESS] — Domestic Energy Sovereignty Score**
- SMR construction permits approved for ≥1 commercial fleet deployment
- Domestic nuclear covers ≥30% of AI/data center electricity demand
- First commercial SMR online or fusion PPA signed with domestic offtaker
- *Scoring: 0.0 (100% fossil import-dependent) → 1.0 (≥60% domestic clean baseload)*

**[RICI] — Robotics Indigenous Capability Index**
- Domestic humanoid or logistics fleet robots deployed at industrial scale
- Indigenous robotics foundation model (not licensed from foreign entity)
- RaaS contracts active in ≥2 major industrial sectors
- *Scoring: 0.0 (no domestic fleet) → 1.0 (indigenous fleet + foundation model + multi-sector)*

### 2. Trigger (The Convergence Gate)

**PASG fires when:**
```
[CRCI ≥ 0.70] AND [DESS ≥ 0.40] AND [RICI ≥ 0.50]
```

**AND one "Velocity Catalyst" is present:**
- Nation-state announces explicit "Triple Independence" policy doctrine
- Foreign adversary deploys sanctions/export controls targeting ≥2 of the 3 layers simultaneously
- Hyperscaler (≥$50B market cap) signs domestic SMR + domestic robotics contract in same fiscal quarter

**Interpretation:** The triple threshold crossing represents the moment at which the sovereign discount structurally disappears from asset pricing — not gradually, but in a step-function re-rating driven by institutional portfolio repositioning.

## 🧠 Strategic Value & Isomorphisms

### Why This Is Non-Linear
Each layer eliminates a dependency that currently forces asset pricing to embed a "foreign risk haircut." When all three are eliminated:
- Sovereign bonds lose their geopolitical risk premium → re-rating
- Currency loses its structural USD subordination → long-term appreciation
- Industrial equities gain moat vs foreign supply chain participants → multiple expansion

Standard macro models price each layer independently. PASG captures the compounding effect — the joint probability of all three completing is systematically underestimated by single-domain analysis.

### Isomorphisms with Prior Indicators
- **QDSI (Sovereign Infrastructure Convergence Gate):** Similar multi-layer mandatory cliff structure, but QDSI operated at the *technology compliance deadline* level; PASG operates at the *nation-state sovereignty re-rating* level.
- **CMAM (Asymmetric Information Retention + Deadline Cliff):** PASG reuses the "known deadline cliff" pattern for ENERGY-Gate (SMR licensing timelines) but extends to FX and bond asset class.
