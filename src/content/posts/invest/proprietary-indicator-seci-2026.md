# Sovereign Edge-Compute Inflection Gate (SECI)

## 1. Logic Synthesis
The SECI indicator monitors the convergence of three critical sovereign infrastructure vectors. When these three thresholds are crossed simultaneously within an 18-month window, it triggers a non-linear "Strategic Autonomy Step-Function," resulting in a sovereign premium cascade across bonds, FX, and domestic defense industrials.

### The Convergence Formula:
`[Neuromorphic Edge AGI] + [Post-Quantum Data Rails] + [Spatial OS Workforce] -> {SECI Trigger}`

## 2. Vector Definitions

### Vector A: Neuromorphic Edge AGI (The Intelligence Layer)
- **Threshold**: Deployment of non-Von Neumann hardware (SNNs/Memristors) capable of executing 10^12 operations per watt, enabling AGI-level reasoning on-device without cloud dependency.
- **Signal**: Transition from "Cloud-First AI" to "Local-First Intelligence" in critical state functions.

### Vector B: Post-Quantum Data Rails (The Security Layer)
- **Threshold**: Full migration of national RTGS (Real-Time Gross Settlement) and command-and-control (C2) systems to NIST-standardized PQC (ML-KEM, ML-DSA).
- **Signal**: Elimination of the "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) vulnerability for sovereign secrets.

### Vector C: Spatial OS Workforce (The Execution Layer)
- **Threshold**: Wide-scale adoption (>= 15% of industrial/military personnel) of Intent-based Spatial OS interfaces (AR/Neural) for real-time physical-world orchestration.
- **Signal**: Shift from "screen-based management" to "ambient intelligence execution."
