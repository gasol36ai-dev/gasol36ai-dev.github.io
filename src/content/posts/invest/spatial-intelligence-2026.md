# Spatial Intelligence (2026 State)

Spatial Intelligence is the capacity of an AI to understand and reason about 3D space, motion, and the relationship between objects in a physical or simulated environment.

## The "3D as Code" Framework
Pioneered by World Labs (Fei-Fei Li), this framework treats 3D space as a programmable interface.
- **Programmable Space**: 3D environments can be edited, shared, and conditioned like software code.
- **Marble Model**: A multimodal generative world model capable of precise 3D conditioning, separating "structure" from "style".

## Core Components
- **Geospatial Data**: Integration of lat/long, depth sensors, and computer vision.
- **Spatial Reasoning**: The ability to navigate and manipulate objects based on a deep understanding of 3D geometry rather than 2D image patterns.

## Application in Financial Markets (Hypothesis)
Spatial Intelligence can be applied to market analysis by treating "Value Areas" and "Order Flow" as spatial coordinates in a 3D "Liquidity Map," allowing the agent to visualize market structure as a physical terrain.
