# Macroeconomic Overview - May 2026

**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Status:** Active Monitoring

## 1. Central Bank Policy Landscape

| Central Bank | Current Rate | Stance | Outlook | Key Drivers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Federal Reserve (Fed)** | 3.5% - 3.75% | Neutral/Hawkish | Potential hike by end of 2026 | Hot CPI (3.8% in April), Middle East inflation risks |
| **ECB** | 2.00% | Hawkish | Likely June hike | Mounting inflation, energy prices (Middle East war) |
| **Bank of Japan (BoJ)** | 0.75% | Hawkish Hold | Potential June hike | Rising inflation forecasts, shift in risk balance |
| **Bank of England (BoE)** | 3.75% | Hawkish | Increasing likelihood of hikes | Oil prices > $108/bbl, energy-driven inflation |
| **RBA** | (Varies) | Hawkish | Third hike expected in May | Persistent inflation, robust household spending |

## 2. Key Economic Indicators

- **US Inflation (CPI):** 3.8% (April 2026) - Highest in nearly 3 years. Core CPI at 2.8%.
- **US Treasury Yields:**
  - 10Y: ~4.455%
  - 2Y: ~3.994%
  - 30Y: ~5.02%
- **Oil Prices:** Surpassed $108/bbl due to Middle East instability.
- **Global Growth:** Steady at ~3% (IMF/BoC projections), but clouded by war and energy shocks.

## 3. Geopolitical Risk Factors

- **Middle East War:** Primary driver of energy price volatility and global inflation. Causes uncertainty in capital flows and increases "flight to quality" demand.
- **US Trade Policy:** Unpredictable trade restrictions (tariffs) impacting global supply chains and inflation (especially in Canada/Mexico).
