# Geopolitical Capital Flows 2026

Geopolitical risk has transitioned from a "tail risk" to a primary determinant of global capital allocation in 2026.

## The "Security Premium"
Investment decisions are now filtered through a "Security Premium," where geopolitical stability and alignment outweigh pure financial returns.

## Key Drivers
- **Trade Frictions**: "Reciprocal tariffs" and trade wars are causing "retrenchment" — capital moving back to home markets or aligned-bloc markets.
- **Fragmentation**: The global financial system is splitting into aligned blocs, reducing the efficiency of cross-border portfolio investment.
- **Impact on EM**: Emerging Markets are experiencing "sudden stops" not based on economic fundamentals, but on political alignment and institutional quality.

## Transmission Mechanism
`Geopolitical Tension $\uparrow \rightarrow$ Risk Aversion $\uparrow \rightarrow$ Capital Retrenchment $\rightarrow$ USD Strength (Safe Haven) $\rightarrow$ EM Currency Pressure`.
