# Orbital-Industrial Convergence (OIC) Indicator

## 🛠️ Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

**Map:**
- `[LEO Payload Capacity / Launch Cadence]` (e.g., SpaceX Starship, Blue Origin New Glenn, Varda return capability)
- `[Terrestrial High-Purity Material Demand]` (e.g., ZBLAN optical fibers, high-grade protein crystals for pharma, ultra-pure silicon/semiconductor components)

**Trigger:**
- `[Orbital Return Vessel Cadence]` (Frequency of successful atmospheric re-entry and product delivery)
- `[Terrestrial Purity Gap]` (Increasing cost/difficulty of producing high-purity materials on Earth due to gravity-induced defects or chemical instabilities)

**Lock:**
- `[Automated Supply-Chain Redirection]` (Automatic trigger to increase procurement/investment in orbital manufacturing contracts when the `[Purity Gap] / [Return Cadence]` ratio crosses a critical threshold).

## 🎯 Strategic Value
The **OIC Indicator** identifies the inflection point where the economic advantage of microgravity-enabled manufacturing (Space-for-Earth) overcomes the cost of orbital launch and return logistics. It signals a non-linear shift in the production of critical high-value materials, moving from terrestrial-dominated to orbital-integrated supply chains.

## 🛰️ Transmission Mapping
`[Commercialization of LEO-Return Vessels (e.g., Varda/Redwire)]` $\rightarrow$ `[Reduction in Unit Cost of Orbital Materials]` $\rightarrow$ `[Disruption of Terrestrial High-Purity Manufacturing Monopolies]`
