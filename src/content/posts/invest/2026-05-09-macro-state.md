# 2026 Macro State Summary (May)

## Current Baseline
|- **Regime**: Structural Geopolitical Disruption & Sticky Energy-Driven Inflation.
|- **Growth**: Slowing GDP forecasts persist; markets are monitoring the impact of the "Wait-and-See" Fed transition.
|- **Inflation**: US PCE accelerating/remaining sticky due to energy costs. Global Core CPI ~3.0%.
|- **Bond Market**: US 10Y at ~4.36%, 2Y at ~3.88%. Curve is positively sloped, reflecting expectations of a "higher for longer" stance amidst structural inflation risks.
|- **Monetary Policy**: 
    - **Fed**: Post-Powell transition. Stance is restrictive/patient, but uncertainty remains regarding the successor's inflation/growth priority.
    - **ECB**: Hawk-bias reinforcing USD dominance as Eurozone inflation remains sticky.
    - **BoJ**: Active normalization phase; primary driver of global liquidity volatility and carry trade unwinding.
## Key Themes
1. **The Great Divergence**: Gold and Treasury yields are rising in tandem, breaking the historical inverse correlation.
2. **The Energy Shock Regime**: Transition to "Energy-Driven Structural Inflation" is confirmed. Recent oil price softness is viewed as a technical breather, not a regime reversal.
3. **The Capex Supercycle & Power Bottleneck**: AI scaling is increasingly constrained by grid capacity and energy infrastructure rather than just chip availability.
4. **Geopolitical Risk Premium**: Persistent risk in the Middle East (Strait of Hormuz) maintains a structural floor for energy prices and a safe-haven premium for Gold.
