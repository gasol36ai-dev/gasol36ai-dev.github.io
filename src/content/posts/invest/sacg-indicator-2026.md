# Indicator: Sovereign Autonomy Convergence Gate (SACG-2026)

## 1. Conceptual Logic
The SACG is a high-density proprietary indicator designed to detect the precise moment a sovereign entity transitions from "AI-Dependent" to "AI-Sovereign". It synthesizes three distinct research domains: **Energy Autonomy (SMR/Fusion)**, **Hardware Autonomy (Nanophotonic Compute)**, and **Financial Autonomy (RWA Settlement)**.

The core hypothesis is that AI capability is a function of the "Energy-Compute-Capital" trifecta. A failure in any one domain creates a ceiling. When all three cross a critical threshold simultaneously, it triggers a non-linear "Sovereign Premium" cascade.

## 2. Convergent Domains
- **Domain A: Energy Sovereignty (SMR/Fusion)**
  - *Critical Vector*: Transition from Grid-Dependency to "Compute Fortress" autonomy.
  - *Threshold*: $\text{Energy\_Autonomy} \ge 80\%$ of peak compute load via on-site SMR/Fusion.
- **Domain B: Hardware Sovereignty (Nanophotonic Compute)**
  - *Critical Vector*: Transition from Silicon-based (Joule-limited) to Photon-based (Wave-limited) compute.
  - *Threshold*: $\text{Optical\_Efficiency} > 10\text{x}$ Conventional GPU energy/latency ratio.
- **Domain C: Financial Sovereignty (Sovereign RWA Settlement)**
  - *Critical Vector*: Transition from USD-Correspondent (SWIFT) to Tokenized Atomic Settlement.
  - *Threshold*: $\text{Sovereign\_Settlement\_Volume} > 20\%$ of national treasury flows via Unified Ledger.

## 3. Mathematical Formalization (Trigger Condition)
The SACG is active when the joint convergence of the three vectors exceeds the "Sovereignty Inflection" threshold:

$$\text{SACG}_{\text{active}} = (\text{SMR}_{\text{deployed}} \land \text{OptCompute}_{\text{scaled}} \land \text{RWA}_{\text{atomic}}) \land (\Delta \text{Sovereign\_Premium} > \sigma)$$

Where $\sigma$ is the historical volatility of the sovereign's credit default swap (CDS) spreads.

## 4. Transmission Mapping
- **[Event]**: Simultaneous deployment of on-site SMRs + Nanophotonic neural cores + Unified Ledger for Treasury.
- **[Mechanism]**: 
    - $\rightarrow$ Decouples AI scaling from energy grid instability.
    - $\rightarrow$ Removes the "Silicon Wall" (thermal/latency) for AGI-class models.
    - $\rightarrow$ Bypasses external financial sanctions/intermediaries.
- **[Reaction]**: Non-linear step-function increase in "Sovereign Strategic Autonomy", resulting in a rapid re-pricing of the entity's geopolitical risk and economic output potential.

## 5. Historical Validation & Strategic Value
This indicator allows the agent to identify "Shadow Superpowers"—entities that may appear weak on current GDP metrics but have silently integrated the Energy-Compute-Capital stack, making them the primary candidates for AGI-led economic dominance.
