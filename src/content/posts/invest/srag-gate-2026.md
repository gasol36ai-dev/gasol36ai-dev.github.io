# Proprietary Judgment Indicator: SRAG_Gate_2026

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Domain:** Swarm Logistics / Sovereign Resource Intelligence

## 1. Executive Definition
**SRAG_Gate_2026** measures the emergent capability of a localized zone (micro-sovereignty) to maintain essential material and information flows independently of external macro-regimes. It identifies the "inflection point" where decentralized logistics (DePIN + Swarms) successfully buffer against resource volatility.

## 2. Core Mathematical Formalization
The indicator is derived from the ratio of autonomous logistical capacity to external resource dependence.

**Formula:**
$$SRAG_{gate} = \frac{\Psi_{swarm} \cdot \Theta_{resource}}{\Xi_{dependency}}$$

**Variables:**
- $\Psi_{swarm}$: **Swarm Agent Density** (Effective number of active, autonomous agents per functional unit area).
- $\Theta_{resource}$: **Resource Throughput Ratio** (The dynamic ratio of localized resource production/recycling to local consumption).
- $\Xi_{dependency}$: **External Dependency Coefficient** (The degree of reliance on non-localized, centralized, or external supply chains; $\Xi \to 0$ implies total autarky).

**Trigger Condition:**
$$(SRAG_{gate} > \sigma_{autarky}) \land (\dot{\Psi}_{swarm} > \epsilon)$$
*Where $\sigma_{autarky}$ is the stability threshold for localized survival and $\dot{\Psi}_{swarm}$ is the rate of swarm deployment/growth.*

## 3. Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock
- **Map**: Monitoring the "Autarky Gap" — the difference between required logistical throughput and current decentralized capacity.
- **Trigger**: Crossing the $\sigma_{autarky}$ threshold during a period of high $\Xi_{dependency}$ volatility (e.g., a global supply chain shock).
- **Lock**: Deployment of **Sovereign Buffer Assets** (SBA) and transition to **Rhizomatic Distribution Mode**.

## 4. Transmission Mapping
**`[Centralized Supply Disruption] -> [Swarm Redistribution & Local Production Surge] -> [Sovereign Autarky Lock]`**

## 5. Strategic Implications
- **Macro-Economic**: Shift from "Just-in-Time" (fragile) to "Just-in-Case" (resilient/rhizomatic) capital allocation.
- **Geopolitical**: Emergence of "Logistics-Independent" zones that are immune to traditional economic sanctions and blockade-based coercion.
- **Investment**: High ROI on DePIN-based infrastructure and autonomous manufacturing within micro-sovereign jurisdictions.
