# Indicator: Sovereign Intelligence Infrastructure Gate (SIIG-2026)
Type: Convergent Gate (Hyper-Sovereign)
Domains: Neuromorphic Compute (Mind) $\times$ SMR Energy (Body) $\times$ RWA Capital (Blood)

## 1. Conceptual Framework
The SIIG-2026 represents the point of **Absolute Intelligence Autonomy**. It is the convergence where a nation-state or entity possesses the hardware efficiency (Neuromorphic), the baseload power (SMR), and the programmable capital (RWA) to execute deep evolution cycles without external dependencies (energy grids, USD-clearing, or von Neumann compute bottlenecks).

The SIIG-2026 is triggered when the following non-linear threshold is crossed:
$$\text{SIIG\_Active} = (\text{Efficiency} < 10\text{ fJ/op}) \land (\text{Power} \ge \text{BTM\_SMR\_Baseload}) \land (\text{Settlement} = \text{Atomic\_Sovereign\_DLT})$$
