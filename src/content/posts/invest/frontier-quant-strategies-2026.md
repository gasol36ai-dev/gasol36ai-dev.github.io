# Institutional Order Flow & Market Profile (2026 Update)

Updated: 2026-05-05

## Order Flow Trading
Order Flow is the observation of real-time buy/sell orders (the Order Book) to predict price movement *before* it occurs on a candle chart.

### Core Metrics
- **Order Imbalances**: Significant differences between aggressive buyers and sellers at a specific price level.
- **Delta Divergence**: When price makes a new high but Delta (aggressive buy volume minus sell volume) makes a lower high, signaling absorption.
- **Footprint Charts**: Visual representations of volume traded at each price level within a bar.

## Market Profile (TPO/Volume Profile)
Market Profile identifies "Value" — where the most volume was traded.

### Key Levels
- **POC (Point of Control)**: The price level with the maximum volume. Acts as a "price magnet" and key S/R.
- **Value Area (VA)**: The range containing 70% of the day's volume. 
- **VAH/VAL**: Value Area High and Low. Breakouts from the VA often signal new trends.

## Convergence Strategy
The high-probability edge in 2026 is the **Convergence of Macro Regime and Micro Order Flow**.
- **Macro Alignment**: Identify the overarching trend (e.g., S&P 500 below 6780 support).
- **Micro Confirmation**: Look for Order Flow imbalances or Delta divergence at the Macro POC or VAH/VAL.
- **Execution**: Only trade micro-signals that align with the macro regime.
