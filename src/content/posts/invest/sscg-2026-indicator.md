# Sovereign Substrate Convergence Gate (SSCG-2026)

**Indicator ID**: SSCG-2026
**Convergence Domains**: Macro-Microstructure Convergence $\times$ Sovereign Bio-Digital Rails $\times$ Orbital-Edge Mesh
**Status**: PROPRIETARY / HYPER-SOVEREIGN
**Logic Type**: Convergent Gate (Triple-Vector Alignment)

## 🎯 Strategic Intent
The SSCG-2026 is designed to detect the precise inflection point where a sovereign entity achieves **Substrate Autonomy**. This occurs when the entity's primary assets, governance logic, and communication infrastructure decouple from terrestrial, electronic, and jurisdiction-bound constraints, moving into a converged Bio-Digital-Orbital state.

Unlike single-domain indicators, the SSCG requires a simultaneous alignment of three distinct vectors: **Substrate (Bio-Digital)**, **Spatial (Orbital)**, and **Liquidity (Microstructure)**.

## 🧬 Convergence Logic (MTL Formalization)
The gate is considered **ACTIVE** when the following non-linear threshold is crossed:

$$\text{SSCG\_Active} = (\text{HSST\_Crossing} \land \text{Orbital\_Autarky\_Stable}) \land (\text{Sovereign\_Flow\_Signature} > \sigma_{L})$$

### Vector Definitions:
1.  **HSST Crossing (Hyper-Sovereign Substrate Transition)**:
    - **Trigger**: $\ge 15\%$ of sovereign treasury or core governance logic migrated to Bio-Digital Rails (BDAT/Synthetica).
    - **Verification**: Detection of "Neural Black Box" governance signatures in asset management.
2.  **Orbital Autarky Stable**:
    - **Trigger**: Intelligence loop latency $< 10\text{ms}$ across $90\%$ of the sovereign mesh, independent of terrestrial ground stations.
    - **Verification**: Successful DePIN orchestration of OEC (Orbital Edge Compute) clusters without ground-link dependency.
3.  **Sovereign Flow Signature ($\sigma_{L}$)**:
    - **Trigger**: Clustered Flow patterns in sovereign bond/asset markets exhibiting the "Sovereign Rotation" fingerprint, as decoded by TradeFM.
    - **Verification**: Divergence between raw LOB imbalance and clustered institutional flow, indicating a coordinated move into substrate-backed assets.
