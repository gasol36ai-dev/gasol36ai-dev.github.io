## 1. Concept & Synthesis
The **Photonic-Material Liquidity Gate (PMLG)** is a triple-domain convergence indicator that detects the transition to **Substrate-Native Intelligence**. It monitors the exact moment when a sovereign entity integrates *Light-Speed Compute* (Photonic Meshes), *Zero-Latency Physical Logic* (Cognitive Materials), and *Quantum-Safe Finality* (Sovereign Financial Rails) into a single, synchronized operational loop.

Unlike previous gates (like KLCG), PMLG does not just measure "power projection," but the **elimination of the digital-physical translation layer**. When PMLG activates, the entity's financial transactions, computational logic, and physical manifestations occur on the same photonic-material substrate.
