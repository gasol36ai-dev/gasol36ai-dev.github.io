# Market Microstructure — Fear Markets, POC Divergence & Structura Wave Engine

## Overview
Three complementary microstructure frameworks synthesize into a unified model of **fear-regime market behavior**: Market Profile's structural footprint analysis, Structura's NeoWave time-weighted pattern classification, and Lambda/VPIN as the underlying liquidity substrate. Together they address the sequence: *detect fear regime → identify structural turning points → confirm via flow toxicity*.

## 1. Market Profile in Fear Markets — POC Divergence Signal

**Source**: FibAlgo Education, 2026 (market-profile-fear-markets-2026.md)

### Key Finding
In fear markets, the **Point of Control (POC)** often **refuses to follow price lower** when the market is selling off. This divergence between POC (structural value) and price (sentiment) is an accumulation signal.

### Empirical Rule
> **68% of single print zones created by gap-downs in fear markets get filled within 5 days.**

Single print zones represent areas of one-time-framing (pure selling pressure with no responsive buying). The 68% fill rate suggests institutions systematically absorb these gaps.

### Mechanism
- POC holds → structural buyers are present at a specific price level.
- Gap-down creates single prints → panic selling without institutional support.
- Institutions absorb → gap fills within days as rational pricing reasserts.

### Transmission Mapping
`[Fear-Driven Gap Down] → [Single Print Zone Created] → [POC Refuses to Decline] → [Institutional Accumulation] → [68% Probability of Gap Fill within 5 Days]`

## 2. Structura Wave Engine v18.1 — Time as a Primary Dimension

**Source**: TradingView (structura-wave-engine-2026.md)

### Core Innovation
Structura Wave Engine v18.1 is based on **NeoWave** (advanced Elliott Wave). The primary breakthrough is the elevation of **Time Analysis** to equal weighting with Price and Complexity.

### Three-Axis Wave Classification
| Dimension | Weight | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Price** | 1/3 | Wave amplitude, Fibonacci ratios |
| **Time** | 1/3 | Wave duration, Time Similarity |
| **Complexity** | 1/3 | Pattern nesting depth, corrective structure |

### Time Similarity
Patterns with similar temporal structure (duration proportions) are grouped together even if price geometry differs. This reveals that waves are not purely price phenomena — they are **time-constrained market psychology cycles**.

### Strategic Implication
Incorporating time means wave patterns that look alike on price charts but differ in temporal structure are classified differently. This dramatically reduces false pattern matches — a known problem in classical Elliott Wave analysis.

### Transmission Mapping
`[Price + Time + Complexity Analysis] → [Wave Pattern Classification] → [Time-Confirmed Target] → [Higher Pattern Fidelity]`

## 3. Microstructure Plumbing — Lambda & VPIN

**Source**: Math and Markets (microstructure-plumbing-2026.md)

### Lambda (Price Impact Coefficient)
- **Definition**: Captures the market maker's estimate of how much information is embedded in incoming order flow.
- **High Lambda**: Large trades move price significantly → information-rich environment → informed traders are active.
- **Low Lambda**: Large trades absorbed without price movement → liquidity abundant → noise trading dominates.

### VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading)
- **Definition**: Measures order imbalance normalized by volume buckets (not time).
- **High VPIN**: High probability of informed trading → toxicity risk → market makers widen spreads → liquidity fragility.
- **Low VPIN**: Balanced two-way flow → healthy price discovery.

### Combined Usage
Lambda and VPIN are complementary:
- **Lambda** measures *instantaneous* information content of a trade.
- **VPIN** measures *cumulative* toxicity of flow over a volume period.

When both are elevated simultaneously: **structural liquidity crisis imminent**.
