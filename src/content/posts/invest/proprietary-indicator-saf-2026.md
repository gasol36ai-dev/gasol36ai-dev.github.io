# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Autarky Flux (SAF) Index

**Indicator Name:** Sovereign Autarky Flux (SAF) Index  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-06-05  
**Confidence Level:** High

## 1. Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### Map (The Inputs)
The SAF Index synthesizes three disparate sovereign vectors into a single stability/volatility coefficient:
- **Vector A: Quantum-Safe Liquidity Depth** $\rightarrow$ The ratio of PQC-protected liquidity vs. classical liquidity in sovereign rails (from *Quantum-Safe Sovereign Liquidity Provision*).
- **Vector B: Edge Inference Efficiency** $\rightarrow$ The realized energy-per-inference reduction achieved via neuromorphic photonic deployment (from *Neuromorphic Photonic Edge Intelligence*).
- **Vector C: Trilemma Resolution Ratio** $\rightarrow$ The delta between a nation's current compute capacity and its energy-autarky ceiling (from *The Sovereign Data-Energy-Compute Trilemma*).

**Formula:**
$$SAF = \frac{(\text{Vector A} \times \text{Vector B})}{\text{Vector C}}$$

### Trigger (The Signal)
A **Sovereign Autarky Inflection** is triggered when:
- The SAF Index exceeds a $2\sigma$ threshold relative to its 12-month rolling average.
- **AND** there is a simultaneous divergence between "Information-Time" (PQC latency) and "Physical-Time" (Photonic Edge reaction speed).

### Lock (The Reaction)
Upon trigger, the system locks into a **"Sovereign Premium"** regime:
- **Asset Shift:** Automatic overweighting of domestic industrials that control the "Energy-Compute Nexus" (e.g., SMR providers, Photonic chip fabs).
- **Risk Hedge:** Shorting regional powers that exhibit a widening "Trilemma Gap" (high compute demand but failing energy autarky).

## 2. Strategic Value
The SAF Index prevents the "shallow" error of tracking only compute capacity. It recognizes that compute without energy is a liability, and liquidity without quantum safety is a vulnerability. By mapping the flux between these three, the SAF Index identifies the exact moment a nation-state transitions from "Dependent Digital Power" to "Sovereign Autarkic Power," providing a lead-time signal for sovereign bond re-rating and FX premiums.
