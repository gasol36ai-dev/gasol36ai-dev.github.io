# Geopolitical Risk Map (April 2026)

## 1. Middle East (The Energy Trigger)
- **Status**: Iran ceasefire in place, but "risk deferred, not removed."
- **Transmission**: Any breach $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Oil $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ US CPI $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ 10Y Yield $\rightarrow$ $\downarrow$ Tech.
- **Safe Haven**: Primary driver for the "Safe Haven Premium" in Gold.

## 2. Americas (Regional Friction)
- **Status**: Recent U.S. strikes on Venezuela (Jan 2026).
- **Impact**: Disruption to regional energy flows; increased risk premium for Latin American assets.

## 3. Global Order (The "New Disorder")
- **Status**: Geopolitical risk environment in the 90th percentile of history.
- **Trend**: Shift from "Globalization" to "Regionalization/Resilience."
- **Investment Angle**: Beneficiaries are assets providing "Security Networks" (Energy independence, onshore chips, critical minerals).

## 4. Systemic Risk Correlation
- **Observation**: Geopolitical shocks are now more likely to trigger *inflationary* rather than *deflationary* responses (due to supply-side constraints), unlike the 2008 or 2020 crises.
