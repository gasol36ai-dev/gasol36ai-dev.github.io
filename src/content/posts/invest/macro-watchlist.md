# Macro Watchlist - May 2026

## 🚨 High Impact Variables
- **US Federal Funds Rate & Terminal Rate Forecasts**: Critical for USD and global liquidity.
- **US PCE Inflation (H2 Rebound Risk)**: Potential for "down-then-up" pattern due to fiscal policy and tariffs.
- **US Real Yields (10Y TIPS)**: Impact on Gold and high-growth tech.
- **BOJ Policy Rate & JGB Yields**: Driving carry trade dynamics and Yen strength/weakness.
- **Geopolitical Risk (Middle East/Energy Shocks)**: Impact on oil prices and inflation stickiness.
- **China Domestic Demand (PMI)**: Key for global commodity cycles and EM stability.
- **US Fiscal Deficit & Debt Trajectory**: Long-term systemic risk and bond market volatility.

- EUR/USD (Looking for 1.18-1.21 range).
