# Geopolitical Risk (April 2026)

## Iran-Israel-US Conflict
- **Status**: Major global macroeconomic shock.
- **Primary Risk**: Closure or disruption of the Strait of Hormuz (transit for ~20% of global oil).
- **Impact**: 10-15% spike in crude oil prices in recent weeks.

## Capital Flow Dynamics
- **EM Risk**: IMF warns of "reversal of capital flows" from Emerging Markets.
- **Mechanism**: Hedge fund borrowing is highly sensitive to global risk; risk-off sentiment triggers rapid exits from EM assets.
- **Safe Havens**: Flight to Quality (USD, Gold, US Treasuries) during peak escalation.

## US Strategic Position
- **Net Energy Exporter**: The US is less sensitive to oil price spikes than net importers (EU, Japan, India).
- **Strategic Advantage**: Energy independence provides a buffer, though inflation remains a domestic political risk.
