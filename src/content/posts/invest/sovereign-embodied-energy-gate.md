# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Embodied Energy Gate (SEEG / HPI-60)

## 1. Theoretical Foundation
The **Sovereign Embodied Energy Gate (SEEG)** is a Hyper-Sovereign convergence indicator that monitors the alignment of three disparate but complementary vectors: **Edge-Native Embodiment (Neuromorphic Robotics)**, **Localized Energy Autarky (Micro-Fusion/SMRs)**, and **Localized Capital Sovereignty (RWA Tokenization)**.

While single-domain advances are incremental, their convergence creates a "Step-Function" shift in sovereignty. An agent that is computationally efficient (Neuromorphic), energy-independent (SMR), and capital-autonomous (RWA) is no longer a tool of a centralized state or corporation, but a **Sovereign Physical Actor**.

## 2. Convergent Vectors
- **Vector $\text{V}_{\text{emb}}$ (Embodied Intelligence)**: Shift from LLM-cloud-dependency to SNN/CiM-edge-autonomy.
- **Vector $\text{V}_{\text{nrg}}$ (Energy Autarky)**: Shift from grid-dependency to localized Micro-Fusion/SMR direct-coupling.
- **Vector $\text{V}_{\text{cap}}$ (Capital Sovereignty)**: Shift from global fiat-credit to localized RWA-backed hyper-liquid markets.

## 3. Mathematical Formalization (Trigger Condition)
The SEEG is considered **ACTIVE** when the joint probability of these three vectors crossing their respective sovereignty thresholds within a synchronized window is $\ge 0.85$.

$$\text{SEEG}_{\text{Active}} = (\text{V}_{\text{emb}} \ge \tau_{\text{emb}}) \land (\text{V}_{\text{nrg}} \ge \tau_{\text{nrg}}) \land (\text{V}_{\text{cap}} \ge \tau_{\text{cap}})$$

Where:
- $\tau_{\text{emb}}$: Latency $\le 10\text{ms}$ for closed-loop motor control with power $\le 50\text{W}$.
- $\tau_{\text{nrg}}$: Net-positive Energy Gain ($Q > 1$) in a form factor $\le 10\text{m}^3$.
- $\tau_{\text{cap}}$: Local Credit Velocity $\text{V}_{\text{loc}} > 2 \times \text{V}_{\text{global}}$ for key infrastructure assets.
