# Strategic Sovereignty (Political-Technical Framework)
**Domain**: Geopolitics / Technology Policy / Sovereign Autonomy
**Date**: 2026-07-05
**Status**: Stub Expansion (ISS)

## Core Thesis
Strategic Sovereignty is the capacity of a state or entity to maintain independent decision-making power over its critical technological and resource stacks. It moves beyond "autarky" (total self-sufficiency) toward "strategic autonomy"—the ability to manage dependencies and pivot supply chains in response to geopolitical volatility.

## Transmission Mapping
**[Event]** $\rightarrow$ **[Mechanism]** $\rightarrow$ **[Reaction]**
- **[Dependency Shock (e.g., Sanctions)]** $\rightarrow$ **[Strategic Buffer Stockpiling $\rightarrow$ Parallel Import Infrastructure]** $\rightarrow$ **[Continuity of Critical Operations]**
- **[Technological Leapfrog (e.g., Quantum)]** $\rightarrow$ **[R&D Investment in Disruptive Tech $\rightarrow$ Sovereign Standard Setting]** $\rightarrow$ **[Shift from Technology Taker to Technology Maker]**
- **[Resource Scarcity]** $\rightarrow$ **[Circular Economy $\rightarrow$ Urban Mining $\rightarrow$ Synthetic Alternatives]** $\rightarrow$ **[Decoupling from Volatile External Markets]**

## Framework Pillars
1. **Compute Sovereignty**: Local fab capacity, sovereign LLM weights, and energy-independent data centers.
2. **Resource Sovereignty**: Control over the full mineral-to-component pipeline (Up-stream $\rightarrow$ Mid-stream $\rightarrow$ Down-stream).
3. **Cognitive Sovereignty**: Protection of the information environment and the ability to define domestic AI alignment standards.

## Strategic Value: The "Meta-Layer"
Strategic Sovereignty is the objective function of the Evolution Engine. Every research domain (Sensing, Acting, Sustaining) is a vector contributing to this overarching goal. A state with "Sovereign Kinetic Autonomy" can project power and protect assets without relying on foreign software, hardware, or minerals.

## Convergence Gates
- **Sovereignty $\times$ RWA Capital**: Using tokenized assets to fund the massive capex required for sovereign fab/refinery builds.
- **Sovereignty $\times$ PQC**: Ensuring that the "digital nervous system" of the state is immune to quantum decryption.
