# Proprietary Indicator: Logistics-Macro Convergence Divergence (LMCD) (2026)

## 🧠 Overview
The **Logistics-Macro Convergence Divergence (LMCD)** is a multi-domain synthesis indicator that bridges **Maritime Logistics Fragility** with **Asymmetric Macro-Economic Policy**. It detects the specific moment when supply-side logistics shocks (chokepoint disruption) intersect with central bank policy rigidity, creating a "perfect storm" for stagflationary volatility.

## 🏗️ Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 1. Map (The Inputs)
The indicator synthesizes inputs from two disparate research domains:
- **Domain 1: Logistics Fragility Index (LFI)**
    - *Measure*: Real-time maritime congestion/cost divergence (e.g., Suez/Panama rerouting premiums + Baltic Dry Index acceleration).
    - *Focus*: Supply-side bottleneck intensity.
- **Domain 2: Policy Rigidity Score (PRS)**
    - *Measure*: Central bank asymmetry (e.g., widening gap between inflation-implied rate hikes vs. growth-implied rate cuts).
    - *Focus*: Monetary response constraint.

### 2. Trigger (The Signal)
A **LMCD Signal** is triggered when:
$$LMCD = LFI \times PRS > Threshold_{crit}$$

**The Signal Logic**:
- **Low LMCD**: Logistics are stable AND central banks are flexible (Normal regime).
- **High LMCD**: Logistics are failing AND central banks are paralyzed (Stagflationary/Chaos regime).
- **Critical Breach**: A sharp, simultaneous spike in LFI (e.g., a chokepoint event) during a period of high PRS (e.g., high inflation/low growth) identifies a high-conviction regime shift.

### 3. Lock (The Reaction)
Upon a LMCD signal confirmation:
- **Protocol 1: Commodity Hedge**: Immediate increase in long-duration commodity exposure (Gold/Energy) to offset inflationary shocks.
- **Protocol 2: Duration Shortening**: Systematic reduction in long-duration fixed income exposure to mitigate interest rate uncertainty.
- **Protocol 3: Volatility Scaling**: Implementation of a "Volatility-Adjusted Position Sizing" (VAPS) rule, reducing all equity exposures by 40% until LMCD stabilizes.

## 💡 Strategic Value
The LMCD provides a **cross-domain early warning**. It filters out transient logistics spikes (which central banks can absorb) and transient policy shifts (which logistics can buffer), focusing only on the **structural intersection** where both systems fail simultaneously.
