# Sovereign Capability Governance (SCG)

## 1. Executive Summary
**Sovereign Capability Governance (SCG)** is the systemic orchestration of a state's critical technological and cognitive capacities—specifically compute, energy, and intelligence—treating them as **Sovereign Capability Assets (SCAs)**. Unlike traditional reserves, SCAs are characterized by **non-linear utility scaling** (convexity); their value increases exponentially as they approach critical thresholds of scarcity or strategic necessity. SCG integrates the **Algorithmic Governance of Non-Linear Asset Classes (AG-NLAC)**, the **Sovereign Energy-Compute Nexus (SECN)**, and **Cognitive-Sovereign Infrastructure (CSI)** into a unified financial and operational framework for national security and economic autarky.

---

## 2. Taxonomy of Sovereign Capability Assets (SCAs)
SCAs are transitioned from static infrastructure to programmable financial instruments via RWA (Real World Asset) tokenization.

| Asset Class | Token/Identifier | Physical Substrate | Financial Characteristic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 3. Algorithmic Governance Framework (AG-NLAC)
The AG-NLAC layer manages the pricing and hedging of SCAs to prevent systemic collapse during geopolitical shocks.

### 3.1 Non-Linear Pricing & The Criticality Threshold
SCAs do not follow linear supply-demand curves. They operate on a **Criticality Threshold ($T_{crit}$)**:
- **Linear Zone**: Above $T_{crit}$, assets are priced based on marginal utility.
- **Convex Zone**: Below $T_{crit}$, the marginal utility of an additional unit approaches infinity, triggering **Power-Law Pricing**:
  $$\text{Value} = \text{Base} \times \left(\frac{\text{Demand}}{\text{Supply}}\right)^k \quad (k > 1)$$

### 3.2 Dynamic Hedging Mechanisms
SCG automates strategic reserves through **Transmission Mappings** ($\text{Event} \rightarrow \text{Mechanism} \rightarrow \text{Reaction}$):
- **GPU Export Ban** $\rightarrow$ Gamma Spike in `CMP` Futures $\rightarrow$ Automated buy-back of `CMP` reserves.
- **Energy Grid Failure** $\rightarrow$ `Sovereign Efficiency Ratio` Decay $\rightarrow$ Liquidation of low-yield RWA to acquire `NRG` credits.
- **AI Breakthrough** $\rightarrow$ Utility Shift $\rightarrow$ Rapid re-valuation of `WGT` and `CMP` assets.

## 4. The Energy-Compute Nexus (SECN) & Autarky
Sovereign Capability is physically anchored by the **Sovereign Energy-Compute Nexus (SECN)**, which aims for **Compute Autarky**.

### 4.1 Hardware-Energy Integration
- **Co-location**: SMRs (Small Modular Reactors) and Micro-Fusion cells are deployed adjacent to compute clusters to eliminate transmission loss.
- **Direct-to-Chip (D2C)**: HVDC delivery directly to accelerators, reducing energy waste by 15-20%.
- **Thermodynamic Recycling**: Waste heat from AI clusters repurposed for industrial/residential use, creating a closed-loop system.

### 4.2 Sovereign Efficiency Ratio
The primary metric for SECN performance:
$$\text{Sovereign Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Aggregate Compute Capability}}{\text{Energy Expenditure}}$$
States hedge against energy price shocks by taking long positions on this ratio, ensuring AI uptime regardless of external energy market volatility.

## 5. Cognitive-Sovereign Infrastructure (CSI)
The final layer of SCG is the governance of the mind as a sovereign asset, preventing "Cognitive Colonialism."

### 5.1 Neural-Sovereign Capital (NSC)
Cognitive output is quantized into **NSC tokens** using BCI (Brain-Computer Interface) and **Local Mind-Models (LMMs)**.
- **Proof-of-Neural-Origin (PoNO)**: Cryptographic signing of neural telemetry to differentiate organic intelligence from synthetic AI output, preventing inflation of cognitive value.
- **Sovereign Cognitive Debt Ratio (SCDR)**:
  $$\text{SCDR} = \frac{\text{Cost of Cognitive Sustenance } (\mathcal{C}_{sus})}{\text{Yield of Sovereign Neural Assets}}$$
  **Governance Goal**: Maintain $\text{SCDR} < 1.0$ to ensure volitional autonomy.
