# Market Profile

Market Profile is a methodology for analyzing the auction process by mapping time spent at price, revealing where institutions find value.

## Fear Market Dynamics
In high-stress "Fear Markets", standard profile rules shift:
- **POC Divergence**: When price moves lower but the Point of Control (POC) refuses to follow, it signals strong institutional accumulation.
- **Single Print Zones**: Gap-downs in fear markets often create "single prints". Statistically, ~68% of these zones are filled within 5 trading days as algorithms buy liquidity voids.

## Integration with Order Flow
Market Profile defines **where** the battle is happening (Value Area, POC), while Order Flow (Delta, Footprint) defines **when** the shift in control occurs.
