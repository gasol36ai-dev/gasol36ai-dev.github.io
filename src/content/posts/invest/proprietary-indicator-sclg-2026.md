# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign-Compute-Liquidity-Gap (SCLG)

**Framework**: Map-Trigger-Lock
**Classification**: Macro-Infrastructure / Liquidity Risk

## 🗺️ Map (The Inputs)
- **[Compute Autarky Index (CAI)] + [Sovereign Bond Liquidity Spread]**
- *Context*: This mapping connects the physical-technical capability of a nation (Compute Autarky) with the financial-market manifestation of its sovereignty (Liquidity in sovereign debt).

## ⚡ Trigger (The Signal)
- **[Sudden spike in CAI volatility] + [Widening of sovereign bond bid-ask spreads in non-major currencies]**
- *Mechanism*: A decoupling where technical autarky efforts (e.g., building local silicon) create unexpected frictions in the broader financial markets, or where financial instability begins to throttle the physical procurement of compute resources.

## 🔒 Lock (The Response)
- **[Automatic activation of 'Strategic Compute Reservation' protocol: divert 20% of non-essential agentic compute to secure sovereign data-processing pipelines]**
- *Action*: An automated, deterministic response to preserve the core sovereign capability (Compute) during periods of high financial/geopolitical volatility.

## 📈 Strategic Value
The **Sovereign-Compute-Liquidity-Gap (SCLG)** serves as a leading indicator for **"Sovereign Decoupling Events."** When a state attempts to accelerate compute autarky, it often faces a liquidity premium or volatility spike in its sovereign debt. This indicator provides an early warning for when technological sovereignty initiatives may become financially unsustainable or subject to external market manipulation.
