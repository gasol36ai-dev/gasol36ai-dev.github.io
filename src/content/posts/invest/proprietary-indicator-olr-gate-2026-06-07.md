## 1. Overview & Theoretical Basis
The **Orbital Liquidity Resilience (OLR) Gate** is a multi-domain judgment indicator designed to assess the stability and liquidity availability of decentralized physical infrastructure (DePIN) and financial settlement layers operating across orbital (LEO) and terrestrial domains. 

As the global financial system migrates towards **Space-Based Edge Computing (SBEC)** to minimize latency for high-frequency sovereign transactions, the physical layer (satellite mesh) becomes a critical component of the financial plumbing. A breakdown in the orbital data-plane (e.g., due to solar flares, orbital debris, or bandwidth congestion) no longer just affects communication; it creates **"Latency-Induced Liquidity Gaps"**. The OLR Gate quantifies this risk.

## 2. Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 🗺️ Map (The Inputs: Multidimensional Data Streams)
To calculate the OLR status, the engine ingests three distinct telemetry streams:

1.  **Orbital Mesh Latency (OML)**:
    *   **Metric**: Real-time round-trip time (RTT) for cross-constellation data packets (LEO $\leftrightarrow$ LEO $\leftrightarrow$ Terrestrial).
    *   **Focus**: Jitter and packet loss in the inter-satellite link (ISL) layer.
    *   **Indicator of**: Physical layer congestion or atmospheric interference.

2.  **Space-DePIN Bandwidth Utilization (SBU)**:
    *   **Metric**: Aggregate load on satellite-based edge nodes and orbital compute clusters.
    *   **Focus**: CPU/GPU utilization on "intelligent" nodes performing edge-AI filtering.
    *   **Indicator of**: Computational bottleneck in the orbital data-plane.

3.  **On-Chain Settlement Velocity (OSV)**:
    *   **Metric**: The rate of finalized transactions across CBDC or RWA-tokenized rails that utilize orbital data-planes.
    *   **Focus**: Time-to-finality (TTF) on heterogeneous settlement layers.
    *   **Indicator of**: Financial liquidity throughput.

### ⚡ Trigger (The Signal: Convergence of Disparate Divergences)
The OLR Gate triggers when we observe a **"Three-Way Divergence"**:

*   **[OML Spike]**: A sudden increase in orbital latency ($>3\sigma$ from the 24-hour moving average) without a corresponding increase in terrestrial fiber congestion.
*   **[SBU Peak]**: Bandwidth/Compute utilization exceeds the 85% critical threshold on primary orbital compute-nodes, indicating an imminent "Edge-Congestion" event.
*   **[OSV Decay]**: A simultaneous, statistically significant drop in settlement velocity ($>20\%$ deviation from the baseline) despite high terrestrial liquidity.

**Formulaic Logic**:
$$OLR_{Signal} = \left( \frac{\Delta OML}{\mu_{OML}} \right) \times \left( \frac{SBU}{100} \right) \times \left( \frac{1}{\Delta OSV} \right)$$
*Trigger occurs when $OLR_{Signal} > \text{Threshold}_{critical}$.*
