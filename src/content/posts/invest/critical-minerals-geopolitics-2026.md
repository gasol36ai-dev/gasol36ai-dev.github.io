# Critical Minerals Geopolitics (2026)

## Overview
The global critical minerals landscape in 2026 is characterized by a **"tactical thaw"** from China masking an accelerating structural race for supply chain independence by the US and EU. The period between now and November 27, 2026 is a known deadline for a potential return to hard controls.

## The China "Tactical Thaw"
- **Nov 2025**: China suspended export controls on **gallium, germanium, antimony, graphite** specifically for US end-users
- **Suspension Expiry**: November 27, 2026 — creates a known "cliff" event
- **Whitelist Model**: 11 firms authorized for antimony; 15 firms for tungsten (2026–2027)
- **Strategic Reading**: Suspension is a trade negotiation lever, not a permanent policy reversal

## Strategic Investment Implications
1. **"November Cliff" Hedge**: Build options positions on semis/defense names by Sep 2026; China's suspension expiry creates a volatility trigger
2. **Mid-Stream Premium**: Capital is moving from upstream mining → mid-stream processing; Canadian processors are structurally advantaged
3. **Africa Long-Term**: High-risk, high-return entry in DRC copper/cobalt; 5+ year thesis requiring governance-compliant structure
4. **Bifurcated Pricing**: Germanium is behaving like a strategic asset (hold exposure); antimony is commoditizing (reduce premium)
