# Macro Transmission Dynamics (2026)
Synthesis of global macro transmission monitors focused on the mid-2026 volatility regime.

## Transmission Mappings
- **[Hormuz Fragility] $\rightarrow$ [Energy Price Shock] $\rightarrow$ [Real Yield Compression]**: Geopolitical tension in the Strait of Hormuz triggers immediate spikes in Brent/WTI, which forces a shift in inflation expectations. If real yields remain stable, the upside in metals is capped as the "cost of carry" offsets the "inflation hedge" premium.
- **[Fed Reaction Function] $\rightarrow$ [Asymmetric Pivot] $\rightarrow$ [Asset Re-pricing]**: The Fed's bar to cut rates is now significantly higher than the bar to raise them. This "asymmetry" means that any drift in inflation psychology leads to immediate hawk-shifts, causing sharp drawdowns in growth-sensitive equities.
- **[Section 301 Investigations] $\rightarrow$ [Stagflationary Constraints] $\rightarrow$ [Trade Flow Degradation]**: Deepening trade investigations increase input costs (cost-push inflation) while simultaneously slowing growth (GDP drag), creating a "stagflationary pincer" that degrades traditional macro-diversification.
- **[Sovereign Debt Spikes] $\rightarrow$ [Credit Spread Widening] $\rightarrow$ [Liquidity Vacuum]**: Sudden spikes in periphery bond yields trigger a flight to quality, draining liquidity from emerging markets and small-cap equities, leading to "gap-down" events in risk assets.
- **[AI Productivity Leap] $\rightarrow$ [Margin Expansion] $\rightarrow$ [Equity Valuation Reset]**: Rapid integration of agentic AI lowers OpEx for S&P 500 firms, shifting the valuation floor higher despite higher nominal interest rates, decoupling productivity from traditional labor costs.
- **[Demographic Labor Shortage] $\rightarrow$ [Wage-Push Inflation] $\rightarrow$ [Structural Rate Floor]**: Chronic labor shortages in developed economies maintain a baseline inflation rate of ~3%, preventing a return to the Zero Interest Rate Policy (ZIRP) era and redefining "neutral" rates.

## Strategic Implications
The current regime is defined by **Policy Paralysis**. The Fed/ECB are trapped between fighting inflation and supporting growth. 

### Key Shifts
1. **Duration Risk**: The "safe" trade has shifted from simple duration to "asymmetric volatility" hedges (long gamma/long volatility).
2. **Diversification Failure**: Traditional 60/40 portfolios fail as equity and bond correlations turn positive during inflation shocks.
3. **Real Asset Pivot**: High-density focus on commodities and infrastructure as the only reliable hedges against the stagflationary pincer.

## Risk Metrics & Monitoring
- **Real Yields (10Y TIPS)**: Monitoring the pivot point where real yields stifle growth before inflation is tamed.
- **Credit Spreads (HY vs IG)**: Using the spread as a primary indicator of "Liquidity Vacuum" onset.
- **Brent/WTI Skew**: Monitoring the options skew to gauge the probability of "Hormuz Fragility" events.
