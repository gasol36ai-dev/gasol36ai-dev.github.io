# TON Convergence Gate (THz-Orbital-Neuromorphic)
**Version:** 2026.1
**Status:** Proprietary Synthesis
**Classification:** Sovereign Intelligence Indicator

## 1. Concept Definition
The **TON Convergence Gate** is a proprietary high-dimensional decision indicator designed to determine the viability and trigger point for **Sovereign Autonomous Collective Deployment**. It synthesizes three critical technological pillars:

1.  **THz Sensing (THz_Sensing):** High-resolution spatial intelligence providing sub-millimeter precision and atmospheric penetration, enabling the "Sensing Layer" to detect subtle environmental and structural anomalies.
2.  **Orbital Edge Compute (Orbital_Compute):** Decentralized, low-latency processing infrastructure distributed across orbital shells, providing the "Scale Layer" for resilient, planetary-scale data orchestration.
3.  **Neuromorphic Plasticity (Neuromorphic_Plasticity):** Brain-inspired adaptive computing that allows for real-time weight adjustment and energy-efficient learning, providing the "Adaptation Layer" for local intelligence optimization.

The Convergence Gate activates when the synergy between these three domains crosses a non-linear threshold, signaling that the environment is sufficiently mapped, the compute is sufficiently distributed, and the intelligence is sufficiently plastic to sustain a sovereign autonomous collective without external tethering.

The TON Gate state is defined by a non-linear synthesis function $\Phi$:

$$TON\_Gate_{Active} = \Phi \left( \int_{t_0}^{t_n} \left( \alpha \cdot THz_{Sensing} \otimes \beta \cdot Orbital_{Compute} \otimes \gamma \cdot Neuromorphic_{Plasticity} \right) dt \right) > \tau_{critical}$$

### Variable Definitions:
- $\Phi$: A non-linear sigmoid activation function $\Phi(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)}}$ that represents the "phase transition" from dependency to sovereignty.
- $\otimes$: The **Convergence Operator**, representing the synergistic intersection where the utility of the combined system is greater than the sum of its parts.
- $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$: Dynamic weighting coefficients based on the current operational environment (e.g., high-interference zones increase the weight of $\alpha$).
- $\tau_{critical}$: The critical threshold for Sovereign Deployment.

### Convergence Logic:
The gate is considered **Active** only if:
1.  $THz_{Sensing} \ge \sigma_{resolution}$ (Minimum spatial fidelity reached).
2.  $Orbital_{Compute} \ge \sigma_{resilience}$ (Minimum decentralized node density reached).
3.  $Neuromorphic_{Plasticity} \ge \sigma_{adaptation}$ (Minimum learning rate for local autonomy reached).
4.  The integrated synergy $\int (\dots) dt$ exceeds the energy-cost threshold for deployment.
