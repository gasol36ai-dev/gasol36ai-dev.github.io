# LSA-Regime Gate (Liquidity-Spatial-Asymmetry Gate)

**Indicator Type**: REGIME TRANSITION GATE (High-conviction, 12-24 month horizon)

**3-Domain Synthesis**: 
1. **Microstructure (Liquidity Flux)**: Captures the fragility of market plumbing via order flow clustering and depth depletion.
2. **Physical AI (Spatial Capability)**: Uses the "Embodied AI" deployment cycle as a proxy for real-world productivity shifts (the transition from digital-only to physical-interaction economy).
3. **Macro (Asymmetric Divergence)**: Monitors the decoupling of fiscal dominance/real yields from traditional growth metrics.

---

## 🗺️ Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 🗺️ MAP (The Three Pillars)

| Layer | Variable | Definition | Source/Proxy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L1: Micro** | `L_FLUX` | **Liquidity Flux Index** | High Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) + Declining LOB Depth (Plumbing Vulnerability). |
| **L2: AI** | `S_CAP` | **Spatial Capability Index** | The delta in "Interaction Readiness" (e.g., WAM deployment/adoption velocity) vs. Digital-only LLM scaling. |
| **L3: Macro** | `A_DIV` | **Asymmetric Divergence** | Divergence between Real Yields (inflation-adjusted) and Fiscal Expansion (debt-driven stimulus). |

### ⚡ TRIGGER

A **"Structural Pivot"** is triggered when:
- `L_FLUX` $\uparrow$ (Liquidity is thinning/clustering aggressively)
- `S_CAP` $\rightarrow$ or $\downarrow$ (The "Physical AI" productivity promise is hitting a spatial/interaction bottleneck)
- `A_DIV` $\uparrow$ (Fiscal dominance is decoupling from real-world productive capacity)

*Window: Signals must converge within a 60-day window to be considered a systemic regime shift.*

### 🔒 LOCK (Actionable Decision Gates)

| Signal State | Regime Classification | Actionable Instruction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[High L_FLUX] + [Stagnant S_CAP] + [High A_DIV]** | **The "Fragile Bubble" Regime** (Liquidity-driven, hollow productivity) | **Aggressive De-risking**: Reduce momentum exposure by 70%. Shift to hard assets/inflation hedges. |
| **[Low L_FLUX] + [Accelerating S_CAP] + [Stable A_DIV]** | **The "Productivity Supercycle" Regime** (Real-world interaction-driven growth) | **Maximal Accumulation**: Increase exposure to Physical AI/Infrastructure/Real Assets. |
| **[Mixed/Partial Signals]** | **The "Drift" Regime** (Transitionary/Uncertain) | **Neutral/Hedged**: Maintain beta; prioritize liquidity over duration. |

## 🔍 Historical Validation & Theoretical Basis

- **Theoretical Basis**: The LSA Gate addresses the **"Epistemic Gap"** between digital financial signals (Micro) and physical-world economic capacity (AI/Macro). It identifies when market liquidity is being driven by "phantom" digital flows that lack the underlying physical-productivity support (S_CAP) required for long-term stability.
- **Validation Analogue**: Similar to the 2008 "Liquidity-Real Economy" decoupling, but updated for the 2026 era where the driver is the transition from **Information Economy** to **Embodied Interaction Economy**.
