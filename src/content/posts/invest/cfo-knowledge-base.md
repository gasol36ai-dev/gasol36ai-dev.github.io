# CFO Knowledge Base - Macro Strategic Intelligence

## Current Regime: "Global Economy in the Shadow of War"
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-23

### Key Macro Indicators (Last 24h / Recent)
- **Global M2 Money Supply:**
  - Global M2 reached a record **$121.9 trillion** (as of May 14, 2026).
  - US M2 remains near $22.67 trillion.
  - Synchronized expansion across US, China, and Europe, despite "tight" policy rhetoric.
- **Federal Reserve Policy (Post-May 20 Minutes):**
  - FOMC Minutes (May 6-7) released May 20 revealed a deeply-divided Fed with clear hawkish bias.
  - Majority saw hike likely warranted if inflation persists; many preferred removing easing bias.
  - Markets now pricing in a **~40.1% probability of a rate hike** by December 2026.
  - PPI (April) rose 6.0% annually; CPI rose 3.8%.
  - 10-year Treasury yield spiked to **4.67%** following minutes release (May 19: 4.61%).
- **IMF World Economic Outlook (April 2026):**
  - Global growth projected at 3.1% for 2026 (revised downward by 0.2pp from Jan 2026).
  - Middle East conflict is the primary counterforce to tech-driven productivity gains.
  - Global headline inflation projected to rise modestly in 2026 before resuming decline in 2027.
- **World Bank Global Economic Prospects & Commodity Markets Outlook (April 2026):**
  - Global growth projected to edge down to 2.6% for 2026.
  - Energy prices projected to surge **24%** this year (Brent avg $86/bbl, potentially $115/bbl in high-stress scenarios).
  - Oil supply shock: initial reduction of ~10 million barrels per day from Strait of Hormuz disruptions.
  - Precious metals forecast to increase 42% in 2026 as geopolitical uncertainty fuels safe-haven demand.

### New Correlations Identified (May 21, 2026)
- **Fed Minutes Hawkishness ↔ 10yr Treasury Yield:** Strong positive correlation; release of dissent-heavy minutes on May 20 triggered immediate yield spikes (10yr ~4.67%, 30yr ~5.20%).
- **Iran-War Inflation ↔ Fed Pivot:** The 'Iran-War' conflict has created a direct transmission: Conflict $\\rightarrow$ Energy Spike ($>$50%) $\\rightarrow$ Broadening Inflation $\\rightarrow$ Removal of 'Easing Bias' (40%+ hike probability). This shifts the Fed from a 'data-dependent hold' to a 'preventative tightening' posture.
- **Global M2 Expansion ↔ Fixed Supply Assets (Lagged):** Record $121.9T M2 supports long-term debasement trade (BTC/Gold), but price transmission shows a 3-4 month lag.
- **Middle East Conflict $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Energy $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Inflation $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Fed Policy:** Direct transmission chain: Conflict $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Oil Spike $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Inflation $\\\\\\\\uparrow$ $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Shift from "easing bias" to "possible tightening."
- **M2 Growth ↔ Tech Divergence:** High liquidity is not lifting all boats; it is fueling a bifurcation where only "monetized AI" (Infrastructure) captures the flow, while "speculative AI" (Software) is dumped.
- **Strait of Hormuz Disruptions $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Oil Volatility $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Inflation Expectations $\\\\\\\\rightarrow$ Fed Policy:** Geopolitical supply shocks have outsized impact on inflation; 1% oil production decline → ~11.5% price increase (geopolitically driven volatility ~2x normal conditions).
- **China M2 YoY (8.6%) ↔ Credit Structure Weakness:** Despite strong M2 growth, social financing (社融) growth slowed to 7.8%; indicates "deposit migration" to equities rather than real economy lending.
- **World Bank Energy Forecast (24% surge) ↔ Inflation in Developing Economies (5.1%):** Commodity-driven inflation transmission is now quantified; developing economy inflation projected 1pp higher than pre-war baseline.

### New Correlations Identified (May 22, 2026)
- **Fed Hawkish Sentiment Persistence ↔ Treasury Yield Stability:** Despite mixed market reaction, continued Fed hawkish rhetoric (more officials warning of rate hikes on May 21) kept 10yr Treasury yields anchored above 4.60%, reinforcing inflation risk premium.
- **Iran Uranium News → Oil Price Surge → Inflation Expectations → Yield Pressure:** Iran's supreme leader directive on near-weapons-grade uranium retention (May 22) sparked renewed geopolitical risk, driving Brent crude ~2% higher and WTI ~2.8% higher, transmitting to 10yr yield rise of ~4-5 bps, confirming oil-yield correlation as leading indicator for Fed policy.
- **Global M2 Expansion ↔ Bitcoin/Gold Performance Lag:** Record global M2 ($121.9T) continues to support debasement narrative; Bitcoin and gold show muted short-term response but maintain upward bias over 3-4 month horizon, validating lagged transmission.
- **China M2 Growth ↔ Domestic Equity Shift:** China's M2 YoY growth at 8.6% alongside slowing social financing (7.8%) correlates with persistent relative strength in domestic equities (CSI 300) vs. fixed income, indicating ongoing deposit migration to equities.
- **Oil Volatility ↔ Tech Sector Rotation:** Oil price spikes (geopolitically driven) continue to trigger rotation from software to energy/infrastructure sectors, as seen in modest Dow outperformance vs. Nasdaq on May 22.
- **Supply Shock Frequency ↔ Fed Reactivity (New):** Emerging thesis from Richmond Fed (May 21) suggests a paradigm shift: the Fed may stop "looking through" supply shocks (energy/inputs) if they become frequent. This shifts the Fed's reaction function toward aggressive tightening even in the absence of demand-pull inflation.
- **Energy-Core Inflation Passthrough ↔ Rate Cut Ceiling (New):** IMF analysis (March/May 2026) confirms that energy price spikes are creating a durable floor for core inflation, effectively removing the "room to cut" interest rates in 2026, regardless of labor market cooling.
