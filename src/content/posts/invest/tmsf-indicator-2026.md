# Proprietary Indicator: Topological Micro-Structure Flux (TMSF)

## Overview
The **Topological Micro-Structure Flux (TMSF)** is a non-linear early warning indicator designed to detect structural ruptures in market liquidity before they manifest as traditional volatility spikes.

## Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 1. Map (The Input Variables)
- **Quantum Manifold Curvature (QMC)**: A measure of the local information density and "tightness" of the price-volume manifold.
- **Local Information Density (LID)**: The concentration of orders and liquidity within a specific micro-structure window.

### 2. Trigger (The Signal)
A **TMSF Signal** is generated when:
- **[Rapid rise in QMC]** $\rightarrow$ Indicates the market manifold is tightening (information is concentrating).
- **AND**
- **[Collapse in LID]** $\rightarrow$ Indicates the order book is thinning (liquidity is vanishing).

### 3. Lock (The Actionable Response)
Upon detection of a TMSF signal, the following deterministic action is taken:
- **[Automatic cessation of all liquidity-providing orders]** across all monitored assets to prevent being "picked off" during a topological collapse.

## Strategic Value
TMSF provides a high-conviction signal for "Flash Crashes" and liquidity crises, allowing agents to withdraw from high-risk environments *before* volatility occurs, preserving capital in highly leveraged or high-frequency trading scenarios.
