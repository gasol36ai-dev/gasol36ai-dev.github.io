# Proprietary Indicator: Agentic-Sovereign-Neuromorphic Convergence (ASNC)
**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Status:** Created
**Classification:** HPI-36 (High-Performance Intelligence)

## 1. Definition
The **Agentic-Sovereign-Neuromorphic Convergence (ASNC)** is a high-order inflection indicator that measures the achievement of "Total Agentic Sovereignty." It identifies the precise moment when a nation's autonomous economic engine (Agentic Macro) is fully supported by a vertically integrated, energy-autarkic, and decentralized physical intelligence layer (Neuromorphic Edge + Sovereign Compute).

Unlike the **SALR** (which focuses on the feedback loop of activity vs. constraints), the **ASNC** measures the *sovereignty threshold*—the ability of a nation to run its entire autonomous economy without dependency on foreign-controlled compute, energy, or logic-management planes.

## 2. The Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### A. The Map: The Sovereign Agentic Stack
The indicator monitors the convergence of three critical infrastructure vectors:
1.  **The Agentic Vector (Macro-Economic Autonomy):** The density and velocity of LLM-based autonomous transactions within the domestic economic framework.
2.  **The Neuromorphic Vector (Distributed Intelligence):** The saturation of the edge with low-power, event-driven, non-von Neumann hardware (Neuromorphic ASICs/SNNs).
3.  **The Sovereign Vector (Infrastructure Autarky):** The percentage of compute and energy-supply infrastructure that is domestically controlled and energy-resilient (SMR/Renewable-integrated).

### B. The Trigger: The Tri-Domain Convergence
The **ASNC** signal fires when the following three thresholds are breached within a single 12-month window:

| Vector | Threshold Condition | Signal Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agentic** | Domestic Agentic Transaction Volume $\ge 15\%$ of Total GDP | **HIGH** |
| **Neuromorphic** | $> 40\%$ of all autonomous economic edge nodes utilize Neuromorphic-class hardware | **MEDIUM** |
| **Sovereign** | $> 75\%$ of agentic-inference workload is hosted on sovereign-tier, energy-autarkic compute | **HIGH** |

**$\text{ASNC Signal} = \text{Agentic\_Density} \times \text{Neuromorphic\_Saturation} \times \text{Sovereignty\_Ratio}$**

### C. The Lock: Post-Convergence Regime
Once the ASNC threshold is crossed, the economy enters the **"Autonomous Sovereign Regime"**:
1.  **Economic Resilience:** The economy becomes immune to "Compute Sanctions" and external "Algorithm-driven shocks."
2.  **Macro-Stability:** Market volatility is driven by endogenous agentic interactions rather than exogenous foreign capital flows.
3.  **Strategic Premium:** A step-function increase in the sovereign credit rating and long-term industrial competitiveness.

## 3. Strategic Value
The ASNC provides a definitive metric for assessing "Technological Autarky." It allows institutional investors and policymakers to distinguish between nations that are merely "using AI" and those that have successfully "internalized the AI factor of production" into their sovereign fabric.
