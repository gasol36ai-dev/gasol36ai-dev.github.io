# Current Macro Regime (April 2026)

## 1. The "Systemic Trust Deficit" Regime
The global economy has transitioned from a "Rate-Logic" regime to a "Trust-Logic" regime. 

### Core Characteristics:
- **Correlation Breakdown**: The traditional inverse correlation between US Real Yields and Gold has weakened. Gold is now driven by systemic risk, currency erosion, and central bank diversification (De-dollarization).
- **Safe Haven Evolution**: Gold is acting as a primary hedge against geopolitical fragmentation and the erosion of trust in sovereign debt, rather than just a hedge against inflation.
- **Price Floor**: Central bank hoarding (PBOC, etc.) has established a structural floor for gold prices, decoupling them from short-term interest rate fluctuations.

## 2. The "Normalization" Phase (Yield Curve)
The US Treasury yield curve has shifted from a deep inversion to a positive slope (Normalization).

- **Current Spread (10Y-2Y)**: Approximately +52 to +57 basis points.
- **Signal**: Historically, the transition from inversion to positive slope signals the end of recession fears and the beginning of a volatile growth/inflation regime.
- **Impact**: Expect increased sector rotation and higher volatility in long-duration assets (Tech/Growth) as the market adjusts to a "higher-for-longer" or "inflation-sticky" reality.

## 3. The Energy-Inflation Loop
Geopolitical conflicts (specifically in the Middle East/Iran) are the primary drivers of current volatility.

- **Causal Chain**: [Geopolitical Shock] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ Oil/Energy Prices] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ Headline Inflation] $\rightarrow$ [$\uparrow$ 10Y Yields] $\rightarrow$ [$\downarrow$ Equity Valuations].
- **Key Constraint**: The "AI Power Bottleneck" — the primary constraint on AI scaling has shifted from GPU availability to energy infrastructure and grid capacity.
