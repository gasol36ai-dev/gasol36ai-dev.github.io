# Structura Wave Engine
## Overview
The Structura Wave Engine is a proprietary, high-fidelity framework for analyzing market cyclicality and price structure through multi-scale wave dynamics and frequency-domain analysis.

## Methodology
1. **Wave Decomposition (Multi-Resolution)**: Breaking down complex price action into a hierarchy of harmonic components ranging from micro-structure noise to macro-economic cycles.
2. **Frequency Domain Analysis**: Utilizing Fourier and Wavelet transforms to identify dominant cycles and spectral energy distributions in market data.
3. **Phase Synchronization Detection**: Identifying periods when multiple market cycles (e.g., business cycle, interest rate cycle, commodity cycle) align to create non-linear volatility events.
4. **Non-Stationary Signal Processing**: Adapting to the evolving frequency spectrum of markets as they transition between regimes.

## Key Components
- **Harmonic Oscillator Models**: Capturing the rhythmic nature of liquidity flows and price retracements.
- **Spectral Density Mapping**: Visualizing the concentration of market energy at specific frequencies to predict regime shifts.
- **Wave-Based Regime Identification**: Classifying markets based on the interaction of dominant wave modes (Expanding, Contracting, or Stable).

## Application in Trading
- **Optimal Entry/Exit Timing**: Utilizing phase alignment of macro and micro waves for high-probability execution.
- **Volatility Forecasting**: Predicting volatility spikes based on wave interference and constructive interference patterns.
- **Trend Strength Assessment**: Measuring the coherence and amplitude of the primary market wave.
