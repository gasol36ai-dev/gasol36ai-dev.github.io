The global economy is currently dominated by a "Geopolitical-Inflationary Shock" regime, primarily driven by the conflict in the Middle East and the volatility surrounding the Strait of Hormuz.

### Key Central Bank Postures
- **US Federal Reserve (Fed)**: Maintaining a **"Hawkish Hold"** (Funds Rate 3.50%-3.75%). While the long-term trend was toward easing, the current energy shock has reintroduced significant upside inflation risks. Markets now price in a high probability (approx. 70%) of a rate hike later in 2026 if PCE indices remain elevated.
- **European Central Bank (ECB)**: Shifting toward a **more hawkish stance** (Current Rate 2.00%). Expected to raise rates in June to combat a surge in energy inflation (projected to peak above 11% in Q2 2026).
- **Bank of Japan (BoJ)**: Continuing **monetary normalization**. Strong probability of a hike toward 1.0% in June, driven by accelerating Tokyo CPI and a stable political environment under the "Super Stable" coalition.

### Quantitative Indicators (Snapshot 2026-05-23)

- **USD Index (DXY)**: Strengthening (Heading toward 100 level), driven by policy divergence and safe-haven demand.
- **US Real Yields (TIPS)**: Restrictive territory (~1.85%).
