# Macro-to-Asset Transmission Mechanisms

This library documents the causal links between macroeconomic variables and asset class performance observed in the 2026 regime.

## 1. Energy Shock $\rightarrow$ Inflation $\rightarrow$ Policy
- **Causal Chain**: $\text{Middle East Conflict} \rightarrow \text{Oil Price} \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Headline CPI} \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Central Bank Hawkishness} \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Bond Yields} \uparrow$.
- **Asset Impact**: 
    - **Equities**: General compression of P/E multiples due to higher discount rates. 
    - **Energy/Defense**: Direct beneficiaries (Positive correlation).
    - **Consumer Discretionary**: Negative impact due to higher input costs and reduced disposable income.

## 2. Geopolitical Risk $\rightarrow$ Safe Havens
- **Causal Chain**: $\text{Geopolitical Instability} \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Risk Aversion} \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Flight to Quality}$.
- **Asset Impact**: 
    - **Gold**: $\uparrow$ (Safe haven demand + Hedge against energy inflation).
    - **USD**: $\uparrow$ (Relative strength against EM currencies).
    - **US Treasuries**: Mixed (Safe haven bid vs. Inflation-driven sell-off).

## 3. AI Evolution $\rightarrow$ Equity Valuation
- **Causal Chain**: $\text{Inference/Agent Shift} \rightarrow \text{Token Demand} \uparrow \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Cloud/Compute Capex} \uparrow \rightarrow \text{Corporate Earnings} \uparrow$.
- **Asset Impact**: 
    - **Big Tech/Semis**: Sustained premium valuations as long as Capex $\rightarrow$ Revenue conversion is visible.
    - **Power/Infrastructure**: Indirect beneficiaries due to massive energy needs for AI data centers.

## 4. Divergent Growth $\rightarrow$ Currency Pairs
- **Causal Chain**: $\text{US Energy Export} \text{ vs. } \text{EU Energy Import} \rightarrow \text{GDP Growth Divergence} \rightarrow \text{Policy Divergence}$.
- **Asset Impact**: 
    - **EUR/USD**: Downward pressure when energy prices spike, as EU growth is hit harder than US growth.
