# Macro-to-Asset Transmission Patterns Library
## Last Updated: 2026-05-21

## 1. Real Yields → Gold (REGIME SHIFT DETECTED)
||- **Mechanism**: Gold has no yield; competes with yield-bearing assets
||- **Transmission**: Rising real yields increase opportunity cost of holding gold → gold prices fall
||- **Correlation**: Historically -0.82 between 10Y TIPS and gold prices. **NOTE: Correlation has structurally broken in the 2024-2026 regime.**
||- **Current Context**: 
||  - US 10Y TIPS yield ~2.0% (High, but not as restrictive as previous cycles).
||  - Gold is behaving as a **Monetary Debasement** and **Systemic Risk** hedge, decoupling from real yields.
||  - Gold price support is now driven more by Central Bank diversification (BRICS+) and geopolitical risk than by the nominal/real yield differential.
||- **Signal**: Monitor 10Y TIPS yield. If real yields > 2.25%, gold faces significant pressure. If < 1.5%, gold momentum is high.

## 2. USD Index → Emerging Markets
||- **Mechanism**: EM debt often USD-denominated; commodity exports priced in USD
||- **Transmission**: 
||  - Stronger USD → higher debt service costs for EM borrowers → capital outflows
||  - Stronger USD → weaker commodity prices → hurts EM exporters
||  - Stronger USD → makes EM assets more expensive in local currency terms
||- **Current Context**:
||  - Fed on hold vs ECB BoJ divergent policies supporting USD strength.
||  - BoJ normalization (June hike prospect) may cause sudden volatility in JPY/USD carry trades.
||- **Signal**: Monitor DXY, EMBI spreads, commodity prices (copper, oil)

## 3. US Term Structure → Bank Profitability → Credit Growth
||- **Mechanism**: Banks borrow short, lend long; NII = spread between loan yields and deposit costs
||- **Transmission**:
||  - Steepening curve (long yields rising faster than short) → widens NIM → boosts bank profits → increases lending capacity
||  - Flattening/inversion $\rightarrow$ compresses NIM $\rightarrow$ reduces bank profitability $\rightarrow$ tightens credit standards
||- **Current Context**:
||  - 10Y-2Y spread is steepening/positive (+0.48% and rising), which is supportive for bank lending capacity.
||- **Signal**: Monitor yield curve shape, bank lending surveys, credit growth metrics

## 4. Inflation Expectations → Currency Strength
||- **Mechanism**: Higher expected inflation erodes real returns on currency holdings
||- **Transmission**:
||  - Rising inflation expectations $\rightarrow$ currency depreciation pressure (unless offset by higher nominal rates)
||  - Anchored inflation expectations $\rightarrow$ currency stability
||- **Current Context**:
||  - US inflation expectations rising (3.2% forecast) vs Euro area falling (1.9%): Supporting USD strength vs EUR weakness.
||- **Signal**: Monitor breakeven inflation rates, central bank credibility surveys, forex forwards

## 5. Geopolitical Risk → Safe Haven Flows
||- **Mechanism**: Risk-off sentiment increases demand for perceived safe assets
|| - **Transmission**: Escalating geopolitical tension $\rightarrow$ flight to quality $\rightarrow$ increased demand for USD, Treasuries, gold, CHF, JPY
||- **Current Context**: Middle East tensions (Strait of Hormuz) creating energy supply risk premium. Supporting gold and safe haven demand despite high nominal yields.
||- **Signal**: Monitor geopolitical risk indices, oil prices, shipping costs, volatility indices (VIX, GVZ)

## 6. Tech Export Momentum $\rightarrow$ Currency Strength (Taiwan Specific)
||- **Mechanism**: Strong export demand increases foreign currency inflows, putting upward pressure on local currency
||- **Transmission**:
||  - AI-driven semiconductor demand $\rightarrow$ increased Taiwan exports $\rightarrow$ TWD appreciation pressure
||  - Strong TWD $\rightarrow$ hurts competitiveness of other Taiwanese exporters $\rightarrow$ potential policy response
||  - **Current Context**: AI-driven semiconductor cycle is extremely strong; watching for TWD/USD appreciation to trigger CBC response.
||- **Signal**: Monitor Taiwan export data, semiconductor sales (TSMC guidance), TWD/USD, central bank intervention

## 7. Central Bank Policy Divergence $\rightarrow$ Capital Flows
||- **Mechanism**: Interest rate differentials drive carry trades and portfolio allocation
||- **Transmission**:
||  - Higher rates in Currency A vs Currency B $\rightarrow$ capital flows from B to A seeking yield
||  -
||- **Current Context**:
||  - Fed on hold (3.5-3.75%) vs ECB (2.50%) vs BoJ (-0.10% transitioning to 1.0%).
||  - Creates significant USD funding advantage vs EUR/JPY.
||  - BoJ hike in June is the primary risk for carry trade unwind.
||- **Signal**: Monitor policy rate differentials, forward rate agreements, cross-currency basis swaps, fund flow data

## 8. Energy Shock $\rightarrow$ Stagflation $\rightarrow$ Asset Class Divergence
||- **Mechanism**: Energy price spikes increase production costs and drive inflation.
||- **Transmission**:
||  - Energy shock $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Inflation $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Yields $\rightarrow$ $\downarrow$ Growth $\rightarrow$ $\downarrow$ Tech Valuations.
||  - Simultaneously, Energy shock $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Safe Haven Demand $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Gold.
||- **Current Context**: 2026 energy shock (Middle East tension) is the primary driver.
||- **Signal**: Monitor Brent Crude and Oil-driven inflation components in PCE.
