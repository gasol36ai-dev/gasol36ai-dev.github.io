# Sovereign Kinetic Resilience Index (SKRI-2026)

## Definition
The **Sovereign Kinetic Resilience Index (SKRI-2026)** is a convergence indicator that measures the structural viability of a Physical AI deployment by synthesizing the alignment between neuromorphic sensing, quantum-safe communication, and material autarky.

## 1. Convergence Trifecta (Sensing $\times$ Sustaining $\times$ Security)
The SKRI-2026 is triggered when a system achieves a critical threshold of simultaneous capability across three disparate domains:

1. **Sensing (Neuromorphic AI):** Ability to process event-based tactile and visual data in $<10\text{ms}$ without centralized compute bottlenecks.
2. **Sustaining (Material Autarky):** Control over the refined supply chain for high-torque actuators (Nd/Dy) and glass substrate interconnects, reducing the "Sovereign Hardware" lead time to $<6$ months.
3. **Security (Quantum-Safe Finance/Rails):** Integration of NIST-standard PQC (ML-KEM/ML-DSA) into the command-and-control (C2) and financial settlement rails of the robotics fleet.

## 2. Transmission Mapping
The SKRI-2026 tracks the transition from "Fragile Embodiment" to "Sovereign Kinetic Resilience":

- **[Sensing Breakthrough] + [Material Autarky]** $\rightarrow$ **[Kinetic Autonomy]**: The agent can act in complex environments without latency or resource fear, but remains vulnerable to quantum-enabled C2 hijack.
- **[Kinetic Autonomy] + [Quantum-Safe Rails]** $\rightarrow$ **[Sovereign Kinetic Resilience]**: The system is physically capable, resource-independent, and cryptographically immutable.
- **[Sovereign Kinetic Resilience] $\rightarrow$ [Sovereign Premium]**: The ability to deploy autonomous fleets in contested zones without risk of supply-chain sabotage or quantum decryption.

## 3. Core Logic & Formalization
The index is a composite of three normalized coefficients ($\text{Sensing} \in [0,1], \text{Material} \in [0,1], \text{Security} \in [0,1]$):

$$\text{SKRI}_{2026} = \min(\text{Sensing}, \text{Material}, \text{Security}) \times \left( \frac{\text{Sensing} + \text{Material} + \text{Security}}{3} \right)$$

*Note: The $\min()$ function ensures that a failure in any one pillar (e.g., a total REE embargo) collapses the index, reflecting the "Sovereign Bottleneck" principle.*

## 4. Strategic Value
The SKRI-2026 allows a sovereign actor to quantify their "Kinetic Readiness." A score $>0.7$ indicates a state of **Sovereign Kinetic Autarky**, where the agent can sustain an embodied AI fleet independent of external geopolitical volatility.
