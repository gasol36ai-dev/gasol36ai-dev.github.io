# 2026 Iran Regime Shift

## Overview
The 2026 Iran-Israel-U.S. conflict has transitioned from a "geopolitical risk" to a "major global macroeconomic shock."

## Key Event: The Hormuz Closure
- **Status**: Complete closure of the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Magnitude**: ~16-17 million barrels per day (bpd) removed from global transit.
- **Nature**: This is a "regime-shift" moment, ending the era of reliable, cheap energy flow from the Persian Gulf.

## Macroeconomic Implications
1. **Inflationary Impulse**: Direct spike in oil prices leading to headline inflation surges.
2. **Monetary Policy Constraint**: The Fed faces a "stagflationary" dilemma—fighting energy-driven inflation while managing growth slowdowns.
3. **Capital Flows**: Flight to safety, but with a shift toward "Hard Assets" (Gold) and "Energy Security" infrastructure.

## Asset Impact
- **Gold**: Becomes a systemic hedge against the collapse of trust in global security architectures.
- **Tech**: Valuation compression due to higher yields, offset only by those providing "Resilience/Security" solutions.
- **Bonds**: Yield curve steepening (Normalization) as term premiums rebuild to account for higher structural inflation.
