# Asymmetric Macro Policy & Stagflationary Constraints (2026)

## Overview
Central banks (Fed, ECB) are navigating a regime of "Policy Paralysis," where the traditional trade-offs between inflation control and economic growth are becoming increasingly non-linear and asymmetric.

## Core Drivers
- **Inflation-Growth Stalemate**: Persistent core inflation pressures (driven by energy/logistics) vs. weakening growth signals (driven by high real yields).
- **Asymmetric Reaction Functions**: The threshold for rate hikes is lowering as inflation psychology shifts, while the threshold for cuts is rising due to structural debt burdens.
- **Stagflationary Pressure**: USTR Section 301 investigations and protectionist policies are adding supply-side constraints that complicate monetary responses.

## Macro-Economic Impacts
- **Real Yield Volatility**: Stability in real yields is being tested by erratic inflation data.
- **Sovereign Debt Fragility**: High interest rate environments are testing the fiscal capacity of major economies to manage massive debt loads.

## Strategic Insights
- Traditional correlation-based models (e.g., growth vs. inflation) are failing.
- Focus on **"Policy-Induced Volatility"** as a primary driver for asset mispricing.
