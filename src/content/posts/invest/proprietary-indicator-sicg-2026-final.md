# Proprietary Judgment Indicator: Sovereign Intelligence Convergence Gate (SICG)

## 1. Overview
The **Sovereign Intelligence Convergence Gate (SICG)** is a high-order judgment indicator designed to identify the precise inflection point where a nation-state transitions from "AI-Adopting" to "AI-Sovereign." This transition triggers a non-linear "Sovereign Premium" across that nation's bonds, currency, and strategic industrial valuation.

## 2. Theoretical Foundation (Cross-Domain Synthesis)
The SICG synthesizes three distinct strategic domains to create a singular decision gate:
1. **Compute Autarky (Infrastructure)**: The transition from reliance on foreign GPUs/Cloud to a domestic, vertically integrated compute stack (including power/cooling).
2. **Cognitive Sovereignty (Data/Model)**: The ability to train and align foundation models on domestic, culturally-specific, and sovereign-classified data without "leakage" to foreign alignment paradigms.
3. **Physical Execution (Robotics/Edge)**: The integration of these models into a domestic "Physical AI" stack (e.g., autonomous drones, humanoid labor, smart-grid control).

## 3. Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 🗺️ Map: Convergence Vectors
The indicator monitors the simultaneous alignment of three metrics:
- **Vector A (Compute)**: $\text{Domestic TFLOPS} / \text{Total Peak Demand} \ge 0.7$
- **Vector B (Cognition)**: $\text{Sovereign Model Performance} / \text{Global SOTA} \ge 0.85$ (on sovereign-critical benchmarks)
- **Vector C (Physical)**: $\text{Domestic Embodied AI Deployment} \ge \text{Critical Infrastructure Threshold}$

### ⚡ Trigger: The Convergence Event
The **SICG Signal** triggers when all three vectors cross their respective thresholds within a 12-month window.
- **Trigger Condition**: $\text{SICG} = \min(\text{Vector A, Vector B, Vector C}) \ge \text{Threshold}_{crit}$
- **Observation**: The transition is not linear; once the third vector locks, the synergy between compute, cognition, and physical execution creates a step-function increase in sovereign capability.

### 🔒 Lock: Strategic Reaction
Upon a positive SICG signal, the following reactions are locked in:
1. **Bond Market**: Transition from "Risk-Premium" to "Sovereign-Premium" pricing.
2. **Currency**: Structural appreciation driven by the "Intelligence-Export" capacity.
3. **Defense Procurement**: Pivot to AI-native, autonomous-first defense procurement (non-linear capex cascade).

## 4. Validation & Strategic Value
Unlike single-domain indicators (e.g., just tracking GPU counts), the SICG prevents "False Sovereignty" hallucinations where a nation has compute but no aligned models, or models but no physical execution.

**Strategic Value**: Provides a lead-time of 6-18 months to identify "Quantum Leap" nations before the market prices in the Sovereign Premium.
