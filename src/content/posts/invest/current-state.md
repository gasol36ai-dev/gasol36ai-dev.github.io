# Global Macro State - May 2026

## 1. Monetary Policy & Inflation
- **US Federal Reserve**: Targeting 2% inflation. Current environment is characterized by uncertainty. March 2026 saw a jump in CPI to 3.3% (from 2.4% in Feb), suggesting a resurgence of inflationary pressure.
- **Core PCE**: Hovering around 3.0% (YoY), remaining above the Fed's target.
- **ECB**: Maintaining a cautious stance, generally keeping rates unchanged to balance growth and inflation.
- **BoJ**: Continuing to manage the transition away from negative rates, though geopolitical shocks complicate the path.

## 2. Geopolitical Landscape (The Dominant Risk)
- **Geopolitical Risk as Top Concern**: Has overtaken traditional economic risks.
- **Trade Wars**: "Liberation Day" tariffs (145% on China) have fundamentally altered trade flows and added to inflationary pressure.
- **West Asia Crisis**: Ongoing instability adding to energy price volatility and supply chain risks.
- **US Credit Rating**: Moody's stripping the US of its top credit rating has introduced structural instability into the US Treasury market.

## 3. Capital Flows & Market Sentiment
- **Emerging Markets (EM)**:Experiencing a "divergence" in capital flows. FDI is declining due to geopolitical risk, but FPI ("hot money") is increasing as speculators seek risk premiums.
- **Flight to Safety**: Strong demand for gold and selective "safe haven" assets, driven by political pressure on Fed independence and US credit concerns.

## 4. Summary Macro Narrative
The 2026 macro environment is defined by a shift from "Cyclical Economics" to "Structural Geopolitics". Traditional correlations (e.g., Real Yields vs Gold) are being rewritten by systemic risks including US credit downgrades, tariff wars, and political interference in central banking.
