# Proprietary Indicator: Physical-Macro Convergence Gate (PMCG)

## Overview
The **Physical-Macro Convergence Gate (PMCG)** is a multi-domain judgment indicator designed to identify the inflection point where geopolitical/macroeconomic shifts (e.g., resource nationalism, supply chain fragmentation) manifest as tangible, non-linear price action in physical AI infrastructure components and energy-intensive compute assets.

## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. Map (The Inputs)
The indicator synthesizes data from two disparate domains to create a unified "convergence" metric:

*   **Domain A: Geopolitical/Macro Volatility Index (GMVI)**
    *   *Components*: Spreads in sovereign bond yields of major energy/mineral importers vs. exporters + USD/Commodity basket volatility + Geopolitical Risk (GPR) Index.
    *   *Trend Analysis*: Tracking the "Resilience vs. Efficiency" regime shift.
*   **Domain B: Physical Compute Scarcity Index (PCSI)**
    *   *Components*: Real-time lead times for high-end GPU/NPU clusters + Energy-to-Compute cost ratios (Electricity price in AI hubs $\times$ Compute demand) + HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) supply/demand ratio.
    *   *Trend Analysis*: Monitoring the "Physicality Gap" in AI scaling laws.

### 2. Trigger (The Inflection Point)
The PMCG fires when both indices exhibit **divergent momentum** or **confluence of volatility**:

*   **The "Resilience Flip" Trigger (Bullish for Physical Assets)**: GMVI spikes (indicating macro uncertainty) while PCSI remains stable or trends upward (indicating supply-side tightness). 
    *   *Interpretation*: Macro volatility is being absorbed by the market via a flight to "real" physical assets (compute/energy) rather than traditional liquid hedges.
*   **The "Infrastructure Squeeze" Trigger (Bearish for Tech/Growth)**: A simultaneous spike in both GMVI and PCSI.
    *   *Interpretation*: Geopolitical shocks are directly impacting the physical supply chain of AI infrastructure (e.g., a conflict impacting semiconductor routes or energy grids).
*   **The "Deglobalization Divergence" Trigger**: GMVI increases while PCSI drops (showing efficient but fragile global chains).
    *   *Interpretation*: Market is ignoring structural geopolitical risks in favor of short-term efficiency.

### 3. Lock (The Actionable Decision)
Upon a PMCG signal, the following operational protocol is triggered:

*   **IF PMCG = BULLISH (Resilience Flip)**:
    *   **Action**: Increase exposure to "Physical AI Sovereignty" plays (e.g., specialized semiconductor miners, energy-integrated data center providers, critical mineral producers).
    *   **Risk Limit**: Tighten stop-losses on highly leveraged digital-only assets (SaaS, pure-play software).
*   **IF PMCG = BEARISH/VOLATILE (Infrastructure Squeeze)**:
    *   **Action**: Move to "Defensive Compute" (e.g., established diversified tech giants with vertically integrated supply chains) and increase cash/gold holdings to buffer against systemic supply shocks.
    *   **Risk Limit**: Reduce overall Risk Budget by 25% across all high-growth technology portfolios.
*   **IF PMCG = NEUTRAL (Deglobalization Divergence)**:
    *   **Action**: Maintain current weights but increase "Monitoring Frequency" for geopolitical news events.

## Strategic Value
The PMCG moves beyond simple macro or simple technical analysis. It captures the **isomorphism** between the "Digital Intelligence" layer and the "Physical Infrastructure" layer, providing an early warning for when macroeconomic shocks transition from "sentiment-driven" to "supply-constrained."
