# Global Inflation & Yields (April 2026)

## 📈 US Inflation Data
- **Core CPI (YoY, March 2026)**: 2.60%.
- **Core PCE (YoY, Feb 2026)**: 2.97%.
- **Observation**: Core inflation has cooled significantly from the previous 4.8%+ spikes, moving closer to the Fed's target. However, headline inflation remains sensitive to energy prices.

## 📉 US Treasury Yields
- **10-Year Yield**: ~4.33%.
- **2-Year Yield**: ~3.79%.
- **10Y-2Y Spread**: ~0.54% (Positive).
- **Regime**: **Normalization Phase**. The shift from inversion to a positive slope confirms the transition away from immediate recession fears toward a regime of volatility and sector rotation.

## 🔗 Implications
- Positive real yields (~1.3% - 1.7%) typically pressure non-yielding assets like [[Gold]].
- The steepening curve supports financial sector margins but signals long-term inflation expectations.
