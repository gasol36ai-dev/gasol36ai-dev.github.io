# Microstructure Plumbing: Lambda & VPIN (2026 Analysis)

## Overview
Understanding the "plumbing" of market microstructure is essential for distinguishing between noise and informed trading. Two key metrics dominate the 2026 landscape: **Lambda ($\lambda$)** and **VPIN**.

## Key Metrics

### 1. Lambda ($\lambda$): Price Impact Coefficient
- **Function**: Captures the expected change in price for a given unit of order flow.
- **Insight**: Lambda serves as a proxy for market maker's estimate of information content. High $\lambda$ indicates a "thin" or "fragile" market where even small trades cause large price shifts.

### 2. VPIN: Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading
- **Function**: Measures order flow imbalance normalized by volume buckets.
- **Insight**: High VPIN levels indicate that a significant portion of the current volume is likely driven by "informed" traders, often preceding periods of high volatility or regime shifts.

## Synthesis: The Plumbing-Volatility Link
The interaction between $\lambda$ and VPIN provides a holistic view of market health. A high VPIN coupled with a rising $\lambda$ is a classic signature of a "liquidity drought" where informed trading is aggressively depleting the remaining depth.
