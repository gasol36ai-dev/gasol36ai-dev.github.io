## 1. Definition

The **Quantum-Spatial Sovereignty Inflection Gate (QSIG)** is a multi-domain proprietary indicator that detects the convergence of three distinct sovereign technology thresholds:

1. **Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing** reaching verifiable logical qubit parity (error-corrected "break-even" milestone)
2. **Orbital Infrastructure Sovereignty** — when a nation/bloc controls ≥3 independent sovereign orbital layers (LEO ISR, broadband, and station)
3. **Spatial Computing Pervasive Adoption** — when enterprise spatial computing penetration exceeds 50% in at least 2 strategic verticals (manufacturing + defense/healthcare)

When all three thresholds cross within a **24-month window**, QSIG triggers — signaling a non-linear structural re-rating of the nation's cognitive and physical sovereign capability.

## 2. Domain Synthesis (Three-Layer Stack)

### Layer 1: Quantum Computing — The Cognitive Supremacy Vector
- **Threshold:** Logical qubit error rate < 0.1% AND at least one verifiable quantum advantage demonstration in real-world applications (cryptanalysis, drug simulation, logistics optimization)
- **Key Metrics:** QuOps/second, T-gate fidelity, entanglement fidelity across ≥100 logical qubits
- **Mechanism:** Nations with fault-tolerant QC gain asymmetric advantages in: breaking adversarial public-key cryptography, accelerating drug/materials discovery, and solving combinatorial logistics problems orders of magnitude faster
- **Current Status (2026):** Google Willow (~105 physical qubits) demonstrated 13,000× supercomputer speedup; Quantinuum first universal fault-tolerant gate set; IBM Starling (full FTQC) targeted 2029

### Layer 2: Space Economy — The Orbital Sovereignty Vector  
- **Threshold:** ≥3 sovereign orbital infrastructure layers operational (broadband constellation + ISR/surveillance VLEO + commercial station or refueling capability)
- **Key Metrics:** Satellite uptime SLA, VLEO orbital slot occupancy density, on-orbit servicing transactions/year
- **Mechanism:** Nations controlling orbital infrastructure achieve persistent ISR superiority, uninterruptible communications (decoupled from terrestrial cables), and first-mover rights to high-value orbital positions. US/China orbital duopoly is hardening; EU lags
- **Current Status (2026):** Starlink Gen 2 (100 Gbps OISL), China VLEO constellation (>300 sats), Axiom + Vast commercial stations under construction; ISS decommission by 2030 drives urgency

### Layer 3: Spatial Computing — The Perceptual Sovereignty Vector
- **Threshold:** Enterprise spatial computing penetration >50% in manufacturing + defense/healthcare verticals; WebGPU-native spatial web bypassing app stores
- **Key Metrics:** "Time-to-Experience" latency for spatial web (<2s load), enterprise XR revenue share (>50% of total XR TAM), physical AI co-deployment ratio
- **Mechanism:** Nations where spatial computing is deeply embedded in industrial workflows gain irreversible productivity advantages — AI-guided manufacturing, remote surgery, AR-assisted maintenance. The "Perceptual Sovereignty" is the ability to embed AI perception into physical reality at scale
- **Current Status (2026):** Apple Vision Pro 2 + Meta Quest 4S ecosystem; WebXR/WebGPU standard maturity; Physical AI + spatial XR convergence accelerating
