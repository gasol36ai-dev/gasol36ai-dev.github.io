# Neuro-Quantum Liquidity Pulse (NQLP)

## 💡 Conceptual Origin
The **Neuro-Quantum Liquidity Pulse (NQLP)** is a proprietary, high-order judgment indicator designed to detect systemic "phase transitions" in market liquidity. It identifies the precise moment when the convergence of neuro-symbolic agentic decision-making, quantum-scale volatility, and edge-computing latency creates a non-linear liquidity vacuum.

## ⚙️ Logic & Heuristic
The NQLP monitors the high-frequency convergence of three disparate, yet structurally linked, signals:

1. **Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning Confidence (NSC)**:
   - **Measure**: The statistical confidence score of neuro-symbolic agentic orchestrators (e.g., NSAO-based agents) in their current market-regime classification.
   - **Signal**: A rapid, non-linear drop in NSC indicates that the agentic layer is encountering a "regime-shift surprise"—a state that its neural perceptions have detected but its symbolic logic cannot reconcile, leading to reasoning paralysis.

2. **Liquid-State Machine Entropy (LSE)**:
   - **Measure**: The information-theoretic entropy of the "liquid" state within neuromorphic edge-computing reservoirs (LSM-based edge nodes).
   - **Signal**: A sudden spike in LSE indicates that the temporal patterns of order-flow at the edge are becoming chaotic and unpredictable, suggesting a breakdown in local liquidity provision.

3. **Quantum-Financial Volatility (QFV)**:
   - **Measure**: The real-time volatility measure of assets subject to quantum-accelerated high-frequency trading (QFT) and potential quantum-cryptographic instability.
   - **Signal**: A breakout in QFV represents the macro-scale consequence of the micro-scale agentic/edge instability, often manifesting as a sudden expansion in bid-ask spreads.

$$\\text{NQLP Score} = \\frac{(\\text{NSC}_{\\text{drop}} \\times \\text{LSE}_{\\text{spike}})}{\\text{QFV}_{\\text{breakout}}}$$

**Signal Condition**: 
- **NQLP $\\uparrow$ (Extreme)** $\\rightarrow$ **Structural Liquidity Phase Transition (SLPT) Trigger**.
