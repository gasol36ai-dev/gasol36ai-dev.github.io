# Proprietary Indicator: Bio-Photonics Compute Gate (BPCG)
**ID**: BPCG-2026-01
**Version**: 1.0
**Status**: Proposed / Synthesis

## 1. Logic Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)
**[Map]**: The convergence of **Wetware Organoid Intelligence** (Low-power, high-associative memory) $\times$ **Neuromorphic Photonics** (Light-speed, high-bandwidth interconnects) $\times$ **Sovereign Energy Autarky** (SMR/Fusion localized power).

**[Trigger]**: 
- **Event A**: Demonstration of a stable wetware-photonic interface with latency $< 10\text{ms}$ and signal fidelity $> 95\%$.
- **Event B**: Integration of a sovereign SMR-powered data center specifically designed for "Hybrid Compute" (Bio + Photonic).
- **Event C**: Deployment of a "World Model" that can translate symbolic biological signals into photonic tensor operations in real-time.

**[Lock]**: 
- When $(\text{Event A} \land \text{Event B} \land \text{Event C}) \rightarrow$ **The Bio-Photonics Inflection**. 
- **Reaction**: A non-linear step-function increase in "Cognitive Density" per watt, leading to the collapse of traditional GPU-based AI scaling laws.

## 2. Sovereign Impact
- **Strategic Value**: Complete independence from CMOS-based fab-dependency.
- **Economic Reaction**: Shift in capital flows from "Compute-as-a-Service" (Cloud) to "Intelligence-as-an-Infrastructure" (Sovereign Bio-Nodes).
- **Judgment Gate**: If the BPCG triggers, any investment in traditional silicon-scaling (e.g., 2nm/1nm CMOS) without a hybrid-pathway becomes a "Stranded Asset."

This indicator synthesizes three distinct research domains:
1. **Biocomputing** (Organoid Wetware)
2. **Post-Silicon Compute** (Neuromorphic Photonics)
3. **Sovereign Energy** (SMR/Fusion)
