# Mineral Sovereignty Stack

## Definition
The **Mineral Sovereignty Stack** is the end-to-end pipeline required to transform raw geological deposits of critical minerals into high-performance AI hardware without reliance on external geopolitical choke points.

## The Stack Layers
1. **Extraction (Upstream)**: Direct control of mines for Neodymium (Nd), Praseodymium (Pr), Lithium (Li), Cobalt (Co), and Gallium (Ga).
2. **Refining & Purity (Midstream)**: Localized chemical processing and purity escalation (e.g., Solvent Extraction - SX) to produce battery-grade and semiconductor-grade materials.
3. **Component Fabrication (Downstream)**: Production of high-coercivity magnets, HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) substrates, and photonic modulators.
4. **Integration**: Final assembly of the AI compute fabric (GPUs, NPUs, Interconnects).

## Strategic Choke Points
- **The Purity Hurdle**: Many nations can mine minerals, but few can refine them to the 99.999% purity required for AI chips. Sovereign autonomy requires mastering the midstream.
- **Substitution Intelligence**: Using AI to develop lattice-engineered substitutes for the rarest minerals, effectively "editing" the stack to remove dependencies.
