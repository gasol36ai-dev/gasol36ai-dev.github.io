# Macro Watchlist 2026
## High-Priority Monitoring Areas
1. **Sovereign Debt Sustainability**: Intense focus on US, EU, and China fiscal trajectories in the face of higher-for-longer or regime-shifting interest rates.
2. **Real Yield Regime Shifts**: Monitoring the decoupling of inflation and nominal yields and the emergence of new equilibrium points.
3. **Commodity-Driven Inflation**: Tracking energy, food, and critical mineral price volatility as drivers of structural inflation.
4. **Global Liquidity Cycles**: Monitoring the synchronized vs. divergent movements of major central bank balance sheets.

## Key Metrics and Indicators
- **Global Liquidity Index (GLI)**: Aggregated central bank liquidity provisions and withdrawals.
- **Geopolitical Risk Index (GRI)**: Quantitative assessment of regional tensions and diplomatic frictions.
- **Real Yield Spread (RYS)**: Spread between major sovereign debts adjusted for local inflation expectations.
- **The Divergence Delta**: A proprietary measure of the rate of change in policy divergence between the Fed, ECB, and PBoC.

## Strategic Implications
- **Asset Allocation**: Shifting towards inflation-linked assets or hard commodities during divergence episodes.
- **Currency Strategies**: Leveraging FX volatility arising from interest rate differentials.
- **Risk Management**: Hedging against sudden supply chain disruptions and geopolitical shocks.
