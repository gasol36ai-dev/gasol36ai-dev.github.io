# Asymmetric Policy Paralysis & Hormuz Fragility Synthesis

## Overview
The current macro regime is defined by "Policy Paralysis" at the Fed and ECB, where the tension between stubborn inflation (Core PPI acceleration) and growth confirmation creates a dead-lock in central bank reaction functions.

## Transmission Mapping
[ Hormuz Closure/Fragility ] -> [ Energy Price Shock ] -> [ Stagflationary Constraint ] -> [ Asymmetric Fed Reaction Function ]

## Key Insights
1. **Asymmetric Reaction**: The Fed's reaction function has shifted: the bar to cut rates is now significantly higher, while the bar to raise (or hold) is lower if inflation psychology drifts.
2. **Stagflationary Pressure**: USTR Section 301 investigations and geopolitical fragmentation are introducing structural cost-push inflation that central banks cannot "cut" away.
3. **Real Yield Stability**: Stable real yields are currently capping the upside in metals, despite the hedge demand generated by Hormuz fragility.

## Strategic Value
Identifies a "Macro-Liquidity Trap" where traditional growth-led recovery signals are invalidated by the asymmetric cost of policy error.
