# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Compute Autarky (SCA) Gate

## Overview
The **SCA Gate** identifies critical thresholds where a nation or regional bloc's dependency on external semiconductor and AI infrastructure crosses a "point of no return," triggering massive, non-linear shifts in strategic procurement and domestic capex.

## The Three Pillars (Convergence Factors)
An SCA event is triggered when at least **two of the three** following conditions are met within a 24-month window:

1. **Hardware Dependency Ratio (HDR) > 0.8**: >80% of mission-critical AI compute (e.g., defense, energy grid, sovereign cloud) is reliant on foreign-designed or fabricated silicon.
2. **Lithography Access Constraint (LAC)**: A verified disruption or restriction in access to EUV/DUV lithography or specialized chip-making tools (e.g., ASML/Nikon) impacting domestic roadmap.
3. **Compute-to-GDP Fragility (CGF)**: A projected shortfall in domestic AI compute capacity relative to projected AI-driven GDP growth, creating a "computational deficit."

## Transmission Mapping
[External Supply Shock] $\rightarrow$ [National Security Crisis / Strategic Bottleneck] $\rightarrow$ [Massive Divergent Capex into Domestic Fabric / RISC-V / Sovereign Clouds]

## Strategic Implication
**Bullish on**: Domestic semiconductor equipment manufacturers, regional sovereign cloud providers, and specialized RISC-V ecosystem companies.
**Bearish on**: Globalized hyperscale providers lacking localized infrastructure in the affected region.

## Verification Logic
- Monitor global export control lists.
- Track regional government subsidy announcements (e.g., CHIPS Act, EU Chips Act).
- Analyze semiconductor import/export data from customs.
