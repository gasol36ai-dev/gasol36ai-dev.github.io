# 📋 Macro Watchlist - May 20, 2026

**Last Updated: 2026-05-20**

## 🚨 High Impact Variables (Tier 1)

1. **Middle East Geopolitical Risk (Strait of Hormuz/Iran/Israel)**
   - **Status**: Extremely Elevated / Active Conflict.
   - **Threshold**: **Brent Crude Oil > $110/bbl**. (Current: Approaching $110; major supply disruption risk).
   - **Impact**: Sustains structural inflation floor; triggers flight-to-quality into Gold and USD. Primary driver of global volatility.

2. **US Inflation & Fed Policy (The Warsh Transition)**
   - **Status**: Hawkish Hold.
   - **Focus**: **PCE-CPI Wedge (~60 bps)**. US CPI (May 12) was 3.8% YoY. PPI was high at 6.0% (April). 
   - **Key Event**: Transition from Jerome Powell to Kevin Warsh. Watch for "regime change" signals (leaner Fed balance sheet, lower policy rates long-term vs. short-term inflation volatility).
   - **Impact**: Determines the trajectory of US Real Yields and the USD.

3. **BoJ Policy Normalization (The June Pivot)**
   - **Status**: Hawkish Hold / Signaling Hike.
   - **Focus**: Potential rate hike in June or July 2026. 10Y JGB yields are rising (~2.69%).
   - **Impact**: Primary trigger for global equity volatility and the potential unwind of the Yen carry trade.

4. **US Real Yields (10Y TIPS)**
   - **Status**: Rising/Elevated.
   - **Current Level**: Nominal 10Y ~4.4%. Real yields are highly sensitive to the inflation premium from energy shocks.
   - **Impact**: Headwinds for high-multiple Tech/AI stocks; supports/pressures Gold (monitoring the decoupling thesis).

5. **USD Index (DXY)**
   - **Trend**: Strong/Resilient.
   - **Drivers**: Policy divergence (Fed Hold vs. ECB/BoJ potential hikes) and safe-haven flows from Middle East conflict.
   - **Impact**: Pressure on Emerging Markets (EM) and Eurozone assets.

## ⚠️ Monitoring Variables (Tier 2)

1. **Energy-Driven Stagflationary Signals**
   - **Focus**: Impact of oil prices on core inflation (PCE/CPI) and second-round effects (wage-price).
   - **Impact**: Drives the "Hawkish Hold" necessity for central banks.

2. **AI Infrastructure Pivot**
   - **Focus**: Shift from GPU Compute $\rightarrow$ Agentic Inference $\rightarrow$ Power/Grid Infrastructure.
   - **Impact**: Energy costs (driven by oil/geopolitics) increase the Capex burden for AI expansion.

3. **US Sovereign Credit Risk**
   - **Focus**: Rising debt interest costs ($97B+ in April).
   - **Impact**: Permanent risk premium on Treasuries; reinforces Gold as ultimate insurance.

## 🛠️ Strategic Themes

- **Geopolitical-Inflationary Shock Regime**: Navigating a world where energy-driven inflation and geopolitical risk are the primary volatility drivers.
- **The Great Policy Divergence**: Managing the fallout from varying central bank responses (Fed Hold, BoJ/ECB potential hikes).
- **Systemic Risk Hedging**: Prioritizing Gold and Energy as core hedges against structural instability.
- **Real Yield vs. Gold Decoupling**: Monitoring the structural break where Gold behaves as a systemic risk hedge rather than just a yield competitor.
