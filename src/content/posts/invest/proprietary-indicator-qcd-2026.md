# Proprietary Indicator: Quantum-Cognitive Divergence (QCD) Gate

## Overview
The **Quantum-Cognitive Divergence (QCD) Gate** is a strategic judgment indicator designed to detect structural decoupling between the rate of cryptographic transition and the speed of information warfare. 

As nation-states transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), the "transition window" creates a period of heightened vulnerability where classical security architectures are being deprecated but quantum-resistant ones are not yet fully integrated into real-time cognitive and financial flows.

## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. Map (The Observable Variables)
- **PQC-Migration Velocity (PMV)**: The rate of adoption of NIST-standardized quantum-resistant protocols (FIPS 203, 204, 205) across critical national financial and communication nodes.
- **Narrative Coherence Score (NCS)**: A metric measuring the semantic stability and logical consistency of public and private information streams (detecting synthetic disinformation/deepfakes).

### 2. Trigger (The Inflection Point)
- **[Rapid increase in Narrative Volatility (NCS decline)] + [Stagnant or lagging PQC-Migration Velocity (PMV)]**
- This divergence indicates a "vulnerability window" where malicious actors are using high-velocity, quantum-enabled (or merely sophisticated) disinformation to destabilize systems that are not yet cryptographically hardened against rapid-scale identity/authenticity attacks.

### 3. Lock (The Deterministic Response)
- **[Automatic Execution of 'Cognitive-Cryptographic Hardening Protocol']**
- **Action**: Immediate shift to "Cold-Storage" cognitive enclaves for sensitive decision-making processes.
- **Action**: Implementation of air-gapped, classical-hardened settlement mechanisms to bypass vulnerable digital rails.
- **Action**: Escalation of "Sovereign Narrative Defense" protocols to counter high-entropy disinformation cascades.

## Strategic Value
Identifies the specific window where technical cryptographic lag meets cognitive-domain instability, allowing for proactive defensive positioning before a "topological rupture" in social or financial trust occurs.
