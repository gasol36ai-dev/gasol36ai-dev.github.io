## Indicator Definition

**WFSG** detects the convergence of three simultaneous structural regime locks — when Physical AI fleet deployment scale, sovereign compute infrastructure commitment, and US fiscal dominance signals all cross their respective thresholds together, they collectively create a **supercycle compression gate** in AI-adjacent equity and infrastructure assets.

The core insight: **scale, sovereignty, and fiscal stress are co-dependent amplifiers**. Physical AI fleet deployment requires massive capital; sovereign AI strategies provide that capital outside normal market mechanisms; US fiscal dominance suppresses risk-free rates that normally would throttle speculative capex. When all three fire simultaneously, AI infrastructure assets enter a **structurally non-mean-reverting price regime**.

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 🗺️ Map
```
[WAM/VLA Fleet Deployment Scale] × [Sovereign AI Capex Commitment] × [Fiscal Dominance Regime Active]
```

**Signal Components**:
1. **FDOM (Fleet Deployment Orchestration Metric)**: Robotic fleet units deployed globally ≥ 500K with WAM-class architectures. Proxy: Tesla Optimus + BYD/Xiaomi + industrial robotics YoY fleet growth ≥ 200%
2. **SCII (Sovereign Compute Infrastructure Index)**: ≥3 sovereign AI datacenter commitments announced in any 90-day window (GCC, EU, US CHIPS). Proxy: state-funded GPU supercluster news flow ≥ 3 events per quarter
3. **FDRG (Fiscal Dominance Regime Gate)**: UST 10Y-2Y spread > 80bps **AND** Fed balance sheet not declining YoY **AND** Moody's/S&P outlook negative/downgrade within prior 365 days

### 🔫 Trigger
All three conditions co-fire within a **30-trading-day synchronization window**:
```
FDOM_SURGE + SCII_THRESHOLD + FDRG_ACTIVE → WFSG = FULL SIGNAL
```

Partial signals (2/3 conditions):
- FDOM + SCII (no fiscal dominance): "Fleet Build" — overweight robotics OEMs, underweight pure-play AI
- FDOM + FDRG (no sovereign): "Domestic Cycle" — overweight US-listed robotics, neutral infrastructure
- SCII + FDRG (no fleet): "Sovereign Infrastructure" — overweight energy/compute, underweight SaaS AI

### 🔒 Lock (Portfolio Response)

**FULL SIGNAL Action**:
- **Overweight**: Physical AI hardware OEMs (fleet owners), sovereign AI infrastructure providers, custom ASIC foundries, energy-to-compute plays
- **Underweight**: Pure-play AI model providers without hardware/sovereignty integration, long-duration USTs (fiscal dominance premium), traditional cloud IaaS without AI specialization
- **Hedge**: Long gold (fiscal dominance hedge), long non-USD sovereigns (G7 divergence), long physical commodity inputs (GCC energy)
