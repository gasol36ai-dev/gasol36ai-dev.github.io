## Definition
Neuromorphic Decision Loops are high-speed, event-driven feedback cycles that process sovereign financial data using brain-inspired architectures. They are designed to maintain monetary stability in hyper-volatile environments where classical polling-based systems are too slow or energy-inefficient.

## Technical Mechanism: Event-Driven Stability
Unlike classical CPUs that execute instructions in a linear sequence, these loops utilize **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)** and **Liquid State Machines (LSMs)**:
1. **Asynchronous Spike Processing**: The system reacts only to *changes* (spikes) in data. For example, a sudden 0.1% shift in a specific liquidity pool triggers an immediate reaction, whereas stable data consumes zero processing power.
2. **Dynamic Reservoir Computing**: The "Liquid State" acts as a high-dimensional temporal buffer, allowing the system to detect non-linear macro-financial regime shifts *before* they propagate through the entire market.
3. **Real-time Rail Integration**: These loops are directly integrated with CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency) rails, allowing for automated, sub-millisecond liquidity injections or interest rate adjustments based on systemic stress indicators.

`[Market Volatility Spike] -> [Neuromorphic Event Detection] -> [Automated Rail Adjustment]`
