## Overview
**CSDI** is the **Carbon-Sovereign-Digital Integration Gate** — a proprietary judgment indicator that detects the emergence of a new class of nation-state that has achieved simultaneous sovereignty across three previously disconnected infrastructure stacks: **verifiable carbon accounting** (environmental layer), **quantum-secure cryptographic infrastructure** (computational layer), and **cryptographic identity provenance** (civic/economic layer).

When all three layers converge, a nation crosses a **Sovereign Integrity Threshold** — a point where it can credibly participate in international carbon markets (ITMO trading), defend its digital economy against quantum and AI-based attacks, and enforce identity-verified compliance at the border. This is a **non-linear premium event** that re-rates that nation's sovereign bonds, currency, and industrial competitiveness simultaneously.

## Domain Synthesis Rationale (3-Domain Cross-Pollination)

| Domain | Contribution to CSDI |
|---|---|
| **Climate Finance / Carbon Markets** | Article 6 ITMOs require tamper-proof, verifiable carbon registries. Only nations with high-integrity dMRV can be net sellers rather than net buyers — a structural sovereign revenue advantage |
| **Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing** | FTQC progress creates a "Quantum Cliff" for nations without quantum-safe infrastructure. Nations that reach FTQC threshold first gain the ability to break legacy cryptography underpinning current carbon registry proofs and identity systems — and to generate quantum-advantaged materials for CDR (Direct Air Capture, enhanced weathering) |
| **Digital Identity / SSI** | eIDAS 2.0 / W3C VC standard adoption creates the **provenance layer** for both carbon credit authenticity (linking dMRV outputs to sovereign DID registries) and border-level CBAM compliance enforcement. Nations without SSI infrastructure cannot participate credibly in the CBAM compliance ecosystem |

**Cross-Domain Isomorphism**: All three domains share the same core primitive — **cryptographic provenance**: carbon credits need it for ICVCM Core Carbon Principles compliance; quantum computing threatens it; SSI provides it at scale. The CSDI detects when a nation has closed all three loops simultaneously.

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### MAP (Structural Conditions Required)
```
[High-Integrity dMRV Carbon Registry] 
+ [Active ITMO Sovereign Carbon Program Under Article 6.2] 
+ [PQC/Quantum-Safe Cryptographic Upgrade of National Infrastructure] 
+ [eIDAS 2.0-Compatible or Equivalent National Digital Identity Wallet]
```
All four structural conditions must be observable before the gate becomes "armed."

### TRIGGER (Threshold Crossing Events)
The gate fires when **≥3 of the following 4 signals** activate within an **18-month window**:

1. **Carbon Integrity Signal**: Nation's carbon registry achieves CCP-equivalent certification AND executes first international ITMO transfer under Article 6.2
2. **Quantum Security Signal**: Nation's critical infrastructure (energy grid, financial clearinghouses, border systems) completes NIST FIPS 203/204 migration OR demonstrates indigenous quantum hardware milestone
3. **Identity Provenance Signal**: National Digital ID wallet achieves >30% population adoption with W3C VC-compatible credential issuance
4. **CBAM Compliance Signal**: Nation's industrial exporters achieve verified carbon accounting sufficient to reduce or eliminate CBAM liability on EU-bound goods

### LOCK (Investment Decision Outcomes)
When the TRIGGER fires:

```
LOCK_1 [BOND]: Sovereign bond spread compression expected (−15 to −40bps) 
               → structural upgrade from "compliance risk" to "compliance premium"

LOCK_2 [FX]:   Currency premium for ITMO net-seller status 
               → sustained carbon revenue inflow creates structural current account improvement

LOCK_3 [EQUITY]: Industrial sector re-rating for exporters 
               → CBAM liability elimination removes margin headwind; 
               → domestic CDR infrastructure companies become long-term infrastructure plays

LOCK_4 [TECH]:  Quantum-safe identity stack becomes export product 
               → "Digital Sovereignty SaaS" revenue stream (replicating the Brussels Effect)
```
