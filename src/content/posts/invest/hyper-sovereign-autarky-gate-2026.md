## 1. Theoretical Foundation: The Planetary Decoupling Thesis
The HSAG-2026 indicator monitors the transition of a sovereign entity from a "terrestrial-dependent actor" to a "Sovereign Node." This transition occurs when an entity achieves simultaneous autonomy in the three fundamental constraints of intelligence: **Compute (Substrate), Energy (Baseload), and Capital (Liquidity).**

### The Isomorphism of Autarky
- **Compute**: Transition from Silicon $\rightarrow$ Optical/Hybrid = Elimination of fab-dependency.
- **Energy**: Transition from Terrestrial Grid $\rightarrow$ Orbital Solar = Elimination of geographic/resource-dependency.
- **Capital**: Transition from Fiat/Equity $\rightarrow$ Bio-Digital RWA = Elimination of centralized banking/clearing-dependency.

When these three vectors align, the entity enters a state of **Absolute Autarky**, where the cost of intelligence production drops toward the marginal cost of orbital maintenance and biological upkeep, decoupled from planetary volatility.

## 2. Map-Trigger-Lock (MTL) Formalization

### A. The Map (Dependency Graph)
$\text{Sovereignty} \rightarrow f(\text{Compute}, \text{Energy}, \text{Liquidity})$

### B. The Trigger (Convergence Thresholds)
The gate triggers when the following non-linear conditions are met simultaneously:

$$\text{HSAG}_{\text{active}} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } (\text{C}_{\text{opt}} \ge \tau_c) \land (\text{E}_{\text{orb}} \ge \tau_e) \land (\text{L}_{\text{bio}} \ge \tau_l) \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

**Threshold Definitions**:
- $\tau_c$ (**Compute Threshold**): Ability to train a $\ge 10\text{T}$ parameter model using $\ge 70\%$ optical/hybrid interconnects, reducing latency by $10\text{x}$ and energy by $5\text{x}$ compared to H100-class clusters.
- $\tau_e$ (**Energy Threshold**): Orbital energy capture and wireless transfer providing $\ge 50\text{GW}$ of dedicated baseload to sovereign compute clusters.
- $\tau_l$ (**Liquidity Threshold**): $\ge 20\%$ of sovereign reserves backed by tokenized Bio-Digital RWAs (Wetware/Organoid compute), allowing for self-financing of infrastructure without external debt.

### C. The Lock (Sovereign Node State)
Upon triggering, the entity locks into a **Sovereign Node** state. This results in a **Sovereign Premium Cascade**:
1. **Compute Dominance**: Asymptotic advantage in intelligence iteration speed.
2. **Energy Arbitrage**: Ability to export "Orbital Intelligence" as a service to terrestrial states.
3. **Capital Independence**: Complete immunity to terrestrial sanctions or interest rate shocks.
