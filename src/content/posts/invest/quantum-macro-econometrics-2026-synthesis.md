# Quantum-Safe Macro-Econometrics

**Quantum-Safe Macro-Econometrics** is an emerging interdisciplinary field that analyzes the impact of the transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) on global financial stability, sovereign credit ratings, and the transmission of macroeconomic signals. Unlike traditional cybersecurity, which focuses on individual firm risk, this field examines **systemic cryptographic risk** as a macroeconomic variable.

## 1. Technical Foundations: The NIST PQC Transition
The shift from classical Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) to PQC is anchored by the 2024-2025 NIST standards. The macroeconomic stability of the global financial system now depends on the deployment of:
*   **ML-KEM (Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism):** Replacing RSA/Diffie-Hellman for secure key exchange. Critical for maintaining the confidentiality of central bank communications and interbank settlement instructions.
*   **ML-DSA (Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm):** Replacing ECDSA/RSA for authenticity. Critical for the non-repudiation of sovereign debt issuance and the integrity of "Digital Central Bank Currencies" (CBDCs).
*   **SLH-DSA (Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Algorithm):** Used as a robust fallback for high-value, low-frequency sovereign signatures.

## 2. Financial Stability & Systemic Risk
The primary macroeconomic threat is the **"Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL)** paradigm. State actors are currently capturing encrypted financial data (SWIFT messages, sovereign debt contracts) to decrypt them once a Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer (CRQC) arrives.

### Systemic Fragility Points:
*   **Settlement Paralysis:** If ML-DSA is not integrated into the global payment rails (e.g., SWIFT, CLS), a CRQC event would invalidate all digital signatures, freezing the global FX and bond markets.
*   **The PQFIF Framework (2025):** The *Post-Quantum Financial Infrastructure Framework* (PQFIF) proposes a tiered migration for digital assets, establishing "quantum-safe corridors" for high-value settlements to prevent systemic contagion.

## 3. Sovereign Secure-Comms & Macro-Economic Signaling
Macroeconomic stability relies on the **credible signaling** of central banks (e.g., interest rate pivots, QE adjustments).
*   **Signaling Integrity:** If a sovereign's secure communication channels are compromised via quantum decryption, "leakage" of policy shifts before official announcements can lead to extreme front-running and market volatility.
*   **Sovereign Crypto-Agility:** The ability of a state to rotate its cryptographic primitives (Crypto-Agility) is now viewed as a proxy for **institutional quality**. Countries that cannot rapidly pivot to ML-KEM/ML-DSA are perceived as having higher "technical debt" and higher systemic risk.

## 4. The 'Quantum-Safe' Premium
A divergence is emerging in the valuation of sovereign debt and FX, termed the **Quantum-Safe Premium (QSP)**.

### Sovereign Debt Markets
Investors are beginning to price in a "Quantum Risk Premium" for nations lagging in PQC adoption. 
*   **QSP Advantage:** Sovereigns that have certified their debt issuance rails as ML-DSA compliant exhibit lower yields (a "security premium") due to the reduced risk of fraudulent bond issuance or ownership disputes in a post-quantum world.
*   **The Laggard Penalty:** Non-compliant sovereigns face higher borrowing costs as the "Quantum-Risk" is factored into the credit default swap (CDS) pricing.

### FX Markets
Currency volatility is increasingly linked to the "Quantum Readiness" of the issuing central bank.
*   **Safe Haven Shift:** In periods of quantum-threat escalation, capital flows toward currencies backed by "Quantum-Safe" infrastructures, creating a new class of "Cryptographic Safe Havens."
