# Concept: Quantum-Safe Financial Rails

## Definition
Quantum-Safe Financial Rails refer to the modernization of global payment, settlement, and clearing systems to be resilient against attacks from cryptographically relevant quantum computers (CRQCs) while simultaneously enabling interoperability between diverse digital asset ledgers (CBDCs).

## Core Pillars
1. **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC):** The use of lattice-based (ML-KEM, ML-DSA) and hash-based (SLH-DSA) algorithms to replace RSA and ECC.
2. **Cryptographic Agility:** The architectural ability to update cryptographic primitives without requiring a full system rewrite.
3. **CBDC Interoperability:** The creation of shared "Unified Ledgers" (e.g., BIS Project Agorá) that allow atomic settlement across different sovereign digital currencies.
4. **Hybrid Security:** The temporary use of dual-signatures (Classical + PQC) to ensure stability during the transition period.

## Key Transmission Mappings
- **Security:** [Quantum Threat] $\rightarrow$ [PQC Migration] $\rightarrow$ [Systemic Stability]
- **Liquidity:** [Fragmentation] $\rightarrow$ [Interoperable Layers] $\rightarrow$ [Atomic Settlement]
