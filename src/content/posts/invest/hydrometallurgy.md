# Hydrometallurgy (Mid-Stream Processing)
**Domain**: Critical Minerals / Material Science / Sovereign Supply Chain
**Date**: 2026-07-05
**Status**: Stub Expansion (ISS)

## Core Thesis
Hydrometallurgy is the chemical process of extracting metals from ores using aqueous chemistry. In the transition to a green economy, the ability to perform high-efficiency, low-carbon hydrometallurgical extraction of Rare Earth Elements (REEs), Lithium, and Cobalt is a prerequisite for sovereign industrial autonomy.

## Transmission Mapping
**[Event]** $\rightarrow$ **[Mechanism]** $\rightarrow$ **[Reaction]**
- **[Raw Ore Ingest]** $\rightarrow$ **[Leaching (Acid/Alkaline) $\rightarrow$ Selective Ion Exchange]** $\rightarrow$ **[High-Purity Metal Concentrate]**
- **[Environmental Regulation Tightening]** $\rightarrow$ **[Closed-Loop Solvent Extraction $\rightarrow$ Tailings Neutralization]** $\rightarrow$ **[Reduction of Ecological Footprint/Regulatory Compliance]**
- **[Supply Chain Blockade]** $\rightarrow$ **[Domestic Urban Mining (E-waste) $\rightarrow$ Hydrometallurgical Recovery]** $\rightarrow$ **[Diversification of Mineral Source Portfolio]**

## Technical Architecture
1. **Leaching**: The use of sulfuric acid or bio-leaching (using microbes) to dissolve metals into a solution.
2. **Solvent Extraction (SX)**: Utilizing organic solvents to selectively separate target metals from the leach liquor.
3. **Electrowinning**: The application of an electric current to recover the pure metal from the solution.

## Strategic Value: The "Mid-Stream" Vector
The "mid-stream" is the bottleneck of the mineral supply chain. While many nations have raw ores (up-stream), few have the hydrometallurgical capacity to refine them into battery-grade materials. Sovereignty in hydrometallurgy prevents "refinement-dependency" on a single geopolitical actor.

## Convergence Gates
- **Hydrometallurgy $\times$ AI-Driven Catalyst Design**: Using ML to find new solvents that reduce acid use and increase recovery rates.
- **Hydrometallurgy $\times$ RWA Tokenization**: Creating "digital twins" of mineral reserves based on verified hydrometallurgical purity.
