# 2026 Macro State Summary (April)

## Current Baseline
- **Regime**: Structural Geopolitical Disruption & Sticky Inflation.
- **Growth**: S&P Global and IMF reporting slowed GDP growth forecasts for 2026.
- **Inflation**: Global Core CPI hovering around 3%. US PCE accelerating due to energy costs.
- **Bond Market**: US 10Y at ~4.31%, 2Y at ~3.78%. Curve is positively sloped (steepening), reflecting a shift from the deep inversion of 2023-2025.
- **Monetary Policy**: Fed maintains a restrictive but balanced stance. Markets are weighing a "higher for longer" narrative against the need to support slowing growth.

## Key Themes
1. **The Great Divergence**: Gold and Treasury yields are rising in tandem, breaking the historical inverse correlation.
2. **The Capex Supercycle**: Geopolitical friction is driving a massive, capital-intensive rewiring of global supply chains (Near-shoring/Friend-shoring), specifically benefiting Mexico and select Asian hubs.
3. **AI Power Bottleneck**: Energy availability and power infrastructure have become the primary constraints on AI scaling, shifting focus from "chip availability" to "grid capacity."
4. **Inflation Premium**: Bondholders are demanding a higher term premium to compensate for structurally higher energy prices (Brent > $80).
