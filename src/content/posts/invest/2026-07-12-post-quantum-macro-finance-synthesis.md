## 1. Executive Summary
The global financial architecture is entering a convergence regime where the fundamental nature of sovereign security (Quantum Resistance) and economic control (AI-Native Policy) are being rewritten. The primary systemic risk has shifted from "policy error" to **Algorithmic Divergence** and **Cryptographic Collapse**. This synthesis analyzes the integration of Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) into the macro-financial "plumbing" and the subsequent transition to a regime of continuous, agentic liquidity management.

## 2. Quantum-Safe Financial Rails: The Infrastructure Layer

### 2.1 The Threat Landscape: HNDL & Systemic Trust
The "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) strategy poses an existential threat to sovereign debt markets and central bank confidentiality. Encrypted communications captured today will be decrypted by Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computers (CRQCs), potentially exposing sovereign strategic positions and destabilizing trust in digital ownership.

### 2.2 Technical Deployment of PQC
To secure the financial rails, the transition to PQC-native infrastructure is mandatory:
- **Sovereign Debt & Settlement:** Transitioning to lattice-based standards (ML-KEM/Kyber for key exchange, ML-DSA/Dilithium for signatures) to ensure the integrity of bond issuance.
- **Root-of-Trust:** Deployment of hash-based signatures (XMSS/LMS) for CBDC infrastructure updates, prioritizing long-term security over payload size.
- **Hybrid Posture:** Adoption of hybrid-quantum architectures combining PQC (algorithmic) and QKD (physical) to eliminate single points of cryptographic failure.

### 2.3 The "Security Tax" and PQC-DoS
The transition introduces a significant increase in payload sizes. Traditional network stacks are vulnerable to **PQC-DoS**, where fragmented quantum-safe keys trigger buffer overflows in legacy edge routers.
- **Mitigation:** Implementation of **PQC-Aware Gateways** utilizing "Payload Stitching" and extended buffer pools to prevent transmission failure of critical macro-financial signals.
- **SARM:** Use of Sovereign Autonomous Resilience Meshes to ensure redundant, quantum-secure paths for policy transmission.

## 3. Macro-Financial Stability in the Agentic Economy

### 3.1 Transition to Predictive Monetary Policy (PMP)
Central banks are shifting from interval-based signaling to **Continuous Liquidity Adjustment (CLA)**. 
- **Mechanism:** Policy is no longer an event but a continuous parameter $\text{r}(t)$ pushed via CBDC smart contracts.
- **Omni-Stream Ingestion:** High-frequency data (IoT, orbital sensors) eliminates transmission lag ($\tau \rightarrow 0$), allowing for real-time sectoral liquidity tuning.

### 3.2 Agentic Liquidity Volatility (ALV)
The rise of Autonomous Agentic Economies (AAEs) introduces a new form of systemic risk: **Agentic Liquidity Volatility**.
- **High-Frequency Clustering:** When dominant agents use similar LLM-based macro-sentiment models, they react simultaneously to policy shifts, creating "Momentum Spikes."
- **Non-Linearity:** These spikes bypass traditional human-interval stabilizers, leading to flash-liquidity compression or expansion.
