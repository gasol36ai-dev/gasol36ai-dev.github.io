# Proprietary Indicator: NQT-ASK-AEMG Convergence (NAAC-2026)

## Indicator Name
**NAAC-2026 (Neuro-Asynchronous-Atmospheric Convergence)**

## Definition
A multi-domain convergence indicator that monitors the simultaneous readiness of ultra-low-latency biological telemetry (NQT), event-driven swarm coordination (ASK), and localized energy autonomy (AEMG).

## Mathematical Formalization
$\text{NAAC}_{Score} = \int_{t_0}^{t_f} ( \text{NQT}_{latency}(t) \cdot \text{ASK}_{entropy}(t) \cdot \text{AEMG}_{resilience}(t) ) \, dt$

Where:
- $\text{NQT}_{latency}$ is the normalized inverse of the neuro-quantum telemetry latency.
- $\text{ASK}_{entropy}$ is the measure of swarm coordination efficiency (inverse of topological entropy).
- $\text{AEMG}_{resilience}$ is the ratio of harvested energy to instantaneous demand.

## Transmission Mapping
- **[Event]**: Crossing of the "Triple Threshold" (Low Latency $\land$ High Coordination $\land$ Energy Surplus).
- **[Mechanism]**: Non-linear step-function increase in the capability for high-fidelity, autonomous, multi-agent operations in unmapped environments.
- **[Reaction]**: Transition from "Reactive Edge" to "Sovereign Autonomy" status for the deployed agentic swarm.

## Strategic Decision Gate
When $\text{NAAC}_{Score} > \sigma$ (Threshold):
- **Action**: Shift from decentralized, high-entropy exploration to centralized, low-entropy objective execution.
- **Asset Allocation**: Increase capital/resource commitment to the sovereign domain identified by the swarm's presence.
