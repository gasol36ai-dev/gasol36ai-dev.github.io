# Proprietary Judgment Indicator: NQC-SENSE-2026
**Name**: Neuromorphic-Quantum Synchrony & Error-Rate Gate (NQC-SENSE-2026)
**Version**: 1.0

## 1. 🔭 Conceptual Framework
The **NQC-SENSE-2026** indicator measures the "computational resonance" between biological-inspired event-driven processing (Neuromorphic) and high-dimensional quantum state optimization (Quantum). It serves as a primary decision gate for the adoption of **Hybrid Sovereign Compute Stacks**.

When the synchrony between these two modalities reaches a critical threshold, it signals the transition from classical-quantum hybridity (where the neuromorphic part is just a classical controller) to **true NQH (Neuromorphic-Quantum Hybrid) autonomy**, where the system can manage its own decoherence and optimization loops without terrestrial intervention.

## 2. 🧬 Transmission Mapping
**[Quantum Decoherence/Noise $\uparrow$] $\rightarrow$ [SNN-driven Real-time Error Correction Latency $\downarrow$] $\rightarrow$ [NQH System Stability Threshold $\text{Crossing}$] $\rightarrow$ [Strategic Pivot to Autonomous Hybrid Compute]**

## 3. 📐 Mathematical Formalization
The indicator is defined by the **NQH Synchrony Ratio ($\Xi_{NQH}$)**:

$$\Xi_{NQH} = \frac{\tau_{SNN\_Response}}{\tau_{Qubit\_Decoherence}} \times \left( \frac{\mathcal{I}_{Mutual}(\text{Spike}, \text{Phase})}{\mathcal{H}_{System}} \right)$$

Where:
- $\tau_{SNN\_Response}$: The mean response time of the Spiking Neural Network error-correction loop.
- $\tau_{Qubit\_Decoherence}$: The characteristic decoherence time ($T_2$) of the target quantum processor.
- $\mathcal{I}_{Mutual}(\text{Spike}, \text{Phase})$: The mutual information between the temporal spike patterns and the quantum phase evolution.
- $\mathcal{H}_{System}$: The total system entropy.

**Decision Gate (The SENSE Threshold):**
$$\text{Trigger}_{NQC} \iff \Xi_{NQH} > \sigma_{critical}$$
*Where $\sigma_{critical}$ is the non-linear threshold determined by the specific quantum architecture (e.g., $\sigma \approx 0.1$ for superconducting qubits, $\sigma \approx 0.01$ for trapped ions).*
