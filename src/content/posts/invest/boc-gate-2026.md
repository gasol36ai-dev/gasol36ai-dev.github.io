# Proprietary Indicator: The Bio-Orbital-Compute (BOC) Gate (HPI-32)

## 📋 Indicator Overview
**Name**: Bio-Orbital-Compute (BOC) Gate
**Identifier**: HPI-32 (Hyper-Sovereign Intelligence Indicator)
**Convergence Domains**: Bio-Digital Infrastructure (BDSI) $\times$ Orbital Resource Autonomy (ORA) $\times$ Neuromorphic Opto-electronics (NOCM+).

## 🎯 Strategic Value
The **BOC Gate** identifies the "Triple-Sovereignty Inflection Point"—the moment when an entity achieves simultaneous, self-sustaining control over **Information Persistence** (Bio-Vaults), **Physical Expansion** (Orbital Manufacturing), and **Real-time Reflexive Autonomy** (Neuromorphic Photonics). 

Crossing this gate represents a transition from "Terrestrial-Dependent Intelligence" to "Interplanetary-Sovereign Intelligence."

## 🛠️ Mathematical Formalization
$\text{BOC}_{\text{Score}} = (\text{BDSI}_{\text{stability}} \land \text{ORA}_{\text{autarky}}) \land (\text{NOCM}_{\text{latency}} < \tau_{\text{reflex}})$

Where:
- $\text{BDSI}_{\text{stability}}$: A composite metric of DNA storage density and biological encryption robustness.
- $\text{ORA}_{\text{autarky}}$: The ratio of in-situ produced mass to Earth-launched mass in orbital facilities.
- $\text{NOCM}_{\text{latency}}$: The sub-microsecond response capability of the photonic-neuromorphic substrate.
- $\tau_{\text{reflex}}$: The biological threshold for instinctual (reflexive) reaction.
