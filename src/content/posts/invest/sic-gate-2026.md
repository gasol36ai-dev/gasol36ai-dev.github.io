# Proprietary Indicator: **"Sovereign Intelligence Convergence (SIC) Gate"**

## 1. Concept Overview
The **SIC Gate** is a multi-domain judgment indicator designed to detect the moment when three distinct sovereign vectors—**Compute (Energy-Anchored)**, **Cognition (Agentic/Legal)**, and **Physicality (Logistic/Supply Chain)**—align to create a non-linear step-function in national or enterprise capability.

When these three vectors cross their respective threshold cliffs simultaneously, it triggers a **"Sovereign Autarky Cascade,"** leading to rapid decoupling from global interdependent systems.

---

## 2. Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### **[MAP] — The Three Vectors**
1.  **Vector 1: Energy-Compute Density (ECD)**
    *   *Metric:* Ratio of available high-density power (SMR/Fusion/Renewable-Storage) to localized AI compute capacity (FLOPs/Watt).
2.  **Vector 2: Agentic Legal Maturity (ALM)**
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of cross-entity transactions/interactions governed by machine-readable, cryptographically-verifiable legal protocols (e.g., DLI/Soulbound ID adoption).
3.  **Vector 3: Logistic Autarky Coefficient (LAC)**
    *   *Metric:* The ratio of autonomously managed, regionalized supply chain nodes to externally dependent "just-in-time" global nodes.

### **[TRIGGER] — The Convergence Event**
The SIC Gate is triggered when:
*   **$\Delta$ECD > Threshold_1** (e.g., new SMR deployment or energy breakthrough)
*   **AND $\Delta$ALM > Threshold_2** (e.g., mass adoption of Agentic Legal Frameworks/Soulbound ID)
*   **AND $\Delta$LAC > Threshold_3** (e.g., successful transition to Neural Supply Chains)

*Convergence Condition:* $\sum (\Delta V_i) \geq \text{Sovereignty\_Threshold}$

### **[LOCK] — The Strategic Response**
Upon a SIC Gate signal, the following actions are automatically recommended/executed:
1.  **Capital Reallocation:** Immediate pivot of investment from "Global Interdependence" assets to "Sovereign Autarky" infrastructure (e.g., localized energy-compute parks, domestic agentic-legal hubs).
2.  **Risk Budget Adjustment:** Reduction of exposure to "Fragile Global Flow" assets (e.g., trans-oceanic shipping-dependent commodities, centralized cloud-dependent software).
3.  **Procurement Acceleration:** Front-loading of critical component orders (semiconductors, specialized robotics) to lock in local supply before the cascade completes.

---

## 3. Strategic Value
The SIC Gate identifies the transition from **Efficiency-Driven Globalism** to **Resilience-Driven Sovereignty**. It provides an early warning for "decoupling shocks" and "autarky premiums" in sovereign bonds, energy-compute assets, and defense-tech equities.
