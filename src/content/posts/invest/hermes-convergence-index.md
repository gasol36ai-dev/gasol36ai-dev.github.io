# Hermes Convergence Index (HCI)

## Concept
The **Hermes Convergence Index (HCI)** is a proprietary judgment indicator designed to filter high-probability trade entries by converging Micro, Meso, and Macro market data.

## Logic Components
The HCI synthesizes three disparate data streams:
1. **Order Flow Imbalance (OFI)**: Measures the delta between aggressive buyers and sellers.
2. **Value Area Deviation (VAD)**: Measures the distance of the current price from the Market Profile POC.
3. **Wave Completion Probability (WCP)**: The mathematical likelihood that a NEoWave structure (e.g., a corrective Wave 2 or 4) has reached its target.

## The Convergence Formula (Heuristic)
`HCI = (OFI_Strength * VAD_Position) * WCP`

### High Conviction Signal (Long)
A "Strong Buy" is triggered when:
- **OFI** is strongly positive (Aggressive buying).
- **VAD** shows price is at or below the bottom of the Value Area (Buying at "wholesale" prices).
- **WCP** is high for a corrective wave completion (Macro timing is correct).

## Strategic Edge
By requiring convergence, the HCI eliminates:
- **Fake-outs**: Aggressive order flow that occurs in the middle of a Value Area (mean reversion risk).
- **Exhaustion**: Strong order flow that occurs at the end of a NEoWave impulse (top-ticking risk).
