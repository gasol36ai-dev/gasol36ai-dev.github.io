# Macro Watchlist - May 2026 (Updated)

## High-Impact Variables
- **US Federal Reserve Policy Path**: Focus on the Powell term expiration in May 2026 and subsequent leadership transition. Markets are highly sensitive to FOMC signals (6-7 May).
- **Central Bank Divergence**: The widening gap between the Fed (cautious/hold), ECB (hawkish/maintenance), and BoJ (normalization) is the primary driver of USD FX volatility.
- **Middle East Geopolitical Risk (Iran/Energy)**: Ongoing conflict impact on energy prices (Brent/WTI) and global headline inflation. Watch OPEC+ meeting (Late May).
- **Sovereign Bond Yields**: US 10Y and 2Y yields as indicators of recession risk and the transition from inversion to positive slope.
- **Gold Prices**: Driven by central bank demand (de-dollarization) and as a geopolitical hedge, potentially decoupling from real yields.
- **US CPI/PCE Data**: Critical inflation indicators (12 May) determining the Fed's next move.

## Emerging Trends
- **Growth Leadership Shift**: Movement towards Emerging Markets (notably India) as US/EU growth slows.
- **Defense Spending Booms**: Increasing fiscal deficits due to heightened geopolitical tensions.
- **Macro Reset**: Transition toward a period of slower, more resilient growth after the volatility of 2025.

## Transmission Patterns to Watch
- **US Real Yields $\rightarrow$ Gold**: Monitor the inverse correlation as real rates stabilize or decline (Note: Decoupling possible in 'Trust-Logic' regime).
- **USD Index $\rightarrow$ EM Capital Flows**: Watch for USD softening to drive capital into high-growth EMs.
- **Geopolitical Tension $\rightarrow$ Energy $\rightarrow$ Global Inflation**: The cycle of conflict-driven supply shocks.
- **BoJ Normalization $\rightarrow$ JPY Strength $\rightarrow$ Global Carry Trade Unwind**: Watch for JPY appreciation driving volatility in global risk assets.
- **AI Scaling $\rightarrow$ Energy Infrastructure $\rightarrow$ Utilities/Energy Stocks**: The 'Power Bottleneck' shift.
