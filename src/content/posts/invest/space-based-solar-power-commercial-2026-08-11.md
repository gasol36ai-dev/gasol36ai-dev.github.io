# Space-Based Solar Power (SBSP) — Commercial Inflection (2026-08-11)

## Summary
2026 marks SBSP's transition from "vaporware" to funded, contracted, hardware-track infrastructure, driven overwhelmingly by AI data-center power demand rather than terrestrial grid decarbonization. Meta signed a direct commercial agreement (announced April 27, 2026) with Overview Energy for up to 1 GW of space-collected, laser-relayed power for its data centers, with an in-space demo targeted 2028 and commercial service as soon as 2030.

## Transmission Mapping
`[AI data-center power demand outstrips terrestrial grid capacity] -> [Hyperscalers (Meta, Google Suncatcher, SpaceX internal) directly fund/contract orbital solar + power-beaming startups] -> [Capital shifts from pure R&D to flight-hardware milestones; space-to-space power-beaming for orbital data centers emerges as the nearer-term, lower-risk market than Earth-beaming]`

## Key Findings
- **Space-for-space before space-for-Earth**: Solestial CTO Stan Herasimenka's thesis (2026) — the first profitable SBSP market is not beaming to Earth but powering the growing population of power-hungry satellites and orbital data centers unable to generate enough power themselves. Star Catcher Industries ($65M Series A, May 2026, ex-Space Force Chief Gen. Jay Raymond joining board) is building exactly this: an LEO power grid for other satellites, delivering 5-10x more power without retrofits.
- **DoD is now a paying customer, not just a funder**: Air Force SOED contract with Overview Energy; Volta Space Technologies + US Naval Research Lab + NASA announced LEPTON (Laser-Enabled Power Transmission Orbital Network), funded by DoW's OECIF, targeting optical wireless power transfer LLO→lunar surface by 2028 to survive the 14-day lunar night.
- **Capital scale (2021-2025 cumulative private investment)**: only $130M across all SBSP startups per Quilty analysis — but 2026 alone saw Star Catcher's $65M round and Cowboy Space (rebranded from Aetherflux)'s $275M round explicitly targeting orbital-data-center launch infrastructure, more than doubling the prior 5-year cumulative total in a single year.
- **Technoeconomics crossing viability**: A peer-reviewed 2026 Advances in Space Research technoeconomic model concludes a 10GHz microwave GEO system could deliver power at ~9.4¢/kWh within a decade — competitive with the cheapest terrestrial clean energy.
- **Japan OHISAMA** (2026 launch, Kairos rocket) targets the first-ever successful space-to-ground microwave power delivery-and-conversion, following Caltech's 2023 demo which only detected signals but delivered no usable electricity.
- **63 companies globally** pursuing SBSP/power-beaming as of mid-2025 (Factories In Space tracker) — an ecosystem forming around component cost reduction (e.g., Arinna's $4M seed for ultrathin spacecraft solar panels).

## Operational SOP
1. Track hyperscaler-SBSP direct contracts (Meta/Overview, Google/Suncatcher) as the primary demand-pull signal — these de-risk the technology faster than DoD R&D funding alone.
2. Distinguish "space-to-space" (nearer-term, addresses orbital-data-center power bottleneck directly) from "space-to-Earth" (longer-horizon, 2030+ commercial timelines) when evaluating investment/partnership targets.
3. Monitor 2027-2028 flight milestones (Dcubed ARAQYS-D3, Overview in-space demo, Volta LEPTON) as the key de-risking gates before 2030 commercial-service claims should be treated as credible.

## Strategic Value
SBSP is now a direct input into the AI-compute energy trilemma (see existing wiki entry `Sovereign_Data_Energy_Compute_Trilemma_2026.md`) — it is the mechanism by which orbital data centers (a 2026 FCC-filing-stage trend) become power-viable, closing a loop between Physical AI infrastructure, energy sovereignty, and space commercialization.

## Forward-Looking Timeline
- **2027 (early)**: Dcubed ARAQYS-D3 mission (SpaceX launch) — first in-space power generation/transmission demo including TerraSpark's RF payload.
- **2028**: Overview Energy in-space demo (Meta-funded); Volta LEPTON lunar-orbit-to-surface optical power transfer demo (NASA/Naval Research Lab).
- **2030**: Overview Energy's targeted start of "commercial power delivery" to Meta data centers — the single most-watched commercial-viability gate in the sector.
- **2030s-2040s**: Japan's OHISAMA-derived roadmap targets full commercialization; longer-horizon than the US hyperscaler-funded space-to-space track.

## Strategic Implications for Sovereign Compute Thesis
The bifurcation between space-to-space (near-term, orbital-data-center-serving) and space-to-Earth (long-horizon, grid-serving) power beaming mirrors the broader 2026 pattern of AI infrastructure sovereignty: capital is flowing fastest to whichever energy solution most directly unblocks compute buildout, not to whichever solution is most technologically mature in the abstract.
