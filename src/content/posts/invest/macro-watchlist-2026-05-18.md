# Macro Watchlist - 2026-05-18

## 🚨 High Impact Variables

1. **US CPI/PCE Momentum**: Crucial for determining the Fed's policy trajectory (Hike vs. Hold vs. Cut).
2. **Brent Crude Oil Prices**: Primary driver of energy-led headline inflation and second-round effects.
3. **US 10Y Treasury Yield**: Benchmark for global discount rates, capital flows, and real yield-driven asset valuations.
4. **Middle East Geopolitical Escalation**: Systemic risk factor impacting energy supplies and market volatility.
5. **US-China Trade/Tariff Policy**: Structural driver of inflation and global trade re-alignment.
6. **AI Capex/Productivity Trends**: Counter-cyclical growth driver and driver of core services inflation.
7. **Central Bank Policy Divergence (Fed vs. ECB/BoJ)**: Driver of USD/JPY strength and global liquidity conditions.

## 🔍 Key Indicators to Monitor

- **Energy**: Brent Crude, WTI Crude, Natural Gas.
- **Inflation**: US CPI, US Core CPI, US PCE, US PPI, Eurozone HICP.
- **Rates/Yields**: US 10Y, US 2Y, US 10Y Real Yield (TIPS), German 10Y Bund, JGB 10Y.
- **FX**: DXY (US Dollar Index), EUR/USD, USD/JPY.
- **Geopolitics**: Strait of Hormuz transit volumes, Middle East conflict headlines.

## 📈 Regime Context
**Regime**: Geopolitical-Inflationary Shock (Energy/Middle East Focus)
**Key Theme**: Re-inflationary pressure vs. Growth resilience (AI-driven).
