### 1. Map (The Terrain)
The CSKG maps the interdependency between three asymmetric layers:
- **The Kinetic/Intelligence Layer**: The ability to process complex tactical data locally using Neuromorphic chips (low power, high speed) at the extreme edge.

- **The Infrastructure/Energy Layer**: The ratio of localized, high-density compute capacity (Sovereign Compute Autarky) to total system demand, powered by decoupled energy sources (SMR/Fusion).

### 2. Trigger (The Inflection)
The "Gate" opens when the **Sovereign Latency Gap** ($\Delta L$) drops below the **Kinetic Response Window** ($\omega$).
The trigger occurs when local neuromorphic inference can predict and react to a kinetic shock faster than a centralized signal can be transmitted and processed by an external adversary or coordinator.

### 3. Lock (The State Transition)
Once the trigger is hit, the system "Locks" into a **Sovereign Autarky State**. In this state, the entity is no longer vulnerable to "Cloud-Kill" switches or "Energy-Blackouts." The governance shifts to an automated SGL protocol that reallocates resources in real-time based on the kinetic reality, creating a self-sustaining feedback loop of intelligence and action.
