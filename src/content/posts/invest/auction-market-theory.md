# Auction Market Theory (AMT)

AMT posits that the primary purpose of a financial market is to facilitate trade by finding a price acceptable to the maximum number of participants.

## Core Indicators
- **Market Profile (TPO)**: Organizes price data by **Time Price Opportunity**. Visualizes how much time the market spent at specific prices.
- **Volume Profile**: Organizes price data by **Volume**.
- **Point of Control (POC)**: The price level with the highest activity.
    - **Super POC**: Occurs when TPO POC and Volume POC align, creating a powerful price magnet.
- **Value Area (VA)**: The price range where 70% of the session's activity occurred.

## Day Types & Strategies
- **Trend Day**: Elongated profile, strong direction. Strategy: Continuation.
- **Normal Variation Day**: Expansion beyond the **Initial Balance**.
- **Neutral Day**: D-shaped profile. Strategy: Fade the extremes $
ightarrow$ return to POC.
