# Bond Yield & Curve Analysis

## Current Levels (April 2026)
- **US 10Y Treasury**: ~4.31% - 4.35%.
- **US 2Y Treasury**: ~3.78% - 3.81%.
- **10Y-2Y Spread**: **~52-54 bps (0.52% - 0.54%)**.

## Regime: Volatile Normalization
The yield curve is in a **normal steepening regime**, approximately 14 months after the historic un-inversion.

## Transmission Logic
- **Positive Slope**: Signals a transition from recession fears to an inflation/growth regime shift.
- **Risk**: Steepening driven by inflation expectations (Oil shock) rather than growth leads to valuation compression for long-duration assets (Tech).
