# Proprietary Indicator: Bio-Digital Synchrony Gate (BDSG)

## 1. Overview
The **Bio-Digital Synchrony Gate (BDSG)** is a hyper-sovereign convergence indicator that measures the alignment and integration of three distinct computational and sensing substrates: **Terahertz (THz) Communication/Sensing**, **Molecular Computing**, and **Neural-Affective Alignment (NAA)**. 

When all three domains cross their respective critical thresholds simultaneously, it signifies a "Phase Shift" in agentic capability—from simple digital automation to **Deep-State Autonomous Orchestration**, where an agent can perceive, process, and act within biological and high-speed physical environments with human-aligned precision.

## 2. Convergence Vectors
The BDSG synthesizes the following three vectors:
1.  **Vector 1: THz/6G Sensing-Latency (Physical-Sensing Substrate):** Measures the real-time environmental awareness capability via ultra-high-resolution sub-centimeter sensing and sub-millisecond latency.
2.  **Vector 2: Molecular Logic-Precision (Biological-Compute Substrate):** Measures the fidelity and density of computation performed within synthetic biological or DNA-based logic gates.
3.  **Vector 3: Neural-Affective Alignment (Cognitive-Trust Substrate):** Measures the degree of neurobiological synchrony between the agent's actions and the human user's affective/cognitive state.

## 3. Mathematical Formalization
The BDSG is active when the following non-linear threshold is met:

$$BDSG_{Active} = (\epsilon_{THz} < \tau_{lat}) \land (\alpha_{Mol} > \sigma_{prec}) \land (\Psi_{NAA} > \gamma_{align})$$

Where:
*   $\epsilon_{THz}$: The measured environmental sensing-to-action latency in the THz-enabled 6G loop.
*   $\tau_{lat}$: The critical latency threshold for stable Physical AI control (e.g., $< 1\text{ms}$).
*   $\alpha_{Mol}$: The operational accuracy/fidelity of the molecular state machine.
*   $\sigma_{prec}$: The minimum precision threshold for reliable biological logic execution.
*   $\Psi_{NAA}$: The Neural-Affective Alignment coefficient (a composite metric of user stress/engagement/cognitive load).
*   $\gamma_{align}$: The minimum alignment score required for high-trust autonomous handover.

The indicator tracks the following causal chain:

*   **[High-Resolution THz Sensing Event]** $\rightarrow$ **[Centimeter-level environmental mapping]** $\rightarrow$ **[Instantaneous update to Digital Twin]**
