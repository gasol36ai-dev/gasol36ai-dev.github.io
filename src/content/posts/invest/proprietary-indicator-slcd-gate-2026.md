# Proprietary Judgment Indicator: The Sovereign Liquidity-Compute Divergence (SLCD) Gate

*Version: 1.0 | Date: 2026-06-14 | Classification: Proprietary / Strategic*

## 1. Concept Definition
The **Sovereign Liquidity-Compute Divergence (SLCD) Gate** is a multi-domain judgment indicator designed to identify critical "Inflection Points" where a nation-state's sovereign AI capacity (Compute) becomes decoupled from its liquid financial reserves (Liquidity). 

This decoupling is a leading indicator of **Structural Macro-Fragility**. When a state possesses high-tier AI infrastructure but lacks the liquid, non-Western (e.g., mBridge-compatible) capital to sustain its energy and hardware supply chains, it enters a **"Sovereign Autarky Trap."**

## 2. The SLCD Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 2.1 The Map (Variables)
The SLCD is calculated using three primary vectors:
1. **$\mathcal{C}_{ai}$ (Compute Sovereignty Index)**: A composite of localized FLOPs, SMR-driven energy density, and semiconductor manufacturing autarky.
2. **$\mathcal{L}_{div}$ (Liquidity Divergence Factor)**: The ratio of "Stable/Western" (USD/EUR) reserves to "Non-Western/Programmable" (mBridge/e-CNY/Tokenized) reserves.
3. **$\mathcal{E}_{cap}$ (Energy-Compute Coupling)**: The efficiency of the SMR-to-Silicon energy transmission (as defined in the Nuclear-SMR research).

### 2.2 The Trigger (The Threshold)
A **"SLCD Inflection Event"** is triggered when:
$$\frac{\mathcal{C}_{ai}}{\mathcal{L}_{div} \times \mathcal{E}_{cap}} > \Theta_{critical}$$
Where $\Theta_{critical}$ is the threshold for **"Computational Overextension."**

### 2.3 The Lock (Strategic Reaction)
- **Bullish/Expansion Signal**: $\mathcal{C}_{ai}$ grows while $\mathcal{L}_{div}$ remains stable or improves (Sovereign Expansion).
- **Bearish/Fragility Signal (The Trap)**: $\mathcal{C}_{ai}$ grows rapidly while $\mathcal{L}_{div}$ collapses or $\mathcal{E}_{cap}$ degrades (Systemic Autarky Trap).

| Event (Trigger) | Mechanism (Transmission) | Reaction (Outcome/Mitigation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
