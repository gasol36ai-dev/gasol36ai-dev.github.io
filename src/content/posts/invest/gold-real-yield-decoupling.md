# Gold-Real Yield Decoupling (2026)

Historically, Gold and US Real Yields (TIPS) shared a strong negative correlation: `Real Yields \uparrow \rightarrow Gold \downarrow`. In 2026, this correlation has effectively broken.

## The New Regime: Systemic Trust Deficit

Gold is now rising or remaining stable *despite* elevated real yields (~1.9%). The driver has shifted from "Opportunity Cost Logic" to "Systemic Trust Logic".

### Catalysts for Decoupling
1. **US Credit Rating Downgrade**: Moody's stripping the US of its final top rating created a permanent risk premium on Treasuries.
2. **Fed Independence Pressure**: Political interference in the Fed reduces trust in the USD as a stable store of value.
3. **De-dollarization**: Central Bank reserves (PBOC, etc.) are diversifying away from USD-denominated assets toward gold.
4. **Trade War**: "Liberation Day Tariffs" (145% on China) accelerate the shift to non-USD reserves.

## Transmission Mapping
`[Trust Deficit / Sovereign Risk] -> [Gold as Ultimate Risk-Free Asset] -> [Positive Correlation with Real Yields (or Neutrality)]`

## Strategic Verdict
In the current regime, **Real Yields are no longer a reliable ceiling for Gold**. The structural floor is now provided by Central Bank hoarding and systemic instability. Gold should be viewed as a hedge against the "US Trust Failure" rather than a bet on falling rates.
