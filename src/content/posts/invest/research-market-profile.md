# Market Profile (TPO)

Market Profile, or Time Price Opportunity (TPO), organizes price data by time and volume to reveal the "Auction Market Theory" (AMT) in action.

## Core Concepts
- **Value Area (VA)**: The price range where 70% of the day's trading occurred.
- **Point of Control (POC)**: The price level with the highest TPO count (the "fairest" price).
- **Initial Balance (IB)**: The price range established in the first hour of trading.

## Auction Market Theory (AMT)
The market's primary purpose is to facilitate trade. It does this by moving price until it finds an area where both buyers and sellers agree on value.
- **Balanced Market**: Price rotates around the POC (Range-bound).
- **Imbalanced Market**: Price trends aggressively away from the POC to find a new value area (Trend).

## Strategic Value
Identifies "unfair" prices (extremes) and "fair" prices (POC), allowing traders to enter at value and exit at extremes.
