# Proprietary Indicator: Adaptive Liquidity-Volatility Coefficient (ALVC)
## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock
### 1. Map (Inputs)
- **Vol_Spike_Z**: Z-score of volatility over a 5-minute rolling window.
- **Depth_Delta_Ratio**: Ratio of current Bid/Ask depth to 24-hour median depth.
- **Logic**: $ALVC = \frac{Vol\_Spike\_Z}{Depth\_Delta\_Ratio}$

### 2. Trigger (Detection)
- **Condition**: $ALVC > \text{Threshold}_{dynamic}$
- **Threshold**: Dynamically adjusted based on the 7-day rolling standard deviation of ALVC.

### 3. Lock (Action)
- **Signal**: When $ALVC$ exceeds the 95th percentile, the system triggers an immediate **"Liquidity-Driven Volatility Alert"**.
- **Automated Response**:
    - Reduce automated execution size by 50%.
    - Flag all pending orders in the "High Volatility/Low Liquidity" category.
    - Notify VIP/CEO of potential "Flash Crash" conditions.

## Strategic Value
Distinguishes between **Informational Volatility** (news-driven, where liquidity typically remains stable) and **Liquidity Volatility** (structural, where liquidity vanishes, creating much higher risk for execution).
