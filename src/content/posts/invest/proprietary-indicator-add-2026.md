# Proprietary Indicator: Agentic Drift Divergence (ADD)

## 1. Concept
The **Agentic Drift Divergence (ADD)** indicator measures the systemic risk caused by the synchronization of reasoning agents. When a majority of market agents converge on the same cognitive architecture or "world model" (Drift), their reactions to new information become identical, transforming normal market volatility into synchronized, high-magnitude crashes (Divergence).

## 2. Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock (MTL)

### 🗺️ Map (The State Space)
The ADD maps the relationship between **Model Concentration** and **Liquidity Depth**.
- **X-Axis:** Model Concentration (Percentage of total market volume executed by agents using a dominant reasoning model family).
- **Y-Axis:** Realized Liquidity Depth (Actual fill rate vs. advertised depth during a 1% price move).
- **Z-Axis:** Narrative Velocity (The speed at which a new sentiment-trigger is propagating through agentic networks).

### ⚡ Trigger (The Execution Signal)
A **"Drift Warning"** is triggered when:
`Model Concentration > 65%` AND `Realized Liquidity < 40% of Advertised Liquidity` AND `Narrative Velocity > 2σ (standard deviations)` from the 30-day mean.

### 🔒 Lock (The Deterministic Action)
Once the Trigger is activated, the system executes the following **Lock Sequence**:
1.  **Immediate De-risking:** Close 50% of high-beta positions tied to the dominant model's "optimism."
2.  **Switch to Heterogeneous Execution:** Move all trade execution to a "Diversified Model Ensemble" (e.g., switching from a dominant Transformer to an SSM or Neuromorphic local model).
3.  **Liquidity Buffer Activation:** Shift capital into "Sovereign Compute" and "Hard Energy" assets to hedge against the systemic flash-crash.

## 3. Strategic Value
The ADD allows the operator to identify "Fragile Liquidity" before it collapses. By detecting when the market has become a "mono-culture of reasoning," the operator can front-run the synchronized exit that inevitably follows a narrative shift.
