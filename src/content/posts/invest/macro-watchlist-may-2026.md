# 📋 Macro Watchlist - May 18, 2026

**Last Updated: 2026-05-18**

## 🚨 High Impact Variables (Tier 1)

1. **US Inflation (PCE Focus)**
   - **Next Major Event**: **MAY 28 PCE RELEASE** (CPI was May 12: 3.8% YoY).
   - **Focus**: The **PCE-CPI Wedge (~60 bps)**. Monitor if AI-driven service inflation and energy costs keep PCE elevated. Note that PPI came in high at 6.0% (April), signaling pipeline pressure.
   - **Impact**: Primary driver of Fed's "Hold vs Hike" decision. Higher-for-longer sentiment is growing.

2. **Middle East Geopolitical Risk (Strait of Hormuz/Iran)**
   - **Status**: Extremely Elevated/Persistent.
   - **Threshold**: **Brent Crude Oil > $110/bbl**. (Recent surge; Brent approaching $90 with upside risk).
   - **Impact**: Sustains structural inflation floor; triggers flight-to-quality into Gold and USD.

3. **BoJ Policy Normalization (JPY Carry Trade)**
   - **Status**: Active / Hawkish.
   - **Focus**: Rate hike signals for June/July. 10Y JGB yields rising (~2.69%).
   - **Impact**: Trigger for global equity volatility and Yen carry trade unwinds.

4. **US Real Yields (10Y TIPS)**
   - **Current Level**: Monitoring for spikes. Nominal 10Y surging (~4.595%), 2Y at ~4.079%.
   - **Trend**: Watching for spikes that could pressure the Gold decoupling thesis.
   - **Context**: Driven by inflation premium and sovereign credit risk.

5. **USD Index (DXY)**
   - **Trend**: Strong USD driven by **Inflation Divergence (US $\uparrow$ vs EU $\downarrow$)** and safety demand.
   - **Impact**: Pressure on EM assets and Euro.

## ⚠️ Monitoring Variables (Tier 2)

1. **AI Power & Grid Demand**
   - **Focus**: Transition from GPU Compute $\rightarrow$ Agentic Inference $\rightarrow$ Power/Grid Infrastructure.
   - **Impact**: Capex shift toward Utilities and Industrial Electrical equipment.

2. **US Sovereign Credit Risk (Moody's)**
   - **Focus**: Potential downgrade threat and rising debt interest costs ($97B in April).
   - **Impact**: Permanent risk premium on Treasuries; reinforces Gold as ultimate insurance.

## 🛠️ Strategic Themes

- **The PCE-CPI Wedge**: Navigating a regime where the Fed sees more inflation than the headlines suggest.
- **Energy-Driven Structural Inflation**: Managing portfolios for "Higher for Longer" real rates.
- **Systemic Risk Hedging**: Gold and Energy as core hedges against geopolitical/sovereign instability.
- **AI Infrastructure Pivot**: Positioning for the Power/Grid bottleneck.
- **Policy Divergence**: Capital flow shifts driven by US (Hawkish/Hold) vs EU (Dovish/Hold) vs Japan (Hawkish/Normalize).
