# Proprietary Indicator: Orbital-Compute Latency Volatility (OCLV)

## Overview
The **Orbital-Compute Latency Volatility (OCLV)** is a strategic judgment indicator designed to detect systemic instability in the emerging intersection of **Orbital DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks)** and **Sovereign AI Compute**. 

As nation-states and mega-corporations increasingly rely on LEO/MEO satellite constellations for distributed, low-latency, and geo-resilient compute and connectivity, the stability of the orbital network becomes a direct proxy for the reliability of the global sovereign compute layer.

## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. Map (The Observable Variables)
- **Mean Orbital Latency (MOL)**: The average Round-Trip Time (RTT) across a distributed mesh of orbital nodes (e.g., Starlink, Kuiper, or sovereign LEO constellations).
- **Network Jitter Coefficient (NJC)**: The standard deviation of latency over time, measuring the consistency of the signal.

### 2. Trigger (The Inflection Point)
- **[Rapid Spike in MOL] + [Sustained Divergence in NJC]**
- This combination indicates more than just routine congestion; it suggests **active signal interference**, **orbital node failure**, or **coordinated jamming** (either kinetic or electronic) targeting the compute-distribution layer.

### 3. Lock (The Deterministic Response)
- **[Automatic Execution of 'Orbital Resiliency Protocol']**
- **Action**: Immediate cessation of high-frequency, latency-sensitive trading/operations reliant on orbital data links.
- **Action**: Mandatory migration of critical compute workloads from orbital/edge nodes to verified **Terrestrial Sovereign Compute Enclaves** (hardened, geographically stable ground-based centers).
- **Action**: Increase hedging on assets directly exposed to the affected orbital corridor (e.s. satellite-dependent fintech infra).

## Strategic Value
Provides an early warning for "Space-to-Earth" geopolitical friction and identifies the exact moment when orbital infrastructure transitions from a "low-cost utility" to a "high-risk contested zone."
