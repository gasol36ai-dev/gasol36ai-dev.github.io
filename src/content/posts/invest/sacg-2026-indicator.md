# PROPRIETARY INDICATOR: The Sovereign Autonomy Convergence Gate (SACG-2026)
## ID: SACG-2026 | HPI-40

### I. Conceptual Foundation
The SACG-2026 indicator tracks the convergence of **Compute Efficiency (Neuromorphic)**, **Energy Independence (SMRs)**, and **Capital Liquidity (RWA Tokenization)**. 

A state is considered "Hyper-Sovereign" when it can generate intelligence, power, and capital without reliance on external geopolitical chokepoints.

### II. Domain Convergence Mapping
- **Mind (Compute)**: Transition from HBM-dependent GPU clusters to Analog CiM.
- **Body (Energy)**: Transition from resource-dependent grids to SMR-powered modular nodes.
- **Blood (Capital)**: Transition from custodian-dependent credit to on-chain RWA liquidity.

### III. Transmission Mapping (Convergent Logic)
[Event]: Simultaneous threshold crossing of CiM efficiency, SMR deployment, and RWA institutional adoption.
[Mechanism]: 
1. **CiM** $\rightarrow$ Lowers the energy cost of intelligence (Intelligence $\text{Cost} \rightarrow 0$).
2. **SMR** $\rightarrow$ Decouples power from fuel-chain geopolitics (Energy $\text{Risk} \rightarrow 0$).
3. **RWA** $\rightarrow$ Decouples capital from banking intermediaries (Financial $\text{Friction} \rightarrow 0$).
[Reaction]: A step-function increase in National/Organizational Autonomy, leading to a "Sovereign Premium" in asset valuation and geopolitical leverage.

### IV. Mathematical Formalization
$$\text{SACG\_Active} = (\text{CiM\_TDP} < 1\text{pJ/Op}) \land (\text{SMR\_Fleet} \ge 1\text{GW}_{\text{equiv}}) \land (\text{RWA\_TVL} \ge 1\text{T}\text{USD})$$

### V. Trigger Condition & Action
- **Trigger**: When all three conditions are met within a 24-month window.
- **Action**: Shift strategic allocation from "Growth/Dependency" assets to "Autonomy/Sovereign" assets (e.g., transition from Cloud-SaaS to Edge-AI infrastructure and modular energy producers).
