# Frontier Technical Analysis & Quant Strategies

## Order Flow Trading
Order Flow Trading focuses on the real-time interaction between aggressive participants (market orders) and passive participants (limit orders). It aims to understand *why* price moves, rather than just *where* it has moved.

### Key Components
- **Volume Delta**: The difference between buying and selling pressure at a specific price level.
- **Liquidity Absorption**: Occurs when a large passive order absorbs all aggressive market orders, preventing price movement despite high volume.
- **Order Book Behavior**: Analysis of the bid/ask spread and the depth of liquidity.
- **Market Microstructure**: The study of how specific trades and liquidity affect price formation (referencing Kyle and Hasbrouck).

### Order Flow vs. Classical TA
| Feature | Classical TA | Order Flow Trading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | Past price behavior | Real-time execution |
| Tools | Candles, Indicators, Patterns | Footprint Charts, Depth of Market (DOM) |
| Perspective | Lagging (Result) | Leading (Cause) |
| Application | Trend/Swing Trading | Scalping, Intraday, Futures |

### Strategic Insights
- **Vulnerable Breakouts**: Breakouts lacking strong positive/negative delta are prone to failure (fake-outs).
- **Weak Pullbacks**: Pullbacks with minimal aggressive selling in an uptrend suggest the trend is still intact.
- **Institutional Footprints**: Large passive blocks (absorption) often mark significant reversal or support/resistance zones.

## Market Profile & Volume Profile
- **TPO (Time Price Opportunity)**: Analyzes how much time price spends at a certain level, identifying the Value Area (VA) and Point of Control (POC).
- **Volume Profile**: Focuses on the amount of volume traded at a level.
- **High Volume Nodes (HVN)**: Levels of high agreement/acceptance.
- **Low Volume Nodes (LVN)**: Levels of rejection; price tends to move through these quickly.
