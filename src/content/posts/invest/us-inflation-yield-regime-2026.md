# US Inflation & Yield Regime 2026

The US macroeconomic environment in early 2026 is marked by sticky inflation and a normalizing yield curve.

## Inflation Trends
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures)**: Holding steady around 2.8% (Jan/Feb 2026).
- **CPI (Consumer Price Index)**: More volatile, with a jump to 3.3% in March 2026, driven primarily by a surge in energy prices.
- **Regime**: Transition from "transitory" or "peak" inflation to a "sticky plateau" where 3% becomes a common baseline.

## Bond Yields & Curve
- **10Y Treasury**: Trading in the 4.25% - 4.31% range.
- **2Y Treasury**: Trading around 3.71% - 3.79%.
- **Yield Curve Spread (10Y-2Y)**: Positive (~+0.51% to +0.53%). 
- **Analysis**: The disappearance of the inversion signals a shift away from immediate recession fears toward a normalized growth outlook, although the "Inflation Premium" remains a key risk factor.

## Transmission Logic
`Energy Surge` $\rightarrow$ `CPI $\uparrow$` $\rightarrow$ `Inflation Expectations $\uparrow$` $\rightarrow$ `10Y Yield $\uparrow$` $\rightarrow$ `Equity Valuation Compression (Growth/Tech)`.
