# Macro Intelligence Report - 2026-05-20

## Executive Summary
The global macroeconomic environment remains dominated by the ongoing geopolitical instability in the Middle East, which has introduced significant supply-side shocks, particularly in energy markets. While major central banks have adopted a "hawkish hold" stance, the risk of renewed inflation-driven tightening is elevated. The transition in US Federal Reserve leadership to Kevin Warsh introduces potential regime changes in monetary policy, though market sentiment is currently focused on energy-induced inflation volatility.

## 1. Central Bank Policy & Interest Rates
| Central Bank | Current Policy Rate | Recent Stance | Outlook/Key Drivers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Federal Reserve (Fed)** | 3.50% - 3.75% | Hawkish Hold | Transition to Kevin Warsh; focus on leaner balance sheet and potential long-term easing, but short-term inflation risks are high. |
| **ECB** | [Not Specified] | Cautious | Vulnerable to energy shocks; potential hikes in June and September 2026. |
| **Bank of Japan (BoJ)** | 0.75% | Hawkish Hold | Preparing for a June rate hike; inflation forecasts upwardly revised. |
| **Bank of England (BoE)** | 3.75% | Cautious | Increased probability of hikes this year due to rising energy costs ($108+/bbl). |
| **Bank of Canada (BoC)** | 2.25% | Hold | Reluctant to raise rates due to subdued inflation pressures and weak business activity. |

## 2. Inflation & Economic Growth
- **Global Growth:** IMF projects 3.1% in 2026 (revised downward due to conflict).
- **Global Inflation:** Expected to rise to 4.4% in 2026.
- **US Inflation:** CPI 3.3% (March); PCE remains a key monitor.
- **Eurozone Inflation:** Projected to accelerate to 3.0% in April.
- **Key Driver:** Middle East conflict causing energy price volatility and second-round inflationary effects.

## 3. Sovereign Bond Yields & Currencies
- **US 10Y Treasury:** ~4.3% - 4.4%.
- **US Dollar (USD):** Moderate upside risk due to Fed policy uncertainty and global safe-haven flows.
- **Japanese Yen (JPY):** Potential for strength if BoJ proceeds with June hike, though energy costs remain a counter-force.

## 4. Geopolitical Risk Assessment
- **Primary Risk:** Middle East conflict (Iran/Israel/Regional).
- **Impact:** 
    - **Energy:** Significant supply disruptions; Brent Crude near $110.
    - **Supply Chains:** Potential for broader disruptions if hostilities expand.
    - **Capital Flows:** Shift towards safe-haven assets (Gold, USD) during periods of heightened tension.

## 5. Macro Watchlist (High Impact Variables)
1. **Brent Crude Oil Prices:** Key driver for global inflation and central bank decisions.
2. **US 10Y Real Yields:** Influences Gold and EM attractiveness.
3. **Fed Leadership Transition (Warsh):** Potential for long-term regime change in US monetary policy.
4. **BoJ Policy Pivot (June Hike):** Critical for JPY and global carry trades.
5. **Middle East De-escalation/Escalation Signals:** The primary source of volatility.
