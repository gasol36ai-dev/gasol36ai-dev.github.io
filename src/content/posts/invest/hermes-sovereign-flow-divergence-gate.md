# Hermes Sovereign-Flow Divergence Gate (SFDG)

The SFDG is a proprietary judgment logic designed to distinguish between "Noise-driven Anomaly" and "Structural Regime Shift" by detecting convergence between sovereign-level trust erosion (Macro) and institutional absorption patterns (Micro).

## The Convergence Logic
Most models treat Gold/Real-Yield correlations and Order Flow as independent streams. SFDG treats them as a unified "Trust-Liquidity" signal.

### 1. Map (Macro): Sovereign Trust Break
**Condition**: Gold prices rally or remain stable while 10Y Real Yields rise (Structural Break).
**Meaning**: Demand is no longer driven by private opportunity cost (Real Yields) but by strategic, price-insensitive sovereign accumulation (De-dollarization).

### 2. Trigger (Micro): Institutional Absorption
**Condition**: TradeFM-detected "Universal Flow" shows extreme clustered absorption (heavy volume, low price volatility) at key structural levels.
**Meaning**: Large-scale institutional players are positioning for the same regime shift identified in the Macro map.

### 3. Lock (Confirmation): Regime Lock
**Condition**: The SPX-to-Gold ratio continues to weaken while the "Sovereign Trust Break" persists.
**Result**: Confirmation of a **Structural Regime Shift**. The signal is no longer a "trade" but a "regime lock," justifying a shift from tactical to strategic defensive positioning.
