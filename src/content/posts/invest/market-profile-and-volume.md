# Market Profile & Volume Analysis

## TPO vs Volume Profile
| Metric | Market Profile (TPO) | Volume Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Axis** | Time | Volume (Contracts) |
| **Measures** | Acceptance (How long price stayed) | Conviction (How much capital committed) |
| **Key Value** | Fair Value Consensus | Institutional Commitment |
| **Use Case** | Structural analysis, Multi-day trends | Precise S/R, Trapped trader detection |

## Advanced Concepts
### Composite Profiles
- **Definition**: Merging multiple sessions (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly) into a single distribution.
- **Value**: Filters daily noise to reveal long-term institutional "Value Areas". The Quarterly POC is a major macro decision point.

### TPO-Volume Convergence
- **Convergence**: TPO POC $pprox$ Volume POC $
ightarrow$ Absolute fair value consensus. High conviction.
- **Divergence**:

    - Volume POC $<$ TPO POC $
ightarrow$ Institutions selling aggressively at a discount (Bearish).

### High/Low Volume Nodes (HVN/LVN)
- **HVN**: Strong acceptance. Price tends to consolidate here. Reversals at HVNs have higher win rates.
- **LVN**: Rejection. Price moves quickly through these zones. Breakouts through LVNs often accelerate.
