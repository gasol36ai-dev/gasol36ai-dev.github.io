# Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Energy Trading

## Definition
P2P Energy Trading allows energy prosumers to sell excess electricity directly to their neighbors without a centralized utility intermediary.

## Enabling Technologies
- **Blockchain/DLT**: Provides a transparent, immutable ledger for transactions.
- **Smart Contracts**: Automatically execute trades when certain conditions (price, energy levels) are met.
- **Smart Meters**: Precise measurement of energy flow at the edge.

## Market Models
- **Direct Trading**: One-to-one exchange between neighbors.
- **Auction-based**: Bidding for energy within a community pool.
- **Tokenized Energy**: Using digital tokens to represent kilowatt-hours (kWh).

## Role in Energy Sovereignty
By removing the middleman, P2P trading ensures that the value of locally produced energy stays within the community, fostering local economic resilience.
