# Proprietary Indicator: Orbital Sovereignty Integrity Gate (OSIG)

## 1. Overview
The **Orbital Sovereignty Integrity Gate (OSIG)** is a strategic judgment indicator designed to monitor the convergence of **Orbital DePIN**, **Sovereign AI Compute**, and **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)**. It measures the systemic vulnerability of nation-state intelligence as critical AI workloads migrate from terrestrial (highly regulated, high-latency) to orbital (decentralized, low-latency, "unstrikeable") infrastructures before the full adoption of quantum-resistant security protocols.

## 2. The Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 🗺️ Map: The Multidimensional Input
The OSIG is derived from the convergence of three distinct vectors:
1. **Orbital Compute Density (OCD)**: The total gigawatt (GW) capacity of compute power hosted in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) and Medium Earth Orbit (MEO) constellations (e.g., Starcloud, Axiom Space).
2. **Post-Quantum Identity Coverage (PQIC)**: The percentage of autonomous agentic workflows and identity signatures in the orbital network that utilize NIST-standardized lattice-based algorithms (e.g., CRYSTALS-Dilithium, Falcon).
3. **Sovereign Silicon Compliance (SSC)**: The ratio of domain-specific, state-backed AI silicon (NPUs/Custom ASICs) to general-purpose hyperscaler hardware (e.g., NVIDIA H100s) within the orbital edge-computing substrate.

### ⚡ Trigger: The Convergence Event
The OSIG signal is activated when the following three conditions occur near-simultaneously:
* **The Migration Cliff**: A step-function increase in high-value, sensitive agentic traffic moving from terrestrial data centers (Ground Layer) to orbital edge nodes (Orbital Layer).
* **The Cryptographic Gap**: A measurable surge in classical (RSA/ECC) handshake attempts or "legacy" identity signatures detected in orbital-to-ground telemetry, indicating a failure to implement PQC at the speed of deployment.
* **Geopolitical Decoupling**: The implementation of new export controls on specialized AI silicon or the sudden deployment of "Compute Reservoirs" in non-extradition/non-aligned jurisdictions via orbital nodes.

### 🔒 Lock: The Strategic Response
Upon a positive OSIG signal, the following "locks" are triggered in strategic decision-making:
* **Risk Re-rating**: An immediate downgrade of "Sovereign Infrastructure Reliability" for any asset or entity whose compute substrate lacks verified PQIC compliance.
* **Identity Hardening**: A mandatory transition of all "Critical Enclave" (government/high-security) workloads to ephemeral, **Zero-Standing-Privilege (ZSP)** identity models to mitigate "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) risks.
* **Capital Realignment**: A strategic shift in sovereign wealth fund allocations from general "Cloud/SaaS" infrastructure toward "Hardened Orbital/PQC" infrastructure.

## 3. Strategic Value & Decision Gate
The OSIG serves as a primary decision gate for:
* **National Security Planners**: Identifying when a nation's "Digital Brain" (its sovereign AI) has entered a state of high cryptographic exposure.
* **Defense Contractors/Space-Tech Investors**: Identifying the "Security Premium" in the next generation of orbital hardware (i.e., the shift from "just launchable" to "quantum-secure").
* **Macro Investors**: Predicting the non-linear re-rating of the "Sovereign Infrastructure" asset class as it transitions from terrestrial-based to orbital-based models.
