# Spatial Intelligence

Spatial Intelligence is the ability of an AI system to perceive, reason about, and navigate 3D physical spaces. It is the "bridge" that allows LLM-style reasoning to be applied to robotic actuators.

## Core Components
- **Context-Aware Sensing**: Integrating multi-modal signals (Lidar, Vision, Haptics) to build a real-time understanding of surroundings.
- **Physics-Based Rendering**: Using engines like **Newton 1.0** for accurate collision detection and dexterous manipulation.
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)**: The mechanism by which spatial understanding is converted into physical motion.

## Strategic Implications
The emergence of spatial intelligence transforms robots from "specialists" (fixed-base, repetitive) to "generalists" capable of unstructured tasks (e.g., household chores, complex surgical assistance).
