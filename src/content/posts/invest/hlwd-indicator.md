# Proprietary Indicator: Hermes Liquidity-Wave Divergence (HLWD)

## Concept
The **Hermes Liquidity-Wave Divergence (HLWD)** is a synthetic judgment indicator that correlates high-level market structure (Elliott Wave), intraday value (Market Profile), micro-structure (Order Flow), and macro-economic drivers.

## Logic & Calculation

### 1. The Divergence Trigger
A "Divergence" is flagged when the following conditions intersect:
- **Structural Context:** Market is in a **Corrective Wave 4** (or a complex B-wave). Price is stalling or slightly declining.
- **Value Shift:** The **Point of Control (POC)** of the current session is migrating *upward* despite the price remaining flat or falling (Value-Price Divergence).
- **Liquidity Absorption:** Order flow shows massive **Buy Absorption** at the bottom of the range (aggressive sellers are being absorbed by passive institutional buyers).
- **Macro Confirmation:** A "Macro Tailwind" is present (e.g., a drop in the USD Index, a dovish Fed signal, or a spike in a correlated commodity like Gold).

### 2. Signal Grading
- **Grade A (High Conviction):** All 4 conditions met. High probability of a powerful **Wave 5** impulsive move.
- **Grade B (Medium Conviction):** Structure, Value, and Liquidity met; Macro is neutral.
- **Grade C (Speculative):** Structure and Liquidity met; Value and Macro are lagging.

## Application
- **Entry:** Long position when the price breaks above the current session's **Value Area High (VAH)** following a Grade A HLWD signal.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed below the most recent **Liquidity Pool** (the absorption zone).
- **Target:** Fibonacci extension of Wave 3 projected onto Wave 5.

## Historical Validation Logic
To test HLWD, the agent should:
1. Identify historical "Wave 4" consolidation periods.
2. Check if the POC migrated upward *before* the breakout.
3. Verify if cumulative delta showed absorption.
4. Correlate the breakout timing with macro event timestamps.
