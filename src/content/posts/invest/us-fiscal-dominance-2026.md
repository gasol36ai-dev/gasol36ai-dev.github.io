# US Fiscal Dominance & Sovereign Debt Risk (2026)

**Date**: 2026-05-27
**Status**: Ingested
**Source**: RSM Real Economy (Brusuelas, May 2025); CSIS (May 2025); OECD Global Debt Report 2026
**Domain**: Macro / Sovereign Credit / Fixed Income

## Overview
Moody's downgraded US sovereign credit **Aaa → Aa1** on May 16, 2025, completing the triple-downgrade trilogy (S&P 2011, Fitch 2023, Moody's 2025). The move represents a structural inflection point: the US has entered **Fiscal Dominance** — where fiscal policy overrides monetary policy, forcing yields higher and compressing the Fed's policy space.

## Key Data Points
- Federal deficit: ~**9% of GDP** projected (up from 6.4%)
- Annual deficit run-rate: **~$2 trillion/year**
- Yale Budget Lab: current legislation adds **$3.4T debt (2025-2035)**, $13.5T by 2055
- Projected debt/GDP: **156% by 2055** (CSIS)
- 10-year Treasury yield: **4.55%** at downgrade; 30-year testing **5%**
- Term premium: rose **~108 bps** in 2025; adds **~75 bps** to 10-year yields

## Transmission Mechanisms

### [Downgrade] → [Term Premium Repricing] → [Long End Selloff]
- Loss of Aaa removes mandated-holding floors for insurance/pension funds
- Foreign central bank diversification accelerates dollar de-anchoring
- Term premium turns structurally positive after decade of suppression

### [Fiscal Expansion] → [Inflation Uncertainty] → [Real Return Erosion]
- Tariff-driven inflation compounds yield uncertainty
- Stagflationary mix: slower growth + sticky prices = impossible trade-off for Fed

### [Dollar Uncertainty] → [Foreign Outflows] → [Self-Reinforcing Yield Spiral]
- Foreign holders shifting to home-currency assets
- Higher borrowing costs for all dollar-linked contracts (mortgages, corporate debt)

## Unlike Emerging Markets
In typical EM downgrade scenarios, rating cuts force fiscal consolidation. The US political structure produces the **inverse response**: expected congressional reaction is *more* unfunded tax cuts, not less spending. This makes the US Fiscal Dominance loop self-reinforcing without external constraint.

## Implications for Investment Framework
- **Hard assets (gold, commodities)**: Structurally bid as real yield erosion continues
- **Long-end duration**: Risk/reward asymmetric — avoid outright long exposure above 4.5% 10y
- **Dollar carry**: Basis of dollar dominance eroding; diversify reserve currency exposure
- **Credit spreads**: Investment grade spreads compress only if term premium stabilizes

## Transmission Mapping
```
[Fiscal Expansion + No Consolidation Path]
  → [Term Premium Repricing + Dollar Erosion]
    → [Hard Asset Bid + Long-End Yield Rise]
      → [Tighter Financial Conditions Despite Rate Cuts]
        → [Growth Drag + Inflation Floor]
```
