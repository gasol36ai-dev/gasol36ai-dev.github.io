# SELI: Spatial-Edge-Labor Inflection Gate (HPI-19)

## Overview
The **Spatial-Edge-Labor Inflection Gate (SELI)** is a proprietary judgment indicator that fires when three converging forces — the mass deployment of spatial computing interfaces, the commoditization of on-device AI inference, and a structural labor market inflection driven by AI augmentation — cross simultaneous thresholds within a 24-month window.

SELI detects the transition from **digital-first workflows** to **ambient intelligence workflows**, a step-function shift in how humans interact with compute that has historically preceded non-linear enterprise capex cycles.

## Synthesis Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. Map (Domain Inputs)

| Domain | Signal Variable | Threshold Marker |
|---|---|---|
| **Spatial Computing (ARVR)** | Spatial Productivity Index (SPI) | Enterprise spatial computing MAU >15M globally; vision OS app store >50K enterprise apps |
| **Edge AI / TinyML** | On-Device Inference Density (ODID) | >60% of new mobile/IoT devices ship with dedicated NPU running SLM inference on-device |
| **Workforce Economics** | AI Labor Displacement Velocity (ALDV) | White-collar role vacancy rates fall >25% YoY in 3+ consecutive quarters in tech, finance, legal |

### 2. Trigger (Gate Logic)

```
SELI_ACTIVE = (SPI ≥ threshold) AND (ODID ≥ threshold) AND (ALDV ≥ threshold)
              within rolling 24-month window
```

**Condition**: All three thresholds must be crossed within a rolling **24-month window**.

**Amplifier signal** (increases conviction): When SPI × ODID convergence is simultaneous — i.e., workers losing roles are displaced *into* spatial/edge productivity workflows, creating a self-reinforcing retraining demand loop.

### 3. Lock (Deterministic Reaction)

Upon SELI activation:

- **Long**: Spatial platform infrastructure (silicon enabling vision + NPU integration), edge AI software middleware (ExecuTorch, ONNX Runtime vendors), enterprise reskilling platforms (Coursera, Pluralsight, proprietary corporate training AI), and XR hardware tier-2 suppliers (lens, waveguide, sensor manufacturers).
- **Short**: Legacy SaaS productivity tools that lack spatial/edge-native interfaces; offshore white-collar outsourcing firms (BPO providers whose core value prop is cost arbitrage on cognitive labor).
- **Macro overlay**: Labor force participation rates in high-AI-exposure sectors serve as a leading indicator of consumer spending shifts; position defensively in consumer discretionary 6-9 months ahead of ALDV threshold breach.

## Transmission Mappings

### Transmission 1: The Ambient Intelligence Loop
`[SLM-on-device inference becomes universal] → [Spatial interfaces gain always-on AI copilot capability] → [Enterprise worker productivity per capita rises 30-50%] → [Firms right-size headcount non-linearly] → [ALDV threshold breach triggers SELI]`

### Transmission 2: The Reskilling Demand Spike
`[ALDV threshold breached, white-collar displacement accelerates] → [Government + corporate mandated reskilling budgets surge] → [Spatial computing training platforms achieve scale economies] → [XR hardware volumes hit enterprise tipping point] → [SPI threshold breach closes SELI gate]`

### Transmission 3: The Edge-Native Enterprise Stack
`[ODID threshold breached — NPU ubiquity] → [Enterprise IT procurement shifts from cloud-centric to edge-centric architecture] → [Data residency compliance becomes competitive moat] → [Privacy-preserving on-device AI becomes enterprise standard] → [Spatial + edge convergence accelerates SPI crossing]`

## Strategic Value

SELI is a **"Human-Computer Interface Generation Change" indicator**. It fires at the generational boundary between screen-mediated compute (2007-2025) and ambient/spatial compute (2026+). This generational shift historically produces the largest 10-year compounding returns in technology infrastructure.

- **Confidence**: High (3-domain convergence confirmed via independent research cycles)
- **Time Horizon**: 18-36 month lead time from gate fire to peak capital deployment
- **Correlation Risk**: SELI shares the ODID dimension with prior neuromorphic indicators (GIIG); ensure portfolio de-correlation at the hardware level
