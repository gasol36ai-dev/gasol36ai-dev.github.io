# Frontier Technical Analysis & Quant Trading (2026)

## Order Flow & Liquidity
The focus has shifted from price-action patterns to **Liquidity Mapping** and **Order Flow Imbalance**.
- **Delta Divergence**: Price moving up while Cumulative Delta is falling indicates 'Absorption' (strong sellers absorbing buyers), often leading to a reversal.
- **Footprint Charts**: Real-time visualization of bid/ask volume at each price level.
- **Market Profile**: Identifying the 'Value Area' (where 70% of volume occurs). Trading outside the value area signals a 'Regime Shift' (trend), while inside signals 'Mean Reversion'.

## Modern Wave Theory
- **NEoWave vs Elliott**: NEoWave emphasizes strict monotonicity and specific correction rules, reducing the subjectivity of traditional Elliott Wave.
- **Quantitative Wave Validation**: Using algorithmic filters to validate wave counts in real-time, rather than retrospective fitting.

## Transmission Mapping
- **[Liquidity Void] -> [Aggressive Order Flow] -> [Rapid Price Gap]**: Identifies high-probability 'slippage' zones.
- **[Value Area Shift] -> [Regime Transition] -> [Trend Acceleration]**: Signals the move from range-bound to trending markets.
