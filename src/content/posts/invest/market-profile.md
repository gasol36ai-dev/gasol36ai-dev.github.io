# Market Profile & Volume Profile

Based on **Auction Market Theory**, Market Profile views the market as a continuous auction seeking "fair value."

## Core Concepts
- **Point of Control (POC)**: The price level with the highest traded volume/time. It acts as the ultimate attractor for price.
- **Value Area (VA)**: The range of prices where ~70% of the day's volume occurred.
- **VAH & VAL**: Value Area High and Value Area Low.
- **Low-Volume Nodes (LVN)**: Price regions where very little trading occurred. These act as "vacuums" where price tends to move rapidly.

## Market Dynamics
1. **Balanced Market**: Price stays within the Value Area, oscillating around the POC.
2. **Imbalanced Market**: Price breaks out of the VA and trends, creating a new "Value Area" as it discovers a new fair price.

## Strategic Use
Professional traders use the profile to identify "unfair" prices (outside VA) and trade back toward the POC, or trade the breakout when the market confirms a regime shift.

## Relationship with Order Flow
Market Profile identifies **where** the value is, while Order Flow (specifically **Clustered Flow**) identifies **when** and **how** large participants are interacting with that value.
