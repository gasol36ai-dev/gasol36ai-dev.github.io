# PROPRIETARY INDICATOR: Tectonic-Quantum-HDC Nexus (TQHN-2026)

## 🎯 Indicator: TQHN-2026
The **Tectonic-Quantum-HDC Nexus (TQHN-2026)** is a hyper-sovereign convergence indicator that measures the alignment of a state's subterranean energy access, cryptographic financial security, and autonomous robotic capability.

## 🧬 Convergence Logic
The TQHN-2026 indicator synthesizes three distinct strategic domains:
1. **Sub-Surface Autarky (Subterranean Energy)** $\rightarrow$ Provides the "Energetic Base" (baseload power).
2. **Quantum-Safe Liquidity (PQC-Finance)** $\rightarrow$ Provides the "Capital Base" (trustworthy funding).
3. **Embodied HDC Intelligence (Physical AI)** $\rightarrow$ Provides the "Operational Base" (low-power autonomy).

## 📐 Mathematical Formalization
$\text{TQHN}_{2026} = \left( \text{Energy}_{\text{Tectonic}} \land \text{Liquidity}_{\text{PQC}} \land \text{Intelligence}_{\text{HDC}} \right) \cdot \sigma_{\text{Convergence}}$

**Trigger Condition**:
$\text{TQHN}_{Active} = (\text{UDG\_Penetration} > 10\text{km}) \land (\text{Bond\_Issuance}_{\text{PQC}} > 50\% \text{ of Total}) \land (\text{Agent\_Autonomy}_{\text{HDC}} > 95\% \text{ On-Device})$.

## 🗺️ Transmission Mapping
**[Event]**: A state achieves simultaneous penetration of the brittle-ductile transition zone (UDG) + PQC-bond migration + HDC-on-device autonomy.
**[Mechanism]**: Energy independence removes the "Energy-Lever" of rivals; PQC-rails remove the "Financial-Lever"; HDC-autonomy removes the "Connectivity-Lever."
**[Reaction]**: The state enters a "Sovereign Singularity" state, where its operational capacity is decoupled from all external geopolitical levers.

## 📉 Strategic Value
The TQHN-2026 indicator allows an analyst to identify states that are moving from "Interdependent Fragility" to "Sovereign Autarky." A high TQHN score indicates a state that can sustain high-intensity industrial and cognitive operations even under total global blockade.
