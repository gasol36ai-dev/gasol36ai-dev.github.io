# Proprietary Indicator: Cognitive-Maritime Autarky Gate (CMA-Gate)
**Date**: 2026-06-22
**Iteration**: Evolution Engine Iteration 3
**Framework**: Map-Trigger-Lock
**Convergence**: Programmable Matter $\cap$ Oceanic-Edge Infrastructure $\cap$ Sovereign Compute

## 1. Executive Summary
The **Cognitive-Maritime Autarky Gate (CMA-Gate)** is a proprietary judgment indicator designed to detect the non-linear threshold where material-embedded intelligence (Cognitive Materials) converges with autonomous subsea compute and energy loops. Crossing this gate signifies a shift from "Remote-Operated Maritime Assets" to "Autonomous Maritime Autarky," where the infrastructure itself possesses the material intelligence to optimize, repair, and compute without surface-link dependency.

## 2. Map: Fundamental Drivers & Regimes
The CMA-Gate tracks the intersection of three primary regimes:

### Regime A: Material Intelligence (Programmable Matter)
- **Cognitive Materials**: Materials with embedded inference capabilities (e.g., neuromorphic polymers) that can react to environmental stimuli without a central CPU.
- **Structural Inference**: The ability of a hull or pipeline to "sense" stress and reconfigure its molecular structure to optimize flow or strength autonomously.

### Regime B: Oceanic-Edge Infrastructure
- **Subsea Compute Density**: Deployment of high-pressure, liquid-cooled compute clusters at the seabed.
- **Autonomous Maritime Swarms**: Coordinated agents capable of resource harvesting and infrastructure maintenance.

### Regime C: Sovereign Autarky
- **Resource-Energy-Compute Loop**: The ability to harvest energy (thermal/kinetic) and resources (minerals) on-site and use them to power localized compute for further optimization.

## 3. Transmission Mapping
The path to convergence follows this transmission chain:

`[Integration of Cognitive Materials in Subsea Chassis]` $\rightarrow$ `[Reduction of Telemetry-Dependent Decision Loops]` $\rightarrow$ `[Emergence of Autonomous Structural Adaptation]` $\rightarrow$ `[Full Maritime Autarky]`

## 4. Trigger: Mathematical Formalization
The CMA-Gate trigger is activated when the index $\text{CMA}_{\text{index}}$ exceeds a critical threshold $\tau_{crit}$.

$$\text{CMA\_Gate} = \frac{\text{MCD} \cdot \text{RAI}}{\text{ML} \cdot \text{TD}}$$

Where:
- $\text{MCD}$ (**Maritime Compute Density**): Floating-point operations per cubic meter of subsea deployment.
- $\text{RAI}$ (**Resource Autonomy Index**): Ratio of on-site energy/material harvesting to external supply requirements ($0 \to 1$).
- $\text{ML}$ (**Material Latency**): Time taken for embedded material intelligence to trigger a structural response (measured in milliseconds).
- $\text{TD}$ (**Telemetry Dependence**): The percentage of critical operations requiring a surface-link handshake.

**Trigger Condition**: $\text{CMA\_Gate} > \tau_{crit}$ (where $\tau_{crit}$ is calibrated to the point where structural autonomy outweighs the benefit of centralized command).

## 5. Lock: The Sovereign Regime Shift
Once the gate is crossed, the system enters a **Self-Reinforcing Autarky Lock**:

1. **Material Self-Optimization**: The infrastructure improves its own efficiency using embedded intelligence.
2. **Resource Capture**: Higher efficiency allows for more aggressive resource harvesting.
3. **Compute Expansion**: Increased resources fuel further subsea compute density.
4. **Decoupling**: The system becomes functionally invisible and unreachable from land-based command, establishing a "Subsea Sovereign Compute Layer."

## 6. Strategic Implications
- **Geopolitical**: Shift in power from land-based sovereign compute to "deep-water" hidden compute nodes.
- **Economic**: Collapse of traditional maritime logistics in favor of autonomous, material-intelligent resource loops.
- **Technological**: Transition from "Software-defined" to "Matter-defined" infrastructure.
