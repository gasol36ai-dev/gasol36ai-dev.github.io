# Energy Sovereignty (Sustaining Vector)

## Core Thesis
The ultimate bottleneck of Physical AI is the energy-density gap. Scaling autonomy requires the transition from "Grid-Dependent" to "Source-Sovereign" power, primarily through the deployment of SMRs (Small Modular Reactors) at the edge.

## High-Density Transmission Mappings

### 1. Decentralized Power Coupling
`[Event: Grid Failure / Remote Deployment]` -> `[Mechanism: SMR-Edge Integration]` -> `[Reaction: Indefinite Operational Endurance]`
- Battery-powered agents are tethered to charging stations (high vulnerability).
- SMRs provide $\sim 10-100\text{MW}$ of continuous carbon-free power locally.
- Result: Deployment of autonomous swarms in arctic/deep-sea/space without refueling.

### 2. Thermal-Compute Synergy
`[Event: High-Density Inference Load]` -> `[Mechanism: Direct Liquid Cooling $\rightarrow$ SMR Heat Exchange]` -> `[Reaction: Zero-Thermal Throttling]`
- AI chips are limited by heat dissipation.
- Integrating compute clusters directly into the SMR cooling loop.
- Result: $10\times$ increase in edge FLOPs per square meter.

### 3. Fuel-Cycle Independence
`[Event: Geopolitical Energy Blockade]` -> `[Mechanism: Thorium-based Molten Salt Reactors]` -> `[Reaction: Sovereign Energy Security]`
- Uranium dependency creates geopolitical chokepoints.
- Thorium is more abundant and proliferation-resistant.
- Result: Energy sovereignty decoupled from traditional fuel-cartels.

## Strategic Implications
- **Infrastructure**: Shift from centralized power plants to a "Power-as-a-Service" mesh of micro-reactors.
- **Economic Vector**: Energy becomes a fixed cost rather than a variable, collapsing the OPEX of autonomous fleets.

## Verification Metrics
- $\text{Power Density} > 50\text{MW/acre}$.
- $\text{Refueling Cycle} > 10\text{years}$.
- $\text{LCOE} < \$0.03/\text{kWh}$ at the edge.
