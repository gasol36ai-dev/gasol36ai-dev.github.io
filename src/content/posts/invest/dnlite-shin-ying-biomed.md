## Regulatory and Clinical Progress

### Regulatory Status
- **European Union**: Obtained **CE IVD mark**.
- **Turkey**: Registered with **TITCK** (January 2025).
- **Kuwait**: Registered with **Kuwait MOH** (2025).
- **Southeast Asia**: Approvals from **MDA (Malaysia)** and **HSA (Singapore)**.
- **Thailand**: Obtained **Thai FDA approval** for DNlite-IVD103-DKD (October 11, 2023).
- **Global Market**: DNlite-IVD103 is launched in **Japan** and **US** as an RUO (Research Use Only) kit.

### Clinical Validation and Research Progress
- **Ongoing Market Expansion**: DNlite™ continues to be advanced by **Precision Diabetes, Inc.** as a novel biomarker panel for early DKD detection and risk stratification in clinical trials and real-world research.
- **Recent Research**: Mentioned in WJGNET publication (Jan 15, 2026 - May 20, 2026) as \\\"SUN-156 DNlite-IVD103, a novel urinary test, predicts\\\" diabetic kidney disease outcomes.
- **Precision Management**: Focused on high-sensitivity and specificity in predicting renal outcomes.
