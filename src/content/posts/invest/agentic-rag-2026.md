# Agentic RAG & Self-Correcting Knowledge Graphs
# 代理式 RAG 與自我修正知識圖譜

## 🧠 The Evolution of Retrieval
# 🧠 檢索的演進
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is evolving from passive document fetching to active, agentic knowledge management.
# 檢索增強生成 (RAG) 正從被動的文件抓取演進為主動、代理式的知識管理。

## 🛠️ Core Concepts
# 🛠️ 核心概念

### 1. Self-Correcting Knowledge Graphs
# 1. 自我修正知識圖譜
Instead of static vector databases, agents are building dynamic, self-correcting knowledge graphs. Agents autonomously audit their own wiki entries, detect contradictions, and update information through a continuous "Observe-Reason-Update" loop.
# 代理不再僅僅依賴靜態向量數據庫，而是正在構建動態、自我修正的知識圖譜。代理自主審核其 Wiki 條目，檢測矛盾，並透過持續的「觀察-推理-更新」循環來更新信息。

### 2. Multi-Step Agentic Reasoning
# 2. 多步代理推理
Agents can now decompose complex research tasks into sub-tasks, execute specialized tools, and synthesize findings across multiple domain-specific knowledge bases.
# 代理現在能夠將複雜的研究任務分解為子任務，執行專業工具，並在多個特定領域的知識庫中綜合研究結果。

## 🚀 Strategic Value
# 🚀 戰略價值
This approach ensures the "Long-Term Memory" of the AI system is accurate, verifiable, and increasingly resilient to information decay.
# 這種方法確保了 AI 系統的「長期記憶」是準確、可驗證的，並且對於信息衰減具有日益增強的韌性。
