## Domains Synthesized (3-Domain Synthesis)

1. **Asymmetric Macro / Policy Paralysis** — Fed/ECB trapped in dual mandate conflict (May 2026). Key signals: Strait of Hormuz energy shock risk premium, USTR Section 301 stagflationary trade friction, KOF Leading Barometer decline (APAC), Western boom/APAC bust divergence.
2. **Market Microstructure / Liquidity Stress** — VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading) and Lambda (price impact coefficient) as quantitative stress gauges. Clustered Flow regime identification; POC divergence as accumulation signal. Market-maker withdrawal chain under toxic order flow.
3. **Physical AI / Efficiency-Forced CapEx Rotation** — Under fiscal dominance + rising cost of capital, AI CapEx allocators are forced to migrate from GPU-first → ASIC/LPU-first. Physical AI (embodied intelligence, world models) is prioritized over digital LLMs under energy cost pressure.

## Core Thesis
When a stagflationary macro regime locks central banks in paralysis AND microstructure shows elevated VPIN/Lambda (informed sellers, market-maker withdrawal), equity markets enter a **Fragility Window** where surface calm masks structural breakdowns. In this window, AI-infrastructure capital rotation from GPU→ASIC accelerates, as enterprises must defend TCO efficiency ratios against rising energy and financing costs. SMRG identifies the onset of this triple-locked regime.
