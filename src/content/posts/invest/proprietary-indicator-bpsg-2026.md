## 🧠 Bio-Physical Sovereignty Gate (BPSG / HPI-16)
*Generated: 2026-05-30 | Evolution Cycle 3 | CTO Role*

### Overview
**BPSG** detects the inflection point at which a nation-state crosses simultaneous independence thresholds across three distinct bio-physical autonomy vectors:
1. **Health AI Sovereignty** — domestic AI diagnostic infrastructure replacing foreign clinical AI platforms
2. **Critical Mineral Control** — secure supply of Co/Ni/Li for medical devices, EV batteries, and aerospace components  
3. **Autonomous Mobility Deployment** — indigenous eVTOL/autonomous aviation fleet operational and certified

When all three gates trigger simultaneously, a step-function re-rating of that sovereign's industrial, healthcare, and defense sectors follows — driven by elimination of three distinct foreign-dependency chokepoints in a single threshold crossing.

### Map (Three-Domain Regime Context)

**Domain 1 — Health AI (Diagnostic Sovereignty Index, DSI):**
- Foreign AI dependency: majority of clinical AI platforms are US/EU-origin (FDA-cleared), creating data liability and supply chain risk for non-aligned sovereigns
- Rising domestic platforms: China (Ping An Health, Tencent Miying), India (NHDS), Gulf states (UAE NABIDH)
- EU AI Act (Aug 2, 2026) forces conformity — creates compliance cost AND opportunity for domestic alternatives

**Domain 2 — Critical Minerals (Ocean Supply Compression, OSC):**
- 70% of global cobalt production: DRC (politically fragile, Chinese-controlled)
- ISA moratorium risk on deep-sea nodule mining = critical mineral supply cliff for battery/medical device manufacturers
- Nations securing CCZ permits or domestic mineral processing gain manufacturing sovereignty

**Domain 3 — Autonomous Mobility (Airspace Independence Score, AIS):**
- First-mover FAA/EASA certification provides export advantage + military dual-use superiority
- Indigenous eVTOL + defense drone integration reduces foreign aerospace supply chain dependence
- LA28 Olympics (2028) as commercial proof event — triggers mass vertiport investment

### Trigger (Gate Conditions)

- **GATE-1 (Health AI):** Domestic platform achieves >30% share of clinical AI deployments + autonomous diagnostic regulatory framework enacted
- **GATE-2 (Minerals):** Sovereign secures processing capacity for >=2 of {Co, Ni, Li, Mn} at >20% domestic demand coverage OR locks CCZ deep-sea permit
- **GATE-3 (Mobility):** Indigenous eVTOL/autonomous aviation program receives type certification + military UAV fleet operational (>=100 units)

**BPSG SIGNAL = GATE-1 AND GATE-2 AND GATE-3 within rolling 18-month window**

Partial signal (2 of 3 gates): "Bio-Physical Pre-Convergence" — directional positioning warranted

### Lock (Execution Decision)

| BPSG State | Signal | Execution |

### Cross-Domain Synthesis Quality
- **Health AI x Minerals:** Data center infrastructure for health AI platforms requires Co/Ni/Li for cooling systems and server components
- **Minerals x Mobility:** Battery eVTOL (Joby, Archer) = critical mineral intensity; mineral-sovereign nations gain cost advantage in aircraft manufacturing
- **Health AI x Mobility:** Autonomous aviation generates health AI datasets (biometrics, emergency transport) → data flywheel for health AI sovereignty
- **All 3:** Nation controlling diagnostic AI data + mineral supply + autonomous mobility controls the physical and biological infrastructure layer of the 21st century

### Reusable Template: "Bio-Physical Sovereignty Gate"
When >=3 bio-physical autonomy vectors (health AI, critical minerals, autonomous mobility) reach independence thresholds within an 18-month window for a single sovereign → non-linear industrial policy acceleration + sovereign premium cascade across healthcare, mining, and aerospace equities in that jurisdiction.

**Distinction from PASG (HPI-15):** PASG = CBDC x Nuclear x Robotics (financial/energy/physical layers). BPSG = Health AI x Minerals x Mobility (biological/materials/transportation layers). Together they form the "Dual Sovereignty Stack" framework for full nation-state independence assessment.
