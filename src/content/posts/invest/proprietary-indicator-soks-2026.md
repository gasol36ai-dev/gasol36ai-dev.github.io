# Proprietary Indicator: SOKS-2026 (Sovereign Organic Kinetic Stack)

## 1. Convergence Logic
The **SOKS-2026** indicator tracks the convergence of three distinct strategic domains to identify a step-function shift in autonomous agency: **Sensing (Neuromorphic Tactile)** $\times$ **Acting (Soft-Robotic Proprioception)** $\times$ **Sustaining (Diamond WBG Electronics)**.

Current kinetic agents are limited by the "Sensory-Actuation Gap" (latency in processing touch) and the "Thermal-Power Wall" (heat generated by high-torque actuators). SOKS-2026 signals the arrival of agents that can feel, react, and power themselves with biological efficiency and industrial resilience.

## 2. Transmission Mapping
`[Neuromorphic Tactile Skin] -> [Sub-ms Event-Based Reflexes] -> [Soft-Robotic Proprioception] -> [Adaptive Morphological Control] -> [Diamond WBG Power Delivery] -> [High-Current, Zero-Cooling Actuation] -> [SOKS-2026 Active]`

## 3. Trigger Condition (Qualitative Analysis)
The SOKS-2026 indicator transitions to **ACTIVE** when the following three thresholds are met simultaneously:
1. **Sensing Threshold:** Deployment of event-based tactile skins with $\le 1\text{ms}$ latency and $\ge 10^3$ sensors/cm$^2$.
2. **Acting Threshold:** Integration of liquid-metal-based proprioceptive feedback loops capable of $10\text{Hz}$ morphological reconfiguration.
3. **Sustaining Threshold:** Use of Diamond WBG power stages enabling $\ge 5\text{kW/cm}^3$ power density without active heat sinking.

**Sovereign Premium:** When SOKS-2026 is Active, the agent transitions from "Remote-Controlled Tool" to "Kinetic Sovereign," capable of independent operation in extreme environments (deep sea, high radiation, vacuum) without a logistics umbilical.

## 4. Strategic Value
SOKS-2026 represents the **Physical Autonomy Inflection**. Agents achieving this stack can perform "Tactile Reasoning"—adjusting their physical form and force output in real-time based on the *texture* and *resistance* of the environment, rather than relying on visual-onlySLAM. This is the primary requirement for Sovereign Kinetic Reflex (SKR) capabilities.
