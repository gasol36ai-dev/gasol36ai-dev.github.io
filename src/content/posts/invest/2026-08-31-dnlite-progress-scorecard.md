## Findings
- Status: Positive Progress
- Key Observations:
  - DNlite-IVD103 received market approval in Guatemala on August 23, 2026, marking entry into Latin American market.
  - DNlite™ assay demonstrated independent renal risk prediction in landmark CREDENCE trial analysis (presented at ADA 2026 as Late-Breaking Abstract).
  - ASR registration with FDA completed (as of August 11, 2026).
  - Commercial Strategy Advisory Committee formed with 4 global experts (August 11, 2026).
  - ERA October 2, 2026 oral presentation confirmed (August 11, 2026).
  - U.S. LDT commercial launch expected in Q3 2026.

- Issues Identified:

- Monitor for upcoming ERA presentation (October 2, 2026) and ADA LBA dissemination for further clinical validation.
