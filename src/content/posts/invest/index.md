# Advanced Technical Analysis: Order Flow & Quantitative Strategies

## Core Frameworks
1. **Order Flow Trading**:
   - **Footprint Charts**: Analysis of volume clusters at specific price levels to identify where aggressive buyers/sellers are entering.
   - **DOM (Depth of Market)**: Monitoring live limit orders to gauge institutional supply and demand before execution.
   - **Delta Divergence**: When price makes a new high but the Delta (buy volume - sell volume) decreases, signaling absorption or a "trap".
2. **Market Profile (TPO)**:
   - **Time, Price, and Opportunity**: Focuses on how much time the market spends at a specific price.
   - **Value Area (VA)**: The range where 70% of the day's trading occurs. Breaking out of VA with high volume signals a trend; remaining inside signals a range.
3. **NeoWave Theory**:
   - An advanced refinement of Elliott Wave, emphasizing deterministic rules over subjective interpretation.
   - Focuses on wave proportions and structural integrity to predict "tipping points" in trend reversals.

## Macro-Micro Linkages (Transmission Mapping)
- **Order Flow as a Macro Bridge**: Order flow acts as the immediate transmission mechanism between macro fundamentals and price.
- **Mechanism**: `[Macro Event (e.g., CPI Release)] -> [Institutional Expectation Shift] -> [Aggressive Order Flow / Delta Spike] -> [Price Movement]`.
- **Insight**: Order flow is most predictive when it confirms a macro-regime shift (e.g., a "broken correlation" where price moves opposite to a macro variable, signaling a new regime).
