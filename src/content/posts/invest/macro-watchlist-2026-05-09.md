# Macro Watchlist - May 2026
(Updated: 2026-05-09)

## 🔴 High Impact (Immediate Action)
|- **US Real Yields (10Y TIPS)**: Currently ~1.93%. Monitor for sudden spikes that might threaten the Gold decoupling thesis. Any rise in real yields alongside Gold would confirm a total breakdown of the traditional correlation.
|- **US Inflation (PCE/CPI)**: Regime: "Sticky/Elevated". PCE ~3.2%, Core PCE ~3.0%. Watch for second-round effects from energy/oil shocks (Brent $85+).
|- **Middle East Geopolitical Risk (Iran/Strait of Hormuz)**: Primary driver for "Safe Haven" premium in Gold and energy volatility. Potential for supply-side inflation shocks.
|- **Fed Leadership Transition**: Monitor for market reactions to the successor's stance on inflation vs. growth.

## 🟡 Medium Impact (Trend Monitoring)
|- **US Dollar Index (DXY)**: Trading near 98.5. Monitor for strength that could pressure EM assets and increase USD-denominated debt servicing costs.
|- **BoJ Normalization**: Highly critical. Monitor for rate hike cues that could trigger a massive unwinding of JPY-funded carry trades, impacting global equity volatility (especially US Tech).
|- **US 10Y Treasury Yield**: Currently ~4.35% - 4.36%. Resistance level at 4.45%. Watch for steepening/stabilization.
|- **ECB Policy Divergence**: ECB maintaining a hike bias (~2.00% target) due to upward inflation revisions (2.6% in March). Monitor for USD/EUR volatility.

## 🟢 Low Impact (Long-term Structural)
|- **US Credit Rating (Moody's)**: Monitor for official downgrades following the loss of top-tier status; creates a permanent risk premium on Treasuries.
|- **US-China Trade War (Tariffs)**: Long-term driver of inflation and de-dollarization (e.g., 145% tariff scenario).
|- **Global Core Inflation (JPM Forecast)**: J.P. Morgan expects global core inflation to stabilize at 2.8% for 2026.
