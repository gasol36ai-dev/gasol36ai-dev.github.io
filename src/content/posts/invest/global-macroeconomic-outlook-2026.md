# Global Macroeconomic Outlook 2026

## Overview
The global economy in 2026 is characterized by slowing growth, persistent inflationary pressures, geoeconomic fragmentation, and rising financial volatility. Resilience, diversification, and credible policy frameworks are becoming more important than pure growth pursuit, particularly for low- and middle-income countries.

## Key Trends and Projections

### Global Growth
- **World Bank**: Projects 2.3% growth in 2025 (slowest since 2008 excluding 2009/2020), recovering to 2.5% over 2026-27
- **IMF**: Projects 3.3% growth for 2026 and 3.2% for 2027, revised slightly up since October 2025
- **Pre-pandemic average**: 3.1%, indicating sustained below-trend growth

### Inflation and Monetary Policy
- **Sticky Inflation**: PCE inflation projected above 3% for 2025 (Fed)
- **Diverging Policies**: Central banks facing constrained policy choices amid geopolitical realignment
- **US Inflation**: Expected to return to target more gradually than global inflation

### Geoeconomic Fragmentation
- **Competing Financial Infrastructures**: Emergence of parallel payment systems, development finance, and cross-border banking
- **Digital Payment Systems**: Increasing interoperability challenges between competing systems
- **De-dollarization Trends**: Global diversification into gold and alternative currency arrangements reducing dollar demand

### Trade and Supply Chains
- **Slower Trade Growth**: Lagging behind GDP due to supply-chain re-shoring, industrial policies, trade tariffs, and security-driven restrictions
- **Trade Volumes**: May plateau or decline as share of GDP
- **Geopolitical Risks**: Escalation of armed conflicts and climate-related disasters posing downside risks

### Debt and Financial Stability
- **Rising Debt Burdens**: Narrowing path to stable growth for many economies
- **Financial Volatility**: Set to increase against backdrop of tighter finance and weaker global trade
- **Interest Payments**: For LICs, expected to exceed 10% of fiscal revenues through 2027

## Risk Factors

### Downside Risks
- **Trade Policy Uncertainty**: Increased tariffs potentially driving inflation and disrupting value chains
- **Financial Stress**: Leading to capital outflows from vulnerable economies
- **Geopolitical Escalation**: Armed conflicts and climate-related disasters
- **Technology Expectations**: Reevaluation creating downside pressure
- **Uncertainty**: Elevated levels affecting decision-making and investment

### Upside Opportunities
- **Trade Tension Resolution**: Potential for rapid growth acceleration
- **Fiscal Spending**: Increased investment in major economies
- **Technological Boosts**: Generative AI and new energy infrastructure investments

## Strategic Implications

### For Low- and Middle-Income Countries (LMICs)
- **Resilience over Growth**: Priority shift due to tighter financing and weaker trade
- **Diversification**: Essential across economic partners and financial instruments
- **Credible Policy Frameworks**: Needed to anchor expectations and attract investment
- **FCS Vulnerabilities**: Nearly 60% of FCS economies face growth slowdown in 2025

### Investment and Liquidity Considerations
- **Market Liquidity**: Affected by geopolitical risks, divergent monetary policies, and debt sustainability concerns
- **Investment Timing**: Requires careful assessment of policy regime stability and geopolitical temperature
- **Safe Haven Demand**: Potential increase for assets perceived as resilient to fragmentation
- **Infrastructure Focus**: Digital inclusion, broadband, and electricity as resilience enablers

## Policy Recommendations
- **Macroeconomic Stability**: 
  - Fiscal: Prioritize simple taxes, shift spending to social programs
  - Monetary: Strengthen central bank independence to anchor inflation
  - Financial: Promote digital financial inclusion supported by broadband/electricity investments
- **Climate & Support**: Invest in climate-smart agriculture and catastrophe insurance for FCS
- **Debt Management**: Utilize frameworks like G20 Common Framework with improved transparency
