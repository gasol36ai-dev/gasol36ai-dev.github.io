# Sovereign Swarm Autarky Gate (SSAG)
**Status**: Proprietary Indicator (Synthesized 2026-06-14)
**Domains**: THz 6G Swarms $\times$ Atmospheric Energy Harvesting $\times$ Molecular DNA Storage

## 1. The Logic of Autarky
The SSAG measures the transition of agentic swarms from "tethered tools" (dependent on grid power, cloud backhaul, and volatile memory) to "autonomous sovereign entities" (thermodynamically and computationally self-sufficient).

When a swarm achieves the **Sovereign Convergence**, it creates a localized zone of intelligence that is immune to traditional grid-based disruption and cloud-latency bottlenecks.

## 2. Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### Map (The State Space)
$\text{Autarky Index} = \frac{(\text{THz Bandwidth} \times \text{Agentic Coherence})}{\text{Energy Deficit} + \text{State Decay Rate}}$

- **THz Bandwidth**: Ability to coordinate sub-ms intent loops (0.1-10 THz).
- **Agentic Coherence**: The ratio of successful swarm-wide consensus to individual node failures.
- **Energy Deficit**: (Compute Demand) - (Atmospheric Harvesting Rate).
- **State Decay Rate**: The energy cost required to maintain long-term memory (mitigated by DNA storage).

### Trigger (The Inflection Point)
The **SSAG Trigger** fires when:
1. **Thermodynamic Zero**: $\text{Harvesting Rate} \ge \text{Inference Power Demand}$ for $\ge 72$ consecutive hours.
2. **Coordination Peak**: Swarm-wide latency $\le 500\mu s$ across $\ge 100$ nodes.
3. **Persistence Lock**: State migration to Molecular DNA Storage is completed, allowing the swarm to "sleep" with zero energy loss.

### Lock (The Strategic Action)
Upon SSAG trigger, the following judgment lock is applied:
- **Infrastructure Valuation**: Shift target asset valuation from "Hardware Capex" to "Sovereign Capability Premium".
- **Risk Model**: Remove "Grid Dependency" and "Cloud Outage" from the risk matrix for the affected physical zone.
- **Strategic Posture**: Classify the zone as a **"Cognitive Fortress"**—capable of autonomous operation regardless of external network or power state.

## 3. Strategic Value
The SSAG identifies the exact moment a physical region becomes a "Black Box of Intelligence"—where an adversary can cut power and data lines, but the local agentic swarm continues to operate, reason, and coordinate using the environment's own energy and the internal DNA-stored knowledge base.
