# Macro-Economic Volatility & Liquidity 2026
# 宏觀經濟波動與流動性 2026

## 📊 Current Regime Analysis
# 📊 當前政權分析
The global macro environment is characterized by structural decoupling. Real yields are increasingly diverging from inflation expectations, creating high-volatility regimes in both bond and commodity markets.
# 全球宏觀環境的特徵是結構性脫鉤。實際收益率與通脹預期日益背離，在債券和商品市場中創造了高波動政權。

## 🔑 Key Drivers
# 🔑 關鍵驅動力

### 1. Yield-Inflation Decoupling
# 1. 收益率-通脹脫鉤
The decoupling of real interest rates from headline inflation is driving structural demand for hard assets (Gold, Real Estate, etc.) as a hedge against regime uncertainty.
# 實際利率與標題通脹的脫鉤，正驅動對硬資產（黃金、房地產等）的結構性需求，以對沖政權不確定性。

### 2. Geopolitical Fragmentation
# 2. 地緣政治碎片化
Geopolitical shifts are restructuring global supply chains, particularly in the robotics and semiconductor sectors, leading to localized liquidity shocks and supply-chain-driven volatility.
# 地緣政治轉變正在重構全球供應鏈，特別是在機器人和半導體領域，導致局部流動性衝擊和供應鏈驅動的波動。

## 🎯 Investment Strategy
# 🎯 投資策略
Focus on "Volatility-Adaptive" frameworks. Use divergence between liquidity flow and price action as a primary indicator for regime shifts.
# 關注「波動率自適應」框架。利用流動性流向與價格行為之間的背離，作為政權轉移的主要指標。
