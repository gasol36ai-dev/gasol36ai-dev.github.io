# Macro Transmission Patterns 2026

## Global Regime: The Great Divergence
The global economy is characterized by a sharp divergence between the Western Hemisphere (led by the US) and the Asia-Pacific/European regions, primarily driven by energy-security asymmetries.

## Key Transmission Chains

### 1. The Energy-Inflation-Growth Loop
`[Middle East Conflict]` $\rightarrow$ `[Strait of Hormuz Supply Shock]` $\rightarrow$ `[Energy Prices \uparrow]` $\rightarrow$ `[Divergent Outcomes]`
- **US Outcome**: Relatively resilient (net energy exporter). Growth maintained by AI capex and corporate earnings, though threatened by "upstream" inflation (PPI acceleration).
- **EU/EM Outcome**: High vulnerability. Energy costs $\rightarrow$ Stagflationary pressure (Growth $\downarrow$ and Inflation $\uparrow$).

### 2. Indicator Divergence (KOF Barometers)
- **Coincident Barometer**: Currently rising (highest since 2022), reflecting current strength in Asia and Western Hemisphere.
- **Leading Barometer**: Falling/Neutral, signaling a potential downturn in 3-6 months, specifically led by negative contributions from the Asia-Pacific region.

## Strategic Implications
- **USD Resilience**: Supported by firmer inflation expectations and relative economic strength.
- **Cross-Asset Alignment**: Incomplete. Inflation pressure (PPI) is currently dominating growth confirmation signals.
- **Safe Haven Shift**: Gold is increasingly driven by "systemic-trust-logic" rather than simple rate-logic, breaking traditional correlations with real yields.
