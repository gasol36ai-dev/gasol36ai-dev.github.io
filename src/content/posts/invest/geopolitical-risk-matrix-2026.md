# Geopolitical Risk Matrix 2026

Geopolitical risk has transitioned from "event-driven" to "structural", driving a fundamental reconfiguration of global trade and investment.

## Key Risk Poles
- **The Middle East**: Ongoing war continuing to impact financial stability and energy markets (IMF).
- **China (Xi Strategy)**: Priority shift toward political control and technological supremacy. Exporting excess capacity to global markets, increasing trade frictions.
- **United States**: Identified as a principal source of global risk in 2026, likely due to policy volatility and regime-change campaigns (e.g., Venezuela).

## Economic Transmission
- **Global Capex Cycle**: A multi-year, capital-intensive cycle has begun as companies relocate production and ensure redundancy (Morgan Stanley).
- **Safe Haven Flows**: Volatility continues to support the USD in the short term, though long-term "de-dollarization" trends persist in response to US policy.
