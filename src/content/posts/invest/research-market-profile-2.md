# Market Profile

## Core Concept
A tool for identifying fair value zones and market structure through Time Price Opportunity (TPO) and Volume Profile. It reveals the "WHERE" of the market.

## Key Metrics
- **Value Area (VA)**: The price range where 70% of the day's volume occurred.
- **Point of Control (POC)**: The price level with the highest volume.
- **Initial Balance (IB)**: The price range of the first hour of trading.

## 2026 Strategic Insight: Value Area Migration
The most critical signal in modern quantitative trading is "Value Area Migration". A shift in the POC combined with [[order-flow-trading]] aggression signals a fundamental regime change rather than a temporary spike.
