# Quantum-Resilient Macro: Cryptographic Sovereignty & Financial Stability

## 1. Overview
The transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) is a macro-prudential necessity. The vulnerability of current asymmetric encryption (RSA/ECC) to Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computers (CRQCs) threatens the entire global trust regime.

## 2. Key Vectors
- **Lattice-based Cryptography**: The primary defense against quantum attacks (e.g., Kyber, Dilithium).
- **RWA Tokenization Security**: Maintaining the immutable link between digital tokens and physical assets.
- **Cross-Border Liquidity Integrity**: Ensuring interoperability between PQC-ready and vulnerable jurisdictions.

## 3. Transmission Mapping: The Quantum Shock
- **Trust Failure $\rightarrow$ Settlement Paralysis**: Broken signatures lead to the cessation of high-value payments.
- **Asset Ownership Collapse $\rightarrow$ RWA Devaluation**: Compromised private keys invalidate the proof of title for tokenized assets.
- **Liquidity Fragmentation $\rightarrow$ Contagion**: Divergent PQC adoption speeds create "liquidity traps" and FX volatility.

## 4. Convergence Gate: The Verification Integrity Threshold
The convergence of PQC adoption and RWA maturity defines the **Verification Integrity Threshold**. Crossing this threshold ensures that programmable finance can scale without being susceptible to "Quantum Heists."
