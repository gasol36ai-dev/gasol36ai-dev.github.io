# The Sovereign Autarky Convergence (SAC) Gate - 2026

## 🎯 Objective
To identify the threshold where an entity achieves absolute operational independence through the simultaneous convergence of Intelligence, Energy, and Financial Security.

## 🧠 Core Logic
The SAC Gate is a non-linear trigger that fires when three distinct sovereign capability vectors cross their respective stability thresholds. Unlike single-domain indicators, the SAC Gate requires the *simultaneous* presence of all three to signal a "Hyper-Sovereign" status.

## 📐 Formalization
The SAC Active state is defined as:
$$ \\text{SAC\\_Active} = (\\text{Neuro\\_Edge\\_Density} > \\sigma_N) \\land (\\text{SMR\\_Autarky\\_Ratio} > \\sigma_E) \\land (\\text{PQC\\_Compliance} = 1) $$

Where:
- **$\\text{Neuro\\_Edge\\_Density}$**: The ratio of local edge inference capacity to total compute demand.
- **$\\text{SMR\\_Autarky\\_Ratio}$**: The percentage of total compute energy sourced from on-site, non-grid, non-renewable-dependent SMRs.
- **$\\text{PQC\\_Compliance}$**: A binary indicator (1/0) of the percentage of settlement volume secured by NIST-validated PQC.

## 🧬 Transmission Mapping
- **[Event]**: Breakthroughs in Neuromorphic scaling, SMR commercialization, and PQC standardization.
- **[Mechanism]**: The "Triad of Autarky" creates a closed-loop system where intelligence drives energy demand, energy fuels intelligence, and quantum-secure rails protect the resulting economic value.
- **[Reaction]**: A step-function increase in the entity's geopolitical leverage and resistance to external coercion.

## 🛡️ Strategic Use Case
A sovereign state or mega-corporation monitoring the SAC Gate can time their transition to a fully autonomous, decoupled operational mode, effectively becoming immune to global macro shocks.
