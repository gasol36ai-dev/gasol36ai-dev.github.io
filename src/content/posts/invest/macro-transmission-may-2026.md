# Macro Transmission Report: May 2026

## Executive Summary
The May 2026 macro environment is characterized by a critical tension between stubborn inflation pressures and lagging growth confirmation. The primary driver is the sharp acceleration in Core PPI, which is now leaking into consumer prices with higher velocity than in the 2024-2025 cycle.

## Key Indicators & Observations

### 1. Inflation vs. Growth Dynamics
- **Core PPI Acceleration**: Recent data shows a sharp uptick in producer prices, particularly in services and energy-intensive components. This suggests a "second wave" of inflation that is structural rather than transitory.
- **Growth Confirmation Gap**: GDP prints remain tepid. The "growth confirmation" needed to justify higher valuations is missing, leading to a "stagflationary tilt" in market sentiment.
- **Real Yield Stability**: 10-year real yields have remained remarkably stable. This stability is acting as a ceiling for metals (Gold, Silver, Copper), as the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets remains high despite inflation.

### 2. Transmission Mechanisms
- **PPI $\rightarrow$ CPI Leakage**: The window between producer price spikes and consumer price increases has shrunk. This indicates that firms have regained pricing power or are facing irresistible cost pressures.
- **Yield Curve Implications**: The curve remains in a state of fragile equilibrium, with the market pricing in a "higher-for-longer" regime that threatens the weakest cohorts of corporate debt.

## Strategic Implications
- **Asset Allocation**: Pivot toward inflation-protected securities (TIPS) and high-margin companies with demonstrable pricing power.
- **Metals Outlook**: Neutral to Bearish in the short term due to real yield floors, unless a growth shock triggers a flight to safety.
- **Risk Vector**: The primary risk is a "policy error" where central banks over-tighten to combat PPI, inadvertently triggering a hard landing before growth is confirmed.
