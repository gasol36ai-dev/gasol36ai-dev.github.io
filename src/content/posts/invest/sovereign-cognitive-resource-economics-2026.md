# Sovereign Cognitive Resource Economics (SCRE)

Sovereign Cognitive Resource Economics (SCRE) is a theoretical framework analyzing the transition of intelligence from a distributed human trait to a centralized, scalable, and sovereignly-controlled utility. In this paradigm, "Cognitive Resources" (defined as the aggregate capacity for high-order reasoning, inference, and agentic execution) are treated as strategic national assets, similar to energy reserves or gold.

## 1. Intelligence-as-a-Utility (IaaU) Models
The IaaU model posits that cognitive capacity is delivered via a "cognitive grid." Instead of local ownership of intelligence (individual human expertise), intelligence is consumed as a utility.
- **The Grid Model:** Intelligence is provisioned in tiers (Basic, Advanced, Sovereign).
- **Cognitive Load Balancing:** National grids dynamically allocate compute to critical sectors (e.g., healthcare, defense) during "cognitive peaks."
- **Sovereign IaaU:** Nations move away from third-party API dependencies to "Sovereign Compute Clusters" to avoid "cognitive colonization"—the state where a nation's decision-making logic is outsourced to a foreign model's weights.

## 2. The Cognitive Gini Coefficient
The Cognitive Gini Coefficient ($\text{G}_{\text{cog}}$) measures the inequality of agentic capability within a population or across nations.
- **Metric:** It calculates the distribution of "Reasoning Tokens" or "Agentic Hours" available per capita.
- **$\text{G}_{\text{cog}} \to 0$:** Universal Basic Intelligence (UBI), where every citizen has equal access to high-order reasoning.
- **$\text{G}_{\text{cog}} \to 1$:** Cognitive Oligarchy, where a tiny fraction of the population controls the most capable models, creating a feedback loop of intelligence-driven wealth accumulation.

## 3. Pricing Models: High-Order Reasoning vs. Basic Inference
SCRE distinguishes between **System 1 Inference** (pattern recognition, basic retrieval) and **System 2 Reasoning** (multi-step planning, verification, novel synthesis).
- **Inference (Commoditized):** Priced at near-zero marginal cost; treated as a public good.
- **Reasoning (Premium):** Priced based on "Compute-Hours of Thought."
- **The Reasoning Premium:** High-order reasoning requires iterative search (e.g., Monte Carlo Tree Search), making its cost non-linear. This leads to "Reasoning Stratification," where only high-value tasks (scientific discovery, strategic planning) can afford the "deep thought" price point.

## 4. National Productivity and the Cognitive Divide
The "Cognitive Divide" is the macroeconomic gap between nations that possess sovereign cognitive resources and those that are "cognitive importers."
- **Productivity Shift:** Productivity is no longer a function of $\text{Labor} \times \text{Capital}$, but $\text{Compute} \times \text{Reasoning Efficiency}$.
- **Cognitive Autarky:** The ability of a state to sustain its entire administrative and scientific apparatus using internal IaaU.
- **The Divide:** Importer nations face "Cognitive Rent," where a percentage of their GDP flows to the owners of the base models, exacerbating global inequality.
