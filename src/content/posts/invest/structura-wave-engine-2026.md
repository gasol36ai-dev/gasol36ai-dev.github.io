# Structura Wave Engine 2026

## Overview
The Structura Wave Engine is a framework for analyzing non-linear regime transitions in complex, interconnected systems. It moves beyond simple trend-following to identify the underlying wave mechanics that drive market and geopolitical shifts.

## Core Mechanics
1. **Phase Resonance**: Identifying when multiple independent oscillators (e.g., interest rates, commodity prices, geopolitical tension) align in phase.
2. **Wave Interference Patterns**: Analyzing constructive and destructive interference between macro waves to predict period of extreme volatility or stability.
3. **Amplitude Modulation**: Tracking the intensity of regime shifts via volatility-adjusted volume and order flow.

## Implementation Framework
- **Input**: Multi-modal data streams (Order Flow, Macro Indicators, Sentiment).
- **Processing**: Wavelet transforms and spectral analysis to decompose signals.
- **Output**: Regime transition probability and expected amplitude.

## Strategic Application
Used for identifying "Black Swan" events before they manifest in price action, by detecting the build-up of structural wave tension.
