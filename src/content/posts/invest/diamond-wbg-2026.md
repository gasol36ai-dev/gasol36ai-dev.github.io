# Concept: Diamond WBG 2026
**Category:** Ultra-Wide Bandgap (UWBG) Electronics
**Status:** Strategic Breakthrough (2025-2026)

## Atomic Definition
The utilization of synthetic diamond (specifically the emergence of hexagonal Lonsdaleite and large-diameter single-crystal substrates) to create power electronics with near-theoretical limits of thermal conductivity, breakdown field, and carrier mobility, specifically aimed at achieving energy infrastructure independence (Power Sovereignty).

## Key Pointers
- **Lonsdaleite Mobility:** Breakthrough in hexagonal diamond showing electron mobility up to $28,000 \text{ cm}^2/\text{Vs}$.
- **vdW Integration:** Use of $\text{SrTiO}_3$ perovskite membranes to enable efficient p-type transistors.
- **Orbray (111):** Industrial scale-up of large-diameter single-crystal diamond substrates.
- **Power Sovereignty:** The strategic shift toward UWBG to eliminate thermal management overhead and break dependence on $\text{SiC}/\text{GaN}$ supply chains.
