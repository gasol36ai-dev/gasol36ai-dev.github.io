## Executive Summary
The **Sovereign Resilience Nexus (SRN-2026)** is a high-density judgment indicator designed to detect the "Step-Function" transition of a nation-state from "Global-Dependent" to "Sovereign-Autonomous." Unlike single-vector indicators, the SRN monitors the synergistic intersection of three critical autarky pillars: **Energy-Compute**, **Financial-Cryptographic**, and **Physical-Kinetic**. 

The SRN signal triggers when these three pillars converge to create a state of "Total Systemic Decoupling," where the state's critical functions can persist indefinitely despite total isolation from global supply chains or financial networks.

- **Strategic Value**: Immunity to "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) and quantum forgery.

### Pillar C: Kinetic Reflexive Autonomy ($\text{KRA}$)
- **Threshold**: Deployment of the Neuromorphic Kinetic Resilience Layer (KRL) across $\ge 70\%$ of critical energy/comms infrastructure.
- **Indicator**: $\tau_{reflex} < 1\text{ms}$ (Average response latency for reflexive infrastructure repair).
- **Strategic Value**: Zero-latency self-healing of physical assets without human/cloud intervention.
