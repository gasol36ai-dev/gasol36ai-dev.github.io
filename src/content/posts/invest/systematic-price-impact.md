# Systematic Price Impact & The Information Flow Value

A critical distinction has emerged in the understanding of price impact: the separation of **Systematic** vs. **Idiosyncratic** information flows.

## 1. The Information Flow Value Hypothesis
The "value" of information in the stock market is not the stock price itself, but the value of the **dynamic flow of information** as it enters the limit order book (LOB).

### Key Insights
- **Systematic Component**: Driven by the volatility of the overall market. This is the only component that "matters" for professional informed traders, as idiosyncratic risks can be diversified away.
- **Slope of the LOB**: The price impact ($\lambda$) is observable as the slope of the limit order book.
- **Information Value $\approx$ Order Flow $\times$ LOB Slope**.

## 2. Transmission Mapping: Macro-Micro Convergence
This framework bridges Macro and Micro:
**[Macro Market Volatility] $\rightarrow$ [LOB Slope Adjustment] $\rightarrow$ [Systematic Price Impact] $\rightarrow$ [Informed Order Flow Value]**.

## 3. Strategic Application
When the "Information Flow Value" is high but the price is stagnant, it indicates a massive absorption of systematic information by a "hidden" institutional player, creating a high-probability reversal or breakout point.
