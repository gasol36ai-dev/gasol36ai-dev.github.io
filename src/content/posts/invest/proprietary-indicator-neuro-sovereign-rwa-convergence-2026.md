# Proprietary Indicator: Neuro-Sovereign RWA Convergence (NSRC)

## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. MAP: The Multidimensional Baseline
The NSRC maps the intersection of three distinct strategic vectors:
*   **[Hardware Plasticity Index]**: A measure of the real-time learning/adaptation capability of distributed agent clusters (Neuromorphic efficacy).
*   **[Compute Sovereignty Score]**: A composite index of a nation's domestic GPU/ASIC capacity vs. its critical mineral control (Geopolitical buffer).
*   **[RWA Liquidity Geometry Efficiency]**: The ratio of on-chain transaction velocity to off-chain legal settlement latency (Economic throughput).

### 2. TRIGGER: The Convergence Signal
The indicator triggers a high-conviction signal when:
*   **[Event A]**: A breakthrough in **In-situ Neuromorphic Learning** allows for massive, low-power agent scaling.
*   **[Event B]**: **Geopolitical Tension** triggers a sudden restriction in critical mineral exports (e.g., Gallium), causing a spike in sovereign compute demand.
*   **[Event C]**: **RWA Tokenization** reaches a critical mass where decentralized liquidity pools begin to decouple from traditional NAV-based pricing.

**The Combined Trigger**: `(Δ Plasticity / Δ Compute Sovereignty) * ∇ RWA Liquidity Gap`

### 3. LOCK: The Decision Gate
Upon an NSRC signal, the following automated actions are executed:
*   **[Asset Reallocation]**: Move 15% of the risk budget into "Compute-Resilient" assets (e.g., companies with vertically integrated chip-to-mineral supply chains).
*   **[Agentic Reconfiguration]**: Deploy high-autonomy neuromorphic agents to manage liquidity defense in tokenized asset markets to mitigate the "Settlement Gap" risks.
*   **[Strategic Hedging]**: Long positions on critical mineral futures and sovereign compute-as-a-service providers.

## Strategic Value
The NSRC provides an early-warning system for the next era of the global economy: where intelligence is no longer a software layer, but a physical, sovereign, and liquid commodity. It bridges the gap between hardware evolution, geopolitical shifts, and the restructuring of global finance.
