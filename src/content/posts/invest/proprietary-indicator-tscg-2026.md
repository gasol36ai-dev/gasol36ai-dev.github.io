## 1. Overview
The **Tri-Sovereign Convergence Gate (TSCG-26)** is a cross-domain multi-vector indicator designed to detect the non-linear step-function transition from centralized/terrestrial-dependent systems to a distributed/sovereign-autonomous multi-substrate state.

It monitors the simultaneous convergence of three critical sovereignty vectors: **Cryptographic Autonomy**, **Physical Autonomy**, and **Orbital Autonomy**.

## 2. The Three Vectors

### Vector A: Cryptographic Autonomy (The Digital Substrate)
*   **Core Metric:** Sovereign PQC-Transition Index (SPTI).
*   **Focus:** Adoption of post-quantum algorithms (Lattice/Hash-based) in central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) and national settlement rails, alongside the deployment of domestic Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) networks.
*   **Critical Threshold:** >75% of high-value settlement volume secured via hybrid PQC/QKD.

### Vector B: Physical Autonomy (The Morphological Substrate)
*   **Core Metric:** Embodied Edge-Latency Coherence (EELC).
*   **Focus:** The ability of embodied agent swarms to operate with <10ms control-loop latency using 3D-native spatial world models (Genie/PanoWorld) and morphological intelligence (mechanical compliance as pre-computation).
*   **Critical Threshold:** Continuous operation of agentic swarms in unstructured environments with zero reliance on centralized cloud-inferencing.

### Vector C: Orbital Autonomy (The Infrastructure Substrate)
*   **Core Metric:** Orbital DePIN Deployment Density (ODDD).
*   **Focus:** The scaling of LEO-based Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN) providing distributed compute, storage, and edge-intelligence nodes that bypass terrestrial fiber/satellite dependencies.
*   **Critical Threshold:** >40% of orbital compute demand met via decentralized/fractionalized LEO-DePIN protocols.

The TSCG-26 activates when all three vectors cross their respective critical thresholds within a single rolling 18-month window. This convergence triggers a **non-linear cascade of sovereign premium across bonds, FX, and defense procurement.**
