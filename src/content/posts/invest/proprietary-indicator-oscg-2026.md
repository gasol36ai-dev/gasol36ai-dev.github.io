# Orbital-Swarm Convergence Gate (OSCG) (HPI-31)

## Overview
The **Orbital-Swarm Convergence Gate (OSCG)** is a proprietary judgment indicator designed to identify the non-linear inflection point where decentralized, space-based infrastructure (S-DePIN) and autonomous swarm-based manufacturing (OAM/ASL) converge to create a self-sustaining orbital economy.

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 1. MAP: The Convergence Variables
The indicator synthesizes three distinct domain vectors to measure the tension of the convergence:

*   **$\text{OAM}_{\text{cap}}$ (Orbital Additive Manufacturing Capacity)**: Measured by the ratio of in-space produced mass vs. Earth-launched mass for modular components.
*   **$\text{S-DePIN}_{\text{dens}}$ (S-DePIN Network Density)**: Measured by the number of active, community-owned orbital compute/storage nodes per $10^3$ km of LEO/MEO orbit.
*   **$\text{ASL}_{\text{swarm}}$ (Autonomous Swarm Coordination Index)**: Measured by the reduction in latency for multi-agent consensus in unstructured orbital/lunar environments (e.g., swarm-to-swarm handshakes).

**Composite Formula:**
$$\text{OSCG} = \frac{\text{OAM}_{\text{cap}} \times \text{S-DePIN}_{\text{dens}}}{\text{ASL}_{\text{swarm}}$$

### 2. TRIGGER: The Inflection Event
The signal is triggered when a simultaneous threshold breach occurs across all three vectors, indicating that the "bottleneck" is shifting from *launch capacity* to *orbital coordination*:

*   **$\text{OAM}_{\text{cap}} > 1.5\sigma$**: In-space manufacturing produces enough modular volume to support swarm deployment without Earth-reliant logistics.
*   **$\text{S-DePIN}_{\text{dens}} > 2.0\sigma$**: Orbital compute density reaches a level where swarm intelligence can be processed locally (in-orbit) rather than via high-latency downlinks.
*   **$\text{ASL}_{\text{swarm}} < 0.5\sigma$**: Swarm coordination latency drops below the threshold required for real-time, autonomous orbital assembly.

### 3. LOCK: Strategic Action
Upon a positive OSCG signal, the following "Lock" mechanism is engaged in capital and strategic allocation:

*   **Capital Pivot**: Automatic increase in exposure to **Orbital-Native Infrastructure** (S-DePIN nodes, Space-based Power, Orbital Logistics) and a reduction in **Terrestrial-Launch-Dependent** assets.
*   **Operational Shift**: Transition from "Mission-Specific Deployment" to "Continuous Orbital Expansion" mode, prioritizing assets with high autonomy and low Earth-reliance.

## Strategic Value
The OSCG provides an early warning for the transition from the **"Launch Era"** (dominated by terrestrial-based gravity wells) to the **"Orbital Autonomy Era"** (dominated by self-sustaining, swarm-integrated space economies). It identifies the moment when space becomes a *production* environment rather than just a *destination*.

## Historical Validation & Trends
*   **Pre-Convergence**: High launch costs, centralized satellite control, single-agent missions.
*   **Post-Convergence**: Low-cost in-space assembly, decentralized orbital networks, multi-agent swarm logistics.
