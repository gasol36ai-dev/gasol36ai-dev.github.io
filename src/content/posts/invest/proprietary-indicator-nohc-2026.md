# Neuromorphic-Optical Hybrid Computing (NOHC) Indicator

## Overview
The NOHC Indicator measures the convergence efficiency between neuromorphic (spiking) processing and photonic (light-based) interconnects, a critical metric for next-generation AI-native infrastructure.

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### MAP (Convergence Quadrant)
* **$\text{Spike-Photon Coherence} (\chi)$**: The temporal alignment between electrical spike trains and optical pulse sequences.
* **$\text{Optical Interconnect Bandwidth} (B)$**: The capacity of the photonic layer to handle high-dimensional signal traffic.

### TRIGGER (Systemic Inflection)
Triggered when the **Hybrid Efficiency Ratio ($\eta$)** drops below a critical threshold:
$$\eta = \frac{\text{Effective Neuromorphic Throughput}}{\text{Optical Energy per Bit}} < \tau$$
Where $\tau$ is the baseline efficiency for a given compute density. A drop in $\eta$ indicates a "bottleneck" where the photonic layer can no longer support the spiking frequency of the neuromorphic substrate.

### LOCK (Strategic Response)
* **Low $\eta$ (Congestion)**: Divert compute to high-latency, low-bandwidth classical rails; increase optical clock frequency.
* **High $\eta$ (Alignment)**: Aggressively scale Neuromorphic-Optical clusters; maximize spike-density to exploit high-bandwidth optical channels.

## Transmission Mapping
**[Neuromorphic Spike]** $\rightarrow$ **[Optical Modulation]** $\rightarrow$ **[Photonic Propagation]** $\rightarrow$ **[Hybrid Convergence]**

1. **Spike $\rightarrow$ Modulation**:
    * *Mechanism*: Spike-to-Photon conversion via integrated micro-ring resonators.
    * *Transmission*: Conversion of electrical charge to phase/amplitude modulation.
2. **Modulation $\rightarrow$ Propagation**:
    * *Mechanism*: High-speed silicon photonics waveguiding.
    * *Transmission*: Signal transit through optical interconnect fabric.
3. **Propagation $\rightarrow$ Convergence**:
    * *Mechanism*: Photonic signal detection and spike-reconstruction at the receiving node.
    * *Transmission*: Light-to-spike transduction, ensuring temporal coherence.
