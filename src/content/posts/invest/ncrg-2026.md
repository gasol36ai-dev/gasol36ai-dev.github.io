# Proprietary Indicator: NCRG-2026 (Neuromorphic-Capital-Resource Gate)

It measures the simultaneous alignment of next-generation compute efficiency, the securitization of the physical resources required to build that compute, and the financial liquidity to scale it.

1. **Neuromorphic Computing (Mind):** The shift to Analog IMC and Event-Based Vision, reducing edge power consumption by 100-1000x.
2. **Critical Minerals (Body):** The establishment of Sovereign Mineral Reserves and "Friend-Shoring" refining capacity for NVM materials (e.g., HfO2, Gallium).
3. **RWA Tokenization (Blood):** The implementation of Atomic Settlement for mineral and compute-capacity futures, enabling real-time capital deployment into sovereign infrastructure.
