# Macro-to-Asset Transmission Mechanisms

## 🏛️ Monetary Policy & Interest Rates
|- **Fed Rate Cuts -> USD Liquidity up -> EM Equities up**: (Expected H1 2026).
|- **Real Yields down -> Gold up**: Traditional inverse relationship. *Note: Watch for decoupling in 2026 due to geopolitical risk and central bank gold hoarding.*
|- **Yield Curve Steepening -> Financial Sector Outperformance**: (US 10Y-2Y spread widening).
|- **USD Strength -> EM Pressure**: Strong USD increases dollar-denominated debt servicing costs and capital flight risk (FPI vs FDI).
|- **BoJ Normalization -> Carry Trade Unwind -> Global Equity Volatility**: Increasing BoJ rates reduces the attractiveness of JPY-denominated carry trades.
|- **Policy Divergence (Fed/ECB/BoJ) -> FX Volatility up -> Global Capital Flow shifts**: (High impact in 2026).

## 💸 Inflation & Fiscal Policy
|- **Tariff Pass-through -> CPI up**: Estimated ~0.3ppt per 10ppt tariff increase.
|- **Tax Cuts (Fiscal Stimulus) -> Real GDP up (Short-term) / Inflation up (Mid-term)**.
|- **Energy-Driven Structural Inflation -> Real Yields up -> Valuation Compression**: (High impact in 2026).

## 🌍 Geopolitics & Commodities
|- **Energy Shocks (e.g., Middle East) -> Oil Prices up -> Inflation up -> Central Bank Hawkishness up**.
|- **Strategic Autonomy (EU) -> Defense Spending up -> Industrial/Defense Sector up**.
|- **Geopolitical Risk/Sovereign Risk -> Gold up (Systemic Insurance)**: (Decoupled from Real Yields in 2026).

## 🤖 Technology & Productivity
|- **AI Capex -> Cloud/Data Infrastructure up**: High correlation with large-cap tech performance.
|- **AI Power/Grid Bottleneck -> Energy/Utility Infrastructure up**: Shift from compute to power as scaling constraint.
|- **AI Labor Displacement -> Structural Unemployment Risks -> Social/Fiscal Policy Shifts**.
