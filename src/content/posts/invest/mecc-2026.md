# MECC-2026 (Materials-Energy-Capital Convergence Gate)

**Definition**: Convergence indicator tracking the three upstream physical-economy constraints on AI/robotics scale-out: agentic-AI-accelerated critical minerals recovery, behind-the-meter (BYOC) power procurement, and sovereign capital-allocation concentration (e.g., China's embodied-AI financing surge).

**Key value**: First registry indicator to connect DOE's Genesis Mission (checkable federal program, 278 awards from 5,000+ applicants) to both the energy-buildout thread (8 sourced BYOC deals) and the capital-concentration thread (Unitree's disclosed 2:1 model-vs-hardware R&D funding ratio).

**Status (2026-08-11)**: Materials condition NOT MET (Phase I only); Energy condition PARTIALLY MET (signed deals, no confirmed first-power yet); Capital condition MET (Unitree ratio disclosed). Materials is currently the binding constraint.

**Full indicator**: See `research/Proprietary_Indicators_2026.md#materials-energy-capital-convergence-gate-mecc-2026`.

**Convergence**: Complements SNPK-2026 (technology stack: Sensing x Acting x Sustaining) by covering the supply-side physical-economy inputs instead.
