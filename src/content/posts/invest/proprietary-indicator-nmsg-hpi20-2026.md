## 1. Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### **Map (Multi-Domain Inputs)**
The NMSG indicator synthesizes signals from three distinct strategic layers to detect the emergence of a "Closed-Loop Sovereign Intelligence Stack" — a state where a nation can deploy human-AI hybrid capabilities with absolute hardware/data integrity and stealth.

- **Input A: $\\text{BW}_{neural}$ (Neural Bandwidth / BCI Integration)**
    - *Metric*: Average bits-per-second (bps) of stable, bi-directional neural data flow in deployed clinical/military systems.
    - *Threshold*: $\\text{BW}_{neural} > 10^5$ bps (enabling "thought-speed" interaction with LLMs/agents).
    - *Source*: BCI clinical trials, DARPA/China Brain Science Initiative reports.

- **Input B: $\\text{S}_{meta}$ (Metamaterial Stealth & Secure Comms)**
    - *Metric*: Level of integration of RIS (Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces) and acoustic/radar cloaking in strategic assets.
    - *Threshold*: Integration of active metamaterial "skins" on $\\ge 20\\%$ of strategic defense platforms (rendering them "ghosts" in traditional sensor spectra).
    - *Source*: Defense procurement budgets, satellite imagery of test facilities, metamaterial patent filings.

- **Input C: $\\text{P}_{prov}$ (Provenance Integrity / Tokenized Trust)**
    - *Metric*: Percentage of the "Neural-Material Stack" (chips, sensors, biocompatible polymers) tracked via immutable, tokenized provenance from mine-to-implant.
    - *Threshold*: $\\text{P}_{prov} \\approx 100\\%$ for critical components (zero-foreign-backdoor guarantee).
    - *Source*: EU DPP compliance logs, US UFLPA audit trails, custom provenance-as-a-service networks.

### **Trigger (The Signal)**
The NMSG indicator is flagged as **ACTIVE** when all three thresholds are crossed within a 36-month window:

$$\\text{NMSG} = (\\text{BW}_{neural} > 10^5) \\land (\\text{S}_{meta} \\ge 20\\%) \\land (\\text{P}_{prov} \\approx 100\\%)$$

**Logic**: When a state can communicate at thought-speed ($\\text{BW}$), hide the hardware from sensors ($\\text{S}$), and guarantee the hardware is not compromised by foreign agents ($\\text{P}$), it has achieved "Cognitive-Material Sovereignty."

### **Lock (Deterministic Reaction)**
Upon activation of NMSG for a target nation-state, the following strategic pivots are triggered:

- **Defense Pivot**: Immediate re-classification of "Neural-Material" assets as Tier-1 Strategic Assets. Shift from "Conventional Stealth" to "Cognitive-Material Stealth" doctrine.
- **Investment Pivot**: Long position in the "Sovereign Stack" providers (Biocompatible polymer firms, RIS antenna manufacturers, Provenance-as-a-Service protocols).
- **Risk Lock**: Short position on legacy "Centralized Trust" defense contractors who rely on foreign-sourced components or non-metamaterial platforms.
