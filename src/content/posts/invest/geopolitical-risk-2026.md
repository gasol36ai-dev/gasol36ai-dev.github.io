# Geopolitical Risk Analysis (April 2026)

## 🇮🇷 Iran & The Hormuz Shock
- **Event**: Ongoing conflict involving Iran and disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Direct Impact**: Brent Crude prices surged above $100/barrel (March 2026), the highest since 2022.
- **Transmission**: $\text{Energy Disruption} \rightarrow \text{Gas Prices} \rightarrow \text{Headline CPI Spike (3.3\%+)} \rightarrow \text{Yield Pressure}$.
- **Strategic Role**: Acts as the primary volatility catalyst for 2026.

## 🇷🇺 Russia & Ukraine
- **Event**: Persistent conflict with intermittent escalations.
- **Impact**: Continued fragmentation of energy markets and supply chain regionalization.
- **Strategic Role**: Reinforces the "Security Networks" paradigm and the shift toward resilient, localized capex.

## 🌏 Global Capital Flows
- **Safe Haven Shift**: Increased demand for Gold as a systemic hedge.
- **Risk Asset Rotation**: Some rotation into Emerging Markets (EM) as the USD eases relative to hawkish ECB/BoJ.
- **Flight to Quality**: Capital moving toward assets benefiting from the "AI Power Bottleneck" (Energy infrastructure, grid capacity).
