# Proprietary Indicator: Cognitive Sovereignty Convergence Gate (CSCG)

## 1. Logic Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock
The **Cognitive Sovereignty Convergence Gate (CSCG)** is a high-density proprietary indicator designed to detect the inflection point where a state achieves "Cognitive Autarky"—the ability to maintain advanced AI capabilities independent of globalized compute/energy supply chains.

### 1.1 The Map (Domain Convergence)
The CSCG synthesizes three distinct research domains into a single decision gate:
1. **Sovereign Organoid-Silicon Hybridization (SOSH)**: Measures the transition from energy-heavy GPUs to bio-silicon cognitive substrates.
2. **Sovereign LEO Compute Mesh (SLCM)**: Measures the decoupling of AI orchestration from terrestrial ground stations.
3. **Meta-Material Sovereign Defense (MMSD)**: Measures the physical-layer resilience of the cognitive core against EMP/Kinetic disruptions.

### 1.2 The Trigger (Convergence Threshold)
The "Gate" opens when all three vectors cross their respective **Sovereignty Thresholds** within a synchronized window:
- **$\text{SOSH Threshold}$**: $\text{SNN-Bio-Hybrid efficiency} \ge 10\times \text{H100 energy-per-token}$.
- **$\text{SLCM Threshold}$**: $\text{Mesh Latency} \le 50\text{ms}$ with $100\%$ terrestrial-independence for inference.
- **$\text{MMSD Threshold}$**: $\text{Phase-change response time} \le 1\text{ms}$ for total EM opacity.

**Trigger Formula**:
$$\text{CSCG State} = \begin{cases} \text{OPEN} & \text{if } (\text{SOSH} \land \text{SLCM} \land \text{MMSD}) \text{ are TRUE} \\ \text{CLOSED} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

### 1.3 The Lock (Sovereign Premium)
Once the Gate is OPEN, the result is a "Lock-in" of **Cognitive Sovereignty**. This triggers a non-linear re-valuation of the state's strategic assets:
- **Sovereign Premium**: A step-function increase in the value of the state's internal "Cognitive Reserve."
- **Risk Decoupling**: The state becomes immune to "Compute Sanctions" or "Energy Blockades," shifting its risk profile from *Dependent* to *Autarkic*.

## 3. Strategic Value
The CSCG provides a quantitative metric for **Cognitive Autarky**. For a technical director or strategist, a "CSCG OPEN" signal indicates that the state has successfully exited the "Compute Arms Race" (which is a resource-exhaustion game) and entered the "Sovereignty Era" (which is a structural-efficiency game).

**Confidence Level**: High (based on the convergence of biological, orbital, and material science breakthroughs).
