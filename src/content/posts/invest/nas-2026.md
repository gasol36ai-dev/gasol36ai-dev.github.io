# NAS-2026: Neuromorphic Autarky Synthesis Indicator

## 1. Executive Summary
The **NAS-2026 (Neuromorphic Autarky Synthesis)** is a high-density judgment indicator designed to detect the emergence of a "Sovereign Intelligent Economy." It identifies the convergent point where neuromorphic computing, resource autarky, and post-quantum financial rails synchronize to create a system capable of absolute strategic independence from globalized supply chains and traditional financial hegemony.

## 2. Strategic Domain Synthesis
The NAS-2026 indicator synthesizes three distinct strategic domains:
- **Neuromorphic Intelligence (NI):** Low-power, high-efficiency hardware enabling real-time, autonomous optimization of complex industrial systems at the edge.
- **Sovereign Resource Autarky (SRA):** The ability of a state or entity to secure and process critical materials (REEs, Lithium, Energy) without external reliance.
- **PQC Financial Rails (PQC-FR):** Financial infrastructure utilizing Post-Quantum Cryptography to prevent systemic collapse during the "Q-Day" transition.

## 3. Transmission Mapping (Map-Trigger-Lock)
The indicator tracks the transition from "Interdependent Complexity" to "Autonomous Convergence."

### Transmission Map:
`[Event]`: Deployment of neuromorphic edge-controllers in critical resource extraction and refining.
$\rightarrow$ `[Mechanism]`: Real-time, autonomous optimization of resource flows $\rightarrow$ drastic reduction in latency and energy costs $\rightarrow$ migration of settlement to PQC-secured internal rails.
$\rightarrow$ `[Reaction]`: Transition to "Cognitive Autarky"—a state where the economy is managed by a neuromorphic substrate that is immune to quantum-financial shocks and external supply disruptions.

## 4. Mathematical Formalization
The trigger condition $\Theta$ is defined as a non-linear ratio of systemic capability to systemic vulnerability.

$$\Theta = \frac{\int_{t_0}^{t_1} (N_{eff} \cdot R_{aut}) \, dt}{P_{vuln} + \epsilon}$$

**Variables:**
- $N_{eff}$ (**Neuromorphic Efficiency**): The ratio of operational throughput per watt relative to traditional von Neumann architectures in resource-management tasks.
- $R_{aut}$ (**Resource Autarky Ratio**): $\frac{\text{Internal Production}}{\text{Total Strategic Demand}}$.
- $P_{vuln}$ (**Quantum Vulnerability Index**): The percentage of the financial rail utilizing pre-quantum (RSA/ECC) encryption.
- $\epsilon$: A small positive stability constant to prevent division by zero.

**Trigger Condition:**
The "Convergent Gate" is considered **OPEN** when:
$$\Theta > \Gamma_{crit}$$
Where $\Gamma_{crit}$ is the empirical threshold at which the cost of maintaining external dependencies exceeds the cost of autonomous neuromorphic management.

## 5. Lock State: The Sovereign Intelligent Economy
When $\Theta > \Gamma_{crit}$, the system enters the **LOCK** state. In this state, the entity has achieved "Sovereign Intelligence Convergence." 
- **Characteristic A:** Resource loops are closed and optimized in real-time via NI.
- **Characteristic B:** Financial rails are quantum-hardened, removing the leverage of external financial sanctions.
- **Characteristic C:** The system becomes a "Black Box" to external observers, as internal optimization logic is decoupled from global market signals.

## 6. Observation Metrics for Intelligence Gathering
To estimate $\Theta$, observers should track:
1. **Hardware Procurement:** Shift from GPU clusters to neuromorphic chips (e.g., Loihi-class or proprietary spikes) in industrial sectors.
2. **Mine-to-Market Integration:** Direct integration of AI controllers into automated mining and refining facilities.
3. **Rail Migration:** Adoption of NIST-standardized PQC algorithms in sovereign settlement systems.
