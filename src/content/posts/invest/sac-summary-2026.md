# SAC Gate: High-Density Summary

**Indicator Name**: Sovereign Autarky Convergence (SAC) Gate
**HPI Classification**: HPI-42 (Hypothetical)
**Status**: Synthesized (2026-06-24)

### 🚀 Core Concept
The SAC Gate detects the emergence of "Hyper-Sovereign" entities that have successfully decoupled their operational capabilities from external, vulnerable global infrastructures (Grids, Networks, and Legacy Financial Systems).

### 📐 The Triad of Autarky
1. **Cognitive Autarky**: Neuromorphic-edge intelligence that scales with local energy availability.
2. **Energetic Autarky**: SMR-based power that eliminates dependence on fragile, centralized macro-grids.
3. **Financial Autarky**: PQC-secured RWA settlement that prevents quantum-enabled asset seizure or manipulation.

### 📉 Trigger Condition
$$\\text{SAC\\_Active} = (\\text{Intel}_{edge} > \\text{Crit}_I) \\cap (\\text{Energy}_{local} > \\text{Crit}_E) \\cap (\\text{Security}_{PQC} = \\text{True})$$

### 🌍 Strategic Value
Provides a single, actionable signal for identifying entities that are transitioning from "participants" in the global system to "autonomous nodes" capable of surviving and thriving during regime shifts.
