# Judgment Indicator: Map-Trigger-Lock (MTL)

## Overview
The **Map-Trigger-Lock (MTL)** framework is a proprietary judgment indicator designed for high-stakes decision-making in non-linear, complex systems (e.g., fragmented macro regimes, emerging tech cycles, or adversarial market microstructures). 

Unlike traditional probabilistic forecasting, which seeks to predict *when* an event will happen, MTL focuses on *how to react* once a specific state-transition is confirmed, eliminating the "hesitation gap" that erodes alpha.

## The Three Pillars of MTL

### 1. The Map (State Space Definition)
The "Map" is a rigorous definition of the current and potential future states of the system. 
- **Landscape Analysis**: Identifying all possible regimes (e.g., "Bullish/Liquid", "Bearish/Liquid", "Bullish/Illiquid", "Bearish/Illiquid").
- **Boundary Mapping**: Defining the precise parameters that separate these regimes. 
- **Avoidance of "The Middle"**: MTL rejects "neutral" or "moderate" stances. The Map forces the analyst to categorize the environment into distinct, actionable states.

### 2. The Trigger (Event Confirmation)
The "Trigger" is a binary, non-discretionary signal that indicates a transition from one mapped state to another.
- **Hard Constraints**: A trigger must be based on observable, verifiable data (e.g., "Real yields cross 2.5% AND Core PPI exceeds 0.4% MoM").
- **Filter Logic**: Triggers must be filtered for noise. A single data point is rarely a trigger; a *cluster* of correlated signals is required.
- **Zero-Discretion**: Once the trigger is hit, the decision is already made. The trigger exists to remove human emotion and cognitive bias from the execution phase.

### 3. The Lock (Committed Execution)
The "Lock" is the pre-defined action that is executed immediately upon the trigger.
- **Asymmetric Positioning**: The Lock is designed to capture the maximum convexity of the regime shift.
- **Hedge Integration**: The Lock includes not just the primary bet, but the necessary hedges to protect against "False Triggers."
- **Execution Lock-in**: This is the commitment to a specific trade, investment, or strategic pivot. There is no "re-evaluating" after the trigger—only executing the pre-mapped Lock.

## Application Example: Macro Regime Shift
- **Map**: 
    - State A: Low Inflation / Stable Growth.
    - State B: High Inflation / Stagnant Growth (Stagflation).
- **Trigger**: (US 10Y Real Yield > 2.2%) $\cap$ (CPI-U > 4% for 2 consecutive months) $\cap$ (GDP Growth < 1%).
- **Lock**: 
    - Long TIPS (Inflation Protection).
    - Short Long-Duration Treasuries (Yield Protection).
    - Long Commodities/Gold (Hard Asset Rotation).

## Performance Advantage
The MTL framework provides three primary advantages over standard analysis:
1. **Speed**: Reduces the time from *Signal* $\rightarrow$ *Action* by eliminating the deliberation phase.
2. **Consistency**: Ensures that the same signal always leads to the same action, regardless of the trader's psychological state.
3. **Clarity**: Forces the analyst to be honest about what they actually believe is the "trigger" for a regime change, exposing vague theses.
