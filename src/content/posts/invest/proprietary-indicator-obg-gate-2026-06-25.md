# Proprietary Indicator: The Orbital-Bio-Governance (OBG) Gate
**Version**: 1.0
**Date**: 2026-06-25
**Status**: Active / Synthesized

## 1. Concept & Convergence Logic
The OBG Gate identifies the inflection point where an entity achieves a "Closed-Loop Sovereign Cycle" across three disparate domains: **Orbital Edge Infrastructure**, **Bio-Digital Finance**, and **Neuro-Symbolic Law**. 

When these three vectors align, the entity moves from "Terrestrial Dependency" to "Hyper-Sovereign Autarky," where its financial assets are biological, its governance is algorithmic, and its infrastructure is orbital.

### Convergence Vectors:
1. **Orbital Edge (Infrastructure)**: Provides the low-latency compute mesh for real-time orbital manufacturing and data-governance.
2. **Bio-Digital Finance (Capital)**: Provides the biological collateral (Bio-Sovereign Bonds) that decouples the entity from terrestrial fiat debt.
3. **Neuro-Symbolic Law (Governance)**: Provides the formal verification of the "Orbital Constitution," ensuring that the orbital assets and bio-capital are managed via deterministic, non-corruptible algorithmic justice.

## 2. Transmission Mapping (The OBG Loop)
`[Event: Orbital-Bio-Synthesis Threshold Crossed] -> [Mechanism: Sovereign Autarky Cascade] -> [Reaction: Hyper-Sovereign Premium]`

### Detailed Transmission:
- **Event**: An entity successfully deploys an Orbital Edge Mesh capable of synthesizing complex bio-assets in zero-G, while simultaneously issuing Bio-Sovereign Bonds verified by a Neuro-Symbolic governance layer.
- **Mechanism**: The "Sovereign Autarky Cascade." The biological assets produced in orbit are used as collateral for the bio-bonds. The bonds fund the expansion of the orbital mesh. The Neuro-Symbolic law ensures the entire cycle is formally verified and secure from terrestrial interference or legal seizure.
- **Reaction**: The "Hyper-Sovereign Premium." The entity's credit rating and economic power decouple from terrestrial GDP and are instead linked to its "Sovereign Autarky Ratio." This creates a step-function increase in geopolitical influence and economic resilience.
