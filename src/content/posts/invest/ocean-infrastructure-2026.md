## Overview
The "Blue Economy" is being revolutionized by the integration of **Subsea Data Infrastructure** with **Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC)**.

## Key Paradigms
- **Subsea Edge Data Centers**: Leveraging deep-ocean pressure and temperature for passive, high-density cooling.
- **OTEC Integration**: Using temperature differentials in tropical waters to provide continuous, carbon-neutral power for subsea nodes.
- **Autonomous Subsea Monitoring (ASM)**: AUV fleets providing real-time security for subsea fiber cables.

## Critical Metrics
1. **Ocean-Edge Connectivity (OEC)**: Bandwidth density per square km of seabed.
2. **Thermal Energy Conversion Efficiency (TCE)**: Net power output from OTEC modules relative to thermal delta.
