# Sovereign Kinetic Reflex (SKR-2026) Indicator
## Executive Summary
The Sovereign Kinetic Reflex (SKR-2026) is a convergent judgment indicator that signals the transition of Physical AI from "Centralized Planning" to "Hardware-Native Reflexes." It occurs when high-bandwidth sensing, soft-actuation, and thermal-sovereign compute converge into a single closed-loop reflexive system.

The SKR-2026 is triggered when the following three domain thresholds are crossed simultaneously:

1. **Sensing (Neuromorphic Reflex)**: $\text{Latency}_{\text{sense}} < 1\text{ms}$ via asynchronous event-based vision and tactile arrays.
2. **Acting (Morphological Agility)**: $\text{Strain}_{\text{max}} > 50\%$ with integrated proprioception via liquid metal actuators.
3. **Sustaining (Thermal Sovereignty)**: $\text{T}_{\text{peak}} < \text{T}_{\text{throttle}}$ under max load via Diamond WBG substrates.

`[External Perturbation] -> [Asynchronous Spike Pattern (Sensing)] -> [Local Morphological Adjustment (Acting)] -> [Thermal Stability Maintenance (Sustaining)] -> [Sovereign Kinetic State]`

## Core Logic & Formalization
The SKR-2026 is **ACTIVE** when:
$\text{SKR}_{2026} = (\text{Sensing}_{\text{neuro}} \land \text{Acting}_{\text{soft}} \land \text{Sustaining}_{\text{diamond}}) \land (\text{Latency}_{\text{total}} < 10\text{ms})$

The emergence of SKR-2026 removes the "Computational Bottleneck" of humanoid robots. It enables agents to survive and operate in high-entropy, high-velocity environments (e.g., disaster recovery, orbital debris capture) where traditional AI control loops are too slow.
