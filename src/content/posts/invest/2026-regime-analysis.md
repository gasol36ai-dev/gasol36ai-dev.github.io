# 2026 Macro Regime Analysis

## Current Regime: "The Great Reset of Trust & Tech"

The 2026 environment is characterized by the intersection of two divergent forces: systemic instability and unprecedented technological scaling.

### 1. The Systemic Instability Pillar
- **Geopolitical Hegemony in Flux**: With geopolitical risk in the 90th percentile, the world has moved from "economic optimization" to "security optimization."
- **US Trust Failure**: Moody's credit rating concerns and political pressure on the Fed have introduced a permanent risk premium into US Treasuries.
- **Energy as a Weapon**: The Iran conflict ensures that energy prices are no longer just economic variables but geopolitical tools, leading to "sticky" inflation.

### 2. The Technological Scaling Pillar
- **The AI Agent Era**: Transition from LLM "Chatbots" to "AI Agents" that execute tasks. This is driving a massive shift in Capex from GPUs (Compute) to Power/Grid infrastructure (Energy).
- **Capex Supercycle**: CSPs are projecting \$716B in spending. This creates a "bottom-up" growth engine that can partially offset "top-down" macro headwinds.

## Strategic Synthesis
In this regime, the "Golden Rule" is **Resilience over Efficiency**.
- **Core Holding**: AI Infrastructure (Compute $\rightarrow$ Power).
- **Hedge**: Gold (Systemic Trust) and Defense Stocks.
- **Risk**: High-duration growth assets that lack real-world cash flow or are overly sensitive to the 4.5% yield threshold.
