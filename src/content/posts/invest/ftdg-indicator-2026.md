# Proprietary Indicator: Fiscal-Technology Divergence Gate (FTDG)

**Date**: 2026-05-27
**Status**: Formalized
**Framework**: Map-Trigger-Lock
**Quality**: 3-Domain Synthesis (Sovereign Debt + AI Hardware + Biological Computing)

## Overview
**Fiscal-Technology Divergence Gate (FTDG)** is a macro-strategic judgment indicator that identifies windows where **sovereign fiscal stress** forces accelerated **technology platform transitions** — specifically, when governments and large enterprises are compelled to adopt radically more efficient AI hardware paradigms (ASICs, NPUs, biological computing) due to the cost pressure of rising long-term yields.

The core thesis: **Fiscal dominance doesn't just affect bonds — it propagates into technology capex allocation.** When the cost of capital rises due to sovereign credit degradation, the efficiency premium of specialized AI accelerators (vs. GPU-based infrastructure) becomes a balance sheet imperative, not just an engineering preference.

## Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 1. Map (Structural Context — Cross-Domain)

Three forces interact to create the FTDG condition:

**Domain A (Sovereign Debt)**: US term premium expansion due to fiscal dominance (Moody's Aa1, $2T/yr deficits). Rising 30y Treasury yields → higher cost of capital for all enterprise technology debt.

**Domain B (AI Hardware)**: Heterogeneous accelerator ecosystem reaching **cost-efficiency critical mass**: Groq LPU (10x speed, 1/10th energy vs GPU), Hailo edge ASIC ($50/module vs $30K GPU), Cerebras WSE (20x training speed). The TCO gap between GPU-first and ASIC-first stacks is now **3-5x over 3-year horizon**.

**Domain C (Biological Computing)**: Wetware-as-a-service emergence signals that 100,000× energy efficiency improvements are on a 5-10 year horizon, creating **option value** on post-silicon transitions.

### 2. Trigger (Operational Signal)

An **FTDG Signal** is triggered when THREE conditions align simultaneously:

```
FTDG Trigger ⟺ [Rising Term Premium (30y > 4.8%)] 
              AND [ASIC/GPU cost-efficiency gap > 3x on inference TCO]
              AND [Hyperscaler CapEx guidance showing ASIC/custom chip > 40% of new AI spend]
```

**In plain terms**: When the bond market is punishing fiscal excess (Domain A), and simultaneously the hardware market is showing that non-GPU infrastructure is now 3x+ cheaper per inference (Domain B), enterprise technology migration from GPU-first stacks accelerates sharply — compressing the transition cycle from 5 years to 2 years.

### 3. Lock (Strategic Response)

Upon FTDG Signal detection:

**Protocol A — Infrastructure Sector Rotation**:
- **Reduce**: Exposure to GPU-dependent hyperscaler CapEx narratives (pure NVIDIA-dependent data center REITs)
- **Increase**: Exposure to specialized inference infrastructure (companies enabling ASIC/LPU deployment)

**Protocol B — Technology Investment Screening**:
- Any AI infrastructure investment must pass **"Fiscal-Technology Efficiency Test"**: Does it achieve >10x energy efficiency vs GPU baseline? If not, it faces rising cost-of-capital headwinds.

**Protocol C — Biocomputing Optionality**:
- Allocate small research budget to monitor wetware organoid lifespan crossover (signal: >12 months survivability). This represents the **"Domain C unlock"** that would trigger the next generation FTDG signal.

## Historical Validation (Analogical)

**Prior Instance: 2011 S&P Downgrade + Cloud Migration**
- S&P downgraded US to AA+ (2011) → rising borrowing costs → enterprises accelerated shift from CapEx (owned servers) to OpEx (cloud). The fiscal stress catalyzed a platform transition.
- FTDG formalizes this isomorphism: **sovereign credit stress → efficiency-driven platform migration**.

**Current Instance: 2025 Moody's Downgrade + ASIC Migration**
- Moody's Aa1 (May 2025) → term premium at 108bps → enterprise AI infrastructure cost scrutiny → ASIC/LPU adoption inflection.

## Strategic Value

- **Synthesizes ≥ 3 distinct domains**: Fiscal macro + semiconductor architecture + post-silicon computing optionality
- **Prevents single-domain tunnel vision**: GPU infrastructure investment analysis cannot be separated from macro cost-of-capital environment
- **Time horizon**: Tactical (12-18 months) via Protocol A/B; strategic (5-10 years) via Protocol C
- **Distinguishes signal from noise**: Term premium < 4.8% on 30y = FTDG inactive; above = monitor Domain B trigger conditions
