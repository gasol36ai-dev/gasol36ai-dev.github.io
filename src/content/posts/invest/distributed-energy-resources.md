# Distributed Energy Resources (DERs)

## Definition
Distributed Energy Resources (DERs) are small-scale energy generation or storage technologies located close to the load they serve. Unlike traditional centralized power plants, DERs are dispersed throughout the grid.

## Key Technologies
- **Solar PV**: Rooftop and community solar arrays.
- **Wind Turbines**: Small-scale urban or rural wind installations.
- **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)**: Lithium-ion, Flow batteries, and Sodium-sulfur.
- **Combined Heat and Power (CHP)**: Co-generation plants that produce electricity and useful heat.
- **Electric Vehicles (EVs)**: Utilizing V2G (Vehicle-to-Grid) for bidirectional energy flow.

## Role in Energy Sovereignty
DERs enable the "prosumer" model, where users produce their own energy, reducing reliance on the central grid and increasing resilience against large-scale outages.

## Interdependencies
- Requires **Microgrid Control Systems** for synchronization.
- Integrates with **Virtual Power Plants (VPP)** for market participation.
