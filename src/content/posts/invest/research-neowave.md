# NEoWave

NEoWave is an advanced evolution of Elliott Wave Theory. While Elliott Wave is often criticized for subjectivity, NEoWave introduces a more rigorous, rule-based framework for analyzing market cycles.

## Core Principles
- **Rule-Based Structure**: Replaces "guidelines" with strict rules. If a rule is broken, the wave count is invalidated.
- **Complexity Analysis**: Focuses on the *complexity* of corrective patterns to predict the magnitude of the subsequent trend.
- **Poly-wave Analysis**: Analyzing multiple timeframes simultaneously to find confluence.

## Key Patterns
- **Double Corrective Structures**: Complex sideways movements that signal a deep accumulation/distribution phase.
- **Waterfall Declines**: Sharp, gap-based drops where each leg is equal in magnitude, indicating extreme panic.

## Strategic Value
Provides a deterministic way to identify the "end of a correction" and the start of a new primary trend.
