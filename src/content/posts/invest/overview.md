# Macro Economics Overview (May 2026)

## 🌍 Current Global Regime: The Great Divergence & Geopolitical Primacy

The macroeconomic landscape in May 2026 is characterized by a shift from synchronized monetary policy to a regime of **Central Bank Divergence** and a transition where **Geopolitical Risk** has overtaken pure economic data as the primary driver of global capital flows.

### 1. Central Bank Policy Matrix
| Bank | Current Rate / Bias | Strategy | Key Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fed** | 3.50% - 3.75% (Hold) | Data-Dependent Pause | Core Inflation Persistence |
| **ECB** | ~2.00% (Hike Bias) | Aggressive Normalization | Headline Inflation Stability |
| **BoJ** | 0.75% (Hold/Easy) | Gradual Normalization | Stimulating Domestic Demand |

**Key Theme**: The divergence between a holding Fed and a hiking ECB creates volatility in the EUR/USD pair and influences global carry trades.

### 2. Inflation & Price Dynamics
- **US CPI (March 2026)**: Jumped to **3.3%** (from 2.4% in Feb), primarily driven by energy price surges.
- **US PCE**: Steady at **2.8% - 2.9%**, creating a "Gauge Divergence" that complicates Fed signaling.
- **Global Outlook**: Core CPI expected to average ~2.8% globally, with the US remaining a sticky outlier at ~3.2%.

### 3. Sovereign Bond Yields & Curve
- **US 10Y Treasury**: ~4.35%
- **US 2Y Treasury**: ~3.81%
- **10Y-2Y Spread**: **+0.53% to +0.54%** (Positive/Normalizing).
- **Regime Shift**: The curve has moved from a deep inversion (recession fear) to a "Normalization Test" phase. This typically signals a transition toward an inflation/term-premium regime, favoring infrastructure and energy over long-duration growth.

### 4. Geopolitical Risk Map
- **Top Risk**: Geopolitical instability (West Asia, Trade Frictions) now ranks higher than macroeconomic risks.
- **Transmission**: Geopolitical shocks $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Energy Prices $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Headline Inflation $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Nominal Yields $\rightarrow$ $\downarrow$ Valuation Compression for Tech.
- **Capital Flows**: Flight to quality (USD) persists during acute shocks, but structural "De-dollarization" (Central Bank gold hoarding) creates a floor for precious metals.
