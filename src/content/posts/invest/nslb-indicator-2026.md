**Status**: PROPOSED (Synthesized)  
**Category**: Cross-Domain (Neuromorphic / Macro / Agentic)

2.  **$\Delta_{spread}$ (Sovereign Spread Delta)**: The widening of bid-ask spreads in Tier-1 sovereign bonds (e.g., US Treasuries, German Bunds).
3.  **$\Gamma_{agent}$ (Agentic Quorum Consensus Score)**: A metric measuring the stability of the "Agentic Governance" layer (AQCS) — specifically the degree of consensus among multi-agent liquidity providers in maintaining market-making obligations.

## ⚡ Trigger (The Inflection Point)
The NSLB signal fires when:
$$\text{Signal} = \left( \frac{\Psi_{neu} \cdot \Delta_{spread}}{\Gamma_{agent}} \right) > \Theta_{threshold}$$

**Where:**
- **$\Psi_{neu} \uparrow$**: Rapid, non-linear spikes in event-driven order flow.
- **$\Delta_{spread} \uparrow$**: Deteriorating liquidity in core sovereign instruments.
- **$\Gamma_{agent} \downarrow$**: Breakdown in multi-agent consensus/collaboration (e.g., agents entering "defensive/withdrawing" mode).

1.  **Risk Budget Compression**: Immediate 40% reduction in exposure to high-beta sovereign assets for all agent-managed portfolios.
2.  **Agentic Liquidity Injection**: Activation of pre-authorized "Stabilization Agents" tasked with executing atomic, cross-layer liquidity injections through CBDC-enabled rails to dampen the $\Delta_{spread}$ spike.
3.  **Governance Alert**: Formal escalation to the "Sovereign Infrastructure Convergence Gate" to assess if the cascade is breaching macro-stability thresholds.
