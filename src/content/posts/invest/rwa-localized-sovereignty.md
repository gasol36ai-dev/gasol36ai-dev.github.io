# RWA Tokenization & Localized Capital Sovereignty

## Executive Summary
The core thesis is that by tokenizing local assets (land, machinery, energy grids, local produce) and managing them via AI agents, communities can create **Hyper-Liquid Local Credit Markets**. This transforms "dead capital" into active, programmable liquidity that remains within the local ecosystem, thereby insulating the community from global volatility (e.g., USD interest rate hikes or global credit crunches).
