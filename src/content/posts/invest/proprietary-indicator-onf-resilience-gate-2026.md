## 🔢 Mathematical Formalization

The ONF Resilience Score ($\Omega_{ONF}$) is defined as the product of the three domain stability vectors:

$$\Omega_{ONF} = \left( \Phi_{Orbital} \cdot \Gamma_{Neuromorphic} \right) \otimes \Lambda_{Synthetic}$$

1.  **$\Phi_{Orbital}$ (Orbital Connectivity & Compute Vector)**:
    $$\Phi_{Orbital} = \frac{B_{available}}{B_{demand}} \cdot \left( \frac{C_{orbital}}{C_{terrestrial}} \right)$$
    *   $B$: Available bandwidth in LEO/MEO constellations.
    *   $C$: Compute capacity (TOPS) available in orbital edge nodes.

2.  **$\Gamma_{Neuromorphic}$ (Neuromorphic Intelligence Density Vector)**:
    $$\Gamma_{Neuromorphic} = \int_{t_0}^{t_1} \frac{\text{Inference}_{event}}{\text{Power}_{avg}} \,dt$$
    *   Measures the energy-efficient intelligence throughput of edge-deployed SNNs (Spiking Neural Networks) during high-flux event periods.

3.  **$\Lambda_{Synthetic}$ (Synthetic Liquidity Resilience Vector)**:
    $$\Lambda_{Synthetic} = \frac{\sum \text{RWA\_TVL}}{\text{Systemic\_Volatility}_{macro}}$$
    *   Measures the depth of RWA-linked synthetic liquidity relative to the current macro-volatility index.

**Trigger Condition (The Gate)**:
$$\text{ONF}_{Active} = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } \Omega_{ONF} > \sigma_{sovereignty} \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
*   $\sigma_{sovereignty}$ is the threshold for maintaining "Zero-Steering" autonomy.
