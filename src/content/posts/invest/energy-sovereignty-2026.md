# Domain: Next-Gen Energy Sovereignty
## Strategic Breakthroughs
The shift toward Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) and compact fusion is decoupling industrial growth from centralized grid vulnerabilities. Recent breakthroughs in high-temperature superconducting (HTS) magnets have reduced fusion reactor volumes, making "micro-fusion" a viable strategic objective for 2026-2030.

## Transmission Mappings
`[Decentralized Baseload] -> [Infrastructure Resilience] -> [Industrial Autonomy]`
1. **[Event]**: Commercial deployment of Gen-IV SMRs with passive safety systems.
2. **[Mechanism]**: Elimination of large-scale evacuation zones and reduction of water-dependency, allowing placement near compute clusters or industrial hubs.
3. **[Reaction]**: Immunity to wide-area grid collapses and a significant reduction in the LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy) for sovereign AI data centers.

## Conclusion
Energy sovereignty is the "Sustaining" pillar for any compute-heavy sovereign capability.
