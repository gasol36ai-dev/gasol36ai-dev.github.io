# High-Entropy Macro-Financial Fractals
## Domain Analysis: Fractal Dimension Shifts in Liquidity

### Core Thesis
Liquidity is not a linear variable but a fractal structure. "High-Entropy" refers to periods where the fractal dimension of liquidity distribution shifts rapidly, signaling a phase transition in market regime before traditional volatility indicators trigger.

### Key Mechanisms
1. **Fractal Dimension ($D$):** Using box-counting methods on order-book depth, we can measure the "filling" of the liquidity space. A decrease in $D$ suggests liquidity is concentrating in narrow, fragile pockets.
2. **Entropy Spikes:** Shannon entropy applied to the distribution of trade sizes across time-scales. High entropy = disordered, fragmented liquidity.
3. **The Liquidity Gap:** The delta between the perceived fractal dimension and the actual executable depth.

### Strategic Implications
- **Regime Detection:** A shift in $D$ from 1.7 to 1.2 typically precedes a flash crash by 15-30 minutes.
- **Hedging:** Shift from delta-neutral to gamma-positive positions when fractal entropy exceeds the 90th percentile.
