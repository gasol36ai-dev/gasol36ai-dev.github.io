# Proprietary Indicator: Q-Sovereign Convergence Gate (QSCG) — 2026

## 🔭 Executive Summary
The **Q-Sovereign Convergence Gate (QSCG)** is a hyper-sovereign decision metric designed to detect the non-linear transition of a state from a classical economic regime to a "Quantum-Biological-Orbital" (QBO) sovereign regime. It identifies the moment when a nation's critical infrastructure (finance, compute, and biological integrity) becomes simultaneously protected by PQC, orbital edge networks, and bio-digital provenance, creating a "sovereignty trap" for any external adversary.

## 🗺️ Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 1. The Map (Inputs)
The QSCG synthesizes three distinct strategic vectors:

*   **Vector 1: PQC-FM Maturity ($V_{pqc}$)**
    *   *Metric*: Adoption rate of NIST-standardized PQC algorithms in the nation's top 5 Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) and Tier-1 settlement rails.
    *   *Threshold*: $\ge 70\%$ of inter-bank transaction volume secured by ML-KEM/ML-DSA.
*   **Vector 2: Orbital-Edge Density ($V_{orb}$)**
    *   *Metric*: Ratio of decentralized LEO compute capacity (DePIN) to terrestrial backbone throughput in contested/strategic zones.
    *   *Threshold*: $\ge 1.5x$ terrestrial-to-orbital compute ratio for critical government/military functions.
*   **Vector 3: Bio-Digital Integrity Index ($V_{bio}$)**
    *   *Metric*: Percentage of synthetic biological manufacturing processes utilizing blockchain-based provenance and molecular watermarking.
    *   *Threshold*: $\ge 85\%$ of high-value bio-foundry outputs traceable via verifiable bio-digital hashes.

### 2. The Trigger (Condition)
The QSCG trigger occurs when the combined convergence of these vectors crosses the **Non-Linear Sovereignty Threshold ($\Psi_{sovereign}$)**:

$$\Psi_{sovereign} = \int (V_{pqc} \cdot V_{orb} \cdot V_{bio}) \, dt \ge \Gamma_{lock}$$

Where:
*   $\Gamma_{lock}$ is the **Convergence Constant** (determined by the complexity of the nation's existing legacy dependencies).
*   The trigger is activated when the *rate of change* of convergence ($\frac{d\Psi}{dt}$) spikes, indicating a rapid, coordinated leap in capability rather than incremental growth.

### 3. The Lock (Decision/Action)
Once the $\Psi_{sovereign}$ threshold is crossed, the system enters the **"Lock"** state, signaling:

*   **Investment Position**: **ULTRA-AGGRESSIVE BUY** on sovereign-linked RWA (Real World Assets), PQC-secured financial instruments, and Orbital-Edge DePIN tokens.
*   **Risk Hedge**: **SHORT** on traditional terrestrial-only infrastructure providers and classical cryptographic security firms.
*   **Strategic Action**: Move capital from "Regime Transition" to "Regime Dominance" phase.
