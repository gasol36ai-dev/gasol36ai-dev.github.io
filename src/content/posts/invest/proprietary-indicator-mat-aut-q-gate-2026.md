## 1. Logic Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### Map: The Convergence Surface
The **MAT-AUT-Q Gate** identifies the ultimate state of "Impenetrable Material Autonomy." It represents the point where a subsea industrial ecosystem is no longer just a collection of machines, but a sentient, self-healing, and mathematically unassailable physical domain.

The convergence surface is defined by the intersection of:
1. **Morphological Autonomy (Programmable Matter):** The ability of physical structures to autonomously reconfigure their molecular or structural architecture to repair damage, optimize energy flow, or adapt to new environmental pressures.
2. **Resource Autarky (Subsea-Oceanic):** The capacity for a subsea mesh to maintain its power, intelligence, and extraction loops entirely independent of surface-tethered or terrestrial-dependent infrastructure.
3. **Cryptographic Sovereignty (Post-Quantum Orchestration):** The guarantee that the control logic and financial settlement layers of the mesh are immune to both classical and quantum-adversary hijacking.

### Trigger: The Non-Linear Threshold
The indicator triggers when the following composite condition is met:
$$\text{MAT-AUT-Q\_Active} = (\text{PM\_Density} \ge \theta_{m}) \land (\text{SAI\_Index} > \sigma_{s}) \land (\text{PQC\_Resilience} \ge 0.95)$$

Where:
- $\text{PM\_Density}$ (Programmable Matter Density): The ratio of reconfigurable meta-atoms/modules to static structural components within the mesh nodes.
- $\text{SAI\_Index}$ (Subsea Autarky Index): The ratio of internally generated energy/resource value to external dependency cost.
- $\text{PQC\_Resilience}$ (Post-Quantum Resilience): The percentage of the control/settlement stack verified as running on Lattice-based or Hash-based cryptographic primitives.

**Trigger Event:** The deployment of a "Sentient Benthic Habitat"—a deep-sea infrastructure node that utilizes programmable matter for self-repair, geothermal/OTEC for power, and a PQC-secured edge compute core for autonomous management.

### Lock: The Resultant State
Once triggered, the system "locks" into a state of **Impenetrable Material Autonomy**.
- **Outcome:** The entity achieves a "Strategic Ghost" status. Its presence is physically resilient, energy-independent, and cryptographically invisible to conventional (and quantum) surveillance/attack vectors.
- **Strategic Reaction:** A step-function increase in the "Sovereign Autarky Premium." The cost of engaging or disrupting such an entity becomes non-linear and prohibitively expensive, shifting the geopolitical focus from *territorial control* to *mesh-integrity management*.
