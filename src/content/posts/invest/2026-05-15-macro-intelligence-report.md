# Macro Intelligence Report - 2026-05-15

## Executive Summary
The macro environment is characterized by a significant inflation surge, driven by both geopolitical tensions in the Middle East (energy prices) and a structural shift in technology demand (AI-driven chip demand). A notable divergence between CPI and PCE inflation measures is complicating the Federal Reserve's outlook. Markets are increasingly pricing in a "higher for longer" stance, with rate cuts unlikely in 2026.

## Key Macro Indicators

### 1. Inflation
- **US CPI (April 2026):** 0.6% MoM, 3.8% YoY (higher than expected).
- **Core PCE Trend:** Significant divergence from CPI. Core PCE has been surging (4.4% in March), driven by heavy weights in technology and microchips due to AI investment.
- **Energy Impact:** Middle East conflict continues to exert upward pressure on oil and gas prices, contributing to headline inflation stickiness.

### 2. Central Bank Policy
- **Federal Reserve:** The policy debate has shifted from "when to cut" to "can the Fed afford to stay tight or must it hike?". Market pricing implies rates on hold through year-end 2026.
- **Global Divergence:** Central bank paths are diverging; Norway and Australia have shown hawkishness, while the Fed remains in a precarious "wait-and-see" mode amidst stagflationary risks.

### 3. Bond Markets & Yields
- **US 2Y Treasury:** ~3.98%
- **US 10Y Treasury:** ~4.45%
- **US 30Y Treasury:** ~4.93%
- **Yield Trend:** Rising yields following the hotter-than-expected CPI print. The long end is sensitive to inflation expectations and energy volatility.

### 4. Geopolitics
- **Middle East:** The ongoing conflict is a primary driver of energy price volatility and supply chain uncertainty (especially in energy-sensitive goods).
- **US-Iran Negotiations:** Ongoing uncertainty provides a "risk premium" to commodity and energy markets.

## Transmission Mechanisms & Asset Impacts
- **AI Demand $\rightarrow$ PCE Inflation:** The rapid buildout of AI infrastructure is creating a structural inflation component in the PCE index via microchip and tech equipment pricing.
- **Energy Shock $\rightarrow$ Global Growth/Inflation:** Higher oil prices are weighing on global growth, particularly in energy-importing regions like Asia and the Euro area.
- **Real Yields $\rightarrow$ Gold:** The combination of rising nominal yields and sticky inflation is keeping a close eye on real yields as the primary driver for Gold.

## Macro Watchlist (High Impact)
1. **PCE vs. CPI Divergence:** Monitoring the widening wedge as a signal of structural inflation.
2. **US 10Y Treasury Yield:** Critical threshold at 4.5% for equity market headwinds.
3. **Oil Prices (WTI/Brent):** Primary driver of energy-linked inflation and global growth deceleration.
4. **AI Capex Cycle:** Tracking technology equipment inflation as a component of core inflation.
5. **Fed Policy Pivot Timing:** Monitoring shifts in market pricing for 2027 rate cuts.
