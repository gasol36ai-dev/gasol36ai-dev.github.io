# Global Macroeconomic State - April 2026

## Central Bank Policies
- **Federal Reserve (Fed):** 
  - Rate: 3.5% - 3.75% (Steady as of March 2026).
  - Stance: Data-dependent, monitoring the impact of Middle East conflict on inflation.
- **European Central Bank (ECB):** 
  - Rate: 2.15% (Steady as of March 2026).
  - Inflation Forecast: Raised to 2.6% (from 1.9%) due to energy cost surges.
- **Bank of Japan (BoJ):** 
  - Rate: 0.75% (Steady as of March 2026).
  - Stance: Gradual policy shift; warns of upside inflation risks from the Iran war.

## Inflation Data (March/April 2026)
- **US Headline CPI:** 3.3% YoY (Significant spike from 2.4% in February).
- **US Core CPI:** 2.6% YoY (Relatively contained).
- **Primary Driver:** Energy costs (Gasoline +21.2% in March) driven by the Iran conflict.
- **Eurozone Inflation:** 2.5% in March (above 2% target).

## Sovereign Bond Yields (April 24, 2026)
- **US 10Y Treasury:** 4.31%
- **US 2Y Treasury:** 3.78%
- **Observation:** The yield curve has shifted; the 10Y is now above the 2Y, suggesting a move away from deep inversion.

## Geopolitical Risks
- **Top Risk:** Geoeconomic confrontation (WEF 2026).
- **Active Catalyst:** Iran War.
- **Impact:** Disruption of energy markets, surge in oil prices, and volatility in global capital flows.
