# Proprietary Indicator: SCLC Gate (Spatial-Compute-Latency Convergence)

## 1. Concept Overview
The **Spatial-Compute-Latency Convergence (SCLC) Gate** is a multi-domain indicator designed to identify the systemic failure point where embodied AI agents lose operational efficacy due to the misalignment of three critical latency vectors: **Spatial Perception**, **Neuromorphic Reasoning**, and **Financial Execution**.

As embodied AI scales, the bottleneck shifts from mere "intelligence" to the "synchronicity" of the perception-reasoning-action loop. A failure in any one of these vectors causes a non-linear breakdown in agentic autonomy.

## 2. Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock (MTL)

### 🗺️ Map (The Input Vectors)
The indicator synthesizes data from three disparate domains:
1.  **Spatial Domain**: `[Geometry-Consistency Error Rate (GCER)]` — Measures the divergence between an agent's perceived 3D world model and the actual physical environment (e.g., via depth-prediction drift or spatial-semantic mismatch).
2.  **Compute Domain**: `[Neuromorphic-Decision Latency (NDL)]` — Measures the time delta between sensory input arrival and the generation of a reasoning-action command, specifically focusing on the overhead of high-density neuromorphic/quantum processing.
3.  **Financial Domain**: `[Agentic Order-Execution Slippage (AOES)]` — Measures the price/resource impact caused by delays in agentic decision-making within autonomous, high-frequency agentic markets.

### ⚡ Trigger (The Inflection Point)
The **SCLC Gate** triggers when:
$$\text{GCER} \uparrow \quad \cap \quad \text{NDL} \uparrow \quad \cap \quad \text{AOES} \uparrow$$
Specifically:
- A spike in **spatial divergence** (agent is "lost" in 3D space).
- Simultaneously with a **compute bottleneck** (reasoning latency exceeds the physical movement threshold).
- Leading to **financial/resource slippage** (the agent cannot execute its intent at the intended cost/position).

### 🔒 Lock (The Automated Response)
Upon the SCLC Gate trigger, the system initiates a **Resource Re-Alignment Protocol**:
- **Action**: `[Dynamic Compute-Priority Escalation]`
- **Execution**: Redirects high-priority Neuromorphic/Quantum compute cycles from non-critical background tasks to the **Spatial-Agent Cluster** experiencing the bottleneck.
- **Objective**: Re-establish the synchronicity of the perception-action loop to prevent catastrophic agentic drift or market decoupling.

## 3. Strategic Value
- **Predictive Utility**: Provides an early warning for "Agentic Collapse" in autonomous logistics, defense, and high-frequency agentic trading.
- **Cross-Layer Intelligence**: Bridges the gap between physical robotics, advanced hardware, and financial microstructure.
