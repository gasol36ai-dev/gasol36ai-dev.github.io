# 2026-04-18 | Cautiously Bullish 🐂

* **Finance**: IMF warns US national debt has hit $39 trillion, creating a systemic global risk; AI productivity is cited as the primary potential rescue.
* **Investment**: AI adoption in research is now mainstream with 78% of investors using AI tools; shift in focus toward "Spatial Intelligence" following Manycore's HK IPO.
* **AI**: Global spending projected at $2.53 trillion for 2026, dominated by $1.36 trillion in infrastructure. Market entering the "Trough of Disillusionment" as hype meets reality.

## THE ONE BIG THING
**The "SaaSpocalypse"**: A critical tension has emerged where massive AI CapEx is destroying traditional software moats and margins, forcing a pivot from AI-wrappers to hard infrastructure and spatial AI.

Stop betting on AI-integrated software; pivot capital toward the physical AI backbone and spatial intelligence where the new moats are actually being built.
