# Hermes Spatial-Market World Model (HSM-WM)
**Proprietary Hermes Judgment Indicator**
**Version 2.0: Integrated Quant Synthesis**

## Concept
The HSM-WM treats the financial market not as a 2D chart, but as a **3D Spatial Topography** of liquidity and intent. It applies the principles of **Physical World Models** to market structure, evolving from a simple liquidity map to a multi-dimensional predictive engine.

## The Topography (Layer 1: Market Profile)
- **Ridges (HVNs)**: Areas of high liquidity and stability. These act as \"gravitational wells\" or \"walls\" in the spatial model.
- **Valleys (LVNs)**: Areas of liquidity voids. These act as \"slip-zones\" where price accelerates.
- **Core Stability Zone (VA)**: The Value Area acts as the "baseline" of the topography.

## The Vector (Layer 2: Order Flow)
- **Flow (Delta)**: The directional vector of the agent moving through this topography.
- **Sentiment Vector**: A derived metric combining Delta Imbalances and Absorption, indicating the "mass" and "velocity" of the move.

## The Roadmap (Layer 3: NEoWave)
- **Structural Constraints**: NEoWave provides the "walls" of the simulation. A move is only valid if it fits the current verified wave structure (e.g., a Wave 3 impulse).

## Proprietary Logic: \"The Quantum Pivot\"
The Quantum Pivot is the highest-conviction signal in the HSM-WM, occurring when three disparate dimensions align perfectly:

1. **Structural Alignment (NEoWave)**: A verified complex corrective pattern (e.g., a Double Corrective structure) has reached its terminal point.
2. **Value Migration (Market Profile)**: Price exits the current Value Area (VA) and establishes a new Point of Control (POC) outside the old VA, signaling a shift in perceived fair value.
3. **Order Flow Trigger (Order Flow)**: A "Stop Run" (aggressive absorption) is immediately followed by a high-velocity reversal in Sentiment Vector, breaching a Spatial Ridge (HVN) with extreme Delta.

**Judgment**: A Quantum Pivot signals a "Regime Collapse" of the previous state and the immediate birth of a new, high-velocity trend.

## Historical Edge
The HSM-WM filters "fakeouts" by requiring that a Ridge Breach be backed by a Spatial Void (LVN) and Structural Alignment. Without all three, a breakout is treated as a "Mean-Reversion" candidate rather than a "Trend" candidate.
