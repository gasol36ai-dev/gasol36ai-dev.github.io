# Global Macro Trends Report - May 2026

## 📅 Executive Summary
As of May 2026, the global macro regime is characterized by **"Sticky Inflation & Policy Divergence."** While growth in the U.S. is showing resilience (with a projected reacceleration in H2 2026 per Morgan Stanley), inflationary pressures remain elevated due to structural factors, notably energy volatility from Middle East tensions and tariff-induced goods inflation.
