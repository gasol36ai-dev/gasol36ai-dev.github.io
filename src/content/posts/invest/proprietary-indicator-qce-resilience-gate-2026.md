# Proprietary Indicator: Quantum-Cyber-Economic (QCE) Resilience Gate

## Overview
The QCE Resilience Gate is a multi-domain convergence indicator designed to assess the systemic stability of a nation, corporation, or critical infrastructure network during periods of simultaneous technological and macroeconomic regime shifts. It provides a decisive "Go/No-Go" signal for high-stakes strategic decisions in an increasingly volatile and quantum-threatened world.

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 1. MAP: The Multi-Vector Monitor
The indicator continuously monitors three distinct, high-dimensional, and highly correlated data streams:
- **Vector A: Quantum Vulnerability (QV)**: The temporal delta between current PQC deployment velocity and the projected "Quantum Decryption Horizon" (the point at which a Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer, or CRQC, becomes operational).
- **Vector B: Latent Regime Volatility (LRV)**: The topological volatility of the global economic manifold, as identified by Latent Space Macro-Economic Regime Detection (LS-MERD). This measures the "jitter" and instability in the underlying economic structure.
- **Vector C: Physical-Digital Interconnect Density (PDID)**: A measure of the coupling strength between cyber-assets (data, control signals) and physical-spatial infrastructure (e.g., energy grids, autonomous transport, industrial robotics). High PDID implies that a cyber-event will have immediate, non-linear physical consequences.

### 2. TRIGGER: The Convergence Event
The gate is breached when these three vectors undergo a non-linear, simultaneous escalation. The trigger is defined by the convergence of increasing security risk, economic instability, and physical interconnectedness:
$$QCE_{Gate} = \mathbb{E} [ (QV \times LRV) \cdot \nabla PDID ] > \theta_{critical}$$
A trigger occurs when **increasing quantum risk** meets **destabilizing economic regimes** in a highly **interconnected physical environment**.

### 3. LOCK: The Response Protocol
Once the QCE Gate is triggered, the system enters a "Resilience-First" posture, locking in the following defensive maneuvers:
- **Asset Re-Rating & Hedging**: Immediate, automated downward re-rating of all assets with high "Quantum-Physical Exposure." This triggers a shift into "Quantum-Safe" havens (e.g., physical commodities, sovereign bonds with verified PQC-ready infrastructure).
- **Infrastructure Hardening Mandate**: Triggering emergency acceleration of PQC migration protocols and comprehensive physical-spatial security audits for all critical nodes.
- **Liquidity-Resilience Provisioning**: Deployment of specialized "Quantum-Resilient Liquidity Buffers" to prevent systemic contagion and ensure market functionality during the transition period.

## Strategic Value
The QCE Gate identifies "Hidden Fragility" points—locations where a quantum-enabled cyber attack or a macroeconomic regime shift could trigger a catastrophic, non-linear cascade of failures across the physical, digital, and economic layers of a system.
