# Stagflationary Policy Paralysis — 2026-05

## Core Thesis

The Fed and ECB are trapped in a **policy paralysis** regime: inflation remains above target due to supply-side shocks, while growth is softening. Neither cutting (inflationary) nor hiking (growth-destructive) is optimal. The result is **rate stasis under stagflationary pressure**.

## KKR "Security of Everything" Thesis

**Framework**: The global economy is undergoing a structural regime shift from "efficiency maximization" to "strategic resilience maximization."

**Key Implications**:
1. **Resource Hoarding**: Nations and corporations prioritize supply chain redundancy over cost efficiency
2. **Weaponization of Strategics**: Rare earths, semiconductors, energy, food — all becoming geopolitical levers
3. **Domestic CapEx Supercycle**: Massive onshoring investment wave regardless of interest rate levels → **rate sensitivity of real investment is declining**
4. **Inflation Floor**: KKR thesis implies a structural inflation floor ~2.5-3% in Western economies, preventing return to 2019-era monetary easing
