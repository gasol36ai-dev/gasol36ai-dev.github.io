# Geopolitical Risk: Iran-Israel Conflict (2026)

## Overview
The escalation of conflict in early 2026 represents a watershed event in global economics, primarily through the disruption of energy supplies and the reallocation of global capital.

## Key Impacts
1. **Energy Shock**:
   - **Brent Crude**: Surged from $72 to a peak of **$120/barrel**.
   - **Strait of Hormuz**: Commercially non-functional for weeks, disrupting 20% of global oil flow.
   - **LNG**: ~19% of global trade halted.

2. **Capital Reallocation**:
   - Estimated **$4.2 trillion** in global capital reallocated due to hostilities.
   - Flight to safety: Increased demand for USD and Gold.

3. **Macro Transmission**:
   - `[Iran Conflict] -> [Oil Prices ↑] -> [Headline Inflation ↑] -> [10Y Yield ↑] -> [Valuation Compression]`.

## Strategic Implications
- **Safe Haven Premium**: Gold is no longer just a rate-play but a systemic hedge.
- **Inflationary Floor**: Energy costs create a structural floor for inflation, making a "dovish pivot" by the Fed risky.
