# Macro Intelligence Report - 2026-05-13

## Key Insights

### Central Bank Policies
- **Federal Reserve**: Maintaining benchmark overnight rate between 3.5%-3.75% since December 2025. Fed funds pricing shows ~25% chance of a 25bp hike by end-Dec 2026, up from 21.5% prior. Chair Kevin Warsh faces a divided FOMC. Outlook: Hawkish hold; de-escalation in Iran conflict could enable cuts; persistence risks renewed hikes.
- **European Central Bank**: Leaning toward a rate hike in June 2026 unless energy prices improve and Iran war ends. President Lagarde signaled consideration of a June increase after rejecting one this week.
- **Bank of Japan**: Markets pricing ~65% chance of a June hike, nearly fully priced for late July. BoJ Governor Ueda failed to prepare market for a hike in April, causing swaps expectations to unwind. Core inflation (ex-fresh food) below 2% target since Feb 2026. Policy normalization underway.

### Inflation & Energy
- **US CPI (April 2026)**: Non-seasonally adjusted consumer prices rose 3.8% YoY, highest since May 2023. Core inflation (ex food/energy) 2.8% YoY, above expected 2.7%.
- **Energy Shock**: Brent crude > $108/bbl due to Iran-Hormuz supply shock. This creates a structural floor for inflation and a flight-to-quality trigger for gold.

### Bond Yields
- **US 10Y Treasury Yield**: 4.455% (up 4bp on 2026-05-12). 2Y yield: 3.996% (up ~5bp). 30Y: 5.02%.
- **Real Yields**: Rising across curve; rising real yields create headwinds for high-multiple AI stocks but increasingly offset by geopolitical hedging.
- **Decoupling**: Confirmed structural breakdown between real yields and gold; gold remains resilient due to systemic risk hedging and central bank buying.

### Currency & Capital Flows
- **USD Index**: Strong, driven by safety flows and Fed hawkishness, pressuring EM currencies and capital outflows.
- **Geopolitical Risk**: Iran-Hormuz stability remains a binary trigger for global risk-off. Closure of Hormuz Strait would remove ~20% of global oil supply, spiking oil prices.

### Asset Class Implications
- **Gold**: Strong as systemic insurance; real yield decoupling confirmed; upside triggered by Brent > $108/bbl.
- **AI/Tech Stocks**: Face headwinds from rising real yields but supported by AI capex boom (projected $716bn CSP capex in 2026) and inference/agent shift.
- **Emerging Markets**: Vulnerable to USD strength and potential capital outflows if risk-off accelerates.
- **Sovereign Bonds**: Credit rating implications (Moody's potential US sovereign downgrade) could add a permanent risk premium to Treasuries.
