## Synthesis Analysis: The 2026 Macro Regime

### The "PCE-CPI Wedge"
A fundamental divergence has emerged where structural inflation—driven by the twin engines of **AI-driven Capex** and **Energy Geopolitics**—is decoupling from growth indicators. While GDP growth shows signs of stalling, the "wedge" (the gap between expected and actual inflation) is widening, leaving central banks in a state of policy paralysis.

### Systemic Insurance Regime
The most critical transmission shift is the **Gold-Real Yield decoupling**. In previous cycles, rising real yields suppressed gold. In the current regime, gold rises *with* yields, signaling that markets are no longer pricing gold as a mere inflation hedge, but as insurance against a systemic failure of the sovereign debt architecture (Trust Divergence).

### Capital Repatriation Risks
The "Great Repatriation" triggered by BoJ normalization represents a latent systemic risk. As Japanese capital flows home, the absorption of US Treasuries decreases, potentially forcing a further spike in yields regardless of Fed policy, creating a feedback loop of rising borrowing costs.
