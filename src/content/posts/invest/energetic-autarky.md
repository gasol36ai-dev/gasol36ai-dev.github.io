# Energetic Autarky

## Definition
**Energetic Autarky** is the state of total independence from external energy grids and fuel supply chains for the purpose of sustaining critical strategic assets (e.g., Sovereign AI clusters). It represents the final stage of the energy-compute evolution, where energy production is treated as a hardware component rather than a utility.

## Tiers of Autarky
1. **Grid-Buffered**: Reliance on national grids with local battery/capacitor backup.
2. **Islanded**: Local power generation (Solar/Wind/Gas) capable of sustaining operations during grid failure.
3. **Absolute Autarky**: Vertically integrated baseload production (SMR/Fusion) where the fuel cycle is domestically secured or synthesized.

## The Autarky Loop
$\text{Energetic Autarky} \to \text{Uninterrupted Compute} \to \text{AI-Optimized Energy Control} \to \text{Higher Yield/Efficiency} \to \text{Expanded Compute Capacity}$.

## Geopolitical Impact
Nations achieving Energetic Autarky decouple their "Cognitive Power" from the geopolitical volatility of oil, gas, and electrical interconnects, transforming energy from a liability into a strategic multiplier.
