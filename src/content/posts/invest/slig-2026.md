# Proprietary Judgment Indicator: Sovereign Liquidity Inflection Gate (SLIG)

## Overview
The **Sovereign Liquidity Inflection Gate (SLIG)** is a high-density judgment indicator designed to detect the critical transition point where global digital liquidity (mCBDCs) decouples from synthetic credit stability, signaling an immediate need for sovereign reallocation into hard-asset reserves. 

SLIG synthesizes three convergent domains: **Cross-Border CBDC Liquidity Corridors**, **AI-Driven Credit Risk & Synthetic Assets**, and **Sovereign Physical Asset Tokenization (SPAT)**. It identifies the "inflection" where the efficiency of settlement (T+0) accelerates the transmission of credit shocks, necessitating a "Lock" into physical, tokenized strategic reserves.

---

## Map (Inputs)
The SLIG monitor tracks four primary variables across the global financial substrate:

1. **mCBDC Settlement Velocity ($\text{mCBDC}_{\text{vel}}$)**: 
   - *Source*: Real-time throughput data from multilateral CBDC bridge ledgers (e.g., Project mBridge).
   - *Metric*: Percentage of total cross-border volume settling via Synchronous Atomic Settlement (PvP).

2. **Real-Time RWA Credit Spread ($\text{RWA}_{\text{spread}}$)**:
   - *Source*: AI-driven synthetic asset markets.
   - *Metric*: The basis point differential between AI-scored synthetic credit tokens (backed by infrastructure cash flows) and traditional Sovereign Bond yields.

3. **Strategic Physical Asset Tokenization Volume ($\text{SPAT}_{\text{vol}}$)**:
   - *Source*: Sovereign Asset Ledgers / SPAT registries.
   - *Metric*: The total value locked (TVL) in tokenized national compute clusters, energy grids, and rare-earth mineral reserves.

4. **Synchronous Settlement Latency ($\text{L}_{\text{lat}}$)**:
   - *Source*: Network telemetry from the mCBDC Interoperability Layer.
   - *Metric*: Average end-to-end latency for atomic settlement across major currency corridors.

## Trigger (Condition)
The SLIG "Gate" opens when the following non-linear thresholds are met simultaneously (The Convergence Event):

$$\text{Condition} = (\text{L}_{\text{lat}} < 5\text{s}) \land (\text{RWA}_{\text{spread}} > 2\sigma) \land (\Delta \text{SPAT}_{\text{vol}} > 15\%_{\text{MoM}})$$

**Logic Breakdown:**
- **$\text{L}_{\text{lat}} < 5\text{s}$**: Indicates that the "plumbing" is fully optimized for instantaneous contagion. Liquidity can exit a jurisdiction or asset class in seconds.
- **$\text{RWA}_{\text{spread}} > 2\sigma$**: Indicates that AI risk models have detected a systemic divergence in credit quality for synthetic assets, signaling a looming credit crunch.
- **$\Delta \text{SPAT}_{\text{vol}} > 15\%$**: Indicates that other sovereign entities are already aggressively moving into "Hard-Asset" tokenization as a hedge, creating a competitive race for physical reserves.

## Lock (Action)
Once the SLIG trigger is activated, the following **Automated Strategic Response** is initiated:

1. **Liquidity Reallocation**: Immediate programmatic shift of Sovereign Risk Capital from liquid mCBDC corridors into high-grade **SPAT Tokens** (specifically Compute and Energy).
2. **Reserve Hardening**: Transition of the treasury mandate from "Liquidity Optimization" (T+0 efficiency) to "Strategic Autarky" (Hard-Asset accumulation).
3. **Ownership Cap Activation**: Deployment of smart-contract-based "Sovereign Guardrails" to restrict foreign acquisition of national infrastructure tokens, preventing predatory "fire-sale" buyouts during the credit spike.
4. **Synthetic Hedge Deployment**: Shorting synthetic credit tokens while simultaneously increasing the collateral weight of physical mineral reserves.
