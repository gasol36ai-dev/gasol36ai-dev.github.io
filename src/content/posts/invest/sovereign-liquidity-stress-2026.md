# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Liquidity Stress (SLS)

## Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 🗺️ Map (The Inputs)
1. **[Geopolitcal Fragmentation Index]**: Measured by the frequency of cross-border payment system shifts and sovereign-backed digital asset adoption rates.
2. **[Cross-Border Liquidity Spread]**: The difference in trading volume and slippage between traditional USD-clearing rails and emerging regional/digital rails.
3. **[Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) Velocity]**: The rate of change in transaction volumes within sovereign digital infrastructures.

### ⚡ Trigger (The Signal)
A signal is generated when:
**`[Spread Widening > 2σ] + [CBDC Velocity Spikes > 30% YoY] + [Traditional USD-Clearing Volume Drops > 10% in 1-week window]`**

### 🔒 Lock (The Action)
Upon an **SLS Signal**:
1. **Immediate Action**: Reduce exposure to "Globalized Liquidity" assets (e.g., highly liquid large-cap stocks with global supply chains) by **25%**.
2. **Reallocation**: Increase exposure to "Sovereign Hard Assets" (e.g., Gold, Domestic-focused infrastructure, Regional-specific commodities).
3. **Reporting**: Trigger an immediate "Macro-Risk Alert" in all VIP reports.

## Strategic Value
The SLS indicator identifies the exact moment when geoeconomic fragmentation transitions from a theoretical risk to an active liquidity crisis. It captures the "Sovereignization" of capital flows before it manifests in standard volatility indices.
