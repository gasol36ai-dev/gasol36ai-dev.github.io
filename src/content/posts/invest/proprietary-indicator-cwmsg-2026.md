## 1. Concept & Sovereign Context

The **Cognitive Wetware-Mesh Sovereignty Gate (CWMSG)** defines the conditions for a nation-state to achieve **"Full-Stack Cognitive Sovereignty"**—the ability to independently produce, distribute, and verify its own cognitive infrastructure without reliance on foreign silicon, terrestrial cloud, or external trust anchors.

CWMSG synthesizes three distinct but complementary sovereign infrastructure layers:
1.  **Sovereign Wetware-as-a-Service (SWaaS):** The *production* layer for biological compute (organoids, wetware).
2.  **Autonomous Maritime-Mesh Sovereignty (AMMS):** The *distribution* layer (subsea compute + OTEC + maritime RWA).
3.  **Cognitive Zero-Trust Architecture (CZTA):** The *verification* layer (formal methods, BCI security, agentic authentication).

## 2. Transmission Mapping

### 2.1. The Wetware Production Trigger (SWaaS)
**Event:** A nation-state certifies its first **Sovereign Bio-Compute Cluster** (organoid compute infrastructure certified by a national bio-security agency, e.g., FDA-equivalent).

**Mechanism:** The state decouples its "cognitive production" from silicon supply chains. Biological neurons (wetware) provide a non-silicon substrate for AI training and inference, insulating the state from semiconductor export controls.

**Reaction:**
- Private sector adoption of "national wetware cloud" for sensitive AI workloads.
- Emergence of "wetware premiums" for high-fidelity, state-protected neural clusters.
- Geopolitical bifurcation: "Bio-Iron Curtain" between states with sovereign bio-compute and those without.

### 2.2. The Mesh Distribution Trigger (AMMS)
**Event:** A nation-state deploys an **OTEC-Powered Subsea Compute Mesh** in its EEZ waters, with autonomous maritime logistics (USVs/AUVs) and RWA tokenization of maritime assets.

**Mechanism:** The state creates a physically isolated, geographically sovereign cognitive distribution network that is decoupled from terrestrial cable infrastructure and terrestrial energy grids.

**Reaction:**
- Maritime "compute-moat" where the physical location of hardware in EEZ waters provides a unique layer of geopolitical leverage.
- "Microstructure arbitrage" premium: ultra-low latency data processing at the intersection of LEO downlinks and subsea cables.
- New RWA asset class: tokenized shares of sovereign maritime compute-and-energy infrastructure.

### 2.3. The Security Verification Trigger (CZTA)
**Event:** A nation-state mandates **CZTA Certification** for all sovereign cognitive APIs and BCI systems, following a high-profile "cognitive breach" (e.g., neural command injection or agentic fraud).

**Mechanism:** The state establishes "cognitive sovereignty" standards that define the trust model for neuro-symbolic AI, agentic systems, and BCIs operating within its jurisdiction.

**Reaction:**
- "Sovereign BCI Security Premiums": users and institutions pay for government-certified "cognitive shields."
- "Secure Cognitive API" pricing models: API costs tiered by verification level (basic vs. formally verified reasoning).
- R&D surge in formal methods and automated theorem proving for "verifiable AI" kernels.
