# Bio-Orbital-Quantum (BOQ) Convergence Gate (BOQCG)

**Classification**: High-Order Proprietary Judgment Indicator (Iteration 3)
**Domains**: NOHI $\cap$ SODF $\cap$ PQAS
**Version**: 1.0 (2026)

## Overview
The **Bio-Orbital-Quantum (BOQ) Convergence Gate (BOQCG)** is a high-order proprietary judgment indicator designed to detect the emergence of **Bio-Digital Sovereign Orbital Intelligence**. It signals the precise convergence point where high-efficiency biological compute (NOHI) is successfully integrated into sovereign orbital infrastructure (SODF) and secured via post-quantum agentic protocols (PQAS).

The BOQCG is not a measure of a single technology, but a measure of **architectural convergence**, identifying the shift from "distributed AI" to "unassailable sovereign intelligence."

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 1. Map (The Landscape)
The convergence landscape consists of three intersecting strategic vectors:
- **Bio-Vector (NOHI)**: The transition from silicon-only compute to bio-hybrid wetware (Neuromorphic-Organoid Hybrid Intelligence), enabling extreme energy efficiency and adaptive plasticity in remote environments.
- **Orbital-Vector (SODF)**: The transition from terrestrial-dependent data centers to the Sovereign Orbital Data Fabric (SODF), providing "high-ground" compute and communication decoupled from terrestrial geopolitical vulnerabilities.
- **Quantum-Vector (PQAS)**: The transition from classical asymmetric encryption to Post-Quantum Agentic Security (PQAS), ensuring that autonomous delegation and command-and-control are immune to quantum-accelerated cryptanalysis.

### 2. Trigger (The Activation Event)
The BOQCG triggers upon the successful execution of a **Bio-Orbital Handshake**.

**Transmission Mapping:**
`[Sovereign Node Launch] -> [PQAS-Verified Bio-Handshake] -> [Unassailable High-Ground Intelligence]`

- **Event**: Deployment of a functional NOHI-enabled edge node within a Sovereign Orbital Data Fabric.
- **Mechanism**: Establishment of a PQAS-verified trust anchor (using ML-DSA/Kyber) between the orbital bio-node and terrestrial agentic controllers.
- **Reaction**: Activation of "High-Ground Autonomous Reasoning," where the node begins processing sovereign intelligence tasks independently of terrestrial power and connectivity constraints.

### 3. Lock (The Stability Threshold)
The indicator is considered "Locked" (Active) when the **Convergence Index ($\Gamma_{BOQ}$)** exceeds the critical threshold $\tau_{crit}$.

**Formal Logical/Mathematical Condition:**
$$\Gamma_{BOQ} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (\sigma_{bio, i} \cdot \lambda_{orb, i} \cdot \chi_{quant, i}) \ge \tau_{crit}$$

**Variables:**
- $\sigma_{bio} \in [0, 1]$: **Bio-Stability Coefficient**. Measure of the NOHI bio-silicon interface stability and neuronal health in microgravity.
- $\lambda_{orb} \in [0, 1]$: **Orbital Connectivity Index**. Measure of the SODF mesh node's availability and latency efficiency relative to the sovereign core.
- $\chi_{quant} \in \{0, 1\}$: **Quantum Integrity Bit**. Binary verification of the PQAS handshake. $1$ if the link is provably post-quantum secure; $0$ otherwise.
- $\tau_{crit}$: **Critical Convergence Threshold**. The minimum value (typically $\ge 0.75$) required to declare the system operational.

## Strategic Implications
A "Locked" BOQCG represents the achievement of **Physical AI Sovereignty**. This is a state of strategic asymmetry where an entity possesses an intelligence layer that is:
1. **Energy-Autonomous**: (NOHI) capable of complex reasoning on minimal power.
2. **Geopolitically Decoupled**: (SODF) immune to terrestrial cable cuts or gateway seizures.
3. **Cryptographically Absolute**: (PQAS) secure against the "Quantum Apocalypse."

This convergence effectively creates a "God-Eye" reasoning layer that is physically and logically unreachable by adversaries.
