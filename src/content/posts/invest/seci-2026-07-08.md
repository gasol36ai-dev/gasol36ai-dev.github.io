# Concept: Sovereign Energy-Compute Integration (SECI)
**Date:** 2026-07-08
**Type:** Strategic Infrastructure / National Security

## Description
Sovereign Energy-Compute Integration (SECI) is the strategic convergence of national energy production and AI compute capacity, treating energy and compute as a single, unified national utility to eliminate dependencies on foreign energy markets and third-party cloud providers.

## Key Components
- **SMR-Compute Co-location:** Deploying Small Modular Reactors directly at the data center site.
- **Energy-Aware Scheduling:** AI workloads managed by real-time energy availability.
- **Sovereign GPU Clusters:** Nationalized hardware fabrics independent of global cloud hyperscalers.
- **Thermal Recycling:** Integration of compute waste heat into civilian energy grids.
