# Macro-to-Asset Transmission Patterns (2026 Edition)

A library of causal chains used to determine strategic positioning across asset classes.

## 1. The Energy-Inflation-Yield Chain (The "Growth Killer")
**Path**: `[Geopolitical Conflict (Iran/Hormuz)]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Brent Oil >$100]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Headline Inflation]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Inflation Premium in Bonds]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ 10Y Real Yields]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\downarrow$ DCF Valuations for Tech/AI]`
- **Asset Impact**: 📉 NASDAQ, 📉 SMH, 📉 Growth Stocks.
- **Verification**: Monitor the delta between Headline and Core inflation. If Headline leads and Core follows, the chain is activating.

## 2. The Policy Divergence Chain (The "Liquidity Drain")
**Path**: `[Fed: Hawkish Hold]` + `[ECB/BoJ: Hawkish Pivot/Hike]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ JPY/EUR Strength vs USD]` $\rightarrow$ `[Unwind of Yen Carry Trades]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\downarrow$ Global Margin Liquidity]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Market Volatility]`
- **Asset Impact**: 📉 High-beta assets, 📉 EM Equities, 📈 JPY.
- **Verification**: Monitor USD/JPY and global volatility (VIX).

## 3. The Systemic Risk Hedge Chain (The "Gold Anchor")
**Path**: `[Sovereign Debt Risk / Geopolitical Crisis]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Flight to Quality]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Gold / $\uparrow$ USD]` $\rightarrow$ `[Decoupling from Real Yields]`
- **Asset Impact**: 📈 Gold, 📈 USD (Short-term), 📉 EM Assets.
- **Verification**: Gold price rising while US 10Y Real Yields also rise = **Confirmed Decoupling**.

## 4. The AI Convergence Chain (The "Capex Burden")
**Path**: `[Shift: Model Training $\rightarrow$ Agentic Inference]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Power/Grid Demand]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Energy Costs]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\uparrow$ Capex Burden for AI Firms]` $\rightarrow$ `[$\downarrow$ Net Margins]`
- **Asset Impact**: 📉 AI Hardware (if energy costs outweigh efficiency), 📈 Power Infrastructure/Utilities.
- **Verification**: Monitor energy prices and utility company Capex announcements.
