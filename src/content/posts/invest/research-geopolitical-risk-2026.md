# Geopolitical Risk 2026: The Security Networks Paradigm

## The Strait of Hormuz Crisis (March 2026)
The closure of the Strait of Hormuz (carrying ~27% of global maritime petroleum trade) transformed geopolitical risk from a "headline risk" to a "systemic supply shock."

- **Immediate Result**: Brent crude > $100/bbl.
- **Secondary Result**: Shortages in fertilizer, chemicals, and plastics in Asia, sparking double-digit price increases.
- **Market Reaction**: Broad "Risk-off" event; significant drawdown in Emerging Markets.

## From Globalization to "Security Networks"
Morgan Stanley identifies a structural shift in the global economic model:
- **Old Model**: Efficiency, open markets, globalized supply chains.
- **New Model**: Security, resilience, regionalized "Security Networks."

### Investment Implications
1. **Capex Supercycle**: Companies are rebuilding supply chains to ensure redundancy. This is capital-intensive and creates a multi-year capex cycle.
2. **Regional Winners**: Mexico and Asia are key beneficiaries of this redeployment.
3. **Active > Passive**: Passive indexing is insufficient in a world of policy-driven distortions. Active positioning around "security enablers" (automation, logistics, localized energy) is required.
