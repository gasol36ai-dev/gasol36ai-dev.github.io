# Proprietary Indicator: Quantum-Sovereign Liquidity Buffer (QSLB)

## 🧩 Overview
The **Quantum-Sovereign Liquidity Buffer (QSLB)** is a proprietary judgment indicator designed to detect systemic liquidity fragility during the transition to post-quantum financial infrastructure. It measures the divergence between traditional settlement asset liquidity and the availability of quantum-secure, high-assurance liquidity channels.

---

## 🗺️ Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 1. Map (Data Inputs)
- **Q (Quantum Transition Velocity)**: The rate of adoption of Post-Quantum Cryptographic (PQC) standards (e.g., ML-KEM, ML-DSA) across major central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) and settlement networks.
- **S (Sovereign Asset Liquidity)**: The 30-day realized liquidity of traditional fiat-denominated settlement assets.
- **L (Liquidity Divergence)**: The delta between the liquidity of "Legacy-Insecure" assets and "Quantum-Secure" assets (e.g., PQC-compliant tokenized RWAs).
- **B (Breach Probability)**: A real-time threat score based on quantum computing capability breakthroughs (e.g., Shor's algorithm progress) and cryptographic vulnerability reporting.

### 2. Trigger (The Signal)
The **QSLB Signal** is triggered when:
`[Q acceleration > 1.5x] AND [S liquidity decline > 10%] AND [L divergence > 2.0 sigma]`

*In plain English: When the world is racing to adopt quantum-secure protocols (high Q), but traditional money is becoming harder to move (low S), and the gap between "secure" and "unsecure" assets is widening (high L), a systemic "liquidity trap" is imminent as capital flees insecure legacy rails.*

### 3. Lock (Actionable Decision)
Upon a **QSLB Signal**:
- **Flight to Security**: Automatically reallocate **40%** of liquid capital from legacy-denominated assets into "Quantum-Secure" RWA (Real World Asset) vaults or PQC-compliant stablecoins.
- **Settlement Hedging**: Increase allocation to "Sovereign Autarky" assets (e.g., physical gold, RISC-V based local hardware-backed reserves) to mitigate systemic settlement risk.
- **Systemic Risk Buffer**: Implement a mandatory **15%** increase in cash/near-cash reserves held in quantum-hardened, non-networked custody environments.

## 📈 Strategic Value
- **Early Warning**: Identifies "Quantum Flight" (mass exodus from legacy financial rails) before it manifests as a market crash.
- **Regime Detection**: Differentiates between "Productive Quantum Transition" (steady Q growth) and "Panic Quantum Transition" (high Q + high L divergence).
- **Non-Linearity Capture**: Captures the step-function volatility that occurs when a major cryptographic standard is broken or a major bank announces a total migration to PQC rails.
