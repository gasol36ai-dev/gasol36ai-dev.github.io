# Frontier Order Flow & Market Microstructure (2026)

## 📊 The Macro-Micro Hierarchy
Professional execution requires a two-step structural filter:
`[Volume Profile (Strategy/Map)] $\rightarrow$ [Order Flow (Execution/Compass)]`

### 1. Structural Map: Volume Profile (VP) & Market Profile (TPO)
- **Volume Profile (VP)**: Measures *actual volume* traded. Reveals institutional commitment.
    - **POC (Point of Control)**: Highest volume; the "Fair Value" magnet.
    - **VA (Value Area)**: 70% of volume; range of acceptance.
    - **HVN (High Volume Nodes)**: Zones of support/resistance (institutional positions).
    - **LVN (Low Volume Nodes)**: Zones of rejection; price accelerates through these "vacuums."
- **Market Profile (TPO)**: Measures *time spent* at price. Reveals market sentiment and rotation.

### 2. Execution Trigger: Order Flow (OF)
- **Delta**: (Aggressive Buyers - Aggressive Sellers).
    - **Delta Shift**: Rapid change from negative to positive at a support zone $\rightarrow$ Bullish Trigger.
- **Absorption**: High volume nodes with minimal price movement. Signals a "Hidden Wall" where institutions are absorbing all opposite-side orders.
- **Stacked Imbalances**: 3+ levels of aggressive dominance $\rightarrow$ Strong directional momentum.

### 3. The Dynamic Filter: VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
- **Fair Value Baseline**: Institutional benchmark.
- **Directional Bias**: 
    - Price $>$ VWAP $\rightarrow$ Longs have the edge.
    - Price $<$ VWAP $\rightarrow$ Shorts have the edge.
- **Absorption Confirmation**: If price tests VWAP and the DOM shows aggressive selling being absorbed by a resilient bid stack $\rightarrow$ High-conviction Long Entry.

## 🎯 Operational Sequence
1. **Identify Zone**: Price enters a prior HVN or Value Area boundary.
2. **Check Bias**: Is price relative to VWAP favorable?
3. **Confirm Trigger**: 
    - Look for **Absorption** (Large volume, no move) $\rightarrow$ Reversal.
    - Look for **Delta Shift** (Power flip) $\rightarrow$ Entry.
4. **Target**: Next LVN (accelerated move) or HVN (target zone).
