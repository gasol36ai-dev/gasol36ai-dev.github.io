# Virtual Power Plants (VPP)

## Definition
A Virtual Power Plant (VPP) is a cloud-based distributed power plant that aggregates the capacities of various DERs (batteries, solar, controllable loads) to function as a single, reliable power plant on the energy market.

## Mechanics
- **Aggregation**: Combining hundreds or thousands of small batteries or solar arrays.
- **Dispatch**: The VPP operator sends signals to the DERs to discharge energy during peak demand or absorb it during surplus.
- **Arbitrage**: Buying energy when cheap and selling it when expensive to generate revenue for the prosumers.

## Benefits
- **Grid Stability**: Provides frequency regulation and peak shaving.
- **Economic Incentive**: Allows small-scale owners to monetize their energy assets.
- **Scalability**: Increases the penetration of renewables without needing massive new power plants.

## Role in Energy Sovereignty
VPPs allow sovereign energy communities to interact with the macro-economy on their own terms, turning energy independence into a financial asset.
