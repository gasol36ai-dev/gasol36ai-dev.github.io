# Macro Intelligence Report - 2026_05_07

## 1. Global Macro Overview
As of May 2026, the global macro landscape is characterized by **regional divergence** in inflation and monetary policy, driven by differing fiscal impacts (e.g., US tariffs, OBBBA) and labor market dynamics.

### Key Regional Dynamics:
- **United States:** Resilient growth driven by consumption, OBBBA fiscal stimulus, and massive AI investment (~$500B). **Inflation remains sticky** (Core CPI ~2.5-3.2%) due to tariff-driven goods price increases and wage pressures. The Fed is likely on an **extended pause** or slow easing, with markets pricing rates near 3%.
- **Eurozone:** Stable but slow growth. Inflation is moderating toward the 2% target, supported by declining goods prices and weakening wage growth. ECB is on hold.
- **China:** Structural headwinds (shrinking labor force, property weakness) offsetting AI-driven gains. Inflation remains near deflationary levels due to weak domestic demand and industrial overcapacity.
- **Japan:** Modestly above trend growth. Rising wages and automation investment are driving inflation toward 2%. **Bank of Japan (BoJ) expected to hike rates at least twice.**
- **Emerging Markets:** Mixed. Latin America sees falling inflation and credible central banks. EM Asia (ex-China/India) faces currency weakness and inflation pressure.

## 2. Inflation & Monetary Policy Divergence
The "common global inflation driver" era has ended.
- **Inflation Gap:** A significant gap is opening between the US (accelerating/sticky) and Europe (moderating).
- **Policy Divergence:**
    - **Hawkish/Hold:** US Fed (due to inflation/growth), BoJ (due to wage/inflation normalization).
    - **Dovish/Cutting:** Bank of England (labor weakness), Philippines (BSP), Poland/Hungary.
    - **Neutral/Hold:** ECB.

| **US Tariffs $\uparrow$** | US Inflation $\uparrow$ | Increases cost of imported goods, driving headline/core CPI. |
| **US Tariffs $\uparrow$** | China/EM Export $\downarrow$ | Dampens export-led growth in trade-sensitive regions. |

| **BoJ Rate Hikes $\uparrow$** | JPY $\uparrow$ | Narrows interest rate differentials, attracting carry trade unwinding. |

## 4. Macro Watchlist (High Impact)
- [ ] **US Core PCE Inflation:** Target < 2%; current trajectory suggests stickiness.
- [ ] **US 10Y Treasury Yield:** Monitoring for levels > 4.5% (risk of rapid tightening).
- [ ] **BoJ Policy Rate:** Monitoring for the first hike signal (JPY volatility).
- [ ] **China Property Sector Stability:** Key for EM/Global growth contagion.
- [ ] **Geopolitical Conflict (Middle East/Iran):** Monitoring energy supply disruptions.
- [ ] **US Tariff Implementation:** Measuring real-world impact on US CPI.

## 5. Geopolitical & Systemic Risks
- **US Policy Uncertainty:** Fed leadership transition (Kevin Warsh) and tariff volatility.
- **AI Bubble Risk:** Potential for overinvestment/valuation correction.
- **Public Debt:** Unsustainable fiscal paths in major DM economies.
