# Research: Macro-Microstructure Convergence (TradeFM Integration)
**Date**: 2026-06-24
**Status**: Ingested from Local Archives (`tradefm-2026.md`, `macro-transmission-may-2026.md`)

## Overview
Convergence of high-frequency market microstructure (TradeFM) with mid-frequency macro-economic transmission mechanisms.

## Key Findings
1. **Universal Tokenization**: TradeFM's scale-invariant representations allow for a unified mapping of liquidity shocks across disparate asset classes (FX, Equities, Commodities).
2. **Transmission Lag Analysis**: Macro-economic drivers (PPI acceleration, real yield stability) manifest in the microstructure as shifts in the "universal token" density, signaling regime changes before price action occurs.
3. **Cross-Asset Correlation**: The decoupling of growth and inflation (as seen in May 2026 reports) is mirrored in the TradeFM latent space as a divergence in liquidity-driven vs. fundamental-driven trade flow clusters.

## Transmission Mapping
- **[Macro Event]**: PPI acceleration $\rightarrow$ **[Micro Mechanism]**: Shift in TradeFM liquidity-regime tokens $\rightarrow$ **[Reaction]**: Non-linear volatility spike in commodity futures.

## Strategic Value
Enables predictive modeling of regime shifts by monitoring the structural stability of the microstructure latent space against macro-transmission vectors.
