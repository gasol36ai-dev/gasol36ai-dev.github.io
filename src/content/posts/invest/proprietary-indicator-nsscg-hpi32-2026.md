## 1. Conceptual Framework

The **NSSCG** is a convergent sovereign judgment indicator that monitors the intersection of three mutually-dependent infrastructure layers required for a nation-state or corporation to operate **fully sovereign AI** — defined as AI capability that is:

1. **Physically producible** (domestic or allied-source silicon)
2. **Cognitively auditable** (symbolic reasoning layer enables formal verification of AI decisions)
3. **Edge-deployed without cloud dependency** (inference executed locally, no data leaves boundary)

The absence of any single layer collapses the sovereignty claim. A nation with domestic foundry (V3) but no edge inference capability (V1) remains cloud-AI dependent. A nation with edge AI but no formal governance layer (V2) faces regulatory exclusion from EU/G7 markets. A nation with formal AI governance but no physical compute production is permanently supply-chain-vulnerable.

**Innovation Logic**: The NSSCG treats these three vectors as a **conjunctive gate** — all three must clear simultaneously for sovereign AI to be operationally realized. The indicator fires (crosses into LOCK) when a nation simultaneously achieves:

- **V1 (Physical-Choice)**: ≥1 domestically-manufactured or allied-source edge-AI ASIC at ≥ 10 TOPS/W, in volume production (≥ 100K units/month)
- **V2 (Governance-Choice)**: Active neuro-symbolic AI governance framework deployed in ≥1 critical infrastructure sector, with formal verification of ≥50% of AI decisions required by law
- **V3 (Infrastructure-Choice)**: ≥1 domestic or allied-source advanced packaging facility (2.5D/3D IC, CoWoS/Foveros class), or secured long-term supply agreement with allied fab
