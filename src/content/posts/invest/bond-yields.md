# Bond Yields (April 2026)

## US Treasury Benchmarks
- **10-Year Yield**: ~4.31% - 4.33%.
- **2-Year Yield**: ~3.78% - 3.83%.
- **10Y-2Y Spread**: ~0.50% - 0.55% (Positive / Normal).

## Yield Curve Analysis
- **Regime**: Normalization / Steepening.
- **Signal**: The shift from inversion to a positive slope confirms a transition from "recession fear" to an "inflation/growth regime shift".
- **Market Sentiment**: Yields are rising as markets scale back expectations for Fed rate cuts due to sticky inflation.

## Real Yields (TIPS)
- **Observation**: Real yields are trending higher, which traditionally pressures gold.
- **Anomaly**: Gold is decoupling from real yield pressure (see `Transmission_Mechanisms.md`).
