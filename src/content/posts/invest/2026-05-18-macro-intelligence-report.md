# Macro Intelligence Report - 2026-05-18

## 📅 Date: 2026-05-18

## 🌍 Executive Summary: The "Warsh Trade" Reversal & Energy-Inflation Shock
The global macro landscape has shifted dramatically from an "easing expectation" regime to a "hawkish uncertainty" regime. The anticipated rate-cut cycle under the incoming Fed leadership (Kevin Warsh) is being unwound by a combination of robust US growth and an escalating energy-inflation shock driven by the conflict in the Middle East (Iran/Strait of Hormuz).

**Core Theme**: *The Return of Inflation Volatility & Yield Surges.*

---

## 🏛️ Central Bank Policy Divergence

### 🇺🇸 United States (Federal Reserve)
- **Stance**: Hawkish Hold.
- **Context**: Inflation remains sticky (CPI 3.8%, PPI 6%). The Fed is debating a shift from easing to a neutral stance. 
- **Outlook**: Market pricing for cuts has been significantly unwound. There is a growing probability of "higher for longer" or even minor hikes if energy-led inflation persists.

### 🇪🇺 European Union (ECB)
- **Stance**: Data-Dependent / Leaning Hawkish.
- **Context**: ECB is facing an "inflationary pincer": rising energy prices (upside risk) vs. slowing growth (downside risk).
- **Outlook**: Markets are pricing 2-3 rate hikes by year-end. A June hike is increasingly likely if energy-led headline inflation does not moderate.

### 🇯🇵 Japan (BoJ)
- **Stance**: Normalization Path.
- **Context**: BoJ is signaling a potential hike as early as next month to combat inflation risks stemming from global energy volatility.
- **Outlook**: Normalization is expected to continue, creating pressure on the Yen and potentially unwinding carry trades.

---

## 📈 Bond Markets & Yield Dynamics
The "Warsh Trade" (betting on cuts) has collapsed.
- **US 30Y Yield**: ~5.12% (Surging on inflation/term premium concerns).
- **US 10Y Yield**: ~4.60% (Benchmark for global discounting).
- **US 2Y Yield**: ~4.08% (Reflecting Fed policy uncertainty).
- **Regime**: Steepening yield curve expectations are being driven by inflation/growth optimism rather than easing.

## 🛢️ Commodity & Geopolitical Risk
- **Energy**: The conflict in the Middle East (Iran) is a primary driver of volatility. Brent crude is under intense pressure, acting as a structural floor for headline inflation.
