# Macro Intelligence Report - May 2026

## 1. Central Bank Policy Landscape

### Federal Reserve (USA)
- **Status:** Hawkish/Wait-and-See.
- **Recent Data:** April CPI (3.8% YoY, 0.6% MoM) exceeded expectations, reinforcing the "no rate cuts in 2026" narrative.
- **Yield Environment:** US 10Y yields climbing (~4.45%), 2Y yields ~3.98%.
- **Key Transition:** Kevin Warsh expected to assume Chairmanship on May 15, 2026. Policy debate is shifting from "when to cut" to "whether to hold or hike" in response to energy-driven inflation.

### European Central Bank (ECB)
- **Status:** Data-dependent/Cautious Hawkishness.
- **Recent Data:** Rates held steady in April, but energy price shocks from the Middle East have intensified upside inflation risks.
- **Outlook:** Consensus points toward a June rate hike to combat energy-driven inflation spikes.

### Bank of Japan (BoJ)
- **Status:** Normalization Path.
- **Recent Data:** Signaling a potential rate hike in June. Inflation risks are being driven by crude oil prices.
- **Policy Stance:** Increasing divergence within the board; significant move towards normalization to address upside inflation risks.

## 2. Inflation & Commodity Dynamics
- **Energy Shock:** The Middle East conflict (specifically threats to the Strait of Hormuz) has pushed Brent crude oil above $100/bbl.
- **Inflation Drivers:** 
    - **Headline:** Driven by energy and shelter (one-time adjustment in US).
    - **Core:** Rising due to service sector inflation and AI-driven capex (cloud/software).
- **Correlation:** High oil prices $\rightarrow$ Higher inflation expectations $\rightarrow$ Sustained high real yields.

## 3. Geopolitical Risk Matrix
- **Primary Conflict:** Middle East/Iran.
- **Main Transmission Channel:** Energy (Oil/Gas) $\rightarrow$ Inflation $\rightarrow$ Monetary Policy.
- **Secondary Channel:** Geopolitical uncertainty $\rightarrow$ Flight to Quality (Gold/USD) $\rightarrow$ Yield Volatility.
