# Macro Regime: May 2026 (Strategic Context)

## Key Drivers
- **Fed Leadership Transition**: Transitioning from Powell (expiring May 2026). High volatility risk in yields/USD.
- **Central Bank Divergence**: Fed (gradual easing) vs. BoJ (normalization) vs. ECB (stability).
- **Geopolitical Tension**: Middle East conflict (Brent $85+ risk).
- **Regime Shift**: "Macro Reset" towards slower, resilient growth. India as growth engine.
- **Inflation Dynamics**: Sticky services inflation vs. goods disinflation. Second-round effects emerging.

## Transmission Mechanisms
- **Fed Transition** → ↑ Volatility in USD/Yields → Impact on high-duration growth.
- **Middle East Conflict** → ↑ Oil → ↑ Inflation → ↑ Yields → ↓ Asset Valuations.
- **Central Bank Divergence** → FX volatility & Carry Trade shifts.
- **Real Yield Breakdown** → Structural demand for gold overrides traditional rate logic.
- **USD Strength** → Emerging Market pressure via dollar-denominated debt.
