# Proprietary Indicator: Kinetic-Sovereignty Divergence Index (KSDI)
**Date**: 2026-07-04
**Domain Convergence**: Orbital Edge/LEO Mesh $\cap$ Critical Mineral Mid-Stream $\cap$ Neuromorphic Computing
**Classification**: High-Order Strategic Hedge / Sovereign Risk Indicator

## 1. Conceptual Architecture
The KSDI measures the delta between the terrestrial concentration of critical mineral processing (Mid-Stream Vulnerability) and the technical capability of non-terrestrial decentralized compute (Orbital Sovereignty). 

Traditionally, supply chain visibility is gated by terrestrial ground stations and centralized cloud providers, both of which are subject to sovereign jurisdiction and geopolitical interference. The KSDI identifies the "Convergence Gate" where the Orbital Edge mesh becomes capable of hosting a fully autonomous, neuromorphic-driven supply chain ledger that can bypass terrestrial governance for real-time logistics optimization and asset verification.

## 2. The Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### A. The Map (Multi-Dimensional Landscape)
The landscape is defined by the interaction of three primary vectors:

1. **$\text{V}_{mid}$ (Terrestrial Mid-Stream Concentration)**:
   - Measured via the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) of refining and processing capacity for Cobalt, Lithium, and Rare Earth Elements (REE).
   - $\text{V}_{mid} = \sum (s_i^2)$, where $s_i$ is the market share of processing capacity per sovereign entity.

2. **$\text{V}_{edge}$ (Orbital Mesh Compute Density)**:
   - Measured as the ratio of in-situ compute capacity to ground-relay latency.
   - $\text{V}_{edge} = \frac{\text{TFLOPS per LEO Node}}{\text{Average Inter-Satellite Link (ISL) Latency (ms)}}$.

3. **$\text{V}_{neuro}$ (Neuromorphic Inference Efficiency)**:
   - Measured as the energy efficiency of event-based spiking neural networks (SNNs) deployed on orbital nodes for predictive supply chain analysis.
   - $\text{V}_{neuro} = \text{Inferences Per Joule (IPJ)}$.

### B. The Trigger (Non-Linear Threshold)
The KSDI triggers a "Sovereign Shift" signal when terrestrial risk exceeds the threshold of orbital capability.

$$\text{Condition} = (\text{V}_{mid} > 0.45) \land (\text{V}_{edge} > \tau_{latency}) \land (\text{V}_{neuro} > 10^{12} \text{ TOPS/W})$$

**Threshold Logic**:
- $\text{V}_{mid} > 0.45$: Indicates a "Critical Monopoly" state where a single sovereign entity controls $>67\%$ of the mid-stream.
- $\text{V}_{edge} > \tau_{latency}$: Indicates that the LEO mesh can coordinate global logistics without relying on ground-based routing, eliminating "Kill-Switch" risks.
- $\text{V}_{neuro}$: Ensures that the predictive models for supply volatility can run autonomously on the edge without depleting satellite power budgets.

### C. The Lock (Deterministic Reaction)
Upon triggering, the following Strategic Lock is executed:

1. **Immediate Risk Reduction**: 
   - **Decentralized Ledger Migration**: Shift all supply chain "Proof of Origin" and "Proof of Reserve" data from terrestrial clouds to the LEO Mesh.
   - **Sovereign Bypass**: Establish peer-to-peer (P2P) logistics coordination between mine-site (Upstream) and factory (Downstream) via LEO-Neuromorphic nodes, bypassing state-controlled monitoring.

2. **Strategic Capture**:
   - **Orbital Futures Hedging**: Long positions in "Sovereign-Neutral" mineral derivatives backed by the orbital ledger.
   - **Compute-Resource Arbitrage**: Leverage the efficiency of neuromorphic nodes to predict supply shocks $T+30$ days before they manifest in terrestrial price indices.
