# SCPG — Sovereign Cognitive-Physical Autarky Gate

**HPI-40 | Composite Sovereign Resilience Gate | Confidence: High**

## Abstract

The Sovereign Cognitive-Physical Autarky Gate (SCPG) is a proprietary macro judgment indicator that synthesizes three sovereign infrastructure pillars — **Cognitive Autonomy** (SCAA), **Data Archival Sovereignty** (QRBC), and **Physical Logistics Resilience** (DAAL) — into a single decision gate for sovereign risk re-rating. SCPG extends the **Triple Sovereignty Stack Gate** template (from PASG/HPI-15) by replacing CBDC and nuclear baseload with cognitive-AI and biological data storage, creating the first sovereign indicator to integrate **neurosymbolic AI infrastructure**, **quantum-resistant DNA archival**, and **autonomous aerial logistics networks** as a unified resilience vector.

The core thesis: When a nation simultaneously achieves threshold-level capability across all three pillars, geopolitical leverage over that nation becomes structurally non-viable — triggering a non-linear re-rating across sovereign bonds, FX, and defense equities.

## Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### MAP (The Convergence Thesis)

The sovereign resilience premium is determined by the intersection of three capability vectors:

```
SCPG = f(Cognitive_Autonomy, Data_Sovereignty, Logistics_Resilience)
```

Where each vector is measured independently and the gate fires when all three cross threshold simultaneously.

**Pillar 1 — Cognitive Autonomy (SCAA)**

A nation's ability to run end-to-end cognitive workflows (perception → reasoning → planning → action) on domestic infrastructure, without foreign AI cloud APIs, frontier model weights, or symbolic reasoning engine licenses.

*Measurement*: 
- `CA_t = (% defense/government AI workloads on domestic inference stack) / 100`
- Domestic inference stack = LLM + neurosymbolic reasoning engine + agentic memory, all on domestically owned GPU clusters.
- Foreign API dependency = any sovereign workflow requiring OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or Chinese cloud AI APIs.

**Pillar 2 — Data Archival Sovereignty (QRBC)**

A nation's ability to store strategic data (semiconductor IP, healthcare records, defense telemetry, national archive) in a medium that is immune to quantum cryptanalysis, EMP attacks, and foreign physical access.

*Measurement*:
- `DA_t = (petabytes of strategic data archived in quantum-resistant DNA storage) / (total petabytes of strategic data requiring >10-year retention)`
- PQC integration: All DNA read/write interfaces must use NIST-standardized PQC (ML-KEM + ML-DSA).
- Target: 80% of classified/sensitive long-term data in QRBC format.

**Pillar 3 — Physical Logistics Resilience (DAAL)**

A nation's ability to maintain critical supply chains (medical, semiconductor, food, energy) when conventional infrastructure (ports, roads, shipping lanes) is disrupted or denied.

*Measurement*:
- `LR_t = (autonomous aerial logistics capacity in strategic corridors / total critical logistics demand) × EW_hardening_score`
- eVTOL cargo capacity (long-range, >400 km range, >1 MT payload)
- DePIN coordination layer active and operational
- Multi-modal fallback: sea-air intermodal enabled.

### TRIGGER (The Convergence Mechanism)

**Gate fires when ALL three conditions are simultaneously met:**

```
CA_t >= 0.70 (70% of sovereign AI workloads on domestic stack)
AND
DA_t >= 0.50 (50% of strategic data in QRBC archival)
AND
LR_t >= 0.60 (60% autonomous logistics coverage of critical corridors)
AND
Convergence_Time <= 36 months
```

**Convergence_Time** is the estimated time for all three pillars to reach threshold simultaneously, calculated from current infrastructure investment pace and policy execution track record.

**Critical Condition**: If any single pillar is below threshold, the gate does not fire. The sovereign discount premium remains elevated because there is still a viable leverage vector.

### LOCK (The Market Reaction)

When SCPG fires, the following market reactions are triggered:

#### Bond Markets
- **Sovereign bond spread compression**: 20-40 bps reduction vs. baseline geopolitical risk premium.
- **Mechanism**: Geopolitical leverage non-viable → tail risk premium eliminated → spreads compress to pure credit-risk pricing.
- **Duration sensitivity**: Long-duration sovereign bonds (10Y+) experience the largest price appreciation.

#### FX Markets
- **Currency re-rating**: TWD (or equivalent) prices in "cognitive reserve currency" premium.
- **Mechanism**: Domestic AI economy + sovereign data infrastructure creates demand for domestic currency independent of trade surplus.
- **FX vol reduction**: Autarky in strategic sectors reduces FX volatility caused by geopolitical risk.

#### Equity Markets
- **Defense sector re-rating**: Domestic defense AI and logistics autonomy companies price in structural demand premium (government mandate for domestic supply).
- **Semiconductor premium sustained**: TSMC and domestic chip makers benefit from QRBC archival of process IP, reducing foreign leverage narrative.
- **eVTOL/aerial logistics**: DAAL infrastructure companies re-rate as "strategic national assets" with sovereign pricing floor.
