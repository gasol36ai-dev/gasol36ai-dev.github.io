# Sovereign Cognitive-Debt Ratio (SCDR)

## Overview
The **Sovereign Cognitive-Debt Ratio (SCDR)** is a proprietary judgment indicator designed to assess the stability of sovereign entities by synthesizing **Sovereign Debt Sustainability** (Macroeconomics) and **Neuro-Symbolic Governance Efficiency** (AI/Policy Theory). 

Unlike traditional debt-to-GDP ratios, the SCDR measures the *capacity* of a state to manage its liabilities using the cognitive efficiency of its governance architecture. It posits that debt sustainability is not merely a function of cash flow, but of the logical coherence and execution speed of the governing symbolic systems (laws, policies) integrated with neural-adaptive responses (market data, real-time feedback).

---

## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

### 1. Map (The Landscape)
The SCDR maps a two-dimensional coordinate system:
- **X-Axis (Fiscal Gravity):** The traditional debt-to-GDP ratio adjusted for interest rate volatility.
- **Y-Axis (Cognitive Coherence):** A metric derived from Neuro-Symbolic Governance. This measures the alignment between "Symbolic Goals" (legislative intent/constitutional mandates) and "Neural Outputs" (actual policy implementation and economic outcomes).

**The Map identifies three zones:**
- **Synergistic Zone:** High debt but high cognitive coherence (Debt is managed efficiently via AI-optimized allocation).
- **Fragile Zone:** Low debt but low cognitive coherence (Inefficient governance leads to wasted resources).
- **Critical Zone:** High debt and low cognitive coherence (High risk of systemic collapse).

### 2. Trigger (The Catalyst)
The SCDR indicator triggers a "Judgment Signal" when a **Cognitive-Fiscal Divergence** occurs.

**Trigger Condition:**
The signal is activated when the *Cognitive Coherence* score drops by >15% within a single fiscal quarter while *Fiscal Gravity* remains stagnant or increases. This indicates that the governing system is losing the "intellectual capacity" to manage its debt, regardless of the absolute debt level.

### 3. Lock (The Confirmation)
To prevent false positives caused by temporary political noise, the SCDR employs a **Structural Lock**.

**The Lock Mechanism:**
A signal is "Locked" and becomes an actionable judgment only if the following conditions are met:
1. **Cross-Asset Correlation:** A simultaneous increase in Credit Default Swap (CDS) spreads of >50bps.
2. **Governance Lag:** A documented increase in the time-to-implementation for critical fiscal adjustments (measured as the delta between legislative proposal and executive action).
3. **Algorithmic Breach:** The "Neuro-Symbolic Gap" exceeds a threshold where symbolic rules are consistently overridden by chaotic neural-market reactions without corrective feedback.

## Application in Investment Logic
When the SCDR is **Locked-Red**, the proprietary judgment is to initiate a short position on the sovereign's long-term bonds and hedge via "Cognitive-Safe" assets (assets tied to entities with high governance coherence).
