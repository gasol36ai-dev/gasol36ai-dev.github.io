# Proprietary Indicator: Compute-Sovereign Liquidity Divergence (CSLD) 2026

## Overview
The **Compute-Sovereign Liquidity Divergence (CSLD)** is a high-order judgment indicator designed to detect systemic instability at the intersection of the emerging "Compute Standard" and sovereign macro-financial regimes. It identifies periods where the physical availability and demand for compute (driven by DePIN and Orbital Nexus) decouple from the financial valuation of compute-backed sovereign assets.

## Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### 1. Map (The Variables)
The indicator maps the relationship between three primary vectors:
- **Vector A: Physical Compute Liquidity ($L_c$)**: Real-time availability and cost-of-access for compute resources across the Orbital-Terrestrial Nexus (OTECN) and DePIN layers.
- **Vector B: Sovereign Compute Valuation ($V_{sc}$)**: The market valuation and liquidity of sovereign assets backed by compute-standard reserves, as detected via Neuro-Symbolic Macro-Econometric (NSME) regime analysis.
- **Vector C: Energy-Compute Scarcity Gradient ($\nabla E_c$)**: The rate of change in the energy-to-compute conversion efficiency and availability across strategic nodes.

### 2. Trigger (The Inflection Point)
A CSLD signal is triggered when:
$$\text{Divergence} = | \log(L_c) - \log(V_{sc}) | > \tau_{\text{threshold}}$$
**AND**
$$\frac{\partial (\nabla E_c)}{\partial t} > \text{Critical Threshold}$$

**Qualitative Trigger Conditions:**
- **The "Compute Squeeze"**: A rapid spike in $L_c$ costs (physical scarcity) without a corresponding appreciation in $V_{sc}$ (financial lag), suggesting a breakdown in the "Compute Standard" parity.
- **The "Regime Mismatch"**: NSME identifies a shift to a high-inflation/low-growth regime while compute-backed assets maintain a "low-volatility" illusion, creating a massive hidden liquidity gap.

### 3. Lock (The Actionable Response)
Upon a validated CSLD signal, the following "Lock" protocols are activated:
- **Immediate Hedge**: Automatic shift into energy-compute futures and localized orbital-compute reserves.
- **Settlement Pivot**: Transition of high-value sovereign transactions from traditional debt-denominated rails to compute-denominated settlement (QR-SFR) to bypass the liquidity divergence.
- **Resource Rationing**: Execution of "Compute-Sovereignty" protocols, prioritizing strategic AI-infrastructure and defense-compute over commercial/retail demand.

## Strategic Value
CSLD provides an early warning for "Compute-Induced Sovereign Defaults," where a nation's compute-backed wealth is rendered illiquid due to physical energy/infrastructure failures that the financial markets have not yet priced in.
