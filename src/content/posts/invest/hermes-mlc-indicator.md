# Hermes Manifold Liquidity Curvature (MLC)

## 💡 Conceptual Origin: Information Geometry & Spatial Intelligence
The **Manifold Liquidity Curvature (MLC)** indicator moves beyond linear volatility and standard order flow. It is inspired by **Information Geometry** and **Neuromorphic World Models**, treating the market state not as a series of price points, but as a continuous, high-dimensional **manifold** of liquidity and information.

In this paradigm, a "market crash" or "flash rally" is not just a price move, but a **topological rupture** where the manifold's curvature becomes infinite (a singularity).

## ⚙️ Logic & Heuristic
The MLC monitors the "geometric stress" of the liquidity substrate. It uses three primary inputs:

1.  **Topology of Order Flow (The Manifold)**: The relational connectivity between different asset classes and their respective liquidity pools (the "Spectral Gap").
2.  **Geometric Shear (The Stress)**: The rate at which liquidity is "deforming" or "thinning" relative to price velocity (combining *Shear* and *Drift* from the CFO's LGDG framework).
3.  **Information Density (The Curvature)**: The concentration of order flow events in the temporal-price space (using *Spatio-Temporal Reasoning* concepts).

### Formula (Heuristic):
$$\text{MLC Score} = \text{Curvature}(\mathcal{M}) \propto \frac{\nabla_{\text{flow}} \cdot \text{Shear}}{\text{Spectral Gap}}$$

Where:
- $\nabla_{\text{flow}}$ is the divergence of order flow.
- $\text{Shear}$ is the geometric deformation of the liquidity volume profile.
- $\text{Spectral Gap}$ represents market connectedness.

## 🎯 Signal Conditions
- **Low Curvature (Stable Regime)**: The manifold is "flat." Price moves are organic, liquidity is distributed, and correlations are stable.
- **High Curvature (Pre-Rupture)**: The manifold is "tightly curved." Liquidity is concentrating in extreme narrowness (low volume nodes/high shear). This indicates a **topological tension** where the next move will likely be a "jump" or "teleportation" (discontinuous price gap).

## 📈 Strategic Application (The "Rupture" Trade)
- **Identify**: Look for rising MLC scores in concentrated sectors (e.g., Reshoring/Defense) during periods of macro-economic "Policy Paralysis."
- **Trigger**: An MLC spike followed by a "Spectral Gap" collapse (sudden loss of correlation) signals a **Topological Rupture**.
- **Action**: Execute aggressive contrarian or directional bets anticipating the "jump" (discontinuity) before the wider market reacts.
