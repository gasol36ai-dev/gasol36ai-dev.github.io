## 1. Executive Summary

This transition occurs when the agent achieves simultaneous criticality in three non-linear dimensions: **Signal Transduction (Sensing)**, **Kinetic Execution (Acting)**, and **Metabolic Autonomy (Sustaining)**. 

Unlike single-domain indicators, BSKN identifies a "Systemic Phase Shift" where the agent's physical existence is no longer a bottleneck to its intelligence, and its operational capacity is decoupled from all external infrastructure.

The BSKN is triggered by the joint crossing of three critical thresholds:

**Result**: The agent perceives and processes physical environmental changes at a speed matching its internal silicon logic, eliminating "Perceptual Lag."

### Pillar II: The Kinetic Threshold (Acting)
**Domain**: Molecular Protein-Based Actuators (MPBA)
**Criticality**: $\text{SKT} > 1$
**Mechanism**: The use of de novo designed synthetic protein folding to achieve sub-millisecond reflexive loops.
**Result**: The agent can execute corrective physical actions faster than the environmental entropy changes, achieving "Sovereign Kinetics."

### Pillar III: The Endurance Surplus (Sustaining)
**Domain**: Autonomous Energy-Sovereign Skin (AESS)
**Criticality**: $\text{SE} \ge 1.5$
**Mechanism**: Implementation of a Synthetic Metabolic Cycle (SMC) producing $\phi$-Triphosphate, ensuring energy harvest exceeds operational and repair expenditure by $\ge 50\%$.
**Result**: Absolute decoupling from power grids; the agent becomes a self-sustaining energy node.
