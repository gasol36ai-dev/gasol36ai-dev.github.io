# Macro-to-Asset Transmission Library - 2026-05-20

## 1. Energy-Inflation-Rate Chain (The "Geopolitical Shock" Pattern)
- **Trigger**: [Strait of Hormuz Blockade / Iran Conflict]
- **Mechanism**: [\uparrow Oil Prices (Brent \$100+)] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow Headline Inflation (PCE/CPI)] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow Central Bank Hawkishness] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow Sovereign Bond Yields] $\rightarrow$ [\downarrow Growth Asset Valuations (Tech/Growth)].
- **Current Status**: **Active (High Volatility)**. Energy prices are surging towards $110/bbl, sustaining high headline inflation and forcing a "Hawkish Hold" from major central banks.

## 2. Monetary Policy Divergence Chain (The "FX/Liquidity" Pattern)
- **Trigger**: [Fed/ECB/BoJ Policy Divergence]
- **Mechanism**: [\uparrow US Rates relative to ECB/BoJ] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow USD Strength] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow Dollar-denominated Debt Servicing Costs for EMs] $\rightarrow$ [\downarrow EM Capital Flows / \uparrow EM Volatility].
- **Current Status**: **Active**. Fed (Hawkish Hold) vs. ECB (Potential June Hike) vs. BoJ (Normalization/June Hike). This creates significant USD/JPY volatility and capital flow shifts.

## 3. Structural Hedge Chain (The "Systemic Risk" Pattern)
- **Trigger**: [US Credit Rating Downgrade / De-dollarization / Geopolitical Instability]
- **Mechanism**: [Loss of Trust in USD-denominated Assets] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow Demand for Non-USD/Non-Sovereign Reserves (Gold)] $\rightarrow$ [\uparrow Gold Price (even if Real Yields rise)].
- **Current Status**: **Structural (Confirmed Decoupling)**. Gold is behaving as a systemic risk and monetary debasement hedge, decoupling from the traditional inverse correlation with real yields.
