# Bio-Digital Asset Tokenization (BDAT) (2026)
**Density**: High-Density
**Domain**: Synthetic Biology x RWA Tokenization

## 1. Executive Summary
BDAT is the process of representing biological assets—ranging from proprietary genetic sequences (DNA) to synthetic organoids and bio-manufactured materials—as on-chain Real World Assets (RWAs).

## 2. The BDAT Framework
### 2.1 Genomic NFTs (gNFTs)
Each unique, synthesized genetic sequence is minted as an NFT. The token contains the sequence data, ownership rights, and usage licenses.

### 2.2 Programmable Bio-Royalties
Smart contracts automatically distribute royalties whenever a tokenized genetic sequence is used in a commercial pharmaceutical or agricultural product.

### 2.3 Bio-Equity Pools
Fractional ownership of "Bio-Foundries" or "Organoid Banks," allowing investors to hedge against biological breakthroughs.

## 3. Technical Convergence
- **DNA Data Storage**: Using the asset itself (DNA) as the cold-storage medium for the token's metadata.
- **Oracles for Bio-Verification**: Using CRISPR-based sensors as "Bio-Oracles" that trigger smart contract payments when a biological milestone (e.g., protein fold success) is reached.

## 4. Ethical & Legal Frontier
- **Bio-Sovereignty**: Conflict between patent law and tokenized ownership of genetic material.
- **Genetic Stability**: The risk of "Asset Decay" where the biological entity mutates, altering the underlying value of the token.
