# Strategic Resource Decoupling Gate (SRDG)

## Concept Summary

The **Strategic Resource Decoupling Gate (SRDG)** identifies **high-conviction reallocation entry points** when three distinct phenomena align:

1. **Macro**: The KKR "Security of Everything" regime is confirmed active — nations/corporations are structurally shifting investment toward domestic resilience assets over global efficiency assets
2. **Monetary**: The Fed/ECB are in Policy Paralysis — unable to cut (inflationary) or hike (growth-destructive), sustaining an elevated rate floor
3. **Microstructure**: Clustered institutional flow is detectable accumulating in "Strategic Resilience" asset categories (domestic infrastructure, energy independence, defense, reshoring plays) — confirming the macro thesis is being acted upon by institutional capital NOW

**The gate fires only when theory (Macro + Monetary) aligns with observable evidence (Microstructure).** Most macro-based indicators miss the timing because they rely on top-down regime detection alone. SRDG adds the institutional flow dimension to confirm the regime shift is being actively priced.
