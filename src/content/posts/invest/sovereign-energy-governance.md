# Sovereign Energy Governance

## Definition
Sovereign Energy Governance refers to the legal, social, and organizational frameworks used by communities to manage their own energy assets and decision-making processes.

## Governance Models
- **Energy Cooperatives**: Member-owned and democratically controlled entities.
- **Community Land Trusts**: Integrating energy infrastructure into shared land ownership.
- **Municipal Energy Utilities**: City-run energy systems decoupled from private corporate interests.
- **Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs)**: Using blockchain for governance and fund allocation.

## Key Challenges
- **Regulatory Hurdles**: Many jurisdictions forbid selling power across property lines.
- **Capital Investment**: High upfront costs for infrastructure.
- **Technical Literacy**: Requirement for community members to understand system limits.

## Role in Energy Sovereignty
Technical infrastructure is useless without a governance model that ensures fair access and sustainable management.
