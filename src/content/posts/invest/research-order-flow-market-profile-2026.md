# Advanced Order Flow & Market Profile (2026)

## The Core Philosophy: Auction Market Theory (AMT)
Trading is viewed as a continuous auction to find "fair value." Price movement is the result of the interaction between aggressive (market) orders and passive (limit) liquidity.

## High-Value Technical Frameworks

### 1. Market Profile (TPO - Time Price Opportunity)
*   **Value Area (VA):** The range where 70% of the time was spent.
*   **Point of Control (POC):** The "fairest" price (most time spent).
*   **Initial Balance (IB):** The range established in the first hour of trading; sets the session's structural tone.
*   **Day Types:**
    *   *Trend Day:* Narrow, elongated profile.
    *   *Normal Variation Day:* IB expansion.
    *   *Neutral Day:* D-shaped, rotating around POC.

### 2. Order Flow Microstructure
*   **Footprint Charts:** Reveal volume at specific price levels.
*   **Imbalances:** When aggressive buying/selling volume is $\ge 3\text{x}$ the opposite side.
*   **Absorption:** Large volume traded at a level without price movement $\rightarrow$ signals institutional defense (e.g., "Iceberg orders").
*   **Delta Divergence:** Price makes a new high, but Cumulative Delta makes a lower high $\rightarrow$ strong signal for reversal.

### 3. Advanced Integration: Composite Profiles & Inventory
*   **Composite Profiles:** Merging multiple days of TPO to identify institutional "Value" zones.
*   **Overnight Inventory:** Tracking whether participants are net long or short heading into the session to predict opening behavior.
*   **The "Super POC":** When the TPO POC and Volume POC align $\rightarrow$ acts as a massive gravitational magnet for price.

## Strategic Setups (Probabilistic)
| Setup | Trigger | Confirmation | Win Rate (Est) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Absorption Fade** | Price hits key level + large volume on Footprint | Price reverses from level | 72% |
| **Delta Divergence** | Price new extreme + Delta divergence | Break back into range | 68% |
| **Stacked Imbalances** | 3+ consecutive imbalances in one direction | Breakout from consolidation | 64% |
| **LVN Breakout** | Price enters Low Volume Node | Rapid traversal of node | High |

## Transmission Mapping: Micro-Flow $\rightarrow$ Macro-Trend
`[Aggressive Order Imbalance] -> [Absorption/Acceptance at Value Area] -> [TPO Value Migration] -> [Macro Trend Shift]`
