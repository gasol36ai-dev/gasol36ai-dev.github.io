# Gold-Real Yield Divergence

A structural break in the traditional inverse correlation between Gold and US Real Yields has emerged in 2026.

## The Traditional Model (Broken)
Historically, `Real Yields $\uparrow \rightarrow$ Gold $\downarrow$`. This was based on the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset.

## The 2026 "Trust Deficit" Regime
Gold is now rising simultaneously with real yields (positive correlation in some windows), signaling a shift in driver:
- **Systemic Trust Deficit**: Investors are treating gold as a hedge against systemic failure and currency erosion rather than just an inflation hedge.
- **Central Bank Hoarding**: Massive accumulation by non-Western central banks to reduce USD dependency.
- **Permanent Protection**: A shift toward "real assets" as permanent protection in a higher-inflation, higher-volatility regime.

## The "Paradox Spread"
When `Gold $\uparrow$ AND Real Yields $\uparrow$`, it confirms a **Structural Bull Market** driven by trust deficits rather than rate logic.
