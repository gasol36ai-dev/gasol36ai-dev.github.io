# Sovereign Micro-Energy Autarky: SMRs & Micro-Fusion

## Executive Summary
The transition from centralized energy grids to decentralized, high-density power sources (SMRs and Micro-Fusion) is driven by the exponential energy demands of AI compute and the strategic need for energy independence. This shift creates a regime of "Energy Autarky" where compute clusters are decoupled from state utility volatility and systemic grid failures.
