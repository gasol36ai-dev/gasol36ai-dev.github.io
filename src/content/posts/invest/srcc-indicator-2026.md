# Proprietary Indicator: SRCC — Sovereign Robotics Carbon-Compute Gate

## 1. Overview
The **SRCC** is a 3-domain synthesis indicator designed to detect the "structural ceiling" for humanoid robot fleet scaling. It identifies the inflection point where the expansion of physical AI fleets is throttled not by hardware cost (BOM) or software capability, but by the intersection of **Sovereign Compute constraints**, **Energy-Intensity**, and **Carbon Debt liabilities**.

## 2. Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### 🗺️ Map (The Constraint Triangle)
- **FEI (Fleet Energy Intensity)**: The total operational energy demand per 1,000 units of deployed humanoid robots, adjusted for local energy grid efficiency.
- **SCQ (Sovereign Compute Quota)**: The available, compliant, and data-resident compute capacity within a nation's sovereign AI stack, specifically the "Inference-Sovereignty" headroom.
- **CDL (Carbon Debt Liability)**: The projected cost to offset the total embodied (manufacturing) and operational emissions of the fleet using "financial-grade" high-durability removals (CDR).

### ⚡ Trigger (The Scaling Bottleneck)
The SRCC signal triggers when the fleet's appetite for energy and compliant compute exceeds the national sovereign capacity, and the cost of "cleaning" that growth exceeds corporate ESG thresholds.

**Formula**:
`[FEI > SCQ_Threshold] AND [CDL > ESG_Cap] AND [Regulatory_Mandate = "Strict Sovereignty"]`

### 🔒 Lock (Investment Response)
**Full Signal: OVERWEIGHT 'Sovereign Efficiency' assets:**
- **Neuromorphic/Edge Hardware**: Low-power, high-efficiency inference chips that lower the FEI.
- **Distributed Sovereign Energy**: SMR (Small Modular Reactor) providers and localized geothermal energy for data-resident compute.
- **High-Durability CDR**: Direct Air Capture (DAC) and Mineralization firms that solve the CDL liability.

**Underweight 'Centralized Legacy' assets:**
- **Non-Sovereign Hyperscalers**: Clouds that lack domestic residency or compliant sovereign stacks.
- **Inefficient Silicon**: Hardware with high power-per-token/action ratios.
- **Nature-Based Offsets**: Low-durability credits that fail the "Financial-Grade" audit for AI Carbon Debt.

## 3. Strategic Value
SRCC allows investors to predict the "crash" of humanoid robotics hype cycles. It identifies the exact moment when the "Robot-as-a-Service" (RaaS) model hits a physical and regulatory wall, forcing a pivot from "growth-at-all-costs" to "efficiency-at-all-costs."
