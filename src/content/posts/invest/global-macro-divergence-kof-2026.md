# Global Macro Divergence — KOF Barometers & PPI Transmission

## Overview
The KOF Global Barometers for May 2026 reveal a critical structural divergence: **Asia/Pacific economies are preparing for a downturn** while **Western Hemisphere economies anticipate a boom**. This bifurcation, combined with accelerating core PPI, creates a complex cross-regional macro transmission environment.

---

## 1. KOF Global Barometers — Bifurcation Signal

**Source**: KOF ETH Zürich, May 2026 (kof-global-barometers-2026.md)

### Key Findings

| Indicator | Signal | Interpretation |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Coincident Barometer | ↑ Rising | Present situation improving globally |
| Leading Barometer | ↓ Falling | Forward-looking outlook weakening |
| Asia/Pacific Region | Preparing for downturn | Positioning defensively |
| Western Hemisphere | Anticipating boom | Positioning offensively |

### The Divergence Problem
A rising Coincident + falling Leading Barometer is the classic **"peak cycle" fingerprint** — current conditions still look good but the forward trajectory is deteriorating. The regional split amplifies this: APAC is already front-running the slowdown, while the Western Hemisphere is still in euphoria.

### Historical Precedent
This pattern historically precedes cross-regional contagion. Western Hemisphere boom expectations become fragile when APAC demand softens, as the latter is the marginal consumer of industrial goods and commodities.

### Transmission Mapping
`[KOF Leading ↓] → [Forward Activity Slowdown] → [APAC Demand Contraction] → [Commodity Pressure] → [Western Hemisphere Profit Warning Cycle]`

## 2. Core PPI Acceleration — Inflation Persistence Signal

**Source**: FX Street Global Macro Transmission Monitor, Week Ending May 15, 2026 (macro-transmission-may-2026.md)

### Key Findings
- **Core PPI accelerated sharply** in the week ending May 15, 2026.
- Inflation pressure is **dominating** over growth confirmation.
- Real yields remained **stable** → limiting upside in metals (gold, copper).

### Mechanism
Stable real yields + accelerating PPI = **inflation premium compression**. Producers face rising input costs but cannot fully pass through because nominal yield stability limits credit-expansion-driven demand.

### Transmission Mapping
`[Core PPI Acceleration] → [Profit Margin Compression] → [Capex Hesitation] → [Growth Deceleration with Sticky Inflation]`

`[Stable Real Yields] → [Limited Metals Upside] → [Commodity Carry Trade Unwind]`

## 3. Synthesized Macro Divergence Framework

### Regime Classification (May 2026)
Based on KOF + PPI signals, the global macro regime is:
- **Type**: Late-cycle divergence with embedded stagflation risk
- **Primary Driver**: PPI-led inflation crowding out real growth
- **Regional Fault Line**: APAC slowdown vs. Western Hemisphere complacency

### Investment Implications

| Asset Class | Implication | Confidence |
|-------------|-------------|------------|
| EM/APAC equities | Defensive positioning justified | High |
| DM/Western equities | Elevated correction risk from complacency | Medium |
| Industrial metals | Capped upside (stable real yields + APAC demand soft) | High |
| Inflation-linked bonds | Structural demand from PPI persistence | Medium |
| Energy | Hormuz + APAC divergence = elevated risk premium | High |
