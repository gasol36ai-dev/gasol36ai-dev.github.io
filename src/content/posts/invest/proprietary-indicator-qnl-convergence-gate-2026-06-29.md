# Proprietary Indicator: The Quantum-Neuromorphic-Logistics (QNL) Convergence Gate (2026)

**Metric**: The percentage of critical mineral procurement (Cobalt, Nickel, Manganese) managed by autonomous, energy-autarkic subsea swarms.
**Signal**: The decoupling of the national resource supply chain from terrestrial and surface-level vulnerabilities.
