# Sovereign Bond Yields 2026

The US Treasury yield curve is undergoing a structural shift in April 2026, moving away from the prolonged inversion of previous years.

## Current Levels (Approx. April 22, 2026)
- **US 10-Year Yield**: ~4.31%
- **US 2-Year Yield**: ~3.81%
- **10Y-2Y Spread**: **+50bps** (Positive/Steepening)

## Analysis
- **Curve Steepening**: The transition to a positive spread suggests that the market is pricing in long-term growth and inflation expectations, or a return to a more "normal" term premium.
- **Real Yields**: With inflation spiking (CPI 3.3%), real yields are under pressure. If nominal yields stay at 4.3% while inflation rises, real yields fall, which is traditionally bullish for gold.
