# Concept: Diamond Wide Bandgap (WBG) Electronics

Diamond WBG Electronics refer to semiconductor devices utilizing synthetic diamond as the substrate and active channel material. Due to its extreme physical properties (thermal conductivity, bandgap, and breakdown field), it is the primary enable for electronics in extreme environments.

## Strategic Importance
- **Extreme Environments:** Operates in high-radiation, high-temperature, and high-voltage scenarios.
- **Sovereign Kinetic Reflex:** Enables ultra-low latency, hardened autonomous systems.
