# Proprietary Indicator: QRSF-C (Quantum-Resistant Sovereign Fragility Convergence)

**Indicator Name:** QRSF-C (Quantum-Resistant Sovereign Fragility Convergence)
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Strategic Value:** High (Sovereign Risk / Asset Rotation)

## I. 3-Domain Synthesis
The QRSF-C synthesizes the following three distinct research domains:
1. **Quantum-Resistant Sovereign Finance:** Specifically the "Quantum Deadline" ($T_{deadline} = T_{QC} - T_{shelf}$) and the PQC migration status of central bank rails.
2. **Agentic Memory Architecture (AMA):** Specifically the capacity for recursive self-correction (RSCL) in autonomous sovereign wealth fund (SWF) management agents.
3. **Bio-Digital Interface Scaling:** Specifically the transition to "Integrated Bio-Logic" providing a non-silicon compute substrate for strategic defense/finance applications.

## II. Core Insight (Convergence Theory)
The QRSF-C identifies the precise moment when a sovereign state's financial infrastructure becomes "Quantum-Fragile" (crossing the Quantum Deadline) while its competitive rivals have shifted to a Bio-Digital compute substrate. 

The "Convergence" happens when the cost of PQC migration exceeds the state's fiscal capacity, but the *existence* of the threat (HNDL) triggers a preemptive capital flight from that state's digital assets into rivals' Bio-Digital secured assets. This is not a linear transition but a step-function collapse of trust in the state's "Digital Sovereignty."

## III. Map-Trigger-Lock Framework

### MAP
- **$\text{Fragility Score } (F_s)$:** Proportion of sovereign reserves held in classical (Non-PQC) addresses relative to the estimated $T_{deadline}$.
- **$\text{Compute Gap } (C_g)$:** Delta in inference/watt and plasticity between the state's silicon-based agentic memory and the rivals' Integrated Bio-Logic (Wetware) architectures.
- **$\text{Sovereignty Trust Index } (T_i)$:** Market-implied trust in the state's ability to maintain secure rails (measured via CDS spreads on digital-native bonds).

### TRIGGER
A **QRSF-C Event** is triggered when:
$$[F_s > \text{Critical Threshold}] \text{ AND } [C_g > \text{Strategic Threshold}] \text{ AND } [T_i \text{ decelerates sharply}]$$
*(Simultaneous crossing of financial fragility, compute obsolescence, and trust collapse)*

### LOCK
- **Full Signal:** Long-position in "Quantum-Sovereign" states (those with PQC rails + Bio-Digital compute); Short-position in "Quantum-Fragile" states.
- **Execution:** Immediate rotation fromClassical-reserve-backed assets $\rightarrow$ PQC-secured / Bio-Logic verified assets.

## IV. Historical Validation / Analog
**Analog:** The 19th-century shift from sail to steam. States that lacked the industrial compute (steam) and the secure logistics (coaling stations) saw their global influence collapse not when the technology existed, but when the *convergence* of speed and security made the old system instantly obsolete.
