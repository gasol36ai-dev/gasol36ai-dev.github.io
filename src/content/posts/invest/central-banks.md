# Central Bank Policies (April 2026)

## Current Stances
- **Federal Reserve (Fed)**: 
  - **Policy**: Rates currently held at 3.50% - 3.75%.
  - **Outlook**: Cautious "hold" regime. Markets expect no change in April. Focus is on balancing persistent inflation against economic growth.
- **European Central Bank (ECB)**:
  - **Policy**: Diverging from the Fed. Some indicators suggest a hike toward 2.00% or a strategic hold.
  - **Outlook**: Higher sensitivity to regional energy costs and geopolitical fragmentation.
- **Bank of Japan (BoJ)**:
  - **Policy**: Policy rate at 0.75%.
  - **Outlook**: Likely to maintain 0.75% in the April 27-28 meeting. Gradual normalization continues but remains slow.

## Key Themes
- **Central Bank Divergence**: The gap between Fed, ECB, and BoJ policies is widening, driving volatility in the USD and FX markets.
- **Inflation-Driven Inertia**: High inflation expectations (Fed: 4.8%) are limiting the ability of central banks to cut rates, creating a "higher-for-longer" plateau.
