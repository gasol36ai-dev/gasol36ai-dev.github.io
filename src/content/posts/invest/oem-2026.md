# Orbital Economic Microstructures (OEM) - 2026 Research Report

## 1. Executive Summary
Orbital Economic Microstructures (OEM) refers to the nascent economic frameworks and logistical systems emerging in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) and beyond. As space becomes an extension of the terrestrial economy, OEM governs the extraction, exchange, and settlement of resources in a vacuum/microgravity environment.

## 2. Core Pillars of OEM

### A. Space DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks)
- **Mechanism**: Leveraging constellations of small satellites to provide decentralized services (compute, sensing, connectivity).
- **Strategic Value**: Resilience, low entry barriers for new actors, and permissionless orchestration of orbital assets.
- **Economic Model**: Tokenized incentive structures for satellite operators to maintain network uptime and data quality.

### B. LEO Resource Extraction & Logistics
- **Mechanism**: Automated mining of asteroids or lunar regolith, coupled with orbital manufacturing (using microgravity for high-purity materials).
- **Process**: Extraction $\rightarrow$ Refinement $\rightarrow$ Orbital Manufacturing $\rightarrow$ Downlink/Upstream deployment.
- **Advantage**: Drastic reduction in launch costs for space-based infrastructure by "living off the land."

### C. Orbital Liquidity & Settlement
- **Mechanism**: Real-time settlement of economic activities in space using space-native digital assets or cross-chain bridges to terrestrial finance.
- **Constraint**: High latency and communication disruptions requiring "Local Settlement" capabilities (e.g., orbital-based RWA tokenization).

## 3. Strategic Implications & Transmission Mapping

| Event | Mechanism | Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LEO Congestion** | Space DePIN orchestration | Automated collision avoidance and dynamic spectrum allocation. |
| **Resource Scarcity (Terrestrial)** | Orbital Resource Supply | Shift in global commodity markets (e.g., Rare Earth Elements) towards space-derived supply. |
| **Orbital-Terrestrial Arbitrage** | Cross-domain liquidity | Emergence of "Space-Macro" volatility driven by orbital asset availability and launch costs. |

## 4. Convergence Point: The "Gravity-Finance" Nexus
OEM represents the decoupling of economic growth from terrestrial mass constraints, enabling a multi-planetary economy where value is generated in the vacuum and settled via decentralized protocols.

## 5. Technical Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk**: Kinetic/Electronic Warfare in LEO.
- **Mitigation**: Robust, decentralized swarm architectures (Space DePIN) that are resilient to individual node loss.
- **Risk**: Regulatory Fragmentation (Space Law).
- **Mitigation**: Standardized, blockchain-based smart contracts for multi-jurisdictional orbital commerce.
