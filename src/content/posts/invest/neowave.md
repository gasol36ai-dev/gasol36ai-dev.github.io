# NEoWave Theory

NEoWave is an advanced, objective evolution of the Elliott Wave Principle developed by Glenn Neely. It seeks to remove the subjectivity inherent in traditional wave counting.

## NEoWave vs. Elliott Wave

| Dimension | Elliott Wave | NEoWave |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| **Approach** | Intuitive / Interpretive | Logical / Scientific |
| **Validation** | Anticipatory (hopes pattern follows) | Post-occurrence (confirms pattern) |
| **Rules** | Broad guidelines | Strict, specific rules |
| **Metrics** | Price levels | Price, Time, and Complexity |

## The Three Core Pillars
1. **Logic**: Every pattern must follow a strict internal logic; if one rule is broken, the count is invalid.
2. **Self-Defining**: The pattern itself must provide the evidence for its identification.
3. **Self-Confirming**: The subsequent price movement must validate the predicted structural outcome.

## Quantitative Edge
NEoWave incorporates **time and complexity** as primary variables, allowing for more accurate forecasting of trend reversals and corrective phases compared to traditional TA.
