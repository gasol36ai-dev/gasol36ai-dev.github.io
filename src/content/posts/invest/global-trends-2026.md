# Global Macroeconomic Trends - April 2026

## 🌍 Current State Overview
As of April 2026, the global economy is transitioning from a period of aggressive tightening to a volatile "Normalization" phase, heavily influenced by geopolitical shocks in the Middle East.

### 🏦 Central Bank Policies
- **US Federal Reserve (Fed)**: 
    - **Stance**: Cautious Hold. Prime rate remains around 6.75%.
    - **Focus**: Balancing the cooling of core inflation (Core PCE ~2.6%) against headline spikes driven by energy costs.
- **European Central Bank (ECB)**: 
    - **Stance**: Vigilant. Monitoring the 2% inflation target while assessing the impact of Middle East instability on Eurozone growth.
- **Bank of Japan (BoJ)**: 
    - **Stance**: Steady at 0.75%, but shifting toward a "light stagflation" outlook.
    - **Key Update**: Core inflation forecast revised upward to 2.8% (from 1.9%) due to supply-side energy shocks (Iran war). Growth forecast cut to 0.5%.

### 📈 Inflation & Yields
- **Inflation**:
    - **Core Trends**: Cooling trend observed (Core CPI ~2.4-2.6%).
    - **Headline Risks**: High risk of spikes due to the closure of the Strait of Hormuz and rising oil prices.
- **Sovereign Bond Yields (US)**:
    - **10Y Treasury**: ~4.31%
    - **2Y Treasury**: ~3.78%
    - **Real Yields**: Rising nominal yields are lifting real yields, which typically weighs on non-yielding assets like gold, but a "Trust Deficit" is creating a decoupling.

### ⚠️ Geopolitical Risk
- **Primary Catalyst**: Iran Conflict / Middle East Instability.
- **Impact**: 
    - Brent Crude: $105+ / barrel.
    - WTI: $94+ / barrel.
    - Systemic risk premium embedded in gold and energy assets.
