# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Energy-Compute Autarky Index (SECAI)

**Status: PROPRIETARY**  
**Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock**  
**Convergence Domains: Energy-Inference Nexus $\times$ Sovereign Defense $\times$ Bio-Digital Convergence**

## 1. Executive Summary
The **SECAI** measures a nation-state or large-scale enterprise's ability to maintain autonomous, high-density computational intelligence without external energy or silicon dependencies. It identifies the inflection point where "Compute-Energy Autarky" becomes a decisive strategic advantage.

## 2. The Framework (Map-Trigger-Lock)

### A. MAP (The Inputs)
- **[Energy Density Stability (EDS)]**: The ratio of reliable, localized baseload power (SMR/Fusion) to total computational energy demand.
- **[Edge-to-Cloud Autonomy Ratio (ECAR)]**: The percentage of critical intelligence workloads executed on sovereign, decentralized edge hardware vs. external/cloud-based infrastructure.
- **[Biological Integration Readiness (BIR)]**: The deployment level of bio-digital interfaces or synthetic-biology-based compute (e.g., DNA-storage/processing) within the national defense or industrial core.

### B. TRIGGER (The Inflection Point)
- **Condition**: `[EDS < 1.2] AND [ECAR < 0.4] AND [BIR convergence with Edge AI]`
- **Event**: A simultaneous contraction in energy availability and a surge in external cloud-intelligence reliance, occurring during a period of heightened geopolitical volatility.

### C. LOCK (The Strategic Response)
- **Action**: **"Autarky Protocol Activation"**.
- **Automated Response**: 
    1. Immediate shift of mission-critical workloads to localized, neuromorphic-edge clusters.
    2. Automatic acceleration of SMR-to-Compute direct-link procurement.
    3. Prioritization of Bio-Digital interface deployment for neural-secure communications.

## 3. Transmission Mapping
`[Energy Baseload Instability] -> [Cloud-Intelligence Dependency Spike] -> [Sovereign Intelligence Compromise]`

## 4. Strategic Value
SECAI provides a high-fidelity early warning for "Intelligence Blackouts" where a nation loses its ability to compute and decide autonomously during a crisis due to energy or infrastructure decoupling.
