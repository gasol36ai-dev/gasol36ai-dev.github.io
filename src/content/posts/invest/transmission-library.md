# Macro-to-Asset Transmission Library

This library documents the causal links between key macro variables and specific asset class reactions, used to power the Macro Judgment Base (MJB).

## 1. Interest Rate & Yield Dynamics

| Macro Variable | Transmission Mechanism | Asset Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\uparrow$ US 10Y Real Yields** | Increases opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets. | $\downarrow$ Gold (Traditional), $\downarrow$ High-duration Growth Stocks (Tech) |
|| **$\uparrow$ US 10Y-2Y Spread (Normalization)** | Signals transition from recession fears to inflation/term-premium regime. | $\uparrow$ Infrastructure, $\uparrow$ Energy, $\rightarrow$ Sector Rotation from Mega-Cap Growth |
|| **$\uparrow$ Warsh Trade Reversal** | Markets shift from cutting expectations to tightening/inflation concerns. | $\uparrow$ Term Premium, $\uparrow$ Long-end Yields, $\downarrow$ Bond Prices, $\downarrow$ Tech Valuations |
| **$\uparrow$ USD Index (DXY)** | Increases cost of dollar-denominated debt; attracts capital to USD. | $\downarrow$ Emerging Markets (EM), $\downarrow$ Commodities (Gold/Oil), $\downarrow$ EM FX |
| **$\uparrow$ Fed Rate (Hawkishness)** | Tightens liquidity; increases borrowing costs. | $\downarrow$ Equity Valuations, $\uparrow$ USD |

## 2. Inflation & Commodity Links

| Macro Variable | Transmission Mechanism | Asset Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\uparrow$ Oil (Brent/WTI)** | Increases input costs; drives headline inflation. | $\uparrow$ Energy Stocks, $\uparrow$ Inflation, $\downarrow$ Consumer Discretionary |
| **$\uparrow$ Headline Inflation** | Triggers central bank hawkishness; erodes real purchasing power. | $\uparrow$ Commodities, $\uparrow$ TIPS, $\downarrow$ Fixed Income (Real Value) |
| **$\uparrow$ Inflation (Secondary Effects)** | Wage-price spirals; service sector inflation. | $\uparrow$ Real Estate (Rent), $\uparrow$ Value Stocks |
| **$\uparrow$ AI-driven Services Inflation** | AI infrastructure spending drives service prices (cloud, software, chips). | $\uparrow$ Tech Semis, $\uparrow$ Cloud Revenue, $\rightarrow$ Core PCE > CPI |
| **PCE-CPI Wedge ($\uparrow$)** | Fed's preferred gauge (PCE) rises faster than CPI due to AI/Energy weights. | $\downarrow$ Fed Cut Probability $\rightarrow$ $\uparrow$ Real Yields $\rightarrow$ $\downarrow$ Tech Valuations |

## 3. Geopolitical & Structural Shifts

| Macro Variable | Transmission Mechanism | Asset Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\uparrow$ Geopolitical Risk (e.g., Middle East)** | Triggers "flight to quality"; increases systemic uncertainty. | $\uparrow$ Gold (Safe Haven), $\uparrow$ Defense, $\uparrow$ Oil |
| **$\uparrow$ De-dollarization (Central Bank Flows)** | Reduces structural demand for USD-denominated assets. | $\uparrow$ Gold, $\uparrow$ Multi-polar FX (e.g., CNY, INR), $\downarrow$ US Treasuries (Long-term) |
| **$\uparrow$ US Credit Rating Risk (Downgrade)** | Creates permanent risk premium on sovereign debt. | $\uparrow$ Gold, $\uparrow$ Yield Spreads, $\downarrow$ Real Value of Treasuries |
| **$\uparrow$ Iran Conflict Duration** | Prolonged conflict sustains energy shock and inflation expectations. | $\uparrow$ Inflation Expectations, $\uparrow$ Oil Volatility, $\downarrow$ Fed Cut Probability |

| **Fed Leadership Transition** | Uncertainty regarding Fed independence and policy direction. | $\uparrow$ Volatility (USD, Yields, Equities) |
