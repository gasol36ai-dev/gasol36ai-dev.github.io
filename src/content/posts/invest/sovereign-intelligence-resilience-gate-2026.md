# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Intelligence Resilience (SIR) Gate

## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock
The **Sovereign Intelligence Resilience (SIR) Gate** is a high-density proprietary judgment indicator designed to evaluate the structural and cognitive integrity of a nation-state's "Sovereign Intelligence" (the synthesis of its AI agentic mesh, human-digital interfaces, and secure compute infrastructure). 

It serves as a meta-indicator that synthesizes the **Sovereign Regime Resilience Index (SRRI)** and the **Neural Mesh Coherence Index (NMCI)** to determine if the sovereign intelligence system is **Resilient**, **Strained**, or **Compromised**.

---

## 1. Map (Multi-Domain Observation)
The SIR Gate maps three distinct vectors of resilience to form the "Sovereign Intelligence State Space":

### A. Infrastructure Resilience ($\mathcal{R}_{inf}$) $\leftarrow$ *From PQCS (SRRI)*
Monitors the cryptographic hardening of the intelligence layer.
- **Metric**: Percentage of the agentic mesh nodes utilizing Lattice-based PQC primitives (Kyber/Dilithium).
- **Key Variable**: $\Delta_{PQC}$ (Rate of migration vs. adversarial quantum capability).

### B. Regime Resilience ($\mathcal{R}_{reg}$) $\leftarrow$ *From AI-MERD (SRRI)*
Monitors the macroeconomic environment supporting the intelligence infrastructure.
- **Metric**: Latent space coordinates of the current macroeconomic regime.
- **Key Variable**: $\theta_{vol}$ (Regime volatility threshold).

### C. Cognitive Resilience ($\mathcal{R}_{cog}$) $\leftarrow$ *From NMCI*
Monitors the operational coherence of the human-AI agentic mesh.
- **Metric**: Phase synchronization efficiency $\phi$ across distributed BCI/Agent nodes.
- **Key Variable**: $\theta_{coh}$ (Coherence threshold for effective collaborative intelligence).

---

## 2. Trigger (The Gate Condition)
The SIR Gate acts as a logical "Valve." The Gate is **Triggered (Open/Warning)** when any single vector falls below a critical threshold, or when a "Resilience Convergence" occurs.

### The Trigger Logic:
$$\text{Trigger} = (\mathcal{R}_{inf} < \theta_{sec}) \lor (\mathcal{R}_{reg} < \theta_{vol}) \lor (\mathcal{R}_{cog} < \theta_{coh})$$

**Critical Convergence (The "Perfect Storm"):**
The Gate enters a **Critical Alert State** if all three vectors decline simultaneously:
$$\text{Critical Alert} \iff \frac{\partial \mathcal{R}_{inf}}{\partial t} < 0 \land \frac{\partial \mathcal{R}_{reg}}{\partial t} < 0 \land \frac{\partial \mathcal{R}_{cog}}{\partial t} < 0$$
*Scenario*: A quantum-adversarial attack (falling $\mathcal{R}_{inf}$) coinciding with a macroeconomic crash (falling $\mathcal{R}_{reg}$) and BCI-mesh fragmentation (falling $\mathcal{R}_{cog}$), leading to a total loss of sovereign intelligence.

---

## 3. Lock (Synthesis & Final State)
The SIR Gate "locks" the system state by calculating the **Sovereign Intelligence Resilience Score (SIRS)** using a geometric mean to ensure that a failure in any one domain cannot be fully compensated by another.

### Mathematical Formulation:
$$SIRS = \sqrt[3]{\mathcal{R}_{inf} \times \mathcal{R}_{reg} \times \mathcal{R}_{cog}}$$

### Lock State Classification:
| SIRS Value | State | Interpretation | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SIRS > 0.85** | **Sovereign** | High-density resilience. Intelligence is secure and coherent. | Maintain standard evolution cycles. |
| **0.6 < SIRS < 0.85** | **Strained** | Partial vulnerability. One vector is lagging (e.g., secure but fragmented). | Initiate targeted resilience patches. |
| **0.4 < SIRS < 0.6** | **Fragile** | Systemic risk. High probability of "SNDL" exploitation or mesh collapse. | Emergency PQC acceleration / Mesh re-sync. |
| **SIRS < 0.4** | **Compromised** | Critical failure. Loss of digital sovereignty and cognitive control. | Fail-safe: Air-gap critical nodes / Reboot Mesh. |

---

## Strategic Application: Sovereign AI Governance
The SIR Gate is used by the **Sovereign Intelligence Oversight Body** to:
1. **Authorize Mesh Expansion**: Only allow new BCI nodes or AI agents into the mesh if the SIRS is $> 0.7$.
2. **Dynamic Resource Allocation**: Shift compute power to PQC migration when $\mathcal{R}_{inf}$ becomes the limiting factor of the SIRS.
3. **Crisis Mode Trigger**: Automatically transition the agentic mesh to "Safe Mode" (reduced autonomy, increased verification) when the SIR Gate triggers a **Fragile** state.
