# Macroeconomic Regime: May 2026
**Status**: Active
**Regime Type**: Geopolitical-Inflationary Shock

## 1. Core Narrative
The global economy is currently dominated by an energy-driven inflationary shock stemming from intensified Middle East (Iran) tensions. This has created a "Sticky Inflation" environment where headline figures are spiking, forcing central banks into a divergent and hawkish posture, which in turn puts pressure on growth-oriented asset valuations.

## 2. Key Macro Variables (Current State)
- **US Inflation**: 
    - April CPI: 3.8% YoY (0.6% MoM) - *Rising*
    - April PPI: 6.0% YoY (1.4% MoM) - *Significant acceleration, signal for future PCE*
    - Core CPI: 2.8% YoY (0.4% MoM) - *Sticky*
- **Central Bank Policy**:
    - **Fed**: "Hawkish Hold". Expected to keep rates on hold throughout 2026 due to core services persistence. Funds rate ~3.64%.
    - **ECB**: Transitioning to hawkish. Single rate hike expected in June (Deposit rate 2.00% $\rightarrow$ 2.25%).
    - **BoJ**: Gradual normalization. Focus on inflation risk (PPI 4.9%). Moving toward 1.0%.
- **Bond Yields**:
    - US 10Y: ~4.59% (Highest in a year)
    - US 2Y: ~4.07%
    - German 10Y: ~3.17%
    - JGB 10Y: ~2.71%

## 3. Geopolitical Drivers
- **Iran Conflict / Strait of Hormuz**: Primary driver of oil price volatility. Brent >$100/bbl acts as a structural floor for inflation and a trigger for flight-to-quality.
- **US-China Tech Trade**: Ongoing tariffs and restrictions maintain a baseline of structural inflation in the tech supply chain.
