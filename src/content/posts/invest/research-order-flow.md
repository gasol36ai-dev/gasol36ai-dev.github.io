# Order Flow Analysis

Order Flow is the study of the actual buy and sell orders entering the market in real-time. It is the "microstructure" that drives the "macro" charts.

## Key Metrics
- **Order Flow Imbalance**: A significant difference between the volume traded at the bid vs. the ask.
- **Delta**: The net difference between market buys and market sells.
- **Absorption**: When aggressive market orders are absorbed by passive limit orders (e.g., an "Iceberg" order), causing price to stop moving despite high imbalance.

## Tools of the Trade
- **Footprint Charts**: Display volume at every price level.
- **Heatmaps (e.g., Bookmap)**: Visualize the limit order book (depth) and where liquidity is resting.

## Strategic Value
Allows the trader to see the "battle" in real-time. While a chart shows *where* price went, order flow shows *why* it went there and whether the move is sustainable.
