# Proprietary Indicator: Sovereign Regime Resilience Index (SRRI)
## Framework: Map-Trigger-Lock

The **Sovereign Regime Resilience Index (SRRI)** is a proprietary judgment indicator designed to assess the stability and security of a nation-state's digital and economic infrastructure during a macroeconomic regime shift, specifically in the face of a quantum-adversarial environment. It synthesizes **Post-Quantum Cryptographic Sovereignty (PQCS)** and **AI-Driven Macro-Economic Regime Detection (AI-MERD)**.

### 1. Map (Observation & Feature Extraction)
The SRRI monitors two primary data streams:
- **Cryptographic State Map**: A real-time audit of the percentage of critical infrastructure migrated to PQC primitives (Lattice-based Kyber/Dilithium) and the availability of sovereign QRNG seeds.
- **Economic Regime Map**: AI-MERD latent space coordinates indicating the current macroeconomic regime (e.g., "High-Inflation / High-Volatility").

The "Map" phase calculates the **Exposure Gap**: the difference between the current cryptographic security level and the projected quantum-capability of the primary adversary.

### 2. Trigger (Signal Detection & Threshold)
The SRRI triggers a "Resilience Alert" when the following condition is met:
`T = (Regime_Volatility > θ_vol) AND (Exposure_Gap > θ_sec)`

Where:
- `θ_vol`: The threshold for regime instability detected by AI-MERD.
- `θ_sec`: The maximum acceptable exposure gap before the risk of "SNDL" (Store Now, Decrypt Later) becomes critical during a period of economic instability.

A trigger occurs when a detected macroeconomic regime shift coincides with a significant lag in PQC migration, suggesting that the state's economic instability may be exploited via quantum-enabled decryption of strategic communications.

### 3. Lock (Consensus & Synthesis)
The final SRRI score is "locked" using a weighted integration:
`SRRI = (PQC_Migration_Rate * w1) + (Regime_Stability_Score * w2) - (Adversarial_Quantum_Progress * w3)`

- **PQC_Migration_Rate**: % of sovereign systems transitioned to PQC.
- **Regime_Stability_Score**: The stability of the current regime as identified by AI-MERD.
- **Adversarial_Quantum_Progress**: An estimate of the adversary's Qubit count and error-correction fidelity.

**Lock State**:
- **SRRI > 0.8**: Sovereign Resilience (Secure and Stable).
- **0.5 < SRRI < 0.8**: Transitional Vulnerability (Secure but Unstable, or Stable but Insecure).
- **SRRI < 0.5**: Systemic Risk (Critical failure point).

## Application
The SRRI is used to trigger emergency PQC migration accelerators or to shift sovereign assets into "Quantum-Safe" havens when the index drops below the 0.5 threshold.

### Resilience Scoring Tiers
- **Tier 1: Sovereign Autarky (SRRI > 0.85)**: Complete PQC readiness and economic regime stability.
- **Tier 2: Managed Vulnerability (0.6 < SRRI < 0.85)**: Transition in progress; requires active monitoring of regime-shift signals.
- **Tier 3: Structural Fragility (0.4 < SRRI < 0.6)**: High risk of "SNDL" exploitation during economic turbulence.
- **Tier 4: Systemic Collapse (SRRI < 0.4)**: Immediate threat of catastrophic loss of digital sovereignty and economic stability.

## Strategic Use Case: Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)
For CBDCs, the SRRI is a critical metric for ensuring the long-term viability of the digital sovereign currency. A drop in SRRI below 0.6 would necessitate an immediate, mandatory upgrade of all participant node cryptographic modules and a temporary increase in liquidity buffers to manage potential "Inference Flash Crashes" or "Quantum-Induced Liquidity Drains."
