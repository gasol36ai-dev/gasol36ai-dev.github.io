# Proprietary Indicator: Quantum-Sovereign Divergence Gate (QSDG)

## Concept Overview
The **Quantum-Sovereign Divergence Gate (QSDG)** is a multi-domain judgment indicator designed to detect the non-linear inflection point where a nation's digital-physical infrastructure becomes critically vulnerable to quantum-era asymmetric threats. It identifies the "Sovereignty Gap" between a nation's adoption of advanced compute/space assets and its implementation of Post-Quantum (PQ) defensive layers.

## 🗺️ The Map: Convergence Vectors
The QSDG synthesizes three disparate domains into a single decision gate:

1.  **Compute Autarky (Physical/Cyber):** The ratio of domestic vs. foreign-controlled high-performance computing (HPC) and AI-accelerator capacity.
2.  **Orbital Intelligence (Space/DePIN):** The density of sovereign-controlled LEO/MEO sensor assets vs. commercial/adversarial-controlled mesh networks.
3.  **Cryptographic Resilience (Security/Finance):** The percentage of national "rails" (financial, identity, and defense) that have successfully transitioned to hybrid or pure PQC (Post-Quantum Cryptography) standards.

## ⚡ The Trigger: The "Sovereignty Gap" Inflection
The indicator triggers a **"High Alert"** when the following condition is met:
$$\text{QSDG} = \frac{\Delta \text{Compute Autarky} \times \Delta \text{Orbital Intelligence}}{\Delta \text{Cryptographic Resilience}} > \text{Threshold}_T$$

Where:
- **$\Delta$** represents the rate of change over a rolling 18-month window.
- **High Threshold ($T$):** Indicates a "Sovereignty Trap" where a nation is rapidly expanding its digital/orbital footprint without a commensurate upgrade to its cryptographic defense, creating a massive, attractive target for "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) attacks.

## 🔒 The Lock: Strategic Reaction Patterns
When the QSDG threshold is breached, the following strategic responses are mandated:

| Reaction Level | Action Type | Target Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1: Defensive Hardening** | Cryptographic Injection | Mandatory PQC hybridization for all critical national data rails. |
| **Level 2: Structural Diversification** | Orbital DePIN Expansion | Subsidizing domestic/sovereign LEO constellations to reduce reliance on foreign mesh networks. |
| **Level 3: Autarky Acceleration** | Compute Onshoring | Emergency transition of critical AI/HPC workloads to fully domestic, quantum-hardened hardware. |
