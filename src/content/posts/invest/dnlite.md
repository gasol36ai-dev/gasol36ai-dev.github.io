# DNlite (Shin-Ying Biomed)

## Overview
DNlite is a diagnostic biomarker developed by Shin-Ying Biomed, focusing on post-translationally modified fetuin-A (uPTM-FeA) in urine for the detection of renal dysfunction.

## Product Detail
- **Biomarker**: Urinary post-translationally modified fetuin A (uPTM-FeA)
- **Assay**: Colorimetric immunoassay (DNLite IVD103) for quantitative measurement of unique Fetuin-A with specific post-translational modification in human urine.
- **Application**: Early detection of kidney graft dysfunction, chronic kidney disease, and acute kidney injury.
