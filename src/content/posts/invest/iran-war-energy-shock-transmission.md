# Iran War & Energy Shock Transmission (2026)

The conflict involving Iran in early 2026 has established a dominant macro-transmission mechanism that overrides traditional cyclical trends.

## The Transmission Chain

**Primary Chain: The Inflationary Spiral**
`[Iran Conflict / Strait of Hormuz Risk] -> [$\uparrow$ Brent Crude (Forecasts $85-$120+)] -> [$\uparrow$ Headline Inflation (US CPI jumped to 3.3% in March)] -> [$\uparrow$ Sovereign Bond Yields (US 10Y ~4.39%)] -> [$\downarrow$ Valuation Compression for Growth Assets]`

**Secondary Chain: The Safe-Haven Flight**
`[Geopolitical Instability] -> [$\uparrow$ Systemic Risk] -> [$\uparrow$ Gold Demand] -> [$\uparrow$ Gold Price (All-time high $5,595 in Jan)]`

## Critical Variables to Monitor
- **Brent Crude Price**: The trigger. A break above $100 usually accelerates the chain.
- **Headline vs. Core CPI**: If energy shocks bleed into core services (second-round effects), the Fed's "patience" will end, leading to a hawkish pivot.
- **US 10Y Yield**: The transmission to equities. Rising yields compress P/E multiples for high-duration AI/Tech stocks.

## Regime Shift
We have moved from a "Growth/Recession" debate to an "Energy/Inflation" regime. In this environment, the "Cost of Energy" is the primary scaling constraint for both global GDP and AI data center expansion (the Power Bottleneck).
